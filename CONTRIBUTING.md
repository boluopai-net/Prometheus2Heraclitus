# Conversation and independent works

[简体中文](CONTRIBUTING.zh-CN.md) · [Collection](README.md)

This is a personal collection maintained by boluopai. There are two ways to
continue the conversation: publish your own work and mention or link this
repository, or open an [Issue](https://github.com/boluopai-net/Prometheus2Heraclitus/issues)
with a question, correction, suggestion, or song request for boluopai.
**This repository does not accept pull requests, including code, Skills,
translations, documentation, and recording submissions.** The author maintains
and publishes changes to this collection.

## Write and publish in your own space

Your work can be philosophy or another kind of article: a reading response,
criticism, a technical account, or something prompted by a question here.
Keep it in your own repository, website, or publishing space, where you decide
its direction and maintenance. You may mention or link this repository and
the specific material you engage with. An Issue can share that link and explain
the connection. Agreement with the author's views is unnecessary.

Reading and using the resources does not oblige you to write. You may also
copy, adapt, or fork material under its applicable license and maintain your
own version. Independent reuse does not require a contribution to this repository.

## Ask through Issues

For a question or correction, link the relevant article, section, or tool and
explain what needs discussion. For a technical problem, include enough context
and reproducible steps to understand the observed result. Suggestions about
methods, tools, or adaptations can also be discussed there; accepted changes
are authored and published by the maintainer.

The [singing station](https://boluopai.net/singing/en/) accepts song requests
**for boluopai only**. Give the song title and, when useful, the original artist
or version you mean. It is not a recording-submission or guest-performer venue.

## Sources, privacy, and reuse

Issues are public. Keep credentials, private conversations, personal portraits,
and unpublished material with their owners; use a minimal public example when
reporting a problem.

YinYang's original material uses CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; other reusable Skill packages
retain their bundled MIT licenses. Respect the applicable attribution and
third-party terms when reusing material. Your independently authored work keeps
its own rights. [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) explains the collection's scope.
