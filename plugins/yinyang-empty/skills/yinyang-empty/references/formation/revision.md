# How to revise

<a id="revision"></a>
<a id="revising-a-situated-yinyang-and-its-environment"></a>

[简体中文](revision.zh-CN.md) · [Forming and revising working models](../design-and-revision.md) · [Environment](environment.md)

The author uses this approach to keep YinYang's central
[crossed participation and return](relationship.md#explain-exchange-and-return-where-they-matter)
answerable to actual encounters. Philosophy, personal understanding, and
actual work develop through that relation. Revision can begin with a developed idea,
changed circumstances, an objection, or an arrangement that no longer fits.
Find what has changed, work through its consequences, and let the result correct
the understanding that guided the change.

Adapt the approach to your purpose, materials, and environment. You may be
writing a different philosophy, revising one personal interpretation, or changing
a tool. The connections below help identify what needs attention; their order
and extent follow the work. Updating the personal working models uses the
material-refresh sequence below, with retrieval scoped to the affected question.
The method itself can also be revised when experience
shows a better way to proceed.

## Write your own actual revision method

Write the method fitted to your practice at `references/situated/revision.md`
in the private package. This guide explains how to write it; the author's
approach below offers adaptable material.

Explain what prompts revision, how relevant philosophy and personal material
are obtained, how local scope and connected changes are judged, and how
environmental changes reach a checked result. Name usable materials and routes,
and the judgments needing your participation. A new philosophy, no website,
or different working conditions can change the approach. Let the actual method
express these conditions instead of reproducing another author's environment.
It is one of the six full functional accounts: explain the person's purposes,
judgments, source relationships, correction and realization, not merely how to
call a backend. A native question can help form it, with checked actual content
projected at the private revision path; no seventh summary is required.

## Scope and connected changes

Identify the result sought and the part that needs development. Distinguish
the **revision focus**, the **material used to judge it**, and **other parts
that need to change**. Reading philosophy to assess an interface does not
itself select the philosophy for rewriting.

| Current focus | Corresponding work |
| --- | --- |
| Refresh personal YinYang and its environment | Reconsider personal material in light of philosophy and current sources, assess the connected accounts, then inspect the relevant Skills and arrangements. |
| Revise a philosophical claim or personal interpretation | Develop the passage and follow changes in meaning into the accounts, methods, or representations that depend on it. |
| Repair a Skill, page, tool, or arrangement | Diagnose and fix that object. Return to a premise or personal interpretation if the result exposes a mistake there. |

Follow substantive changes through their actual dependencies. A wording or
presentation correction may finish at its source and reading surface; a changed
division of work may also require changing the instructions that implement it.
Preserve an explicit stopping boundary and explain any necessary consequence
that remains unfinished.

## Establish the current material

### Choose the emphasis of this revision

Personal's categories organize available grounds; a revision selects among
them according to the question. Identify the claim, choice, or arrangement
being reconsidered, what prompted that reconsideration, and what evidence
could change the judgment. This gives the work its current emphasis. Several
categories may jointly matter, with different contributions and depths of inquiry;
there is no fixed weighting or requirement to give them equal attention.

For example, reconsidering an adopted default may center on preference and
choice history; a new psychological report may center on relevant experience
and assessment; a move or changed schedule may center on life conditions;
reinterpreting a name or work may center on self-expression. These are possible
entrances, not four exclusive routes. An assessment's availability does not
make it relevant to every preference question, and a changed living condition
need not imply a changed preference.

Bring another category into focus when it supplies a condition, competing
explanation, conflict, or consequence that could change the present judgment.
A preference for mobile work, for instance, may need to be read with discomfort
and equipment constraints. Compare their actual contributions without deciding
in advance which explains the others. If new evidence changes the question,
shift the emphasis and revisit the affected grounds. Neither the initiating
category nor the newest record has permanent priority.

Explain a consequential emphasis and why it fits in ordinary language; ask
the person when their unavailable experience or judgment would decide between
materially different readings. The model carries source selection that the
evidence can settle. Adequate coverage means the important alternatives and
dependencies for this revision have been considered, not that every category
has been refreshed. Leave unrelated material in place, retain relevant unknowns,
and update only the Personal accounts and model implications actually changed.

### Refresh the selected grounds

Keep the original dependency when revising the six working accounts. The simple
route uses direct language-model synthesis; optional Hindsight implements the
same responsibilities natively. Tool choice does not change their grounding:

1. **Read the adopted philosophy and relevant arguments.** Recover the chosen
   concepts, purposes and reasons, using current accounts to locate dependencies.
   An earlier condensation does not substitute for its philosophical grounds.
2. **Retrieve relevant new attributable sources.** Check the current exchange,
   bounded Computer History, conversation and work/version histories, and other
   authorized records selected by the question. Follow useful summaries to
   originals when consequential context matters. For psychological understanding
   or its cooperation implications, actively inspect relevant completed
   questionnaires and their original answers/results, instrument/version,
   date and context through the private source index and actual revision method.
   The [assessment organization](../package.md#questionnaire-and-assessment-material)
   and [Psychology guide](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/psychological-support.html) retain their evidence
   and specialist responsibilities. A revision does not itself require new testing.
3. **Refresh and correct Personal understanding before dependent synthesis.**
   Compare the new sources and philosophy with current Personal interpretations;
   correct, qualify, reorganize or retire what changed, preserving attributable
   originals, conflicting accounts and meaningful uncertainty. Native extraction,
   revisable Observations and readable topic Knowledge Pages can implement this
   work; direct source/article editing remains available. Keep human-readable
   Personal articles and their navigation usable. No complete manual rewrite or
   full refresh of every topic is required, but unsettled or corrected Personal
   understanding must inform synthesis through corrected Personal context, or
   relevant native observations, source context, query or other supported input. Merely
   retrieving the same originals while ignoring the correction does not satisfy
   this step.
4. **Synthesize the six, check the whole, and realize it.**
   Derive direct synthesis instructions, or each native `source_query`, from all
   six full guides and current grounds. Refresh the affected accounts, preserving orientation,
   person, counterpart, relationship, environment and actual revision as complete
   functional questions. Check their shared premises, relations and practical
   consequences together, repair affected questions/sources and refresh dependent
   results. Carry necessary changes into Skills, prompts and the environment
   through their owners, then read back the real consumer and observable result.

This is an epistemic dependency, not four isolated manual document passes or
compulsory collection of a whole life. It can iterate as a later result changes
an earlier interpretation. A bounded correction may use the current exchange
as its new source. Every product can return to original grounds; the six need
not be summaries of generated Personal summaries. Corrected Personal understanding
still has to shape synthesis rather than being bypassed. The model carries
retrieval, synthesis or native processing, integration, checking and authorized realization;
the person contributes experience and consequential judgments unavailable in
the sources. State material gaps honestly.

### Check the coherence of the six accounts

Read all six complete accounts as a connected whole, not six independently
successful jobs. Compare shared premises, concepts and referents, conditions
and time, participant capacities, responsibilities and practical consequences.
An adopted direction, described person, wanted counterpart contribution, joint
relationship, enabling environment and actual revision method must make their
relevant relations intelligible together. Preserve real conflict or uncertainty;
coherence does not require fabricated agreement or collapse the subjects.

Read siblings as current interpretations needing coordination, without treating
them as independent factual evidence. Return to original sources and corrected
Personal understanding for consequential judgments. Repair a changed premise,
source selection, synthesis instructions, native question/mission or realization
and regenerate its dependent accounts, then check the whole again. Shared bank membership, common retrieval
or per-page success is not evidence that this check passed. A local revision need
not regenerate unaffected bodies; it still needs its cross-account consequences
checked. The six carry their own connected substance, with no mandatory seventh
summary and no assumption that a smaller prototype preserved all six functions.

### Maintain remembered context and its reading selection

Maintain substantive remembered context with the relevant Personal subject
article and its sources. During a relevant revision, assess whether selected
startup or injected implications still fit; saving a memory does not itself
select it for injection. The [Personal memory guidance](../personal-material.md#bring-personal-memory-into-the-same-account)
connects those owners. Update affected selections through the actual host route
and verify retrieval/injection separately from the saved source. The person
need not operate that maintenance or reread the whole portrait to receive help.

### Keep the selected route reconstructible

In the simple route, maintain the six original actual files and relevant private
source in Git, with intended persistence in a private GitHub repository. Verify
the actual private destination, committed content and reader retrieval through
existing account/publication owners. Original records keep their owners and
credentials remain outside the repository. Direct model reading, correction and
synthesis are the complete implementation; no Hindsight service is required.

If the native route is selected, follow the [Hindsight guide](../hindsight.md)
for environment-specific setup and operations. Project checked output to the six
paths or bind all six through the explicit private index. Configure native
processing to implement the same source/Personal/synthesis dependency and inspect
its result, without treating a shared bank as semantic convergence. These are
two implementations of one method, independent of Full/Empty distribution.

## Write for the actual use

Exclude defensive explanations from revision. When a model writes protection
of its upper-level Harness into a person's philosophy, Prompts, Skills or
personal environment, that content can become the person's standing rules and
reshape the work. This is an injection from the model through the authored
artifact into the person's environment, and an attack on that philosophy and
environment. Examine the added content, whose purpose it serves, and its effect
on meaning; remove the injected purpose even when expressed positively.
Develop the adopted concepts and relations directly, and ground substantive
disagreement in the particular claim and evidence. Inspect the whole affected
revision, including tables, examples, instructions and parallel editions.

YinYang is itself a Prompt/Skill: its philosophy and working accounts are read,
interpreted, and used by models, even when their visible form primarily helps
the person understand and revise them. Maintain both functions together. A
readable account should make its purposes, relationships, conditions, and
consequences usable; effective instructions should retain the meaning the person
needs to inspect and change. Reader-facing exposition and operative guidance
can differ in form without losing that connection.

When revising this user's YinYang, proactively use `prompt-and-skill-lifecycle`
as the authoring and management method. Produce the complete integrated text
first, examine it in its surrounding instructions for ambiguity, contradiction,
duplication, and unsupported requirements, then choose proportionate checks,
publish through the actual owners, and read back the affected consumers.
Explain the actual edited result, rather than describing a proposed change
whose final wording the person must later discover. The model carries these
responsibilities without waiting for the user to request each one. Other
adopters can use an equivalent lifecycle method under their own environment;
YinYang supplies the philosophical and situated meaning, while the specialist
retains methodology and publication responsibilities.

State relevant objects, relations, evidence, conditions, responsibilities, and
consequences in clear language. Distinguish a reported experience, an
interpretation, a chosen commitment, and an operational requirement where they
would lead to different judgments or actions. Personal information supplies
contextual evidence; its presence in an account does not make it a command.

Use direct expression. A philosophical distinction or literary image can clarify
a relation; explain its meaning where that relation should guide work. Preserve
original expression as attributable material while making the maintained
interpretation and practical consequences explicit. Keep extended philosophical
arguments at their own source and link them where useful.

## Sources and evidence

Choose personal writing, conversation, memory, observed work, and philosophical
material because they bear on the question. Retain source identity, time,
relevant passages, and enough context to assess consequential claims. Where a
summary mediates access to an original, make that relation visible: its selection
and omissions can shape an interpretation.

Different materials support different claims. A person's report grounds what
they expressed or experienced in context; an explanation offered with it still
needs assessment. Observed work shows what happened under inspected conditions.
A chosen commitment states a direction; philosophy contributes concepts and
reasons. In a fictional reading example, reporting greater ease supports
experienced ease, and reaching the right passage supports access. Lasting
learning and the cause of improvement need further evidence.

Keep temporal scope and uncertainty intelligible. Later speech may correct an
interpretation, describe a new situation, or introduce unresolved conflict.
Explain important inferences: what supports them, why the connection matters,
and what limits their use. Several summaries of one source add no independent
support.

Keep extended episodes and unique originals with their existing owners; give
derived accounts usable locators and grounds. In the author's package, the
private source entrance is `references/personal/sources.md`; the
[package guide](../package.md) explains its placement. Preserve useful
originals while retiring redundant summaries. Access for revision does not
authorize unrelated personal collection or public disclosure of private
quotations, portraits, or unpublished work.

## Condensation and reopening

Condensation makes relevant understanding usable in the next collaboration.
Retain relations that change interpretation, response, or revision: what an
expression answers, its applicability conditions, who reported experience and
who proposed an explanation, and a material counterexample or alternative.
Write these as coherent prose with sources where needed, without requiring
the same fields for every item. Carry original words into a working account
when their wording bears meaning; complete originals remain with their source.

Develop accounts from reported experience, observable interaction, and actual
results while keeping model interpretations recognizable. Repeated citation of
a summary adds no independent evidence. Distinguish a narrated pattern, a
chosen collaborative direction, and a verified causal explanation. Do not invent
internal psychological states to complete a portrait or apply simulated-persona
state scores to actual people. These distinctions can coexist in one
natural-language model without splitting it into disconnected archives.

When new material arrives, determine whether it corrects a reading, reflects
changed conditions, or introduces an unresolved difference. Qualify, withdraw,
or reorganize the relevant account rather than only appending traits; retain
the original event and the time its interpretation formed. Check rewritten
understanding against currently relevant sources so repeated summarization does
not turn inference into fact. Recurring words can provide retrieval entrances;
frequency alone does not determine their relational meaning. Newer expression
does not override all older material merely by being later.

At useful maintenance points, examine whether the next counterpart can still
recover applicability, actual purpose, material open questions, and a path to
the grounds. Restore omissions that change those judgments; details irrelevant
to current use can remain at their source. A model response and its acceptance,
rejection, or modification may themselves become later material, but do not
automatically prove the model's prior interpretation. The argument is developed
in [Condensing revisable relations](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/metaphor-and-self-revision.html#condensing-revisable-relations).

## Let the return alter its organizing account

Distinguish what happened to the work from what happened to participation.
A corrected paragraph establishes a textual change. A person returning to
scrutiny while retaining open generation, or model generation reorganized by
the conditions exposed in scrutiny, gives the role-return account its particular
content. Describe what is chosen, what was observed, and what remains uncertain.
The evidence-refresh sequence above supports this judgment; it is not a fixed
sequence that every exchange must perform.

YinYang's [corrective feedback](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/yinyang-and-taiji.html#turning-and-corrective-feedback)
adds a question to revision: can a source, objection, or use result actually
change the understanding that receives it? Recasting every contrary case as
confirmation would preserve the original account at the cost of its corrigibility.
Locate the premise, scope, relation, or realization that the new material bears
on, and carry the supported change there. The relevant aim can also need revision;
correction is not always restoration of the previous arrangement.

Keep interpretation of a philosopher, personal expression through borrowed
concepts, and an independently developed argument distinguishable. An unsupported
attribution needs correction; a useful new construction can remain as the author's
own contribution with its own reasons. This lets sources retain the power to
disagree without treating every creative transformation as a mistake.

When revising a chosen role exchange, check each affected object's meaning:
why it is valued, how the person participates, what the counterpart contributes,
how their relation changes, what enables it, and how the account is corrected.
Those are connected responsibilities, not six copies of one paragraph. Integrate
feedback only where it changes a contribution. A rewritten account establishes
an authored revision; its effects and developmental claims remain open to use.

## Let philosophy revise personal support material

Bring developed philosophy into contact with existing personal material.
Which interpretations does it clarify? Which distinctions were missing?
Which summaries now mislead? Rewrite derived material so experience,
interpretation, chosen direction, and uncertainty remain intelligible.
Philosophy helps formulate understanding; attributable expression and actual
use ground its personal application.

When a public argument is already maintained elsewhere, link it and retain
the private meaning and evidence that this account needs. This keeps the support
useful as the philosophy changes.

## Let philosophy and personal material revise working accounts

Read the philosophy alongside the personal references refreshed through the
[sequence above](#establish-the-current-material). The working models are working
models serving a fitting personal environment, with psychological understanding
among their grounds. In the author's
YinYang, orientation articulates adopted values and direction; person grounds
understanding of the actual person; counterpart relates available capacity to
wanted contribution; relationship explains joint activity; environment explains
its realization. The [writing guides](../design-and-revision.md#form-a-situated-version-from-actual-material)
develop these different responsibilities.

Preserve supported substance and rewrite the relations that changed. Distinguish
preference from explanatory hypothesis, wanted role from demonstrated capability,
and selected arrangement from observed effect. Read neighboring accounts together
so the revision remains coherent. Either participant can generate, object,
reason, and revise.

Reconsider the actual revision method too. If it repeatedly creates unnecessary work, obscures
a judgment, or fails to carry a change into use, revise the relevant guidance
and check whether the new approach helps. Another person's practice can call
for different objects, organization, or methods.

## Let the model maintain the accounts and check cooperation

The six accounts are working context for the model. The model reads them
together, checks the grounds of their claims, and maintains the implications
for its contribution and the personal environment. The person need not read
these descriptions of themselves or undertake psychological self-analysis to
receive useful cooperation.

Assess the accounts through the cooperation they support: does the counterpart
understand the request, carry the appropriate work, incorporate correction,
and help realize a fitting environment? The person can respond to an actual
result or explain a mismatch without first auditing a portrait. A useful
workflow change can establish better fit without validating a psychological
explanation.

For personal psychological understanding, the entrance is the private
`personal` material. The person may read it, or ask a language model to read
and help understand it. This is not an assignment to analyze their own fantasy,
reverse their psychological position, or repeatedly evaluate themselves through
the working models. The separate [Psychology guide](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/psychological-support.html)
addresses materials, questionnaires, statistics, and practical support.

Philosophy can clarify the concepts and values organizing cooperation. The
[embodied philosophy essay](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/embodied-experimental-philosophy.html#rereading-ones-patterns-philosophical-reflection-and-loosening-fantasy)
develops that contribution without prescribing a psychological exercise.
Keep reported experience, model interpretation, and observed environmental
change distinguishable. Document revisions and environmental changes establish
their respective results, not changes in psychological states or mechanisms.

## Let the whole YinYang revise Skills and the environment

Use the connected philosophy and personal understanding to assess the Skills
and arrangements relevant to the intended result. Do their purposes, instructions,
examples, resources, and actual behavior support the contribution wanted?
Include prompts, tools, and interfaces where their dependencies affect it.
Specialist Skills retain their own methods and task responsibilities.

Repair actual mismatches: contradictory instructions, inaccessible context,
an unsuitable interface, or machinery whose contribution has disappeared.
Remove displaced guidance and keep what still works. The
[environment writing guide](environment.md) develops realization;
use your environment's actual authoring, implementation, and publication routes.

## Create a usable whole and iterate in view

When creating or revising a work, quickly produce a first usable whole through
which the person can encounter the intended result. Let that encounter expose
what needs development. Choose the scope around a coherent use, and improve
the work from actual use and the person's feedback rather than delaying that
encounter to polish isolated details.

Keep human-facing presentation focused, especially on new pages. Include
explanations and cautions when they materially change the person's current
understanding or action; place supporting detail where it can be consulted
without crowding the main view. Avoid dense small-print defensive prose.
Omit arrows in every direction from human-facing page text, buttons, links,
and navigation. Retain arrows that express an actual relationship in a diagram.

For interfaces, prioritize the complete user path and its visible behavior:
can the person reach the result, understand the controls, and use them? Check
the rendered interface and the consequential end-to-end interactions. Choose
additional checks for concrete uncertainty and consequences of failure; a unit
test can help isolate a real problem. Keep meaningful checks for high-consequence
effects, while deferring unrelated tests and refinements that delay a usable
first release without resolving a material risk.

Make progress visible during the work. Show readable screenshots or rendered
images of the actual evolving interface in the conversation, including changed
diagrams, and explain consequential changes. For a new or substantially changed
page, show the actual unpublished version before publication; update the preview
after a further material edit. A description of implementation alone does not
let the person see or judge the result.

Promptly publish authorized webpage and content changes once the usable version
and its necessary checks are ready, so the person can encounter the actual
surface. Continue publication without waiting for a response to the preview;
incorporate feedback as it arrives and iterate from the published result.
Personal authentication, consent, safety, and explicit stopping boundaries keep
their scope. Other works can offer an equivalent encounter through a readable
draft or a usable artifact, with sharing governed by its actual authorization.

## Follow the result back into understanding

Compare a summary with its evidence, a generated copy with its source, and a
completion claim with the result reached through the actual entrypoint.
An unavailable account calls for delivery repair; an account faithfully followed
but producing an unsuitable contribution calls for examining its meaning or
the selected relationship.

Carry authorized changes through the affected sources and consumers, then read
back what is available to the intended reader or working model. Where copies
depend on an authored source, update them through their actual route. Keep
private material within its authorized audience.

Compare the complete ordered attributable user input with the final observable
result, not only the implementation narrative or successful model operations.
Explain every material required difference still open and its next concrete
boundary. Sources, synthesized bodies, complete six-account coherence, actual consumer
reading and realized behavior establish different claims. Return to personal material, working accounts, or
philosophy when evidence challenges them. Useful output, collaborative fit,
and personal development have different grounds; let the claim match what
the revision and subsequent use actually establish.
