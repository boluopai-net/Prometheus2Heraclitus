# YinYang formation Skill

**Explore a worked philosophy, borrow methods for understanding and realization,
or form a grounded YinYang collaboration in your own environment.**

[简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) · [Model entrypoint](SKILL.md) ·
[Documents & diagrams](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/en/)

## What this package supplies

YinYang helps a person develop their own philosophy and distill personal models
through writing and practice. A model can contribute exploration, authorship,
implementation, and correction; the person need not begin with a completed
philosophy or self-portrait. Common ideas, private understanding, and actual
work can develop together.

The author's chosen purpose is **reverse alignment**: the person develops understanding,
generation, and action through encounters with generative intelligence. The
agent's adaptation to the person supports that development. Both participant
models matter: whose purposes are being served, and what this counterpart can
understand, contribute, question, and carry. Chosen roles do not exhaust either
participant or establish identical mechanisms and inner experience.

YinYang is a Skill carrying a **meta-harness**—a philosophical and relational
frame for understanding and revising actual prompts, Skills, roles, tools, and
environments. Memory can preserve evidence; the models organize its relations
and possible consequences. Specialist Skills retain their own methods. The
[The trinity of philosophy, psychology, and engineering](references/docs/local-source-and-website.md) connects philosophy,
psychology, and engineering within one physical reality while distinguishing
their explanatory and practical work.

This is a developed example, including its own literary form. You can adopt it,
combine resources, or write something with different concepts, images, and
organization. [From adoption and combination to creating your own work](references/docs/example-and-commons.md)
explains that invitation. Maintaining this implementation and authoring a
different work are different choices; its fixed file paths apply to the former.

This directory is the complete YinYang member of the **Prometheus2Heraclitus**
collection. Its essays and diagrams are supporting resources, not separate
Skills. Other collection members retain their independent purposes and methods.

## Use and understand YinYang

Read the philosophical arguments for their own questions, borrow a formation or
adaptation method for a particular task, or adopt the complete maintained Skill.
These uses can overlap; they are not stages everyone must pass through. Borrowing
a method does not require writing a personal philosophy, adopting the author's
ontology or imagery, or forming the five files of a maintained situated version.

For full adoption, give the complete package to the model through its actual
Skill mechanism. The model forms or revises the accounts from relevant authorized
material and realizes the working environment where the purpose requires it.
It carries grounded writing, investigation, judgment, and implementation; the
person contributes experience, purposes, and decisions unavailable to it.
Downloading guidance does not itself form a personal relationship.

Choose the entrance that serves the current question:

| Purpose | Entrance |
| --- | --- |
| Participate in ordinary collaboration | [SKILL.md](SKILL.md), which owns the initial reading contract and routes. |
| Form or revise the coupled personal accounts | [Design and revision](references/design-and-revision.md). |
| Change how the actual environment works | [Harness adaptation](references/harness-adaptation.md). |
| Understand files, personal content, and common updates | [Package guide](references/docs/skill-package.md). |
| Explore an argument or its sources | [Read by the work being done](references/reading-map.md). |

For full adoption, this maintained implementation fixes five situated accounts:
**orientation** (adopted philosophy, values, direction), **person** (grounded
understanding), **counterpart** (actual model and wanted contribution),
**relationship** (joint activity, correction, division), and **environment**
(working realization). Person, counterpart, and relationship remain three
modeling objects; the five are not five psychological models.
Permanent public guides in `references/formation/` help form the actual private
accounts in `references/situated/`; distribution includes no private account files.
Another person's portrait or fictional substitute cannot supply them.
State unknowns honestly. Supporting private material follows actual evidence,
with originals retained at their existing owners when suitable. The package
guide gives the exact paths, completion evidence, and synchronization command.
The private evidence entrance is `references/personal/sources.md`, outside the
five core accounts and selected for reading when needed.

Formation can start with philosophy, personal experience, preferences, actual
work, or an environment to revise. If engineering is missing, develop a usable
foundation with model assistance so files, tools, execution, and verification
can support the work. An existing environment can supply clues for personal
modeling, but defaults and constraints also explain arrangements. The design
reference develops these conditional routes without requiring mastery of three
disciplines or a complete portrait before creation.

A person may keep the five-file structure while replacing the philosophy, or
organize a different work altogether. A philosopher's thought can supply a
framework; it cannot substitute for an account of the actual user. Independently
changed philosophy stays at its own source, outside common-file projection.
Copies following this repository's common updates retain byte-identical common
content while preserving their private accounts.

with their private owner.

## Philosophical ground

The author's worldview takes one changing physical reality as its ground and
expresses its becoming through contemporary fire monism. Computational ontology
is representation: objects, events, states, relations, and transitions through
which this framework constructs a process ontology. It can represent actual
people and psychological activity, hypothetical situations, or fictional objects.
Physical grounding concerns the activity and carriers of representation; what
is represented has its own status. Person and language model develop the question, model,
realization, and feedback together, dividing contributions by actual capability
and purpose. Representations need usable meanings; factual claims are true when
they correspond to what is actually the case. Satisfaction and inference within
a representation depend on its interpretation and assumptions. Clear
model-interpreted language can serve these uses. The
[World essay](references/docs/world-and-expression.md) develops this connection.
Activity histories can be represented as event streams; conceptual person and
counterpart models are constructed from those histories and other materials.
The participants have their own capacities and mechanisms. Coherent working
concepts support stability while evidence and competing interpretations remain
open to correction.

Sun and Moon express differentiated generation, reception, and shaping. Person
and model participate in those movements, retaining or exchanging emphases;
Earth is the answering material and shared living environment. Model companies,
researchers, and the wider collective keep acquiring fire; a finite person
borrows it to cultivate understanding, judgment, and generation through practice.
The little fire person emphasizes kindling further fire; Baby Heraclitus emphasizes
that this creator keeps becoming, including through changing and conflicting possibilities. Abilities can internalize
or remain reliably supported; AI use, agreement, and output alone do not establish
growth. The essays develop these images and claims at their own scale.

## Maintenance and contribution

[MODEL.md](MODEL.md) owns interpretation and publication boundaries;
[AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) is the maintenance entrance. English reader texts have
complete Chinese counterparts. Maintain common material at this shared source
and situated accounts at their actual private owner.

Use [Issues](https://github.com/boluopai-net/Prometheus2Heraclitus/issues) and
[pull requests](https://github.com/boluopai-net/Prometheus2Heraclitus/pulls)
under the repository's actual access settings. Return only authorized general
insights to this collection. Personal records, formalized portraits, private
quotations, and unpublished manuscripts remain outside public distribution.
