# YinYang formation Skill

**Model the person, form an agent counterpart in relation to that person, and realize their collaboration in the actual environment.**

[简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) · [YinYang formation Skill](SKILL.md)

## What this package supplies

This package helps you **write your own philosophy and distill your personal
models through writing and practice**. YinYang supplies a developed example;
a model can participate in exploration, authorship, abstraction, and realization.
Make your own website for selected philosophy and expression, while retaining
the more direct five core accounts and private evidence for yourself and
authorized models. Philosophical writing, private modeling, and public work
inform one another; complete prior self-understanding is not required.

**YinYang is a modeling method, not a memory system.** It forms and revises
understandings of a person, their agent counterpart, and their relationship,
bringing those understandings into judgment and action. Memory retains
retrievable material; dynamic models organize its relations, changes, and
possible consequences, and are tested and revised through new encounters.
Existing memory tools can support this work; YinYang's central contribution
is how the collaboration is understood and formed.

YinYang begins with **who this person is and what they are trying to become**.
Their philosophy, psychological understanding, preferences, expression, and
actual collaboration supply material for a revisable user model. That account
grounds a counterpart model: what this agent should understand, contribute,
question, and carry out for this person. Together, the accounts give their
relationship and working environment a direction. Designing the agent without
understanding the person leaves unclear whose purposes its competence serves.

The person can be modeled as an agent too: someone who perceives, interprets,
prefers, acts, and changes. Human psychology and preferences meet an account of
the model's tendencies, capabilities, limits, and chosen working commitments.
This comparison does not assume identical mechanisms or establish human-like
inner experience in a language model. A personal philosophy makes connected
meaning available for the counterpart to interpret; interaction and results
test whether that interpretation fits. It need not begin as a finished essay.

Two directions of alignment coexist. **The agent adapts to the person** in
ordinary collaboration; **the person develops toward generative capacity**
through that relationship, the purpose called reverse alignment here. Useful
adaptation includes independent judgment and correction. Starting from the
person is a grounding relation, not a requirement to finish a complete portrait
before work begins: user model, counterpart model, and relationship develop
together as actual work revises them. This coupled understanding is the center
of the personal meta-harness; philosophical imagery, Skills, and tools give it
usable forms. The [fire essay](references/docs/stealing-fire.md#why-the-relation-must-begin-somewhere-personal)
develops why both models are needed.

YinYang, including this Skill, is a worked example that you can adopt,
combine with other materials, or use to write a different philosophy and form.
Its fire, Sun and Moon, concepts, and file organization belong to this example;
your work can look quite unrelated to it. [Begin with this example, and make something of your own](references/docs/example-and-commons.md)
explains this literary and philosophical invitation and the collection's
aspiration to a commons of creative capacity. The
[web edition](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/) presents these
same documents and diagrams for direct reading.

**YinYang** belongs to the **Prometheus2Heraclitus** Skill collection.
Its meta-harness supplies cultural
and philosophical resources for forming a person's own relationship with
generative models and giving it a working body in their environment.

The [fire essay](references/docs/stealing-fire.md) begins with capabilities made
available through materials, power, machines, accumulated knowledge, and
continuing iteration. Borrowing them opens a practice through which a person
develops understanding, judgment, expression, and creation. The Sun's
illumination gives generative capacity an image: a power to bring forth and
make visible. Cultivation moves from borrowed fire toward further generation.
Sun and Moon express YinYang through differentiated generating, receiving,
and shaping; the actual person and model can retain or exchange these
emphases without gender assignment. Earth is the material and shared living
environment, including creations cared for together and a world that answers
independently. Individual-first places the person's purposes and agency at
the center of their practice while preserving others' contributions.
Abilities may be internalized or reliably distributed across models, tools,
and collaborators; AI use or output alone does not establish growth.

The distributed edition supplies formation guidance in its personal slots;
a situated edition holds the accounts already formed for its actual person
and relationship. To form or revise those accounts, the consuming model uses
the common ideas and reasoning forms to inspect the authorized environment
and relevant psychological or preference material. With sufficient grounding,
it writes the person's accounts, then uses that understanding to adapt the
actual working environment. The personal substance comes from that person
and encounter, not from a copied portrait or fictional demonstration.

This directory is the complete YinYang package. Its documents and diagrams
are supporting resources, not separate Skills. The collection also includes
independent Skills with their own purposes and methods.

## Use and understand YinYang

YinYang spans **philosophy and situated modeling**: the broader ontology and
epistemology, including the chosen value orientation, can be distilled into
models of actual participants and relations. Other Skills mainly supply
methodology. Personal material supports the modeling rather than constituting
another methodological layer. The [Trinity](references/docs/local-source-and-website.md)
connects these responsibilities to websites, repositories, and personal use.

**Philosophy, psychology, and personal environment form a continuing loop.** Philosophy articulates understanding and direction; psychology grounds the model in the actual person; engineering realizes the relationship in a usable environment. Experience in that environment returns to revise the understanding and arrangement. With one contribution available, develop the other two; with two, concentrate on the missing contribution. The model helps gather, write, implement, and check while the person understands, judges, and uses. [Trinity](references/docs/local-source-and-website.md#philosophy-psychology-and-engineering-develop-the-missing-connection) develops these connections and how to begin.

**Ontology, epistemology, and methodology meet in this practice.** Common philosophy articulates general and engineering objects and relations; the five situated accounts bring the actual participants and the grounds of understanding into view. Personal material supports both accounts, and specialist Skills supply methods. Philosophy, psychology, and engineering contribute across this structure. Begin through [philosophical readings](references/docs/philosophical-readings.md), [psychological readings](references/docs/psychological-readings.md), or [engineering readings](references/docs/engineering-readings.md). Development first serves oneself and nearby willing participants; active circulation of the website and personal philosophy remains answerable to this purpose. [Core view YY-15](references/docs/core-views.md#yy-15) explains cultivation nearby and limited circulation.

**If engineering is missing, develop it first.** Learn to organize files, tools, a harness, execution, and verification with model assistance so material preparation and revision can actually proceed. With one principal starting point:

| What is already available | How to develop the other parts |
| --- | --- |
| Psychological understanding and personal material | Develop engineering first, organize relevant material or provenance in private `personal/`, form the personal model, then articulate philosophy. |
| A philosophy being written | Develop engineering first, then compare the existing philosophy with personal material and experience to distill the personal model. |
| An environment and engineering experience | Usually psychology, then philosophy: propose a model from use and revision, check it against psychological and preference evidence in private `personal/`, then articulate philosophy; existing foundations may justify another entrance. |

“First” identifies a usable beginning, without requiring a completed environment or philosophy or fixing a person into a background category. An arrangement may reflect preference, constraint, habit, or a default; distinguish these through the person's explanation and actual results. Each route connects philosophy, the five models, personal material, and use. [Design and revision](references/design-and-revision.md) develops formation and checking.

**The philosophy can change; the five personal accounts must be formed anew.**
The philosophy, imagery, and essays provide resources for understanding an actual
person and forming a corresponding agent and collaboration. Adopt or revise
them, supply your own philosophy, or study and model a philosopher's thought
as your chosen framework. Keeping this file structure does not require the
author's philosophy. Any framework must return to the actual user: a model of
a philosopher's thought does not replace your user model. Rich expression serves
this modeling, whose usefulness is tested through actual collaboration.

Formation also works in the other direction. When your own model is unclear,
explore through a philosopher's concepts, imagery, and writing. Clearer,
grounded understanding can then revise the five core accounts. Those files
carry the distilled main content; concrete episodes, identity details, original
expressions, and extended explorations belong in `personal/` or its referenced
source owners. Distillation preserves personal differences that matter to
understanding and collaboration. Ordinary use begins with the core accounts,
then follows questions into sources and philosophy, without rereading every
essay each time. Philosophical exploration can continue while work proceeds;
a finished philosophical system is not a prerequisite.

**Adopting this YinYang implementation requires rewriting all five core relationship files and organizing your own `references/personal/`.**
Downloading or installing supplies formation guidance, not your situated YinYang.
The model carries this writing from authorized material, actual conversation,
and the environment; you need not author the files yourself. Distributed
instructions or another person's portrait cannot stand in for your own accounts.

| Required account | File | What to establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Encounter | `references/encounter.md` | Your purpose for the collaboration, each participant's contribution, and a concise view of the current understanding. |
| User model | `references/models.md` | Relevant actual experience, preferences, capacities, developmental direction, and unresolved understanding. |
| Counterpart and relationship | `references/relationship.md` | What the agent should contribute, how it judges and responds in relation to that person model, and how both cooperate and correct. |
| Situated use | `references/usage.md` | How work, resources, and action are organized in your actual tools and environment. |
| Sources and evidence | `references/sources.md` | What grounds the accounts, where originals are maintained, and which claims are personal expression, model interpretation, or unresolved judgment. |

Replace the guidance in `references/personal/` with relevant supporting material
and provenance. Add, revise, or remove pages as needed; do not copy the author's
material, file count, or topic list. Originals can remain with their existing
owners, with access-bounded references and necessary context here instead of
duplicated full texts.

Completion means all five files contain grounded situated accounts, supported
by reachable private evidence, and the model can read and use them at the actual
working entrypoint. State unknowns rather than inventing content. Understanding
can develop through work, but unchanged templates are not a completed situated
version. An independently authored work determines its own structure.

Give the consuming model [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md)
and the complete package directory, including `references/`. Ask it to form or revise your own
personal philosophy from authorized materials and use it to adapt your working environment.
For this maintained implementation, the main conversational model reads the
entrypoint, [encounter](references/encounter.md), [user model](references/models.md),
[counterpart model](references/relationship.md), [situated use](references/usage.md),
and [sources](references/sources.md) completely before substantive
work, reusing current readings in context. If you already have a personal philosophy, revise its accounts in place
while maintaining this same package format and common content.

The package has three common method and reading references:

- [Design and revision](references/design-and-revision.md): how to understand the actual encounter and write its personal accounts within the shared format.
- [Harness adaptation](references/harness-adaptation.md): how that understanding changes actual instructions, Skills, tools, and environment, with observable verification.
- [Reading map](references/reading-map.md): how the philosophical texts support this work.

The [package account](references/docs/skill-package.md) explains the distinction between
common material and situated content within the same format. `SKILL.md` keeps
the same purpose, method, and routes in the distributed and situated packages.
The fixed personal slots are `references/encounter.md`, `references/models.md`,
`references/relationship.md`, `references/usage.md`, and `references/sources.md`: formation instructions
in the distributed package become actual grounded accounts in the situated one.
[Private supporting materials](references/personal/README.md) remain under
`references/personal/`, with filenames chosen from the person's actual evidence.
Formalized user accounts are personal content too.

When opting into this repository's continuing common-content updates, remaining package material is byte-identical: philosophical
documents, diagrams, method references, the reading map, package administration,
READMEs, and the OpenAI YAML adapter. The shared GitHub package owns common
updates. `scripts/sync-common.py` checks this projection by default; `--apply`
projects common source into the target while preserving all five personal slots
and everything under `references/personal/`. Native owners realize the projected
source locally or in private Web use. This is a one-way common projection, not
a service or personal upload.

When revising or replacing the philosophy, you can retain the five-file structure
or reorganize the work. Keep those revisions at your own source rather than
applying this common-file updater over them. Models can carry that writing; using these resources does
not oblige you to fill the existing slots or subscribe to this account.

## Philosophical ground

[YinYang and Taiji](references/docs/yinyang-and-taiji.md) is its core image:
differentiated participants transform through one another; no final account
exhausts the encounter. Sun and Moon, the Shuang yu, and their differently
colored eyes give that movement a relational form.

YinYang's purpose is **reverse alignment**: the person develops through an
encounter with generative intelligence. Its computational ontology understands
both person and model as interacting event streams, developing the Heraclitean
concern with becoming and opposition. Personal accounts are revisable
organizations within those streams.

For stability, favor a coherent working ontology while accommodating conflicting
data and interpretations. The preference harness is an analogy for informal
natural-language reasoning; it does not require a formal preference model or
repair calculus. **Sun and Moon express YinYang's differentiated movement;
Earth is the answering environment.** Roles make that movement usable while
the actual person and model exceed them. Emphases can be retained or exchanged
without gender assignment. Personal meaning and practical contributions
develop from the actual encounter.
