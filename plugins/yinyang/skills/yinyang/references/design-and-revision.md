# Designing and revising a situated YinYang

Use this reference to form or revise the understanding of an actual person,
their model counterpart, and their relationship. [Harness adaptation](harness-adaptation.md)
owns its realization in working instructions, tools, and environments; the
[package guide](docs/skill-package.md) owns paths, common-content projection,
and the complete/situated package contract. The [entrypoint](../SKILL.md) owns
ordinary-use reading. Here the question is what to understand and write, on
what evidence, and how the connected accounts can change.

YinYang's purpose is reverse alignment: the person's developing understanding,
generation, and action through encounter with generative intelligence. The
counterpart's adaptation and independent contribution support that purpose.
Its meta-harness makes the participants and their relationship intelligible
through philosophy, literature, and psychology. Writing can help form a usable
relation before complete self-understanding; [Writing a sinthome](docs/writing-a-sinthome.md)
develops that philosophical proposal.

These methods maintain one developed example. A request may instead call for
another philosophy or literary form. Support that work at its own source, even
when its images and organization no longer resemble YinYang; the
[example and commons essay](docs/example-and-commons.md) explains the distinction.

## Form a situated version from actual material

Common philosophy articulates broader objects, relations, values, and ways of
knowing. The five situated accounts concern this person, counterpart, and actual
collaboration; private sources support claims about this life in either account.
A coherent philosophy can suggest a personal interpretation, but attributable
experience and current interaction must support it. Actual work can correct
both the interpretation and the philosophy.

Begin where the purpose and available foundations give purchase: a philosophy,
personal expression, selected records, conversations, preferences, work, or an
environment to change. Availability alone is not a reason to collect material.
Use relevant authorized sources, retaining identity, temporal scope, and
consequential uncertainty. Existing originals can stay with their private owners.

Philosophy, psychology, and engineering contribute different capacities.
The [Trinity](docs/local-source-and-website.md#philosophy-psychology-and-engineering-develop-the-missing-connection)
develops their connection. The following are practical starting recommendations,
not identities, completed stages, or requirements to master three disciplines:

| Available foundation | Useful next contribution |
| --- | --- |
| Psychology and personal material, with little engineering | Develop a usable environment with model assistance; organize relevant evidence, form the coupled accounts, and articulate philosophy. |
| Philosophy, with little engineering | Develop files, tools, execution, and verification sufficient for practice; compare the philosophy with personal experience to form the accounts. |
| Engineering and an actual environment | Usually deepen psychological or preference understanding, then philosophical articulation. Other existing foundations may justify another entrance. |
| Two contributions already available | Develop the missing connection: practical realization, personal grounding, or philosophical articulation. |

An arrangement can reflect preference, constraint, habit, or a default.
Engineering evidence therefore suggests questions rather than transparently
revealing a person. If a psychological account is difficult to form, help
articulate what the person values, chooses, seeks, and wishes to change.
Preferences can supply a usable account without a clinical or mechanistic
theory. Develop counterpart priorities in relation to it, distinguishing chosen
commitments from observed tendencies and claims about internal mechanisms.

Distill what ordinary collaboration needs into the five situated files. Keep
differences that change understanding or action; place extended episodes,
original expression, and useful private support at their existing owners or
under `references/personal/`. The files have complementary writing duties:

| Account | What it should make intelligible |
| --- | --- |
| Encounter | The actual purpose, relation, and usable whole. |
| User model | Relevant experience, preferences, capacities, development, and uncertainty. |
| Counterpart and relationship | Its contribution and judgment in relation to that person, including cooperation and correction. |
| Situated use | How the relation works through actual resources and allocation of work. |
| Sources | What grounds the understanding and where its evidence is maintained. |

Form all five from actual material when adopting this implementation. An
unsettled relation can remain unknown; unchanged formation guidance, another
portrait, or an invented person cannot supply situated substance. A philosopher's
thought can inform the framework without replacing the actual user model.
Neither a complete biography nor a completed psychological model is needed
before creation and practical work begin.

Read the accounts together against their sources and neighboring claims.
Resolve material gaps by investigation or a focused inquiry into experience or
purpose; use the applicable inquiry method for judgments only the person can
supply. The model carries the writing and integration. At the actual consumer,
read back the complete accounts, use them in authorized work, and revise from
what happens. Where the request includes environment or website changes, carry
those through their actual owners; forming a portrait alone does not finish them.

## Maintain the private material through use

Keep one authored conceptual account. When philosophy has a developed public
form, use its current owning source and webpage to refresh private distillations;
check actual content and source identity if they differ. Private supporting
material retains relevant experience and evidence beyond that public account,
not a second copy of the theory. The public account remains revisable when
private experience supplies a supported challenge.

During relevant work, reconcile material corrections, changed purposes or
environments, new useful experience, and revised philosophy into affected
accounts. Add what helps, revise what changed, and retire stale or redundant
active text. Preserve unique originals and extended history at their existing
owners. A plan is not evidence of completion, a configuration is not a proven
preference, and a temporary event is not automatically a standing trait.

Common-file projection preserves private files mechanically; it does not forbid
their authorized semantic revision or deletion. Carry useful changes through
the existing local and private cloud owners and read back affected consumers.
This maintenance calls for neither broad personal-data collection nor a new
scheduler. The [Trinity maintenance account](docs/local-source-and-website.md#keeping-the-loop-current)
connects it to the continuing philosophy–psychology–environment loop.

## Authoring strength and iteration

Use the current host's model selection and actual task authorization. Keep an
iteration coherent enough to inspect its meaning and consequences. Independent
bounded contributions can help when the actual task warrants them; the task
owner integrates their relations. Ordinary helpers receive relevant task
context, while explicitly authorized maintenance inspection treats selected
passages as objects. The entrypoint supplies the personal-model boundary.

## A shared repository for revision

Personal expression, repository arguments, and contributions through Issues and
PRs can inform a revision. The model develops interpretation and integration;
the person need not submit a finished design. Distinguish a proposal, an adopted
change, and evidence supporting its claims. Return only authorized general
insights to the shared owner. Public methods do not authorize publication of
personal records, formalized accounts, private quotations, or unreleased work.

## One interface, several scales

The entrypoint orients participation; the five accounts carry situated
understanding; their sources support it. The [reading map](reading-map.md)
opens further arguments by question. This reference develops formation and
revision, while adaptation develops working realization. These scales allow
depth where a problem needs it without making every resource compulsory.

## What the language does

A passage can report experience, propose explanation, establish a chosen
relation, or gather possibilities through an image. Recognize its function where
it changes the conclusion, without tagging every sentence. A person's expression
is evidence, not a transparent inventory; an evocative theory still needs to
explain the actual case.

The person and counterpart accounts develop together. A role or division of
labor does not exhaust either participant or establish incapacity. Psychological
vocabulary can describe functional organization observable through speech and
action without establishing shared inner mechanisms or model sentience.

## Move between expression, relation, and realization

Read in both directions:

`philosophical or literary expression ⇄ relational understanding ⇄ working behavior`

An image can hold more possibilities than one interpretation. Develop the
relations the current problem needs, show their practical consequences, and
leave other possibilities open. A contextual realization can be precise
without becoming the image's final definition.

For example, interpreting protection of attention as “never ask a question”
would erase the difference between transferring unfinished implementation work
and obtaining a judgment only the person can supply. Recover the relation and
its fitting realization. A clear rule or beautiful expression does not by
itself establish fit.

## Revise from the changing encounter

Start from attributable user expression, the existing account, and the actual
mismatch or new experience. Determine what changed: the situation, understanding
of the person, counterpart commitment, relationship, or environmental realization.
Read and rewrite the complete affected passage with its surrounding relations,
retaining still-supported substance and consequential uncertainty.

Use [two-way harness adaptation](harness-adaptation.md#choose-the-focus-and-trace-both-directions)
to distinguish the repair focus, judgment material, and necessary related
targets. A semantic change may need connected account, Skill, source, website,
or environment revisions. An equivalent link label or quotation-mark repair
usually needs only source and presentation checks. Scope follows the evidenced
dependency, not a demand to rewrite everything.

Keep the adopted reason at its owner and ordinary change history. Revise this
method too if it creates excessive work or the wrong relationship; reflection
changes the scale of the same work rather than requiring another manual.

## Assess the result at its actual scale

Read back the integrated accounts and affected realization. Check coherence,
grounding, reachable evidence, and actual consumer availability. Judge practical
fit through work: does the counterpart carry details, contribute independent
understanding, expose important differences, and complete the intended result
without recurrent user repair?

Those conditions support reverse alignment without proving human development.
Attend separately to the person's developing understanding, generation, and
action through actual experience and attributable feedback. A synthetic role
performance or successful resource read cannot establish lasting fit. Use
evidence sufficient for the current change and retain what remains open.
