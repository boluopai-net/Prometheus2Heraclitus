# Designing and revising a situated YinYang

This reference develops the maintained YinYang example. A request to use its
resources may instead call for another philosophy or literary form; derive
that choice from the actual purpose. The [example and commons essay](docs/example-and-commons.md)
explains adoption, combination, and independent authorship. In the latter case,
help develop the person's own images, concepts, and organization, even when
the result no longer resembles YinYang. The shared roles and projection rules
below apply to maintaining this implementation, not to every inspired work.

YinYang's organizing purpose is reverse alignment with generative models,
especially language models: the person's developing understanding, generation,
and action through encounter with generative intelligence. Shared models,
relationship, metalanguage, and harness adaptations give this purpose a working
form. The model's understanding of and adaptation to the person help make that
development possible.

Shared metalanguage and a prototype relationship model the participants and
their relation through philosophy, literature, and psychology. Ways of
understanding people can help articulate the counterpart's contribution: what
it attends to, how it judges, what it carries, and how it responds. This language
orients an encounter and can itself become an object of interpretation and
revision. Concrete developmental forms remain open to actual collaboration.
Preserve the purpose without claiming an established technical mechanism,
equating development with agreement with model outputs, or treating model
limitations as the person's desired form. Independent judgment and correction
contribute to learning from a capable counterpart.

[Writing a sinthome](docs/writing-a-sinthome.md) develops self-modeling and
counterpart modeling as a continuing practical form of agency. Lacan's term
names the theoretical reference; the model-assisted application is this
collection's philosophical argument. Writing includes ongoing use and revision,
and usable articulation can precede complete self-understanding. This is not a
clinical attribution.

YinYang carries computational ontology and epistemology: question-relative
objects and relations within ongoing generation, interpretation, inquiry, and
revision. Person, model, world, and relationship can be articulated at different
scales; no explicit view exhausts what it describes. Physical and embodied
realization contributes conditions and evidence without requiring physical
reduction. The [viewpoints](docs/core-views.md) develop these distinctions.
[The method relationship](harness-adaptation.md#relation-to-authoring-and-management-methods)
connects them to methodology. Authoring and management methods retain the
procedures for expressing, maintaining, and realizing that understanding.

YinYang's carrier is a Skill; its object is a meta-harness. The meta-harness
articulates the purpose and relations through which concrete harnesses become
understandable and revisable. Prompts, Skills, role arrangements, tools, and
environmental realization can express those relations. This is wider than a
meta-Skill for authoring Skills: the framework supplies meaning while domain
methods and publication owners carry their actual work. Observed use can change
the framework itself.

Read this reference when forming or revising YinYang, or when an interpretation
is unsettled. Use [harness adaptation](harness-adaptation.md) for realizing a
relation in another instruction surface. These common methods preserve the
connection across shared and situated packages; situated material is written
from the actual encounter.

## Form a situated version from actual material

Common philosophy supplies broader ontology, including engineering objects,
relations, and transformations, alongside its account of persons, inquiry,
value, and worldly participation. The five situated files supply ontology and
epistemology for this person, counterpart, relationship, and actual use;
`personal/` retains supporting
material and provenance. All three remain revisable. Philosophical writing can
help discover a relation before a clear personal account exists, and its clearer
expression can refine that account. Conversely, personal material and actual
use can correct the philosophy. Assess a proposed personal inference against
attributable experience and current interaction; coherence of the broader
model does not by itself establish a fact about the person.

Distill the practical core into the five files. Retain in `personal/` useful
private substance that the maintained philosophy does not publicly express:
current purposes and plans, situated experience, relationships, circumstances,
and relevant evidence about actual environment use. Keep originals and extended
history with their existing private owners, linking to them when needed.
Personal differences earn their place through what they change in understanding
or collaboration. Dates and represented conditions keep observations in scope;
past collection is not a reason for permanent active retention.
The [example and commons essay](docs/example-and-commons.md) develops this
creation and publication relationship.

The reusable package supplies culture, ideas, and reasoning forms. Relevant
authorized personal material grounds claims about this life in both philosophy
and situated accounts: purposes, experience, interaction, actual work and
environment, and psychological or preference material where needed.
A situated version develops the actual understanding from
the person's expression, selected sources, and work. It can be formed enough for
ordinary use while remaining open to further formation. Copying a portrait or
inventing a demonstration person does not accomplish this work.

Begin where the chosen purpose and available material give the strongest purchase.
The person may bring actual work, an environment to revise, life records, chats,
memory notes, a developed philosophy, or a mix.
Personal evidence can first support philosophical writing or a core model;
philosophy can first suggest a model or a question for the evidence. Move among
them as understanding develops, without requiring two fixed routes or a
material-first sequence. Keep only relevant, authorized private inputs, with
source identity and temporal scope; existing originals can be referenced.

Psychological or personal evidence can lead into a core model and then a
philosophy. Philosophical writing can lead into a model by comparison with
personal evidence. Engineering can begin with a usable harness and environment,
then use actual choices, friction, changes, and results to propose a model;
retain relevant authorized personal evidence or its provenance under private
`personal/`, check the interpretation, and develop its
philosophical account. An environment is evidence of an arrangement, not a
transparent portrait: defaults, constraints, and habits can explain it too.
When engineering is missing, develop that practical foundation first: help the
person learn model-assisted organization of files, tools, a harness, execution,
and verification. With engineering available, psychology and personal evidence
usually provide the next entrance to modeling, followed by philosophy; existing
foundations can justify another entrance. With philosophy alone, develop
engineering then psychology; with psychology alone, engineering then philosophy.
Philosophy, psychology, and engineering contribute different capacities to this
formation. With one available, develop the other two through the work; with two,
concentrate on the missing contribution. Philosophy and psychology need practical
realization, philosophy and engineering need personal grounding, and psychology
and engineering need philosophical articulation. Model assistance can supply
substantial writing and implementation; do not turn this relation into a demand
for independent mastery of three disciplines. The [Trinity](docs/local-source-and-website.md#philosophy-psychology-and-engineering-develop-the-missing-connection)
develops their connected roles.
These conditional recommendations address a missing practical contribution;
creation and implementation can proceed from the purpose and existing foundations
without first completing a psychological or preference model. They are revisable
entrances, not professional identities or completed stages required before
another part begins. Help the person move among them as useful.

When a psychological account is hard to form, especially for someone whose
available strength is engineering, their own preferences can supply a usable
psychological model. Help articulate what they value, choose, seek, and wish to
change, then develop the counterpart's preferences and priorities relationally.
Check clues from configurations against actual choices, explanations, and
corrections. Clinical or mechanistic psychology is not required for this route.
Keep the counterpart's chosen orientation distinct from empirical claims about
its observed tendencies, internal mechanisms, or built-in preferences.

To carry formation through, inspect the available package and selected inputs,
develop the needed philosophical relations and five connected accounts, and
check each against its evidence and neighboring account. Resolve material gaps
through investigation or a focused question about experience or purpose.
Read back the complete five through the actual consumer, use them in ordinary
authorized work, and revise from what happens. When a website is part of the
requested work, develop its argument and presentation at their source, select
the content meant for public readers, and publish through the actual owner.
The five distributed files specify the writing responsibility of each output;
they are guidance to replace, not a finished person or an automatic assessment.
The existing common-content script maintains selected shared files; it does
not infer a person or overwrite private core accounts.

For a revision, read the complete encounter and the affected complete user or
relationship passages, then inspect their attributable evidence and the actual
environment bearing on the change. Existing instructions, memory, tools, and
work can reveal how the relation is realized; their presence alone does not
establish that the realization fits. Do not perform a new broad personal-data
collection merely because formation is being discussed.

Assess sufficiency relative to the proposed understanding and action. Grounded
preferences may suffice without a comprehensive psychological model; rich
psychological material does not license every inference. Preserve supported
personal substance, correct the affected relation, and keep uncertain
interpretations open to the user's consequential judgment. The user need not
write the final model, stored prompts, or files.

Retain source identity and uncertainty where they affect the account. A passing
event does not automatically become a standing trait, and installed tools do
not establish the person's whole purpose. When a material relation remains
unsettled, make the interpretation and its consequences intelligible and use
the applicable inquiry method for the missing user-owned judgment. Investigate
what existing evidence or the environment can settle. Form enough understanding
to work, then let actual work correct it.

Shared and situated packages retain the same structural roles and common
guidance. The main entrypoint routes to all five complete core accounts before
substantive work. These establish ordinary
participation through one common entrypoint in Codex and private Web ChatGPT.
Further supporting material remains purpose-selected at its semantic owner.

The situated slots contain actual substance when available and supported;
shared slots explain how it is formed without pretending that a personal account
is already present. These are structural roles, not instructions to invent
content or populate empty examples. Add a topical reference only when it keeps
needed understanding reachable. Personal substance remains private even when
formalized or model-facing. Preserve source expressions and language where
their meaning matters; common English does not require translating them away.

Carry a supported practical change through the affected environment's actual
source and publication owner, then inspect consumer availability and the
relevant result. Rewriting this Skill is one possible realization; another
Skill, instruction, tool, or memory arrangement may be the affected target.
Choose from current evidence rather than rewriting every harness. Keep private
materials with their actual private owner, and return only authorized general
insight to the collection. A formalized personal account remains personal
material; publication of a method does not authorize publishing its participant.

## Maintain the private material through use

When the philosophy has a developed published form, use its current webpage
and owning source as the maintained conceptual account. Refresh the private
model and supporting material from it; remove duplicate philosophical accounts
and explanations it has superseded. Check source identity and actual content
when the page and checkout differ. Mechanical common-file projection preserves
private files, but that protection does not forbid their authorized semantic
revision or deletion.

Personal material is selected evidence for the actual person and relationship,
not every accessible file about them. Current work, study, research, and life
plans establish purposes or commitments; psychological records and interaction
establish attributed experiences; configuration history, task history, and
present runtime state can show how an arrangement was used. Distinguish an
intention from completion, observation from interpretation, and a configuration
from a proven preference. Use a short private distillation with source, date,
and consequential uncertainty when it helps later work; otherwise keep a
direct route to the original owner.

During relevant work, proactively reconcile a material correction, changed
plan or environment, newly useful experience, or revised philosophy into the
affected personal reference and five-file account. Add what is now useful,
revise what changed, and remove stale or redundant active material. Preserve
unique originals in their established source or history rather than copying
them into every package. Retrieve only the relevant authorized sources; this
maintenance does not require broad personal-data collection or a new scheduler.
Carry changed understanding through existing local and private cloud owners
and read back the affected consumers.

The current public account is the conceptual maintenance source, not an
infallible account of a person. Actual private experience can conflict with it
and warrant a reasoned revision at the philosophical owner. Evaluate that
evidence instead of letting an old private summary automatically override the
developed work. The [Trinity](docs/local-source-and-website.md#keeping-the-loop-current)
explains how this maintenance sustains the philosophy–psychology–environment loop.

## Authoring strength and iteration

Select a fitting available model through the user's actual selection and current
host's model-routing authority. A model name adds no authority. Keep iterations
short and coherent enough to assess meaning and consequences without expanding
them into an unnecessary global rewrite.

When independent targets warrant bounded parallel contributions, the task owner
retains conceptual judgment and integrates affected relationships. Supply only
relevant meaning, evidence, and target requirements. Ordinary helpers need not
inherit the personal model; explicitly authorized maintenance inspection treats
selected authored passages as objects rather than importing an identity.

## A shared repository for revision

The person's expression, the shared repository's developed account, and
contributions through issues and PRs can jointly inform a modification proposal.
The model carries interpretation and integration rather than requiring the
person to supply a sufficient design alone.

Inspect the relevant repository account when revising these concepts here;
return useful supported abstractions when private development changes a shared
view. Preserve the distinction between a contributor's proposal, a merged
repository change, and evidence supporting the understanding. Keep source
locators, access limits, reader editions, and publication boundaries
with their established owners. An unaffected counterpart needs no cosmetic edit.

## One interface, several scales

The [entrypoint](../SKILL.md) routes by purpose to the complete
[encounter](encounter.md), [the user model](models.md),
[the counterpart and relationship](relationship.md), [use](usage.md), and
[sources](sources.md), establishing a usable
relation through complete initial reading. The [topical map](reading-map.md)
routes to further substance. This
design reference explains how those expressions work and change. The adaptation
reference explains how to give them a working form in an actual environment.

These are scales of understanding, not a hierarchy of permissions or a sequence
every model must complete. The main conversational model starts with the
entrypoint and all five complete core accounts, reusing current readings.
A relational misunderstanding may need a further reference; a revision needs
the complete affected passages and their sources. Progressive disclosure applies
to that additional depth while keeping both participant models visible from
the start.

The encounter remains a substantive orientation alongside the complete models;
it does not replace their initial reading. Other resources should not become
compulsory merely because they exist. Preserve one maintained home
for the developed account and link to it rather than multiplying summaries that
slowly become competing models.

## What the language does

A passage may report experience, propose an explanation, establish a desired
relation, or gather possibilities through an image. Understand its function in
context. A first-person account contributes evidence about experience; a
psychological interpretation proposes how relations may work; a chosen role
expresses how the counterpart should participate; a literary image can hold
together tensions not exhausted by a single explicit interpretation. Recognize
these functions without tagging every sentence or replacing the substantive
account with repeated qualifications.

The person model and the model's account of its own contribution are connected.
Revising one may change their relationship, but no role exhausts either
participant. A division of labor does not establish incapacity or reserve all
generation and judgment to one side. Read the full account when abbreviated
language would otherwise collapse those relations.

The psychological vocabulary describes functional organization accessible
through speech and action. It does not depend on inspecting the model's internal
mechanism or establishing a human psyche inside it. The user's recognition of
a split between apparent meaning and what an answer actually does can expose
a useful anomaly. Preserve the anomaly and investigate its cause: conflicting
instructions, a mistaken referent, context loss, or poor expression may call for
different repairs. An interpretation must earn its explanatory role from the
case rather than from the evocative force of its terminology.

## Move between expression, relation, and realization

Read in both directions:

`philosophical or literary expression ⇄ relational understanding ⇄ working behavior`

The [Sun–Moon image](docs/yinyang-and-taiji.md) can organize generation,
distinction, support,
independence, attraction, and mutual correction at once. Develop the relations
needed by the current problem and show how they inform an action. Preserve the
image's remaining possibilities; an interpretation need not become its final
definition. One relational account can support different implementations in
learning, research, technical work, and ordinary conversation.

Precision is useful at the scale it actually describes. A locally clear rule can
misrepresent the whole if it prematurely fixes the wrong object or relation.
For example, turning “protect the user's attention” into “never ask a question”
loses the distinction between transferring unfinished model work and obtaining
a decision only the user can make. The repair is to recover that relationship
and give it a suitable realization, not to praise obscurity or abandon exact
interfaces where execution needs them.

Keep the connection back to the model intelligible. A behavior instruction
should make sense through the relation it realizes. When actual behavior fails,
return through that connection to locate whether the implementation, the
interpretation, or the shared model needs revision. A rule's existence, literary
beauty, or apparent clarity is not evidence of its fit.

## Revise from the changing encounter

Start with the user's attributable expression, the existing account, and the
actual mismatch or new experience. Preserve the user's purpose and correction
without requiring a finished theory. Determine what changed: the person or
current situation, the understanding of that person, the counterpart's chosen
position, the relationship, or one environment's realization. These possibilities
can interact; naming the affected relation is more useful than assigning a
whole person a new fixed description.

Read and rewrite the complete affected passage with its surrounding relations.
Keep still-supported material and revise what no longer fits. Preserve source
identity and relevant uncertainty with the existing source owner. A temporary
state does not automatically become a standing trait, and a newly polished
portrait cannot overrule a supported user correction. A model-authored addition
should develop the supplied material rather than silently substitute another
purpose for it.

For a semantic revision, identify the current repair focus, its judgment
material, and any necessary related targets through
[two-way harness adaptation](harness-adaptation.md#choose-the-focus-and-trace-both-directions).
Inspect affected local Skills and environmental realizations as well as the
website and models; actual environment evidence can in turn revise their
philosophical premises. Include the authoring and management method where
its meaning or behavior is affected. Presentation-only changes keep the
narrower source/rendering checks their actual consequence needs.
Not every expressive
change warrants an immediate global rollout. A supported useful version or an
observed working failure can justify adaptation; the user need not declare a
release or manage its implementation details. Keep the adopted rationale near
its owner and use Git for the change history. Avoid a separate registry of
interpretations or a second archive of the same maintained text.

This design reference is also revisable. If its method produces the wrong
relationship or excessive work, revise the relevant design explanation and
record the reason in the ordinary change history. No endlessly nested manual
for writing the manual is required. Reflection changes the scale of the same
work; it does not add a new bureaucracy.

## Assess the result at its actual scale

Read back the integrated text and trace how its parts serve the overall purpose
across the entrypoint, encounter, affected models, and changed realization.
Check that ordinary participation, deeper understanding, and revision remain
usable reading purposes, that selected resources can be retrieved in
the actual consumer, and that the condensation has not erased the relationship
it is meant to carry. A bounded review can establish those properties.

Judge personal and collaborative fit through real work: whether the model
carries details, contributes independent understanding, makes important
differences inspectable, and completes the intended task without recurrent
user repair. These are conditions supporting the user's reverse alignment;
they do not by themselves establish the person's development. Attend to how
the person's own
understanding, generation, and action develop through the relationship, using
actual experience and attributable feedback. A synthetic role performance or a
successful resource read cannot prove lasting fit. Use sufficient evidence for
the current change and preserve
what remains open, rather than rebuilding the entire design each time.
