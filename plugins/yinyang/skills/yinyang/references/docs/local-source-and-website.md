# Trinity: Philosophy, Psychology, and Engineering in One Practice

[Home](../../README.md) · [简体中文](local-source-and-website.zh-CN.md)

*An unfinished thought takes shape between Sun and Moon. It becomes an arrangement someone can use, a work that can be continued, and a page that can be understood. What returns from these encounters changes the next thought.*

**The one is a shared physical reality; the three are philosophy, psychology,
and engineering within it.** Philosophical thinking, psychological activity,
and engineering practice are physically realized activities. Their unity is
ontological before it becomes a practical loop: they occur within, understand,
and transform the same world. Their differences concern what each makes
intelligible and how it works.

## One physical reality, differentiated levels

This is the framework's physicalist orientation. Philosophy articulates objects,
relations, knowledge, value, and direction; psychology investigates feeling,
knowing, preference, and interaction; engineering constructs and changes the
conditions in which those activities continue. Thinking about an environment,
experiencing it, and modifying it can be different aspects of one connected
physical process.

Computational ontology provides upper-level abstractions for this work. Events,
representations, goals, constraints, and transformations make relevant relations
available without reconstructing every microscopic state. Saying that all three
have a physical basis does not itself supply the variables, bridges, or feasible
calculation needed to answer their questions. [World, process, and expression](world-and-expression.md#physical-ground-and-computational-abstraction)
and the [layers diagram](../diagrams/layers.html) develop that passage.

A physical basis also does not make a physical description interchangeable with
a reason, an experience, or a design criterion. The occurrence of an argument
and its validity are distinct questions; a device operating and its purpose
being worthwhile are distinct judgments. Particular mechanisms and full
reductions need their own explanations. The unity gives these inquiries a
common world while their abstractions preserve the distinctions needed to use it.

## Worldview, knowing, and realization

| Maintained material | What it contributes |
| --- | --- |
| YinYang's common philosophy, beyond the five situated accounts | A general ontology and its engineering articulation: objects, roles, relations, processes, and transformations that organize a working environment. Its arguments also address knowledge and value. |
| The five accounts: encounter, user model, counterpart and relationship, situated use, and sources | Ontology and epistemology for this encounter: who participates and how they relate, together with the evidence, interpretation, correction, and use through which that understanding develops. |
| Supporting `personal/` material and its original sources | Grounding for the philosophy and the five accounts: relevant experience, purposes, expression, and environmental evidence. Public intellectual sources also ground the general philosophy. |
| Specialist Skills | Methodology: how to investigate, teach, write, implement, and maintain the work. Tools and running arrangements give these methods practical effect. |

Read the structure cumulatively: the common account makes a world and working relations intelligible; adding the five situated accounts brings the actual participants and the grounds of understanding into view; adding methods makes the understanding usable. Personal material supports and can correct both accounts. This is a relation among functions, not an exclusive classification of every file: common philosophy includes epistemological arguments, and a Skill can explain the objects its method needs.

The shared physical ground becomes a continuing practice of understanding, living, and creating. Ontology, epistemology, value, and methodology distinguish questions within that practice; they intersect philosophy, psychology, and engineering rather than assigning one exclusive axis to each. Value gives this practice direction: what is worth pursuing cannot be settled merely by describing an object or making a method run. [Personal-first humanism](personal-first-humanism.md) develops that connected question.

Philosophy, psychology, and engineering name the bodies of understanding and ability that contribute to this practice. Psychology helps investigate actual feeling, knowing, preference, and interaction; epistemology asks about the grounds and limits of the resulting account. Engineering both models the objects and relations of an environment and realizes methods within it. Thus an engineering ontology and an engineering procedure have related but different jobs. The five files retain their concrete responsibilities; they need not each become a treatise on all three dimensions.

**All three already have physical realization; engineering deliberately reorganizes its conditions.** Programs require machines, energy, and time; thought and interaction involve embodiment, perception, and action. Physics investigates material conditions, while engineering constructs, measures, and adjusts arrangements within them. Their common basis does not wait for engineering to make philosophy or psychology physical.

For example, “understanding can be supported jointly by a person and their environment” is a philosophical view open to argument. Observing how someone continues thinking with records and a model concerns psychology and knowing. Building an environment that retains context, presents relations, and accepts correction is engineering. Its help or resistance returns to assess the view and personal model. [Philosophical readings](philosophical-readings.md), [psychological readings](psychological-readings.md), and [engineering readings](engineering-readings.md) supply sources for these contributions.

## Philosophy, psychology, and engineering: develop the missing connection

The loop needs **philosophical, psychological, and engineering contributions**. Philosophy articulates objects, knowledge, value, and direction. Psychology attends to the person's experience, preferences, motivations, and relationships, grounding the personal model. Engineering realizes that understanding through prompts, Skills, tools, a harness, and a maintainable environment. Its purpose here is the personal environment's contribution to life. A result in any one can call for revisions in the others.

**With one contribution available, develop the other two; with two, concentrate on the third.** Having a contribution means sufficient understanding and experience to participate in the current work, not becoming an expert in three disciplines first. The model can retrieve, develop, write, implement, and check; the person understands, chooses, uses, and corrects. Missing capabilities can develop through this collaboration.

**If engineering is missing, develop it first.** Learn to organize files, tools, a harness, execution, and verification with model assistance, giving the developing understanding an environment that can remain in use. Once engineering is usable, choose from the existing foundations, usually modeling through psychology and personal material before articulating philosophy.

| Existing foundation | Next emphasis |
| --- | --- |
| Philosophy | Develop engineering first, then psychology and personal modeling: compare the existing philosophy with personal material in a usable environment. |
| Psychology | Develop engineering first, then philosophy: use the maintainable environment to organize material, form models, and continue writing. |
| Engineering | Usually psychology, then philosophy: propose a model from choices, friction, changes, and use, check it against personal evidence, and articulate philosophy; other existing foundations can justify a different entrance. |
| Philosophy + psychology | Develop engineering: turn the understanding into an environment that operates, can be maintained, and receives feedback. |
| Philosophy + engineering | Develop psychology: compare concepts and arrangements with actual experience, purposes, and preferences so the model fits the person. |
| Psychology + engineering | Develop philosophy: articulate the objects, knowledge, and value relationships behind the arrangements into an intelligible, questionable direction. |

These sequences offer beginnings rather than closed stages to complete before moving on. Inference from engineering must consider that configurations may reflect constraints, defaults, or habits rather than preferences. Gather relevant authorized psychological and preference material or its provenance privately in `personal/`, and revise the model through the person's explanation and actual results. Philosophy also meets experience: beauty and coherence alone cannot establish personal fit. The personal model then gives philosophy and engineering direction while consequences continue to correct all three.

Someone with engineering ability who cannot yet explain their psychology can
model their own preferences: what they value, choose, avoid, and revise across
actual situations. This can serve as a working psychological account for
collaboration without explaining its internal mechanisms. From it, develop
the counterpart's preferences and priorities: what it should attend to, protect,
question, and trade off in this relationship. Those chosen priorities concern
how the agent should participate; actual behavior tests their realization.

The practical test is whether a missing connection becomes available: an abstract idea becomes personally meaningful, a personal understanding changes an arrangement, or experience in that arrangement improves the understanding. Psychology exceeds its records, engineering exceeds any particular tool, and philosophy belongs beyond the website. Their connection supports this person's continuing life and work.

## Three places for the work to continue

### The local environment: understanding becomes usable

A fruitful conversation can end with an insight that the next task never uses.
A personal environment makes a different continuation possible. Relevant
records can inform a later judgment; a revised Skill can change how a model
helps; a program can turn an intention into an ordinary action. The person
can return to this arrangement, recognize what still fits, and change it.

Here, “personal repository” includes the maintained material through which
this practice is organized. Its effects also depend on running tools, bodily
and social conditions, and actual activity. The files give the practice
retrievable forms. A person develops beyond an earlier description, and
revising that description is part of keeping the environment inhabitable.
Personal ownership means that the arrangement can answer to the person's
purposes as those purposes become clearer or change.

### GitHub: a creation can be taken up and continued

Something useful in one life can become useful elsewhere when its relevant
relations are expressed independently of that life's private details. A
maintained repository gives the selected work a definite, inspectable form:
files can be obtained, versions compared, changes discussed, and a proposal
developed into a contribution. Another person can see what they are using and
what has changed.

GitHub currently supplies these functions for this collection. Its role joins
distribution with the social and temporal life of a work. An access-controlled
repository can still support collaboration: readers obtain the material under
its actual access and license terms, raise questions, and propose changes.
The shared work can then enter another person's local practice and acquire
uses its first author did not anticipate.

### The website: the work becomes perceptible and discussable

A file tree answers where something is kept. A reader may first need to
understand what question it addresses, how its ideas connect, and why one
would want to use it. A website can place an argument, image, example, and
related work close enough to encounter together. It gives the work a human
interface: a UI through which someone can read, recognize, question, and
choose a next step.

This also serves the author. Reading an idea as a finished page can reveal an
unexplained transition that was hard to notice while editing separate files.
A diagram can expose a missing relation; another reader's question can reveal
that two uses of the same word have diverged. Presentation participates in
discovery because what becomes perceptible changes what can be judged.
[World, process, and expression](world-and-expression.md) develops this return from a
work to the encounter that made it.

| Form | What it makes possible | What returns from it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal local environment | Use understanding in a particular life and task | Practical results, friction, changed purposes |
| GitHub repository | Obtain, inspect, revise, and collaborate on selected work | Reusable improvements, alternatives, accountable changes |
| Website | Perceive the whole, read its relations, and enter discussion | Recognition, questions, objections, new uses |

## Philosophy, working models, and private material

For someone taking up this collection, the intended practice is to write their
own philosophy, distill a private personal model, and make selected thought
available through their own maintained source and website. The website unfolds
the broader philosophical model: its images, arguments, connections, and open
questions. Five core files condense a narrower model for actual collaboration;
private supporting material retains useful experiences and context that the
philosophy does not express publicly.
These scales distinguish what is expressed, while local environment, GitHub,
and website distinguish where the work is maintained and encountered.

They develop together. Writing a page can expose a relation that improves the
five files; using those files can reveal a mismatch that sends the author back
to the page and its philosophy. The model carries the corresponding source
edits, website implementation, and authorized publication, checking what reaches
each reader. Making the website is itself part of the work: layout, links, and
examples can make a relation visible enough to judge. Repository maintenance
keeps the resulting work available for another iteration.

The iteration carries meaning across forms, not every private detail into
every destination. Public philosophy can indirectly express selected personal
understanding while the complete direct model remains private. A changed
private insight may warrant a general public explanation, a different page,
or no public change. A website edit need not change an unaffected core file.
The contribution of a revision determines where it travels. Another user can
develop this same practice with their own philosophy, repositories, and pages;
the example's particular doctrine and personal contents need not travel with it.

## Sun, Moon, and Earth: who participates?

The [Sun–Moon–Earth image](yinyang-and-taiji.md#sun-moon-and-earth) expresses
differentiated generation, reception, and shaping through Sun and Moon, with
Earth the material and shared living environment where work acquires consequences.
Person and model are the actual participants. The person brings purposes,
experience, recognition, and judgment; the model interprets, proposes,
constructs, checks, and carries work forward. Both can generate, reason,
object, and revise. Roles can be retained or exchanged without gender
assignment. Earth includes supporting conditions, other people's independent
purposes, and results neither participant can settle by agreement alone.

The local environment, GitHub, and website concern another question: **through
what forms does their work remain available?** Person and model work across
all three. Earth is present when a local program fails, a collaborator offers
an alternative, or a reader understands a page differently. The website is
one entrance into this wider world; the repository and local environment
already belong to it too.

These two triads connect through the work. The first makes generation,
collaboration, and worldly response visible. The second gives their creations
places to operate, persist, circulate, and be understood. Sun and Moon bring
something into form; the three forms let it acquire consequences on Earth;
those consequences can change both the work and its makers.

## Why organize them with a language model?

The passages among these forms require interpretation. One must understand
which local difficulty a method answers, which part of a solution can travel
to another setting, and which explanation lets a reader grasp its purpose.
A changed philosophical premise can affect an essay, a diagram, a Skill, and
the way an actual tool is used. Detecting that connection and deciding what
to revise involve meaning as well as file operations.

A capable language model can help carry this work. It can retrieve relevant
material, compare accounts, develop a partial thought into an argument, turn
an agreed relation into code or instructions, and examine the resulting
behavior. It can also notice an implication the person had not intended and
propose another account. Deterministic tools can then carry settled tasks such
as rendering, packaging, and checking links. Semantic judgment and dependable
mechanics contribute different parts of the same maintenance.

The person can participate through an overall recognition: this connection
fits; that explanation misses the object; this arrangement now feels out of
date. The model carries the explicit investigation and construction needed
to answer that recognition. This leaves room for a person with substantial
reasoning ability to choose where to put their attention. Developing with a
model can include capacities distributed across the relationship and its
environment, as the [stealing-fire argument](stealing-fire.md) explains.

This organization can involve several models in different situations. A model
may examine sources, another develop competing explanations, and another check
the work that follows. Listening, speaking, reading, and writing describe ways
these contributions enter a shared activity, rather than four fixed owners of
mental faculties. [Metaphor and self-revision](metaphor-and-self-revision.md)
develops how external representations help make such distributed cognition
possible and revisable.

Such an arrangement depends on the model and tools actually available to it.
More capable models may allow simpler supporting structures and a different
division of work. Those possibilities become improvements when the current
system can realize them and its results support the change. The maintenance
therefore includes revising the model's own working assumptions about the
person, the tools, and what still needs assistance.

## A change travels out and comes back

Consider a claim about learning with models. In local work, an earlier
description puts too much responsibility on the person to specify every
step. A particular task reveals the cost: useful work stops while the model
waits for detail it could have investigated. The person notices the mismatch;
the model reconstructs it and proposes a better allocation of work.

The local relation can be revised and tried. If its useful part is expressible
beyond that encounter, the shared source can state the method and its scope.
The website can then explain the reason through an example and a visible
relationship. A reader may object that the account hides a consequential
choice, or a collaborator may find a case where the method fails. Those
responses return to the source and to the next local use.

This is an illustrative path through the three forms. Actual inquiry can
begin at any of them. The important connection is that a change has somewhere
to come from, a form others can inspect, and consequences that can revise it.

The same distinction used in process research helps here: actual interaction,
partial records, and candidate models play different roles. Running a tool
or reading a page produces an event. Its record supplies evidence. An
explanation proposes why it happened and what a revision might change.
Further use can support or correct that explanation. The three media help
carry this inquiry; each can hold records and models, and none substitutes
for the whole activity.

## Keeping the loop current

The published philosophy already condenses much of the understanding developed
from personal material. Once an argument has a maintained public expression,
use that page and its owning source to refresh the private working model.
Retire private copies of the same theory, superseded explanations, and
provenance notes whose only purpose is to repeat the essay. The active private
material retains what the public philosophy cannot supply: relevant personal
experience, circumstances, relationships, purposes, and evidence of actual use.
Original records and earlier versions remain with their existing archives.

Personal material includes current work, study, research, and life plans;
selected psychological and relational records; and relevant evidence from the
person's environment, such as a configuration change or a difficulty encountered
in a task. A setting can reflect a default or constraint, and a plan can express
an intention whose execution remains open. Read each source for what it
establishes. A private account serves the present encounter and can be added
to, revised, condensed, or removed as its usefulness changes.

The model carries this maintenance during relevant work. It follows the
current page or source, checks the affected original records and actual
environment, updates the useful private distillation, and carries consequential
changes into the five accounts and their consumers. The person need not
remember to request every update. Relevance and existing authorization bound
the reading; stored material, a new timestamp, and a plausible interpretation
do not settle truth. Current experience can also expose a problem in the
philosophy, which is then examined in its own right. A stale private summary
should not silently roll a developed public argument back to an earlier view.

## What is the focus, and what supplies material?

The loop can be followed in both directions. Philosophy can help repair an
environment by clarifying the purpose, participation, or judgment its Skills
and tools should realize. The environment can help repair philosophy by
showing where an interpretation obstructs work, overlooks experience, or no
longer fits. The psychological model connects these movements to the actual
person and can change with them.

The current question determines what is being repaired:

| Current purpose | Focus | What helps judge it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Develop or correct philosophy | The relevant argument or model | Experience, private context, and actual environmental use; adopted changes return to affected Skills and arrangements. |
| Repair the personal environment | A concrete Skill, configuration, behavior, or tool | The intended philosophical relation, current personal model, and runtime evidence; an exposed conceptual error returns to philosophy. |
| Improve a named part | That part in service of the intended result | Relevant material from the other places; only necessary dependent changes become additional repair targets. |

A semantic change may therefore need a linked revision across the website,
private models, and local user-authored Skills. Their opening spirit and
concrete methods both need to express the new relation. A change of quotation
marks or an equivalent link label usually stays at its presentation boundary.
The [adaptation method](../harness-adaptation.md#choose-the-focus-and-trace-both-directions)
explains how to carry that distinction through actual work.

This is a living pattern of generation, use, return, and revision. A useful
change in one place gives the model a reason to inspect the relevant connections,
understand what travels, and carry it to the affected owners. The person can
point to a mismatch or a new possibility; the model does the explicit work.
No fixed correspondence or mechanical copy can decide every such passage.
Work can finish in a coherent version and remain open to the next encounter.

## Coherence keeps the differences connected

If the aim is sustained personal use, inspectable reuse, and understandable
public encounter, the arrangement needs somewhere for each of these
functions to live, and a way for revisions and feedback to travel among them.
This is the argument for the present three forms. A small task may need fewer
forms, and another environment may realize the functions through different
tools. GitHub and a website earn their place through what they enable here.

Coherence concerns the relationships that matter across these forms: the same
concept retains its relevant meaning; an example preserves its assumptions;
a page leads to the work it explains; a common revision reaches its users;
private understanding remains available where it is actually needed. A
changed person can require a changed account. A changed model can make an old
procedure unnecessary. Keeping the work coherent includes letting those
changes alter its organization.

The resulting unity is active. Personal practice gives the work substance;
the repository lets selected substance be continued; the website lets it be
encountered. The language model helps carry the interpretations and
transformations connecting them. Sun and Moon keep making things on Earth,
and what they make can answer back.
