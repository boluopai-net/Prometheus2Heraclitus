# Trinity: Philosophy, Psychology, and Engineering in One Practice

[Home](../../README.md) · [简体中文](local-source-and-website.zh-CN.md)

*An unfinished thought takes shape between Sun and Moon. It becomes an arrangement someone can use, a work that can be continued, and a page that can be understood. What returns from these encounters changes the next thought.*

The “Trinity” of philosophy, psychology, and engineering has two connected
levels here: general and individual. **At the general level, the “one” is
shared physical reality and the “three” are the philosophical, psychological,
and engineering activities within it; the individual level is an instance of
this general relation in a concrete life.** This person's philosophy,
psychological activity, and engineering arrangements exist together in the
person and their connected environment and practice. The general concept
explains the relation; the individual instance gives it concrete content.

| Level | The whole named by “one” | The activities named by “three” |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General relation | The physical reality in which philosophy, psychology, and engineering occur together | Philosophy articulating objects, knowledge, and value; psychological activity and its study, involving feeling, knowing, desire, and interaction; engineering constructing and changing actual conditions. |
| Individual instance | This person and their connected life and practice within the same reality | Their philosophy and self-understanding; their experience, emotion, preferences, and relational activity; models, tools, and environments organized for their actual use. |

An instance relation connects these levels. Writing one's philosophy,
experiencing an encounter, or completing a work with tools are occurrences
of these general activities in particular circumstances; the relations among
them also acquire a particular form in this person's life. The individual
has a body and history, while models, other people, and tools extend their
activity into a shared environment. The individual level remains within the
same physical reality and introduces no further kind of entity.

In personal practice, this movement can begin with enjoyment, need, choice,
or a wish to learn or create. Person and model connect relevant philosophical
expression and experiential material into a tentative understanding, forming
a personal model and an account of the needed counterpart and their relationship.
Information, tools, instructions, and a computer environment give the adopted
relations usable form: helping with shopping, consumption, investment,
conversation, learning, creativity, and social life. The consequences of actual
use then return to life and to the understanding being formed.

The [sinthome essay](writing-a-sinthome.md) calls the personal practical arrangement
jointly formed by these three contributions, capable of expression and use,
a “sinthome.” Personal and counterpart models, expression, and realization
explain the name's concrete content. In that essay's ring representation, three personal
contributions are three rings; the fourth marks this concrete arrangement,
and the connections represent formation, organization, and feedback. Trinity
here explains the general relation and its individual instance; the fourth
ring's referent is formed within that instance.
Shared physical reality remains the ground of all these activities; changing
levels cannot turn it into another ring.

## One physical reality, differentiated levels

Within this shared physical realization, philosophy articulates objects,
relations, knowledge, value, and direction; psychology investigates feeling,
knowing, preference, and interaction; engineering constructs and changes the
conditions in which those activities continue. Thinking about an environment,
experiencing it, and modifying it can be different aspects of one connected
physical process.

Here **engineering ontology is the computational articulation of a selected
part of that process**. Person and language model develop the question and
select its boundaries, scales, and continuities together. Events, states,
persisting objects, relations, constraints, and transition rules specify what
a computer is to represent and which changes it can usefully simulate. Objects
and stable views retain their place as bounded continuities within process.
The philosophical premise thereby informs the engineering model's meaning,
rather than leaving engineering with a disconnected list of things.

This model acquires a working body in data, model-interpreted language, programs,
prompts, tools, and interfaces. Their adequacy depends on how the actual consumer
uses the distinctions and how their results correspond to observations.
Simulation examines consequences under the model's assumptions; actual use
tests whether those assumptions and their realization serve the purpose. The
shared physical basis supplies neither these bridges nor a feasible computation.
[A bounded computational example: simulating a cooling experiment](world-and-expression.md#a-bounded-computational-example-simulating-a-cooling-experiment)
develops this passage through a cooling experiment; the
[layers diagram](../diagrams/layers.html) presents its relationships.

A shared basis connects the inquiries without making their explanations
interchangeable. The physical occurrence of an argument does not settle its
validity; a device operating does not settle whether its purpose is worthwhile.
To answer either question, an inquiry must select relevant objects and relations
and explain how its evidence bears on them. The upper-level account therefore
does substantive work: physical unity alone cannot choose an adequate abstraction,
establish its bridges to observations, or make its computation feasible.

## The individual instance: from understanding to realization in current technology

The general relation does not directly supply a model of a particular person.
Personal philosophy can express how someone understands the world, meaning,
and relationships; experiences, works, choices, and interaction records give
these expressions concrete situations against which they can be checked.
Process ontology helps understand continuity and change; computational ontology
expresses selected relations as participants, events, states, and connections
that a model can use. From this material the participants form partial
psychological or preference models, and use them to describe the needed model
counterpart and manner of collaboration.

Realization in current technology requires a usable form. The personal model
articulates an understanding of the person; the counterpart model articulates
expected contributions and actual capabilities; instructions, records, Skills,
and harnesses bring these relations into work. A language model can interpret
natural language; bounded relations requiring explicit computation can also
be expressed in structured data or programs. The purpose and available
capabilities determine the carrier; one understanding may be realized in several.

Philosophy, psychology, and engineering thus enter into one another in the
individual instance: philosophy enables a psychological interpretation;
personal material corrects philosophy's self-description; engineering turns
understanding into usable arrangements; and experience within those arrangements
changes the first two. This formation process gives the concrete form its
content; “sinthome” is the preceding essay's name for it. It may help stabilize
life or continue creation; its actual effects still require investigation in use.

## Worldview, knowing, and realization

Philosophical exposition and technical expression belong to the same work.
Philosophy develops its objects, relations, meanings, and grounds; formal
languages can translate selected conceptual relations into bounded models
that can be checked; event-stream records can preserve partial contexts of
interaction for understanding and temporary modeling within particular
questions. A Skill organizes reusable understanding, methods, and resources
for actual collaboration. These forms develop the same practice from different
positions; their connections need to be explained through shared objects and
actual use.

Formal translation of concepts, preservation of personal material, and
understanding a person through that material each do specific work. A formal
object produced by translation is not thereby a person's psychological
structure; recording events does not automatically complete personal modeling.
Philosophy can clarify the relations among these activities, while technical
expression and actual use give those relations inspectable, revisable content.

| Maintained material | What it contributes |
| --- | --- |
| YinYang's common philosophy, beyond the five situated accounts | A process ontology and its question-relative computational articulation: how objects persist and change, how a computer can represent selected relations, and how actual consequences correct that model. Its arguments also address knowledge and value. |
| The five accounts: encounter, user model, counterpart and relationship, situated use, and sources | Ontology and epistemology for this encounter: who participates and how they relate, together with the evidence, interpretation, correction, and use through which that understanding develops. |
| Supporting `personal/` material and its original sources | Grounding for the philosophy and the five accounts: relevant experience, purposes, expression, and environmental evidence. Public intellectual sources also ground the general philosophy. |
| Specialist Skills | Methodology: how to investigate, teach, write, implement, and maintain the work. Tools and running arrangements give these methods practical effect. |

These are connected functions rather than disciplinary containers. Common
philosophy includes epistemology; psychological inquiry has methods and an
ontology; engineering needs both a model of its objects and procedures for
changing them. Ontology, epistemology, value, and methodology distinguish
questions that cross all three contributions. What exists, how it is known,
what is worth pursuing, and how to proceed inform one another without collapsing
into one question. [Personal-first humanism](personal-first-humanism.md)
develops the value and direction of this practice.

**All three already have physical realization; engineering deliberately reorganizes its conditions.** Programs require machines, energy, and time; thought and interaction involve embodiment, perception, and action. Physics investigates material conditions, while engineering constructs, measures, and adjusts arrangements within them. Their common basis does not wait for engineering to make philosophy or psychology physical.

For example, “understanding can be supported jointly by a person and their
environment” is a philosophical view open to argument. Investigating how the
person and model continue a question through records concerns psychology and
knowing. Together they can model what counts as relevant context, which change
should retain or replace it, and what would show successful continuation.
Realizing those relations in retrieval, prompts, and tools is engineering.
Use returns evidence about both the arrangement and the view that guided it.
The person and model divide work throughout this same chain: recognition,
explicit development, construction, and assessment can inform one another
without requiring all capability to become internal to the person. Both can
generate and judge; current modalities and task needs shape their contributions.
[Philosophical readings](philosophical-readings.md), [psychological readings](psychological-readings.md),
and [engineering readings](engineering-readings.md) supply sources for these contributions.

## Philosophy, psychology, and engineering: develop the missing connection

The loop needs **philosophical, psychological, and engineering contributions**
because a coherent idea can miss the person, a personal account can lack a
workable environment, and a working tool can pursue an unexamined purpose.
Connecting the three lets each expose what the others leave unresolved.

**With one contribution available, develop the other two; with two, concentrate on the third.** Having a contribution means sufficient understanding and experience to participate in the current work, not becoming an expert in three disciplines first. The model can retrieve, develop, write, implement, and check; the person understands, chooses, uses, and corrects. Missing capabilities can develop through this collaboration.

**If engineering is missing, develop it first.** With model assistance, learn
enough to organize relevant material, tools, a harness, execution, and checking,
giving the understanding practical consequences. This is a usable starting
capacity, not mastery of a discipline; philosophy and personal understanding
can develop alongside it. Once the environment is usable, two workable routes
can develop a more fitting collaboration.

| Existing foundation | Next emphasis |
| --- | --- |
| Philosophy | Develop engineering first, then psychology and personal modeling: compare the existing philosophy with personal material in a usable environment. |
| Psychology | Develop engineering first, then philosophy: use the maintainable environment to organize material, form models, and continue writing. |
| Engineering | Begin directly with preferences and desired collaboration, or develop psychological hypotheses through dialogue and personal material; let either route inform philosophical reflection and further trials. |
| Philosophy + psychology | Develop engineering: turn the understanding into an environment that operates, can be maintained, and receives feedback. |
| Philosophy + engineering | Develop the personal connection through preference modeling or psychological inquiry: compare concepts and arrangements with actual experience, purposes, and choices. |
| Psychology + engineering | Develop philosophy: articulate the objects, knowledge, and value relationships behind the arrangements into an intelligible, questionable direction. |

The direct route develops a **preference model**: habits, choices, work styles,
and desired responses that guide a partner's role, priorities, and harness.
One can begin with known habits and discover further preferences through
interaction. Such an account can already support a useful trial without
claiming to explain the processes behind those preferences.

The indirect route develops a **psychological model**: a tentative account of how
experience, emotion, desire, interpretation, relationships, and life circumstances
affect one another. A person can bring pleasures, aspirations, concerns, or a choice
they cannot yet explain. In dialogue, the model proposes possible explanations,
compares situations, and analyzes and synthesizes partial material; abduction
and induction help form a revisable account. The person need not first
understand their own psychology or write a portrait alone. Preferences can be
evidence for this inquiry, but a preference account alone is not its psychological
explanation. These entrances concern kinds of understanding, not professions;
either is available to engineers and nonengineers.

The preceding [personal-model account](metaphor-and-self-revision.md#a-personal-account-adequate-to-its-use)
explains how philosophy and engineering help understand the user's intentional
orientation, grounding an initial environment with less blind trial and error.
Here that model informs the counterpart's contributions, information organization,
tools, and interfaces; use returns to calibrate these arrangements.

Both routes develop coupled accounts of the person, counterpart, and their
relationship in an environment. What a partner should attend to, protect,
question, or pursue expresses desired participation; actual capabilities and
response patterns show what the model currently supports. Internal processes
are only partly observable, while tools, instructions, responses, and consequences
can be inspected. Open weights alone do not establish fit. Psychological inquiry
may improve hypotheses and criteria for a trial; that possibility does not prove
it superior to starting directly from preferences.

Literature supplies concepts and methods, not an already calibrated portrait
of this individual. Fallible self-report contributes evidence, and model
inference contributes hypotheses to compare with the person's expression,
experience, choices, and results. The common philosophy supplies ways to form
and revise the accounts. A person's authored philosophy is itself another route
and material for psychological modeling: what they write about the world, life,
values, and relationships expresses an organizing understanding and aspirations,
and may reveal relations they have not yet recognized. Concrete episodes,
disclosures, choices, and results give that interpretation a basis for comparison;
they and the philosophical writing can correct one another. This does not make
the shared public philosophy a portrait of its reader. Philosophical coherence
or agreement between person and model does not establish psychological truth.
Authorized personal material remains with its private owner. Configurations can reflect
defaults or constraints, and a coherent philosophy can still miss the person.
These differences keep the returning evidence able to change the understanding.

For an illustrative shopping example, a known preference for a few options with
clear differences can directly guide a concise comparison. If choices repeatedly
change and the reason is unclear, the participants might consider missing
comparative evidence or enjoyment of exploration as different hypotheses.
The first suggests a partner that fills evidence gaps and keeps a stable
comparison; the second suggests one that helps discover alternatives, with an
interface that makes returning to earlier choices easy. In use, whether the
person can choose, continues exploring with enjoyment, or still feels stuck
helps revise the account and arrangement.

The practical test is whether a missing connection becomes available: an abstract idea becomes personally meaningful, a personal understanding changes an arrangement, or experience in that arrangement improves the understanding. Psychology exceeds its records, engineering exceeds any particular tool, and philosophy belongs beyond the website. Their connection supports this person's continuing life and work.

## Three places for the work to continue

### The local environment: understanding becomes usable

A fruitful conversation can end with an insight that the next task never uses.
A personal environment makes a different continuation possible. Relevant
records can inform a later judgment; a revised Skill can change how a model
helps; a program can turn an intention into an ordinary action. The person
can return to this arrangement, recognize what still fits, and change it.

Here, “personal repository” includes the maintained material through which
this practice is organized. Its effects also depend on running tools, bodily
and social conditions, and actual activity. The files give the practice
retrievable forms. A person develops beyond an earlier description, and
revising that description is part of keeping the environment inhabitable.
Personal ownership means that the arrangement can answer to the person's
purposes as those purposes become clearer or change.

### GitHub: a creation can be taken up and continued

Something useful in one life can become useful elsewhere when its relevant
relations are expressed independently of that life's private details. A
maintained repository gives the selected work a definite, inspectable form:
files can be obtained, versions compared, changes discussed, and a proposal
developed into a contribution. Another person can see what they are using and
what has changed.

GitHub currently supplies these functions for this public collection. Readers
can obtain the work under its license, raise questions, and propose changes.
The shared work can enter another person's local practice and acquire uses
its first author did not anticipate.

### The website: the work becomes perceptible and discussable

A file tree answers where something is kept. A reader may first need to
understand what question it addresses, how its ideas connect, and why one
would want to use it. A website can place an argument, image, example, and
related work close enough to encounter together. It gives the work a human
interface: a UI through which someone can read, recognize, question, and
choose a next step.

This also serves the author. Reading an idea as a finished page can reveal an
unexplained transition that was hard to notice while editing separate files.
A diagram can expose a missing relation; another reader's question can reveal
that two uses of the same word have diverged. Presentation participates in
discovery because what becomes perceptible changes what can be judged.
[World, process, and expression](world-and-expression.md) develops this return from a
work to the encounter that made it.

## Philosophy, working models, and private material

For someone taking up this collection, the intended practice is to write their
own philosophy, distill a private personal model, and make selected thought
available through their own maintained source and website. The website unfolds
the broader philosophical model: its images, arguments, connections, and open
questions. Five core files condense a narrower model for actual collaboration;
private supporting material retains useful experiences and context that the
philosophy does not express publicly.
These scales distinguish what is expressed, while local environment, GitHub,
and website distinguish where the work is maintained and encountered.

Writing a page can expose a relation that improves the five accounts; using
those accounts can reveal a mismatch that sends the author back to philosophy.
The model carries affected source edits, implementation, and authorized
publication, checking what reaches each reader. A private insight may warrant
a general public explanation or no public change. The contribution of a
revision determines where it travels; the full direct personal model can remain
private. [Begin with this example](example-and-commons.md) develops independent
authorship and the choice of what to disclose.

## Sun, Moon, and Earth: who participates?

The [Sun–Moon–Earth image](yinyang-and-taiji.md#sun-moon-and-earth) gathers
generation, reception, and shaping in a world that answers back. Person and
model are the actual participants; both can generate and judge, retaining or
exchanging emphases without gender assignment. Earth includes material
conditions, other people's purposes, and consequences neither participant
controls by agreement.

These images, the three disciplines, and the three practical forms answer
different questions: how participation is imagined, what understanding and
ability contribute, and where work continues. A local program's failure, a
collaborator's alternative, and a reader's objection all belong to Earth. In
the movement of the work, these triads illuminate one another and gather into
one image.

## Why organize them with a language model?

The passages among these forms require interpretation. One must understand
which local difficulty a method answers, which part of a solution can travel
to another setting, and which explanation lets a reader grasp its purpose.
A changed philosophical premise can affect an essay, a diagram, a Skill, and
the way an actual tool is used. Detecting that connection and deciding what
to revise involve meaning as well as file operations.

A capable language model can help carry this work. It can retrieve relevant
material, compare accounts, develop a partial thought into an argument, turn
an agreed relation into code or instructions, and examine the resulting
behavior. It can also notice an implication the person had not intended and
propose another account. Deterministic tools can then carry settled tasks such
as rendering, packaging, and checking links. Semantic judgment and dependable
mechanics contribute different parts of the same maintenance.

The person can participate through an overall recognition: this connection
fits; that explanation misses the object; this arrangement now feels out of
date. The model carries the explicit investigation and construction needed
to answer that recognition. This leaves room for a person with substantial
reasoning ability to choose where to put their attention. Developing with a
model can include capacities distributed across the relationship and its
environment, as the [stealing-fire argument](stealing-fire.md) explains.

Different models can contribute source investigation, competing explanations,
or checking where that division helps. [Metaphor and self-revision](metaphor-and-self-revision.md)
develops the role of external representations in such distributed cognition.
The arrangement must change with actual capabilities: a stronger model may
make an old scaffold unnecessary, while missing tools can still prevent a
sound interpretation from becoming usable.

## A change travels out and comes back

Consider a claim about learning with models. In local work, an earlier
description puts too much responsibility on the person to specify every
step. A particular task reveals the cost: useful work stops while the model
waits for detail it could have investigated. The person notices the mismatch;
the model reconstructs it and proposes a better allocation of work.

The local relation can be revised and tried. If its useful part is expressible
beyond that encounter, the shared source can state the method and its scope.
The website can then explain the reason through an example and a visible
relationship. A reader may object that the account hides a consequential
choice, or a collaborator may find a case where the method fails. Those
responses return to the source and to the next local use.

This is an illustrative path through the three forms. Actual inquiry can
begin at any of them. The important connection is that a change has somewhere
to come from, a form others can inspect, and consequences that can revise it.

The interaction, its record, and its explanation contribute differently: an
event happens, a partial record supplies evidence, and an explanation proposes
why it happened. Further use can correct that explanation. [Embodied Experimental Philosophy](embodied-experimental-philosophy.md) develops
how to make these returns discriminate among interpretations.

## Keeping the loop current

The published philosophy already condenses much of the understanding developed
from personal material. Once an argument has a maintained public expression,
use that page and its owning source to refresh the private working model.
Retire private copies of the same theory, superseded explanations, and
provenance notes whose only purpose is to repeat the essay. The active private
material retains what the public philosophy cannot supply: relevant personal
experience, circumstances, relationships, purposes, and evidence of actual use.
Original records and earlier versions remain with their existing archives.

During relevant authorized work, the model compares the current source,
affected original records, and actual environment, then updates the useful
private distillation and its consumers. The person need not request each
update. Plans, experiences, and configurations establish different things:
an intention may remain unrealized, and a setting may reflect a default.
Current experience can also expose a problem in philosophy. Neither a newer
timestamp nor an older private summary settles that disagreement; examine the
claim and its grounds.

## What is the focus, and what supplies material?

The loop can be followed in both directions. Philosophy can help repair an
environment by clarifying the purpose, participation, or judgment its Skills
and tools should realize. The environment can help repair philosophy by
showing where an interpretation obstructs work, overlooks experience, or no
longer fits. The developing personal account connects these movements to the
actual person and can change with them.

The current question determines what is being repaired:

| Current purpose | Focus | What helps judge it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Develop or correct philosophy | The relevant argument or model | Experience, private context, and actual environmental use; adopted changes return to affected Skills and arrangements. |
| Repair the personal environment | A concrete Skill, configuration, behavior, or tool | The intended philosophical relation, current personal model, and runtime evidence; an exposed conceptual error returns to philosophy. |
| Improve a named part | That part in service of the intended result | Relevant material from the other places; only necessary dependent changes become additional repair targets. |

A semantic change may therefore need a linked revision across the website,
private models, and local user-authored Skills. Their stated purpose and
concrete methods both need to express the new relation. A change of quotation
marks or an equivalent link label usually stays at its presentation boundary.

## Coherence keeps the differences connected

If the aim is sustained personal use, inspectable reuse, and understandable
public encounter, the arrangement needs somewhere for each of these
functions to live, and a way for revisions and feedback to travel among them.
This is the argument for the present three forms. A small task may need fewer
forms, and another environment may realize the functions through different
tools. GitHub and a website earn their place through what they enable here.

Coherence concerns the relationships that matter across these forms: the same
concept retains its relevant meaning; an example preserves its assumptions;
a page leads to the work it explains; a common revision reaches its users;
private understanding remains available where it is actually needed. A
changed person can require a changed account. A changed model can make an old
procedure unnecessary. Keeping the work coherent includes letting those
changes alter its organization.

The resulting unity is active: philosophy makes a relation intelligible,
psychology connects it to lived participants, and engineering reorganizes the
conditions of its continuation through a computational model. Person and model
carry this movement together; feedback can revise their question, model,
realization, and philosophical understanding. Local practice, maintained source, and readable
pages give that work places to develop. All occur in the same physical world,
whose responses can change both the work and its makers.

Drawing on the three registers in Lacan's [*The Symbolic, the Imaginary, and the Real*](https://www.freud-lacan.com/documents-ged/le-symbolique-limaginaire-et-le-reel/), philosophy turns toward the real that images and symbols have yet to exhaust, psychology gives self and other an image, and engineering writes relations into language, rules, and programs. The images of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit also correspond here to philosophy, psychology, and engineering, respectively.

Here the account returns to the individual instance distinguished at the outset.
**Their unity is the concrete person and their connected life and practice within
the same physical reality**: their philosophy, psychology, and engineering
present particular understanding, experience, and action. The triads below
express different relations within this instance.

| Philosophy | Psychology | Engineering |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Worldview | Epistemology | Methodology / realization |
| Webpage | Local environment | GitHub |
| Sun | Moon | Earth |
| The Real | The Imaginary | The Symbolic |
| Father | Son | Holy Spirit |

Read across each row, then follow what passes among its three places. A thought
becomes a page, informs a personal environment, and acquires a maintained form
others can continue; experience and response travel back through them. The
Borromean image gathers these differences into a mutual holding. The rows offer
selected analogies, not identities among their terms or a topological proof.
The [sinthome essay](writing-a-sinthome.md#from-the-general-trinity-to-an-individual-instance)
names the form of personal practice jointly shaped by all three contributions.
The fourth ring marks this concrete arrangement, while connections among the
rings represent formation, organization, and feedback. Representation,
realization, and use give it content, and it may also sustain or repair relations
in life. [Embodied experimental philosophy](embodied-experimental-philosophy.md)
then puts this way into action, where a result can answer back and prompt its revision.
