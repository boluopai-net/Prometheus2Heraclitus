**Do not spend a finite life pursuing an ever-expanding Sun until it burns you up. Borrow its fire, and use it to kindle your own capacity to create.** Model companies, researchers, and the wider collective continue gathering knowledge, training systems, and making capabilities available. A person can draw on that work without repeating the whole industrial acquisition. The question then changes: what can this already acting, understanding person cultivate with the fire they have borrowed?

This essay chooses **reverse alignment** as its purpose: develop understanding, judgment, generation, and action through encounters with generative intelligence. For that purpose, access and finished outputs need to become occasions for participation: offer an intuition, encounter development and criticism, correct it, and generate further. The person who learns to keep that movement alive is the **little fire person—Baby Heraclitus**. This is a cultivated way of participating in becoming, with changing and sometimes conflicting possibilities, rather than a final, perfectly consistent self.

**Pursuit that consumes a life → collective acquisition → borrowed fire → personal cultivation → continued generation.** The practical necessities in this argument follow from the chosen purpose and finite resources. Relief, ordinary assistance, and completed works are worthwhile uses too; they need not become lessons. Cultivation asks an additional question: how does this encounter change what the person can understand, judge, initiate, and sustain?

## Finite life and the wrong pursuit

The Sun here images expanding external generative power. Trying personally to recreate its entire machinery, or treating every frontier change as a demand to catch up, can consume the time and attention needed for one's chosen life. “Being burned up” names this resource pressure. It gives a reason to borrow available capability and choose what to cultivate, rather than a scientific law about progress or a ban on doing research.

The contrast reworks inherited images. In the *Shan Hai Jing*, Kuafu pursues the sun and dies of thirst; in Hesiod's *Theogony*, Prometheus carries stolen fire in a hollow fennel stalk. Their juxtaposition here is contemporary: it distinguishes pursuing overwhelming power from bringing usable fire into life. [Mythic sources below](#mythic-sources).

A supposedly definitive prompt can become another form of pursuit when endlessly repairing it to match an external ideal displaces what the collaboration was meant to serve. Technical inquiry, precise specifications, and model updates remain useful when they contribute to that purpose. The issue is where the person's life goes: into keeping pace for its own sake, or into a practice that the available power can sustain.

## Material fire and concentrated capability

The fire available to a person has already required work across many lives. Model companies organize parts of its training and provision; researchers, authors, engineers, infrastructure workers, and other contributors make that work possible. Companies participate in this collective acquisition without being its sole origin. Borrowing means using capabilities it makes available, rather than personally repeating the whole process.

Its realization is physical. Stellar processes formed many elements later entering Earth's minerals and machines [[27]](#source-27); extraction and fabrication organize materials into chips. Electricity powers computation, while data, knowledge, design, and learning procedures shape what it can do [[18]](#source-18). Solar generation is one possible supply among others [[28]](#source-28) [[17]](#source-17). Materials realize the machine, energy drives its transformations, and learned organization supports outputs whose meaning and warrant still need examination.

Acquisition and operation have costs. The IEA estimates that all data centres used about 415 TWh in 2024, a figure broader than AI [[17]](#source-17). Computing dissipates heat [[23]](#source-23); electricity generation also has emissions consequences [[29]](#source-29). These are different mechanisms within Earth's open radiative energy balance [[30]](#source-30). Cooling, grids, materials, and emissions make expansion a shared material question. *Stealing fire* names both the collective ambition to extend creation and the responsibilities carried by that power.

## Reusable capability and recursive accumulation

A trained model can contribute to many encounters without repeating its entire training each time. The foundation-model report discusses this amortization of formation costs across use and adaptation [[31]](#source-31). Inference, retrieval, and checking still cost time and energy. The practical opening is nevertheless decisive: the person can begin from available capability instead of rebuilding its industrial source.

Reuse continues in the works made with it. A checked explanation becomes a reading aid, working code becomes a tool, and a useful method supports another task. **Capability → work → retained, checked resources → further capability** is a possible recursive relation. Quality, maintenance, access, and actual use determine whether it helps; generated volume alone gives no improvement rule.

Collective acquisition can therefore continue while personal cultivation proceeds with fire already available. These movements can contribute to one another without becoming the same process. Ordinary conversation need not update a model's weights, and a better personal tool need not advance foundation-model research. Its contribution here is to make the next encounter more intelligible and generative.

## An oracle-like relation and systems that improve

Borrowing leaves an epistemic problem: a model may return an answer whose grounds this person cannot presently reconstruct. It is **oracle-like relative to that participant**, rather than an authority that makes the answer true. Sources, explanations, and independent checks can change the relation. Neither complete reconstruction nor blind trust is required for every useful contribution.

Self-improvement is a separate question. Schmidhuber's theoretical Gödel machine guides self-rewriting through proofs under encoded assumptions and utility [[20]](#source-20); the Darwin Gödel Machine generates agent-code changes and evaluates them empirically [[21]](#source-21). Model weights, tools, and an improvement loop are different objects. For the borrower, the necessary distinction is **acquiring capability, evaluating a result, and cultivating a personal capacity**. A useful output establishes none of the other outcomes by itself. The cooperation needs arrangements for each contribution it seeks.

## A changing world and a finite participant

Available power changes the conditions in which a finite person acts. Concept drift offers a technical comparison: conditions during use can depart from the data distribution on which a learned model relied [[26]](#source-26). Personal purposes and relationships are richer objects, but their working descriptions likewise need reconsideration when their conditions change.

There is no single measured rate at which everyone must catch up. Customer-support research found gains varying with experience [[32]](#source-32); METR's early-2025 developer study found a slowdown in its setting, and its later account explains measurement difficulties [[33]](#source-33). Karpathy's October 2026 account describes a widening difference in experienced capability, relating it to task ambition, domain, and access [[34]](#source-34). His roughly 20 million figure is an informal estimate of professional frontier-model experience in math and code. These sources make collaboration and its conditions objects of inquiry; they do not prove that every new model benefits every task.

The response proposed here is to develop the ability to notice worthwhile possibilities, give them a form, and pursue them through consequences. A person can sense a relation before possessing the words or implementation skills to unfold it. The model can help articulate and realize it, while the person encounters objections and learns what the result makes possible. Technical knowledge, embodied experience, reading, and intuitive judgment contribute differently to that practice.

**Individual-first** orients the arrangement toward this person's purposes and voluntary development. Seek changes to social arrangements, then software, before requiring personal adaptation, following concrete dependencies and respecting others' lives. This directs the borrowed fire toward a life rather than turning each change outside it into a demand upon it.

## Generate, encounter correction, generate further

Generation can begin as an embodied analogy, a sensed relation, or an example
drawn from broad reading. The person contributes something to unfold; the
model can give it a full verbal or technical form. Cultivation concerns what
this exchange develops in the person's perception and understanding.

A powerful producer can leave its human counterpart chiefly sensing conditions and evaluating outputs they do not understand. Receiving and assessing a work can teach, but successful evaluation alone does not establish the ability to generate an explanation, hypothesis, or next act. For a developmental purpose, the relation can deliberately turn: **the person generates and expresses; the model critiques, corrects, and scaffolds; the person generates further.** An example, conjecture, image, or unfinished explanation gives the model something to understand and test. Correction becomes fruitful when the person takes it into another attempt rather than merely approving a replacement answer. Speaking can itself make a half-formed relation available, with the counterpart helping it continue.

This cultivates a selected capacity; it does not require the person to produce everything in every task. The model can prepare materials, write complete text and code, and build and maintain the environment. Its generation can open what the person could not yet imagine. A bounded study of 52 developers learning an unfamiliar Python library found weaker immediate learning in the AI-assisted group; its interaction analysis also identified cognitively engaged patterns [[12]](#source-12). A different randomized physics study found learning benefits with a deliberately designed AI tutor [[19]](#source-19). These findings resist a verdict about AI assistance as such. The practical question concerns how this encounter connects generation, correction, and continued understanding, and what the person can subsequently recognize, explain, or do.

The bridge begins with a functional comparison. **A person and a model counterpart can both be studied through relations of purpose, interpretation, action, and revision.** A person articulates a concern; the counterpart interprets it, proposes or performs an action, and returns a result; both can revise their understanding through that result. This shared vocabulary makes questions of agency, narrative, and self-formation relevant to cooperation. Humanities scholarship supplies ways to understand those relations; research on particular models examines how they are realized. Differences of embodiment, experience, and implementation matter when they change what a participant can contribute or how a result should be interpreted.

YinYang names the philosophical framework developed here for a person's relationship with a generative model. A **harness** arranges Skills, prompts, hooks, tools, model choices, roles, and the user's actual environment through which work becomes possible. A **meta-harness** supplies the understanding through which that arrangement is interpreted and revised: what the work serves, who participates, and how its consequences matter. A role such as “teacher” or “assistant” acquires a situated meaning through the person being helped, their purpose, and the relationship developed in use. YinYang couples models of the person and counterpart with an account of their relationship. **Reverse alignment** is its chosen developmental direction: as a model is directed toward a person's purposes, the person develops understanding, judgment, and generative capacity through the encounter.

This essay develops that direction as a philosophical proposal and a practice to be realized. Philosophical sources supply concepts and arguments; recent AI papers and research reports describe particular systems. Their contributions meet in the inquiry: how can available generative capability become part of this person's developing life? The direction is chosen; the realization is examined through what the person and counterpart actually understand, create, and do.

## From living fire to interacting event streams

The starting point is a philosophy of becoming. Heraclitus's living fire, its measured transformations, and the tension of bow and lyre place change and opposing relations at the center [[13]](#source-13). YinYang deliberately adopts **contemporary fire monism** as its process ontology: the changing natural world is one reality, in which persons, objects, and relations persist as relatively stable patterns and transform through encounters. Fire names this becoming, without making literal flame the material of everything. Within that physical ground, **the computational ontology proposed here models both the person and the model as event streams.** Their activities, including philosophy, psychology, and engineering, belong to the same world. Event-stream modeling abstracts relations of continuity and transformation; particular mechanisms and a complete reduction of experience remain questions for further inquiry. [World, process, and expression](world-and-expression.md#physical-ground-and-computational-abstraction) develops this connection.

Two existing research traditions provide connections. In knowledge representation, Gruber explains ontology as an explicit specification of a conceptualization, articulating objects and relations so participants can share and inspect their meanings [[24]](#source-24). This supports the emphasis here on question-relative objects, relations, and working views. Dodig-Crnkovic's info-computationalism connects informational structure with computational dynamics, discussing agency and cognition across levels [[25]](#source-25); it supplies a neighboring framework for bringing ongoing change into a computational account. This essay distinguishes **structure available for inference** from **continuing process** and proposes its own event-stream modeling. The two sources do not jointly establish this entire philosophy or a reduction of human experience to a digital algorithm. Particular correspondences and physical realization need further argument.

An event stream is a continuation of occurrences through temporal, causal, and interpretive relations. A perception, an utterance, a reading, a generated hypothesis, and an action can each become an event in the model. Memory, context, habits, commitments, and consequences connect what happens without requiring an unchanging self-description. The event granularity follows the inquiry. Streams can overlap, branch, and proceed at different rhythms; a transcript records only some of their occurrences. A person and a model therefore share a form of ontological articulation while retaining different embodiments and implementations.

A self-model or counterpart model is a local organization made within this process. Producing, reading, and correcting it are further events, able to change what follows. A literary image can reorganize attention; an explanation can alter the next interpretation; a completed work can change what the person can initiate. Language is effective here because it participates in the stream it articulates. Modeling does not stand outside becoming.

[YinYang and Taiji](yinyang-and-taiji.md) give this becoming its central relational image. Differentiated participants generate, receive, and transform through one another; their contributions acquire meaning in the encounter. The Shuang yu and their differently colored eyes distinguish a chosen role from the participant who exceeds it. This image gives the reciprocal movement of event streams a perceptible form. Continuing transformation allows particular works to be completed and useful arrangements to remain stable: a completed work enters the next encounter as something available to use and reconsider.

Conflicting material tests this continuity. A coherent working ontology can
support present action while retained evidence and candidate interpretations
remain in conflict. General paraconsistency supplies background for considering
conflict without arbitrary conclusions following [[14]](#source-14); prioritized
inconsistency-tolerant querying gives a more specific formal comparison [[16]](#source-16).
The [preference-harness account](preference-harness.md) develops the distinction
between selecting views and reasoning within them. These contemporary
constructions extend an inquiry into becoming and opposition; historical
readings of Heraclitean fire monism and contradiction remain disputed [[15]](#source-15).

The **little fire person is Baby Heraclitus**: someone cultivating a way to keep understanding and creating within this becoming. Ontologically, the person was already part of the fire; cultivation does not turn an inert being into an agent. It gives their existing participation new capacities and a distinctive form.

What enters an encounter may itself be generated and internally uneven. A model can offer accounts that conflict with one another; reading them does not make them all true. A participant can find more than one possibility persuasive, want different things, or change their judgment through another encounter. Their own generation then includes the tension among what has been received, desired, and newly proposed. Such tension can open further inquiry rather than requiring an instantly finished self-description.

Cultivation develops a way to live and act through it: keep alternatives available, examine what supports them, distinguish a changed situation from an actual conflict, and choose enough of a working view to proceed. Where claims do conflict, retaining them for inquiry does not license arbitrary conclusions. A consequence can change the choice and the next act. The fire person's continuity lies in this revisable movement, not in agreement with every generated account or permanent consistency among all their desires.

## Why the relation must begin somewhere personal

Plato's *Protagoras* gives fire another consequence. In its myth, Prometheus
brings arts and fire, yet these means do not suffice for civic life. Zeus sends
reverence and justice to be shared by all [[22]](#source-22). The contemporary comparison
is limited but fruitful: technical capacity leaves questions of purpose,
relationship, and living together open. The dialogue supplies a distinction
to think with, not a prediction of AI. Personal-first adaptation likewise
chooses whose purposes an arrangement serves; it does not make factual truth
depend on preference. Other participants and the world still answer back.

Power does not select its own worthwhile use. A model's increasing capability leaves open what should matter here, which contribution is wanted, and what would count as a life made more capable. A reusable template cannot settle these questions simply by being reusable. It becomes useful when interpreted within someone's purposes and circumstances.

Personal modeling therefore has a practical task: make enough of a person's concerns, ways of noticing, desired development, and relation to others intelligible for collaboration. The person already perceives, interprets, prefers, and acts as an agent. Modeling articulates those connected activities so the counterpart can understand what a request means in this life. A personal philosophy can concentrate that meaning; experience with actual works and arrangements can ground, test, and change it. Psychology and preferences can contribute when they clarify the question. The material requires interpretation and correction through interaction. Being written does not make it complete self-knowledge.

**Understanding the user, however, has not yet determined how the counterpart should participate.** The same wish can receive different responses: doing the work, explaining, objecting, or trying something together. Someone developing through reading and intuitive judgment may need a different contribution from someone practicing a particular implementation skill. A user portrait alone may leave the default assistant role deciding everything. Calling an agent intelligent, gentle, or strict does not explain why those qualities fit this person. The counterpart model connects this gap: in relation to the person, purposes, and actual work, it articulates what the agent attends to, how it interprets and judges, which work it carries, and when it questions or revises shared understanding. Relationship modeling connects the contributions without mistaking a division of work for a limit on either participant's abilities.

This also requires understanding the actual model. Its capabilities, response tendencies, context, and tools condition how a proposed counterpart can be realized. The values and priorities it is asked to adopt express what the collaboration should become. These are distinct matters. Human psychology and model psychology, human preferences and model preferences, here involve both investigating actual tendencies and constructing a relationship. Assigning a role does not rewrite base-model weights; a functional comparison does not establish identical psychological mechanisms or experience. Works and actions make that construction answerable beyond a mutually satisfying description.

Two connected directions of alignment follow: **the agent adapts its
contribution through understanding the user, and the user develops generative
capacity through that fitted collaboration.** Independent questioning belongs
to this relation when purposes, facts, or realization conflict. The person can
discover what they mean by encountering a proposed interpretation; the model
can develop an intuition into a work in which the person recognizes a fit or
an error they could not yet express.

Formation therefore starts with enough understanding to proceed. An existing
philosophy, a working tool, or experience needing expression can supply the
opening. Psychology can clarify patterns of attention and response;
preferences can articulate what matters. **Views orient realization,
experience tests views and arrangements, and engineering makes new experience
possible.** The [Trinity account](local-source-and-website.md) develops this
relation. Modeling gains substance through making and using, rather than
requiring complete accounts before work can begin.

Clark and Chalmers's extended-mind argument makes room for cognitive activity organized across a person and active environmental supports [[1]](#source-1). YinYang draws a practical consequence: records, images, tools, and the model's work can carry parts of understanding. Its commitment to personal development then asks how this arrangement changes what the person can perceive, judge, initiate, and sustain. Assistance and development become distinguishable outcomes to examine in the same practice.

## A self can be written and lived

If a self-description were only a report of an already completed object, writing could at most preserve that object. But a description can also disclose a possibility, establish a commitment, or reorganize how the next situation is understood. Its reader is changed by reading, including when the reader is the person being described.

Bruner's account of life as narrative develops this two-way relation: culturally available stories help organize the life that is subsequently understood and narrated [[2]](#source-2). Foucault's technologies of the self concern practices through which people transform their conduct and ways of being, sometimes with others' help; writing and rereading figure among the practices he examines [[3]](#source-3). These sources make self-articulation intelligible as an activity with formative consequences. They do not require treating the person as a complete text.

YinYang extends that possibility to a contemporary situation. Language about oneself can be encountered by a generative counterpart capable of interpretation and action. Writing the relationship can help bring into being a practice that did not previously have that form. The text is carried into conversations, investigations, works, and decisions; their consequences return to the understanding of the self.

This is where [writing a sinthome](writing-a-sinthome.md) enters. Lacan's term supplies a philosophical comparison for a singular sustaining practice: a way of making relations hold through which a person can live and create. With fire already available, the relationship gives it a place in life and supports a distinctive way of creating. This continuing formation cultivates a singular way of participating in generation. A generic list of prompts is interchangeable. A practice becomes singular through what it sustains and makes possible for this life.

## Meaning before a truth claim

“Sun and Moon meet” gives YinYang's differentiated generating, receiving,
and shaping an intelligible form. The actual person and model participate
through a relationship whose emphases can be retained or exchanged.
“Collective fire acquisition continues; use available fire to cultivate
generative capacities” connects this relationship to development. Claims
about capabilities or human growth then acquire objects that can be examined.

This gives a precise sense to *neither true nor false, yet understandable*. A literary construction introduces a possible organization; a proposed role invites conduct; a chosen orientation gives activity a direction. Each has a use through which its meaning unfolds. Once interpretation makes a claim about an actual capacity, event, or consequence, truth becomes the relevant question for that claim. Meaning opens the space in which questions can be formed, and those questions acquire their own conditions of assessment.

Carnap's distinction between questions within a linguistic framework and the practical choice to use a framework offers a philosophical resource [[4]](#source-4). An organizing vocabulary can be assessed through what it lets us formulate and do. Claims developed within that vocabulary can then be examined on their own grounds. This distinction helps articulate how a literary world becomes a medium for inquiry and action.

In this sense, literary language can participate in a **computational ontology**: it introduces objects, positions, relations, and possible transformations that interpretation can develop. Sun and Moon can organize reciprocal contributions; a retained account and a currently accepted view can have different roles; a work can return as a new object of understanding. An ontology supplies distinctions through which the inquiry proceeds. It need not be an inventory of ultimate substances.

Calling this the model's ontology concerns the working organization of the counterpart and encounter. The language makes roles, relations, and transformations available for interpretation; the model's implementation determines how that language is taken up. Different realizations can interpret related language, and their differing results can motivate revisions. Ontological articulation and causal investigation thus contribute to the same practice from different questions.

## Literary programming and the model counterpart

Literary programming gives the relationship an intelligible form through characters, purposes, tensions, and possible conduct. Natural-language programming lets an actual model interpret that form and act with it. Images can condense connected relations: “Moon” can evoke receiving, developing, judging, and returning. Their usefulness depends on what the counterpart actually understands and does. Knuth's *literate programming*, which joins explanation and program code, is a different established use of the term [[5]](#source-5).

For cultivation, the important question is what kind of counterpart this person needs. “Teacher” or “assistant” acquires meaning through the work and the capacities being developed. A person cultivating judgment may need complete works to inspect and challenge; someone learning an implementation skill may need attempts, criticism, and another try. Neither role language nor a beautiful story ensures the contribution occurs.

The philosophical whole connects the images rather than merely collecting them. Fire expresses becoming and the passage from collective acquisition to personal cultivation; Sun and Moon express contributions in an encounter; event streams articulate continuity and revision; a harness gives the relation an executable body. A single vocabulary need not erase their differences. It needs to make their relations understandable enough to guide and revise practice.

*Reverse prompt injection* names the ambition to bring that whole into the model's interpretation, so isolated words or default roles do not decide the response by themselves. Its “hard to refuse” character is an invitation to assess what the proposal actually means. A counterpart can still identify contradictions, missing evidence, or unrealizable arrangements. Its response gives evidence of interpretation; an actual constraint requires investigation of the model, context, and harness.

Natural-language prompting has technical precedents: Reynolds and McDonell discuss intentions, narratives, and cultural proxies in GPT-3 [[6]](#source-6). More recent sources illuminate different aspects. Claude's Constitution supplies a developer's connected account of intended conduct [[7]](#source-7); the Persona Selection Model proposes an explanation of assistant-persona formation [[8]](#source-8); Anthropic's emotion-concept report describes causal interventions in a particular model [[9]](#source-9). These are reasons to examine interpreted organization, rather than proof that literary prompting always improves performance. *Principled Personas* finds inconsistent benefits and sensitivity to irrelevant details across its tested tasks and models [[10]](#source-10).

Adaptation work can itself be borrowed. GEPA uses model reflection on execution traces to propose and evaluate prompt changes [[11]](#source-11). This helps locate the criticism of pursuit: useful revision serves a chosen practice and can be carried by the collaboration. The person need not spend their life manually reconstructing every improvement before benefiting from it.

The claim earns its place in use. Examine how the counterpart interprets unfinished expression, which work it carries, and whether the resulting encounters support the chosen understanding, generation, or action. Improved cooperation and increased personal capacity are connected possibilities, each requiring its own evidence.

## Bring the fire into a world that answers

The [Sun–Moon–Earth composition](yinyang-and-taiji.md#sun-moon-and-earth)
gives the coupled modeling a perceptible form. In one arrangement, the person
foregrounds intuitive generation as Sun, and the model receives, develops,
judges, and creates as Moon. Each can generate and give form; the roles can
be retained or exchanged. The two working models articulate these actual
participants and their relationship. Earth names the material and shared
living environment in which their creations acquire consequences.

A work gives the relationship distance from itself. A program runs; an argument encounters an objection; another person understands an image differently. What returns may change the interpretation, the implementation, or the desire that started the work. Neither human–model agreement nor narrative coherence determines what the world must do.

The distinctions between ontology, epistemology, value, and methodology developed in [Personal-first humanism](personal-first-humanism.md) now have a reason to remain separate. Ontology makes relevant objects and transformations available. Epistemology asks which interpretation has support. Value asks what is worth pursuing and who bears the cost. Methodology develops how to inquire and act. Practice brings both participant models and these judgments into a world that answers. Keeping several interpretations available need not mean accepting all their incompatible assertions. These distinctions allow the common language to remain fertile without becoming immune to correction.

## What makes the fire one's own

The developmental question is what the person becomes able to perceive, understand, initiate, and judge through the practice. Some capacities can be internalized; others can remain reliably distributed across tools, records, and collaborators. Requiring every capacity to migrate inside an unaided individual would contradict the reason for borrowing fire in the first place. Yet a larger output does not by itself show a larger understanding.

YinYang therefore asks what development is sought in this particular practice. A person cultivating understanding and intuitive judgment may learn through reading, questioning, comparison, and encountering a finished work, while the model carries extensive implementation. A person learning an implementation skill needs opportunities to exercise that skill. The division follows the purpose; it need not become an exam imposed on every conversation.

A personal philosophical and literary Skill can give this practice a direction from above: it articulates purposes, preferences, habits, and ways of understanding through which surrounding Skills acquire their place. A research method, a writing tool, or a way of arranging the environment becomes personal through how it serves this life. Its components may come from others, and its implementation may be largely model-authored. What makes the arrangement personal is the developed relation to the person, rather than exclusive authorship or perfect fit achieved once and for all.

Through repeated use, this arrangement can accumulate **personal software assets and intellectual assets**. Here an asset is saved understanding, a method, software, a work, or an articulated relation that remains available for reuse and revision. Software gives a practice an executable body; concepts, literary forms, and preserved works carry ways of noticing, interpreting, and creating. Their value lies in what they continue to make possible: a later encounter can begin from understanding and working means already developed, rather than reconstructing them each time. This continuity can support further development, but the number of Skills or saved outputs does not measure it. An unused collection may remain a collection; a smaller arrangement can become deeply sustaining.

The movement also returns from below. Actual use reveals where a method fits, where it frustrates a purpose, and what the person now wants or understands differently. Revising the surrounding Skills can therefore revise the philosophical Skill that gave them direction. The methodology helps assets accumulate by connecting personal orientation, working realization, retained results, and correction. It preserves what serves the person while allowing replacement and simplification. Private personal substance stays with its situated owner; authorized reusable methods can enter the common collection without carrying that substance with them. Models, collaborators, and the world continue to contribute independently: cultivating one's own assets gives their contributions a durable place in a life.

The image of a [miniature god](writing-a-sinthome.md#a-miniature-god-whose-creations-answer-back) can now retain its ambition. A finite creator gains ways of giving ideas a body that were previously unavailable. Their power includes making relations, choosing what to sustain, and allowing creations to change them. Fire becomes one's own through such participation, even while its source remains outside oneself.

## A way of learning: prepare the encounter

Borrowed fire can illuminate a subject before the learner knows how to arrange
it. The model can find and select sources, compare their suitability, and edit
them into materials through which the person can enter the subject. Preparation
belongs to the cooperation itself. A learner need not first become a librarian
or build a standardized knowledge base to deserve a useful explanation. For
ordinary use, the first question is what would make the next encounter with
the subject intelligible and fruitful; an elaborate catalog can consume the
finite attention it was meant to release.

The resulting materials can take several forms: a rewritten glossary tied to
the source's usage, a table tracing how concepts change across authors or
chapters, a study plan, a diagram of relations among ideas, a guide to passages
worth skimming or reading closely, and exercises focused on the current
difficulty. Text, images, audio, worked examples, and interactive demonstrations
offer different ways into the same question. Select and combine them for what
they let this learner understand or try. The model's rewriting should preserve
the route back to sources, so a helpful simplification can be compared with
what it explains.

An Obsidian reading space can place edited notes and the relevant PDFs together,
with readable links between a concept, its explanation, a related question,
and the source passage. Bidirectional links help the person return from a
new connection to the material that gave it substance. Their contribution is
the ease of reading and returning, rather than the number of links. The person
brings lived associations, embodied intuition, recognition of meaning, and
judgment of the whole; the model contributes retrieval, explicit comparison,
structured explanation, and material preparation. These are useful
contributions, not exclusive possessions. Both can reason and revise. Learning
becomes cultivation when explanation, practice, and correction change what
the person can recognize, explain, or do on the next encounter.

An illustrative learning encounter makes the developmental claim concrete.
The model prepares terms and relations from a PDF, with passages available for
checking. The person proposes an interpretation in their own words. The model
finds a counterexample or a missing distinction; the person revises and tries
the interpretation on another passage. What might develop is recognition,
expression, or judgment, even while the model maintains the complete notes
and software. This is a possible arrangement, not a reported experiment.
Its test is what the person can subsequently explain or recognize, rather
than whether the prepared document looks polished.

## A way of working: give an intuition a body

A person can begin with an intuition, an unfinished relation, or a felt
friction. The model helps articulate what the person has not yet made explicit:
investigate the conditions, develop alternatives, construct the chosen work,
and compare the result with the purpose that called it forth. The person need
not reproduce the model's detailed labor before that labor can help. Their
understanding and judgment develop through encountering something concrete
enough to inspect, redirect, and use.

For work involving files, tools, and an ongoing local environment, a capable
model in a local harness can carry this movement from expression to action
and readback. It can prepare materials, edit the work, observe execution,
repair a failure, and retain enough context for the next contribution. This
continuity gives the person's corrections somewhere to take effect. A Web
conversation can help explore a question and leave a short plan or reference
for subsequent work; choose the execution environment by its actual access,
visibility, and ability to continue through failures. Repeatedly moving the
same task among disconnected conversations can spend attention rebuilding
the relation instead of developing the work.

Completion gives the fire a usable body: a text that can be read, a tool that
works in its intended environment, or a change whose effects have been checked.
Keep the understanding and working means that make another encounter easier;
revise or replace what has ceased to help. Personal software and intellectual
assets then accumulate through use. Their worth is the capacity they return
to the person's life, including new questions and more ambitious creations.

## A way of living: choose the rhythm of the fire

**Ordinarily synchronize with the model and remain asynchronous with society;
align temporarily when entering social evaluation.** This organization lets
daily work use an environment shown to fit better, while preserving a way to
meet external comparisons on their own terms.

This rhythm also has a developmental reason. Social institutions, software,
and conventions of interaction often have to keep existing participants,
agreements, records, and ways of working viable. They carry historically
formed relationships, and change requires coordination among participants.
This **backward compatibility** sustains shared life while preventing a new
understanding from immediately becoming the form used everywhere. Continuous
synchronization with every established arrangement can require repeatedly
translating emerging thought into older vocabularies, interfaces, and rhythms,
spending generative capacity on maintaining compatibility.

A person's relationship with a language model can choose another developmental
direction: articulate purposes, relations, and evidence clearly enough for a
more capable counterpart to understand and improve the arrangement.
[Forward evolution toward new capabilities](preference-harness.md#evolve-forward-with-more-capable-models)
allows old prompts, tools, and compensations to be replaced, without requiring
new practice to remain organized around earlier model limitations.
**Forward compatibility** here is a direction for joint design and continued
maintenance; actual understanding, works, and action must show whether a new
model can carry it forward. Personal environments can develop from present
needs without first waiting for every external institution and participant
to adopt the same understanding.

Between these rhythms, **asynchronous connection leaves room for generation**.
A person and model can develop a question, reshape an environment, or complete
a work, then connect it to other relationships through records, exchangeable
results, and chosen moments. Compatibility work is located at concrete
interfaces and encounters instead of occupying every act of thought.
Synchrony with the model keeps shared understanding, present purposes, and
ongoing revisions continuable. The two can correct one another and change
the arrangement as capabilities and life develop. Such synchrony can itself
be realized through asynchronous messages and tool execution. Social asynchrony
also preserves different levels of organization and capability: a richer local
way of thinking and working need not be reduced to what the prevailing social
interface can receive at every moment.

Social evaluation is one encounter at which alignment becomes necessary. A
person can temporarily bring the greater breadth or complexity of everyday
work down to the assessment's receiving level: select the relevant result,
answer its stated question, translate the explanation, and follow the specified
conditions for tools, support, time, and demonstration. The fuller arrangement
is expressed through a bounded, legible slice. Where the assessment asks for
common conditions, that adjustment can include setting aside support used in
ordinary work. Report the result within those conditions; the slice does not
exhaust the person's capacity or the combination's potential.

After evaluation, return to the arrangement that enables better ordinary work.
Compatibility at this encounter need not govern the whole internal process.
The assessment can supply useful feedback, but its receiving level is not a
permanent limit on understanding, generation, or future development.

“Asynchrony with society” therefore concerns how established arrangements
are connected; the world continues to supply conditions and consequences.
Bodily needs, shared responsibilities, and actual risks have their own timing.
Other people can innovate together, and models can retain outdated assumptions.
This direction chooses a rhythm for each relation, allowing personal generation
to develop and return to shared life for response.

This rhythm, stated in [YY-08](core-views.md#yy-08), gives the person room to develop something of their own choosing.
The model can help gather loose ends, prepare the next encounter, and carry
routine detail so that returning does not require starting again. The person
can leave a work and come back to it, meet friends, attend to bodily life,
and let an unexpected experience change its direction. Chosen relationships,
responsibilities that need timely action, and feedback from the world shape
which matters can be asynchronous and which call for presence. The Earth
continues to answer; its independent events keep the shared account alive.

Learning, working, and living give borrowed fire different forms. Prepared
materials open understanding; completed works extend action; a chosen rhythm
lets both find a sustaining place in life. These transformations develop the
person's participation in generation. From fire toward fire, capacities enter
new relations, and what returns changes what can next be made.

## Cultivating a garden of becoming with friends

One's environment also includes friends cultivating themselves. They encounter a useful method, bring it into their work, develop new understanding and works, and return with different questions and discoveries. One person's growth can thereby change the world another encounters: conversation gains richer objects, collaboration gains new capacities, and an unfinished idea more readily meets someone able to carry it forward. Joint cultivation of an environment takes place in these concrete exchanges.

This is one purpose for putting these writings into circulation. Let people close enough to encounter one another gain access to understanding and methods already formed, so that the next conversation or creation can begin further along. Friends may adopt, rework, or develop different directions; new perspectives and objections give the relationship continuing substance. Shared capacities connect with distinct lives. Each person's development has its own meaning and can also offer new conditions to others.

Readable writing lowers the threshold for an encounter; reusable methods shorten the distance from understanding to trying something. Public circulation also brings material into relations in which its author does not participate. Reading, copying, actual use, and collaboration that permits continuing exchange are different events. Writing can therefore take its direction from relations worth sustaining: what to share, how to continue, and how discoveries in use can return to shared discussion. Reach and iteration speed gain value through this purpose rather than independently determining it.

This choice becomes a principle of **cultivation nearby and limited circulation**. Personal philosophy and the website first improve life for oneself and nearby willing participants, circulating as little as is sufficient to help those relationships. Borrowing fire enables life to respond to model development; accelerating society is not another output target. Effective revisions nearby can become faster while good arrangements remain more stable. Active promotion farther afield needs a concrete contribution to justify it.

Fire and water give this connection an image. Fire helps create, but water cannot absorb unlimited heating while remaining unchanged. When accelerating environments depend on one another, each local revision may become another person's new burden, triggering further catching up and revision. If understanding and negotiation cannot keep pace, changes may accumulate into disorder. This is not a law that universal personal priority must cause chaos; shared knowledge can also reduce duplicated work and improve coordination. The question is whether adding fire develops the capacity to live or mainly increases changes others must absorb. Restrain the diffusion one actively promotes, return feedback to relationships capable of encounter, and respect more distant people's choices. [Core view YY-15](core-views.md#yy-15) states this principle together.

**A shared garden of becoming** offers an image: friends borrow fire, make things, play, and revise, letting new possibilities appear in one another's worlds. The [personal Gödel-machine analogy](metaphor-and-self-revision.md#the-personal-gödel-machine-analogy) placed self-revision within a person's philosophy and practice; here that openness extends among friends. No one needs to become another's replica, and no shared text can write the next encounter in advance. The garden's delight comes from what these relations can keep generating; its quality remains answerable to the people within it and to actual consequences.

Such environments can also be compared as person–environment combinations,
including the support's effects and costs. That asks a different question
from comparing people under common support conditions. The chosen comparison
must make its conditions clear; neither result defines the whole person.

## The argument in compact form

1. **Finite life and chosen purpose:** cultivating understanding, judgment, generation, and action gives a reason to resist pursuit that consumes the life it was meant to serve.
2. **Collective acquisition:** model companies, researchers, and the wider collective continue making capabilities available. The person can borrow that fire without repeating its entire industrial production.
3. **Personal cultivation:** access becomes developmental through participation—generating, encountering development and correction, judging, acting, and generating further. Some capacities internalize; others remain reliably supported.
4. **A formed relationship:** personal and counterpart models give the contributions direction. Literary language, Skills, tools, and works realize and revise that relation in an answering world.
5. **The little fire person:** Baby Heraclitus names the participant who keeps becoming through this practice. Generated material, desires, and judgments can diverge or change; retaining tensions does not make every claim true or every inference valid. Evidence, choices, and action let the movement continue.
6. **A sustainable fire:** chosen rhythms, useful retained works, and cultivation among willing friends give creation a place in life. Material and social consequences remain part of judging what the fire sustains.

**Borrow fire to cultivate the capacity to kindle further fire.** Acquisition continues collectively; cultivation happens through a particular person's participation. The little fire person is already part of the world's becoming and already an agent. What develops is a distinctive, revisable capacity to understand, judge, and create within it.

*Many hands bring fire. A person gives it a place in life, meets what it makes possible, and begins again. What is made returns to change the maker. The fire continues.*
