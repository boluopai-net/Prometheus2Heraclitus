This page maps propositions to their developed arguments. YY-01–03 establish
the developmental purpose and participants; YY-04–08 organize their encounter;
YY-09–12 distinguish how its claims are formed, checked, and expressed;
YY-13–15 connect this practice to a singular life and relations with friends.
The stable IDs support discussion, not a compulsory sequence. Both reader
editions carry the same content; English-capable readers and models should
use this English edition.

The foundation is a contemporary development of [Heraclitean fire (B30)](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Fragments_of_Heraclitus_%28annotated%29#Fragment_30),
becoming, and [opposing tension (B51)](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Fragments_of_Heraclitus_%28annotated%29#Fragment_51).
Person and model are understood as interacting event streams: continuations
of sensing, expression, interpretation, and action that generate and revise
local views. This computational account abstracts question-relevant relations
within a common physical reality. It does not establish identical mechanisms
or a complete reduction of experience. [Stealing fire](stealing-fire.md#from-living-fire-to-interacting-event-streams)
develops the foundation; [The stream and time](flow-and-time.md) develops its
continuity. [YinYang and Taiji](yinyang-and-taiji.md) gives their differentiated,
changing contributions an image. Participants exceed their roles, and an open
process can still produce a finished work.

<a id="yy-01"></a>

## YY-01 · Reverse alignment: people develop with generative models

Reverse alignment chooses human development as its purpose. Collaboration
should enrich what the person can perceive, associate, understand, judge,
express, and create, until cultivated understanding can also work through
intuition. Models contribute by understanding context, developing possibilities,
and adapting their work; the person can change through encountering those
contributions. Reciprocal adjustment gives this development practical means.

Agreement, a useful output, and human growth are different outcomes. Growth
requires changes in the person's actual participation and capacities, whether
internalized or reliably supported by the environment. [Stealing fire](stealing-fire.md#what-makes-the-fire-ones-own)
develops this distinction between using available capability and cultivating it.

<a id="yy-02"></a>

## YY-02 · The person as an embodied multimodal generative model

The **embodied multimodal generative model** is a functional and developmental
analogy. Body, senses, emotion, movement, and lived situations participate in
understanding; images, sounds, language, and action connect to generate new
conceptions and responses. Embodiment concerns this continuing encounter with
the world, beyond receiving several data formats. It does not imply that human
biology and model architecture are identical.

This orientation favors embodied perception, broad reading, intuitive
recognition, and cross-modal understanding. A person can voice a partial
intuition or indicate a mismatch through an image while the model develops
explanations, formal representations, programs, and checks. Access to these
contributions need not wait for the person to reproduce the model's linguistic
strengths. Fluency still requires verification. [Participation, access, and support](participation-and-support.md#why-organize-participation-this-way)
explains the developmental reasons and conditions for this arrangement.

<a id="yy-03"></a>

## YY-03 · Model oneself, the model counterpart, and their relation together

A role acquires its situated purpose through a relationship. An account of how
the person attends, desires, and interprets, or of how they prefer to learn
and work, can guide an account of the model counterpart. Both accounts develop
through interaction. Other participants' purposes matter where a shared task
depends on them; their accounts remain partial and revisable too.

Model the coupled activity: what each notices, expresses, initiates, interprets,
and changes. The person differs from their account and from its written or
visual expression; the actual model system likewise exceeds its working
description. “Model psychology” names observable patterns and revisable
explanations without presuming human feelings inside the system. [A personal account adequate to its use](metaphor-and-self-revision.md#a-personal-account-adequate-to-its-use)
develops this bounded modeling; [Stealing fire](stealing-fire.md#why-the-relation-must-begin-somewhere-personal)
explains why a relation needs situated content.

<a id="yy-04"></a>

## YY-04 · Let literature, history, philosophy, and aesthetics organize collaboration

Literature offers characters, situations, and expressive possibilities; history
makes their formation and transformation visible; philosophy articulates
objects, reasons, and values; aesthetics participates in perception, judgment,
and creation through form. Together they can organize a relationship whose
possibilities would fragment across isolated instructions.

Literary programming lets a character, image, or narrative orient interpretation
and action. A coupled portrait can change expectations about who initiates,
judges, or carries work forward. A harness—the instructions, tools, and
environment shaping interaction—can realize that arrangement within actual
authorization and safety boundaries. An image admits several interpretations;
articulate the relevant relations and examine their effects in conversation,
works, and action. [Metaphor, analogy, and self-revision](metaphor-and-self-revision.md#what-an-image-makes-available)
develops this movement from image to revisable relation; [Stealing fire](stealing-fire.md#literary-programming-and-the-model-counterpart)
connects it to literary programming. The images themselves are developed in
their owning essays, including [YinYang and Taiji](yinyang-and-taiji.md).

<a id="yy-05"></a>

## YY-05 · Both participants generate and correct

A person may recognize an unspoken relation or a felt mismatch in the whole.
The model can investigate details, propose explanations of its own, and carry
construction and checking forward. Either can generate, interpret, infer, and
revise. The person need not complete all reasoning before the model contributes.

Sun and Moon give differentiated generation, reception, and shaping an image. Their
roles can be retained or exchanged without assigning them by gender or
exhausting either participant's abilities. The answering world also contributes
conditions neither controls. [YinYang and Taiji](yinyang-and-taiji.md) develops
this mutual grounding and the relative independence that creations acquire.

<a id="yy-06"></a>

## YY-06 · Arrange contributions across listening, speaking, reading, and writing

These four activities offer entrances into cognition distributed across people,
models, representations, and tools. One useful arrangement lets the person
cultivate intuition through interfaces, broad reading, and lived encounters,
while the model maintains sustained exposition, code, tools, and materials.
Both can reason and generate; teaching or another purpose can change the
division.

| Mode | The person's participation | The model's contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listening | Attend to meaning, sound, and rhythm; recognize a question or possibility | Develop grounded explanations and examples; adapt to feedback |
| Speaking | Voice intuitions, intentions, and objections; discover relations while speaking | Reconstruct unfinished meaning, investigate, and return developed possibilities |
| Reading | Read broadly, compare meanings, and examine works and results | Retrieve originals, check details, and make key relations inspectable |
| Writing | Bring prompts, ideas, critiques, and conception drafts into shared work | Develop and maintain complete arguments, programs, and works; verify results |

For example, a short spoken objection to an article can lead the model to check
its source and build a comparison; reading or hearing that result can reveal
the next mismatch. A conversational prompt and a maintained manuscript are
different contributions within writing. A transcript supplies words; hearing
tone requires actual audio. [Listening, speaking, reading, and writing across roles](metaphor-and-self-revision.md#listening-speaking-reading-and-writing-across-roles)
develops the allocation; [Participation, access, and support](participation-and-support.md)
examines access and its costs. [Preparing the encounter](stealing-fire.md#a-way-of-learning-prepare-the-encounter)
connects selected texts, diagrams, audio, and exercises to learning.

<a id="yy-07"></a>

## YY-07 · Let the environment carry memory and return attention to understanding

Retrievable records and works provide shared external memory. The model can
retrieve, compare, and maintain relevant material so the person need not
restate every detail. A useful handoff retains the question, evidence, working
interpretation, and consequential alternatives needed to continue.

A source record, a candidate interpretation, and an accepted working view
have different roles. Storage establishes availability, not truth or present
applicability; selection should preserve relevant authorization and privacy.
[Externalization and distributed cognition](metaphor-and-self-revision.md#externalization-and-distributed-cognition)
develops this arrangement, while [Continuation, records, and relief](participation-and-support.md#continuation-records-and-relief)
asks when support actually reduces the burden of participation.

<a id="yy-08"></a>

## YY-08 · Synchronize with the model, stay asynchronous with society, align for evaluation

Ordinary collaboration can stay synchronous with the model and asynchronous
with society. Synchrony means continuing questions, context, corrections, and
environmental development together, even across pauses. Social asynchrony
allows a local arrangement to develop without translating every internal step
into prevailing institutional forms. Chosen relationships and timely
responsibilities still determine where immediate coordination matters.

Social evaluation calls for temporary alignment: narrow or translate fuller
capability and work into what the assessment can receive, aligning tools,
support, timing, and task conditions where its rules require them. Afterwards,
return to the ordinary arrangement shown to work better. The assessment's
slice need not become a permanent ceiling on capability or life.
[YY-15](#yy-15) distinguishes common-condition performance from supported
person–environment capability. [Choosing the rhythm of the fire](stealing-fire.md#a-way-of-living-choose-the-rhythm-of-the-fire)
develops this chosen rhythm and its limits; it does not presume that every
model or local arrangement is superior.

## YY-09 · Distinguish ontology, epistemology, value, and methodology

Modest naturalism names the framework's worldly scope; contemporary fire
monism articulates the dynamic unity of that world. Philosophy, psychology,
and engineering are physically realized within it. The whole framework uses
computational abstractions over this common reality. For a question,
computational ontology articulates relevant events,
states, roles, relations, and transformations. Shared physical grounding does
not itself supply a feasible calculation, establish detailed reduction, or
erase differences among explanatory levels. Useful abstraction weighs the
distinctions gained against the assumptions, representation, inference, and
revision they require.

| Dimension | Its question |
| --- | --- |
| Ontology | Which objects, relations, and transformations make the inquiry articulable? |
| Epistemology | What warrants an interpretation or inference, and when should it change? |
| Value | What is worth pursuing, for whom, and at whose cost? |
| Methodology | How should inquiry and collaboration be carried out and maintained? |

These intersecting questions distinguish responsibilities, rather than file
types or a fixed hierarchy. YinYang primarily develops ontology and epistemology
and articulates a value orientation; specialist Skills supply their methods.
Practice brings all four into action and revision. Concrete implementation
gives practice an executable, inspectable form; it does not replace value or
add a fifth axis of the same kind. A tool's success and an improved life
therefore require different evidence.

In process modeling, distinguish actual interaction, its partial event records,
and candidate computational representations. Propose structures from records,
derive consequences within candidates, and compare them with further
interaction. Chronology alone establishes no cause; revising a representation
differs from changing the participant. Human and AI activity may share an
agent-level representation while differing in state, update rules, and
realization. This is a research orientation requiring empirical support,
not an established universal cognitive mechanism.

[World, process, and expression](world-and-expression.md#physical-ground-and-computational-abstraction)
develops grounding; [Finite inquiry and economical abstraction](finite-inquiry-and-abstraction.md)
develops reduction and computational cost; [Reasoning within an unfinished stream](reasoning.md#shared-scientific-inquiry)
develops candidate inquiry. [Personal-first humanism](personal-first-humanism.md#four-connected-axes)
explains the four dimensions, and [Trinity](local-source-and-website.md)
connects philosophy, psychology, and engineering within one physical reality
to their practical forms.

## YY-10 · Retain conflict while distinguishing retention from acceptance

Keeping incompatible candidates available differs from jointly accepting their
claims. Establish whether they concern the same referent, time, and conditions;
then compare their consequences and discriminating evidence. Mention, denial,
and acceptance also differ: recording a denial does not establish its content,
and denial provides no rule for inferring affirmation. An expression's
occurrence time, the source time of its content, and the time represented
can differ. Recency alone
establishes neither truth nor present applicability.

The repair-based analogy favors a coherent working ontology with possibly
inconsistent data. Stability can require revising an unsuitable ontology.
Priority and repair strategy select views; AR, IAR, brave, and parameterized
inference semantics determine answers differently. Incomparability, consistent
support, and agreement on results must remain distinct. Chosen purposes orient
selection; they do not supply evidential warrant. Ordinary collaboration uses
these distinctions through informal natural language, without requiring a
formal calculus. [A preference harness](preference-harness.md) develops the
qualified formal comparison and [Reasoning](reasoning.md#sources-candidates-and-the-active-workspace)
develops its relation to retained sources and current inquiry.

### A small fictional example

Suppose a demo shows a yellow indicator. One help-page record says yellow means
“ready”; another says “waiting.” What does yellow mean **in this demo version**?

1. **Records:** retain both statements and their version information without adopting either.
2. **Candidates:** assign yellow “ready” in one interpretation and “waiting” in the other; leave the working meaning unresolved.
3. **Discriminating consequence:** assume both candidates specify that color changes exactly when the ready/waiting state changes. Completion changes “waiting” to “ready” and leaves “ready” unchanged. Yellow should therefore persist under the first interpretation and change under the second.
4. **Feedback:** a controlled run records completion, yellow turning green, and readiness at green. Under the stated rules this supports the second interpretation for the tested version. The working view changes; both records remain.

The transition assumptions make the observation discriminatory. If color can
change for other reasons, include those causes before drawing the conclusion.
A candidate's removal from current use neither erases its source nor proves
it false in every setting.

## YY-11 · The world constrains the shared account

Texts, theories, programs, and actions meet use, resistance, counterexamples,
and unintended consequences. Agreement between participants and coherence
within a candidate cannot establish external fit. World-directed evidence
arrives through observations and records that also require interpretation;
this makes accounts corrigible while leaving them constrained.

Changed norms or worldly conditions can revise premises, purposes, and
completion criteria. A finite task can still finish under a warranted working
view without resolving every retained disagreement. An account never exhausts
the process it represents, and a work can return to change its maker's
understanding. [World, process, and expression](world-and-expression.md#a-work-returns-to-the-encounter)
develops this return; [Finishing situated tasks](preference-harness.md#finish-situated-tasks-amid-changing-norms-and-worlds)
connects revisability with completion and replacing obsolete means.

<a id="yy-12"></a>

## YY-12 · Make the views perceptible, discussable, and continuable

Expression gives others something they can encounter and respond to. A diagram,
dynamic process, or manipulable example can show what changes when a premise,
candidate, or contribution changes, while making its basis inspectable. Such
forms select relations from a wider process; their clarity does not establish
every claim they convey.

English and Chinese reader editions carry the same views; maintenance prose
connects their meaning to realization. Accessible expression and retained
philosophical substance let the account develop without exposing private
portraits or sources. [World, process, and expression](world-and-expression.md#screenshot-and-front-end)
develops selective expression; [Trinity](local-source-and-website.md#three-places-for-the-work-to-continue)
explains how the environment, repository, and website let work be used,
continued, and discussed.

## YY-13 · Write a sinthome through an individual meta-harness

The human agent is already present. A meta-harness supplies a philosophical
and relational framework for interpreting and revising the instructions,
roles, tools, and environment through which that person works with models.
Formation develops a personal philosophy and its singular lived, maintained
form. This proposal calls the practice of giving experience, expression, and
action a livable form **writing a sinthome for oneself**.

Usable articulation can begin before complete self-understanding; the person
always exceeds the text. Capabilities, tools, authorization, others, and worldly
conditions mediate enlarged agency. [Writing a sinthome](writing-a-sinthome.md)
develops this practical extension, distinguishing it from [Lacan's historical account in *Seminar XXIII*, 17 February 1976](https://recherche-lacan.gnipl.fr/2013/07/26/lxxiii-le-sinthome-1975-1976-lecon-du-17-fevrier-1976/),
a clinical conclusion, or a guarantee of happiness and unlimited power.

## YY-14 · Cultivate generative capacities with available fire

Model companies, researchers, and the wider collective make generative capability
available: materials, electricity, machines, knowledge, data, and training
contribute in different ways. Reuse distributes formation costs across
applications; verified tools and works can support further capability.
Acquisition and cultivation continue together. Physical computation consumes
energy and dissipates heat, while knowledge and development require judgments
of meaning, evidence, and life consequences.

A finite person can borrow this fire without repeating its industrial acquisition
or exhausting their life pursuing every expansion of the Sun. For the chosen
developmental purpose, participation, criticism, correction, and renewed
generation cultivate what they can understand, judge, express, initiate, and
sustain. The little fire person—Baby Heraclitus—names this continuing becoming:
generated possibilities, desires, and judgments can change or conflict. Claims
remain answerable to evidence, and choices remain revisable. Capacities may become internalized or remain
reliably distributed across supports. Reusable understanding, methods, works,
software, and relations can accumulate as personal assets. Chasing each model
change loses its purpose when it displaces this cultivation. Personal purposes
and voluntary development guide adaptation: seek changes to social arrangements,
then software, before asking the person to adapt, while respecting others'
purposes. This is a design direction, not an unconditional priority or a fixed
algorithm. [Stealing fire](stealing-fire.md) develops the integrated material,
developmental, and practical argument; [Personal-first humanism](personal-first-humanism.md)
develops its value orientation.

<a id="yy-15"></a>

## YY-15 · Cultivate a garden of becoming with friends

One person's development can become another's condition for growth. Friends
bring questions, works, methods, and objections into one another's environments
while retaining their own purposes and directions. This writing seeks such
nearby exchanges: **cultivate local life and restrain wider diffusion**, sharing
as much as helps actual relationships rather than treating adoption scale as
success. Keeping a good arrangement can be sound cultivation too.

The restraint has a conditional reason. If changes in interdependent local
arrangements accelerate beyond what relationships can understand, negotiate,
and absorb, local gains may impose shared coordination costs. Open sharing
can also improve coordination; this possible feedback is not an established
social law. The chosen response regulates one's own promotion and acceleration
pressure, without preventing others' learning or depending on their stagnation.

Local difference needs actual results to establish superiority, including life
quality and costs passed to others. Evaluation must specify its object:
performance under common tools, support, time, and task conditions answers
one question; what a person–environment combination can achieve, including
maintenance costs, answers another. Both can be reported with their conditions;
neither substitutes for the other. [YY-08](#yy-08) locates temporary comparison
within ordinary life. [Stealing fire](stealing-fire.md#cultivating-a-garden-of-becoming-with-friends)
develops this garden's reciprocal cultivation and limited circulation;
[the personal Gödel-machine analogy](metaphor-and-self-revision.md#the-personal-gödel-machine-analogy)
develops the connected image of self-revision. The garden remains a chosen
image whose value is answered by participants' experiences and consequences.
