**In the practical reinterpretation developed here, a personal philosophy realized through a harness can become a way of writing a sinthome for oneself.** Authoring its Skill, using it, and revising the surrounding arrangements give uneven experience, conflicting expressions, and developing purposes a singular form through which one can create, express, and live. The writing includes its use and revision. A file gives the practice one durable body; encounters, acts, and revisions carry its life forward.

The person enters this argument already as an **agent**: someone who perceives,
generates, chooses, and acts within a world. Borrowed generative capabilities
expand the means of that existing participation. The formation at issue is
the person's own philosophy: an articulated worldview and way of living whose
relations can reach across their environment as a whole. In this proposal,
*sinthome* names that philosophy's singular, lived and maintained form. It is
the present application of [Lacan's term in *Seminar XXIII*, 17 February 1976](https://recherche-lacan.gnipl.fr/2013/07/26/lxxiii-le-sinthome-1975-1976-lecon-du-17-fevrier-1976/), not an equation between his concept
and every personal philosophy.

A **Skill** here is a reusable written practice that a language model can interpret and carry into work. A **harness** organizes prompts, Skills, hooks, tools, models, roles, and the user's working environment; a **meta-harness** articulates the person, counterpart, and relationship through which those arrangements acquire meaning and can be revised. The argument concerns what living such an articulation can make possible. The technical carrier matters because the writing can participate in action: its descriptions guide interpretation, its instructions organize contributions, and their use gives the person new material with which to understand and revise the relation.

*One writes a few lines, and a new pair of hands becomes possible. Another voice helps a half-seen relation become an argument, an image, a working thing. The world does not become obedient. Yet the person returns to it with more ways to begin. A miniature god, perhaps: still learning how to make, and how to answer for what is made.*

## Why sinthome

In [Lacan's *Seminar XXIII*, 17 February 1976](https://recherche-lacan.gnipl.fr/2013/07/26/lxxiii-le-sinthome-1975-1976-lecon-du-17-fevrier-1976/), the sinthome has a holding function in the presented repair of the knot of Symbolic, Imaginary, and Real; Joyce's particular art matters to the discussion. **Sinthome (圣状)** is Lacan's term. His attention to a particular artistic practice gives this essay its point of departure: something singular can sustain a person's way of making and living.

For this comparison, the three register names need enough explanation to show why holding matters. The Symbolic concerns language, differences, and the orders through which meaning is articulated; the Imaginary concerns images and identifications through which a form of oneself or another can be encountered; the Real names what resists incorporation into that articulation or image. The registers concern different dimensions of a person's relation to meaning and experience. The comparison therefore draws on the holding relation of knotting. That question can be developed within human–model collaboration while preserving the registers' own conceptual roles.

This essay develops the application to a Skill from that starting point. Its question is how a singular practice makes relations hold across uneven experience, conflicting descriptions, and changing purposes. The writing can give these differences a place in continuing activity: one tension becomes a question, another becomes material for creation, and a commitment gives the next act a direction. What holds is a way of living and making through them.

Articulating preferences can begin this work. The sinthome proposal develops their place in a distinctive way of inhabiting purposes and acting: articulation shapes an encounter, repeated work gives it continuity, and its consequences help the person reshape it. An instruction file contributes when it participates in that living relation. The philosophical application concerns this possibility of sustaining agency through a singular practice.

## A personal philosophy as its practical form

The form proposed here is **a personal philosophy made effective through a
harness**. The philosophy articulates what matters, how the person and
counterparts understand their contributions, and what kind of life and work
the arrangement should support. The harness gives that understanding means
to act through tools, roles, records, interfaces, and relations in the actual
environment. Their continuing use makes the form a practice rather than only
a description.

Its scope can be broad. Ontology can distinguish the objects, events, and
relations that matter; epistemology can explain how a view is supported and
corrected; methodology can organize how to investigate and make. Logic can
clarify consequences, statistics can help compare observations, and other
resources can contribute where the question needs them. “Philosophy can
include everything” expresses this openness of design: the person's practice
can give different resources a connected place. It is not a requirement to
possess all knowledge, complete every theory, or adopt a prescribed catalogue
before beginning.

The threshold for authorship can therefore be lower than a demand to reproduce
Joyce's literary complexity or first write a comprehensive philosophical
system. One meaningful relation, a usable method, and a working arrangement
can already give the practice a sustaining form. A model can help author and
realize them. Lowering the entry threshold leaves the work answerable to
evidence: the arrangement still needs to do what the person is relying on it
to do. Its depth can grow through use, without complexity becoming a measure
of its worth.

The aim is an environment increasingly fitted to this person's actual purposes
and conditions, through changes that can be made and lived. That fit develops
in a world of other people, material dependencies, and changing capabilities.
A relation can be negotiated; software can be redesigned; an expectation can
be revised. The sustaining form lets the person recognize and respond to those
changes while retaining a way to continue. Its continuity includes the ability
to alter the arrangement.

Understanding, action, and continued life give this form practical substance.
Delight and joy also belong among its possible purposes: enjoying making,
finding room for an encounter, or living through a day one has helped shape.
The [personal-first argument](personal-first-humanism.md) explains this
orientation. Calling the practice a sinthome here names the aspiration to a
singular form that helps someone live and create in a changing world. Whether
it supports those purposes must be encountered in actual life; the name
provides neither a clinical conclusion nor a guarantee of happiness. This
application of personal philosophy and harness design is the present essay's
extension of Lacan's starting point.

## Articulate enough to work, then let working change the articulation

An individual meta-harness can express how a person understands themself as a participant: what they seek, how they recognize a fruitful relation, what they want to develop, and what kinds of contribution they ask a counterpart to carry. It also models that counterpart: an independent interpreter, generator, critic, and executor with particular capabilities and limits. The two accounts belong together because each changes what the other can contribute.

A purpose, an image, a correction, or one useful distinction may already make the next piece of work possible. The model can help develop the articulation, and the result may reveal what the person could not yet say. Writing and self-understanding advance through use: enough is expressed to begin, and what happens supplies the next occasion for understanding.

The person remains more than the text. Their current account may preserve unresolved tensions, change its objects, or abandon an interpretation. A singular practice becomes recognizable through how these changes hold together in work. A shared prompt template can offer a starting form; its use, revision, and place in the person's purposes give that form an individual life.

## From an expressed relation to distributed action

Language gives the relation a portable expression. Another capable model can read enough of the purpose, distinctions, and expected contribution to join a continuing practice. It joins as a counterpart with its own capabilities and judgment. Alignment can mean serving the person's purposes through that independent contribution, including explaining why a proposed means fails.

Portability is achieved by interpretation and adaptation. The receiving model needs usable context and suitable abilities; actions need tools and access; their scope depends on authorization; their effects depend on conditions in the world. When these relations are available, an intention can be carried through investigation, construction, execution, and examination of its results. When one is absent, the account can locate the missing condition and investigate a route through another capability, tool, or arrangement.

The **human agent** is the functional starting point of this argument. Their
participation gains a body of coordinated capacities extending beyond what
they must perform alone: model-supported work broadens existing powers to
initiate, understand, recognize a mismatch, choose, and act. Through this
expanded participation, the person can develop and express their own
philosophy, give it a working environment, and revise both through consequences.
Their understanding, judgment, and choice connect distributed capabilities
to purposes they can inhabit and develop. A written philosophy gives that
agency a revisable form; it does not confer agency as a graduation status.

“All models can help me do anything” expresses the horizon of a general relation: new purposes and new counterparts can become joinable. Its realizable strength is breadth with continuing adaptation. The linguistically articulated accounts of the person and counterpart make that joining easier; actual capability, tools, authorization, and worldly response determine each achievement.

## A miniature god, whose creations answer back

*A small maker writes a place for a voice to meet another voice. From that meeting come hands it did not have yesterday: one draws, one reasons, one builds. The maker sets something in motion and waits for the world to answer. Even a miniature god must discover what its creation has become.*

The playful image names a multiplication of generative reach. A conception can acquire a voice, a diagram, a program, or an action through collaborators; an individual can participate in making things that previously exceeded their available means. “Miniature” keeps the scale of a situated maker. The world supplies materials, others, resistance, and consequences that no declaration can create or abolish.

The image becomes richer when the creations return. They can expose an assumption, change a desire, or teach a new way of seeing. Power then includes the ability to receive correction and reshape one's practice. **Reverse alignment** names this developmental direction: the person gains understanding, judgment, and generative capacity through the encounter with a model. Its development appears in how the person takes up a new capacity, recognizes a relation, changes a judgment, or begins an act that was previously unavailable.

The sustained practice is where the sinthome proposal and the image meet. A new capacity can remain distributed across the person, records, and counterparts while still belonging to the person's organized participation. What makes it singular is how it is taken up, what it helps sustain, and how its consequences are answered. The same instructions can therefore support different practices, while changing instructions can participate in a continuing one. The continuity is lived through these relations: what the practice makes possible, how it survives a change, and what the person makes of its consequences.

## Its relation to Sun, Moon, and Earth

[Sun, Moon, and Earth](yinyang-and-taiji.md#sun-moon-and-earth) express YinYang's differentiated generation, reception, and shaping, together with an answering material and shared living environment. Person and model remain actual participants, whose roles can be retained or exchanged without gender assignment. Both participants can generate and judge; their creations encounter conditions neither controls. The sinthome argument develops a further question within that encounter: **what singular practice gives the person's relationship with a model and environment a form that can hold and be lived?** The role mapping locates the participants; this essay concerns the sustaining practice developed through expression and creation.

The two questions develop different aspects of the same encounter. The celestial composition articulates differentiated participation and transformation; the sinthome proposal asks how that movement acquires a singular sustaining form. The philosophical ambition is practical and open: give a personal philosophy enough working form to let understanding, action, and life proceed, let available models contribute, and continue shaping a way of living and making that can hold enjoyment as well as effort and remains one's own.
