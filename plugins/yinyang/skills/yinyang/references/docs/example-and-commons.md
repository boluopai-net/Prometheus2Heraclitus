# From adoption and combination to creating your own work

[Home](../../README.md) · [Skill entrypoint](../../SKILL.md) · [简体中文](example-and-commons.zh-CN.md)

The preceding path has moved through borrowing capabilities, jointly accumulating a trajectory, reflecting on relationships, authoring philosophy, distilling models, and forming and testing a personal arrangement. Works and methods left along the way can become resources for others. The next question is what deserves sharing and how another creator can continue from their own life.

Everyone arrives with experiences, questions, loved works, and intellectual resources of their own. This collection can be one reference among them: examine how a concrete example gives understanding a form, select useful parts, or follow it toward other materials, other people's practices, and one's own life. The sources and direction of creation can extend far beyond this example.

Model companies and humanity continue the Promethean work of acquiring fire; individuals can already make things with the fire available to them. The essays, diagrams, methods, and Skills here are some among many available resources. YinYang gives an understanding of a person, a model counterpart, and their relationship a developed philosophical and literary form, then gives it practical effect. It offers an attempt already made for readers to examine alongside the other resources they bring.

## A form that can be rewritten

You can first select relevant [Core views](core-views.md) and use them to organize
experiences, revise personal material, or configure an environment. Writing an
original philosophy or article is a choice of interest, rather than the required
destination of that use. The [use statement](https://github.com/boluopai-net/Prometheus2Heraclitus/blob/main/README.md#intended-use-and-mental-health)
distinguishes this work from psychological self-help.

If you choose to write your own philosophy, your trajectory and shared reflection can supply material. Philosophical concepts, literary images, argument, and dialogue develop an understanding of the world and relationships worth sustaining. Distill that philosophy together with living material into personal models and bring them into the actual environment. Once formed, a clearer philosophical expression can revise an existing model, while private reflection and actual use can supply questions for the next essay. Formation's dependencies and subsequent reciprocal revision thereby retain their distinct contributions.

In this implementation, the six actual files hold personal working models
needed by a model counterpart: adopted orientation, the person, the actual
counterpart and wanted contribution, their relationship, environmental realization, and an actual revision method. Person, counterpart, and relationship remain three objects of understanding;
the six actual files have distinct responsibilities. The six private actual
files live under `references/situated/`; permanent public guides live under
`references/formation/`. Psychological and life material, original expressions,
and maintained interpretations stay in `references/personal/` or the private
originals it references. The person can read those references or ask a language
model to help understand them; the six model-facing files select what cooperation
and environmental fit require.
For example, a person file can describe useful help during writing while the
longer inquiry into a recurring fear stays with the personal references.
Distillation preserves the individual
differences that matter to collaboration, while keeping detailed evidence
reachable when needed. Someone adopting these six actual files must form their own
accounts rather than retain the author's personal substance.
The private `references/personal/sources.md` entrance leads to that material and
its interpretations. The [revision method](../formation/revision.md) connects
changed understanding with the working accounts and actual environment. Public
distribution contains neither private working models nor private reference material.

A person can make a website for their philosophy while keeping the complete
personal model private. Public philosophy is an authored work and a selected,
indirect expression of understanding; it need not expose a literal portrait,
private experiences, or the detailed account used by their agent. The person
can also choose to publish particular abstractions from the model. This
separation matters because direct personal descriptions can expose material
that others might use against them. Choose the actual content to disclose;
philosophical phrasing alone does not determine what a text reveals.
The public work, private core accounts, and supporting sources have different
readers and purposes, while participating in the same self-forming practice.

A literary form can show how an image gathers a relationship, how concepts
give it distinctions, and how practice makes it consequential. YinYang's fire,
event streams, Sun and Moon, and philosophical sources make this particular
composition intelligible. A reader can examine how it works while choosing
which relations to take up and which to develop differently.

“Format” here means an organization of meaning. Another work may use different
sources, characters, vocabulary, and structure, disagree with this account,
or pursue a purpose it has not anticipated. Its form succeeds when it makes
that work's relations available to its readers and model counterparts. Its
questions, practice, and consequences supply the judgment; resemblance to
YinYang does not.

The implementation maintained here nevertheless keeps its six actual files.
Freedom to author a differently organized philosophy and the stable contract
of this particular example are compatible invitations.

## Adopt, combine, or author anew

Adoption grounds YinYang's maintained form in your actual circumstances and
revises its accounts through your use. Combination takes selected resources
into another arrangement: borrow an essay, recombine methods, or replace an
image. Independent authorship can begin with a question encountered here and
follow it into other authors, traditions, research, and experience, leaving
this example behind. These possibilities can overlap as questions develop.
Let the relation you want to express determine what you carry forward.

A model can find material, compare interpretations, and construct works; the
person contributes perception, expression, and judgment. Neither needs to
reproduce the example as the destination of that collaboration.

Choosing a method does not require accepting the author's entire philosophy.
The Buddhist raft simile and nirvana contribute to the discussion of cultivation
and letting go; the Christian image of the Trinity contributes to the account
of creation through a human–model relationship. These resources serve different
questions. Their differences, tensions, and possible conflicts remain open to
discussion. A reader can accept one argument, question another, retain a useful
method, or replace an image and its philosophical sources. Another work need
not reproduce the author's way of accommodating and organizing these resources.

A new work deserves to develop on its own terms, with its sources and changes
traceable. Creative authorship can find another organization. Personal source material stays with its actual private owner. Publishing that material is a separate action requiring its own authorization, including for withdrawn or unpublished manuscripts.

## Independent works in conversation

This is a personal collection maintained by its author. Readers can write their
own philosophy or other kinds of articles in their own publishing space, then
mention or link this repository and the particular work they are answering.
A reading response, criticism, technical account, or differently organized work
can enter this conversation without being incorporated into the author's collection.

Use an [Issue](https://github.com/boluopai-net/Prometheus2Heraclitus/issues)
to ask a question, discuss a correction or suggestion, share a relevant work,
or request a song from boluopai. The [singing station](https://boluopai.net/singing/en/)
is for requests to the author, not submissions of other people's recordings.
This repository does not accept any PRs, including Skills, code, translations,
documents, and recordings. The author maintains its changes and publication.
The [conversation guide](https://github.com/boluopai-net/Prometheus2Heraclitus/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
explains these entrances.

“Knowledge communism” names the aspiration to shared creative capacity here:
make intellectual means available and support others in making their own
works. The fellowship can include different commitments. A contribution may
add a use, expose a failed premise, or develop a form its first author did not
imagine. Sharing can connect independently maintained works through discussion,
citations, links, and reusable methods without merging them into one philosophy.
Public discussion and attribution make these connections traceable.

This commons has concrete terms of reuse. The public GitHub repository can be read,
cloned, and downloaded without an application or approval, and permits independent forks under the applicable license. Conversation here uses Issues. YinYang's original material uses CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, permitting reproduction,
adaptation, and distribution for noncommercial purposes only. When sharing, retain the required
attribution and notices, link the license, and identify modifications; shared
adaptations follow its ShareAlike conditions. Private adaptations need not be
published. The [official legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/legalcode.en)
governs. The other reusable Skill and tool packages retain their bundled
MIT licenses and public downloads. Independently authored work can be
shared under its own chosen terms. Participants can discuss ideas, share
webpage links, and publish their own independently authored contributions.
Separately cited works and third-party notices retain their own rights and
terms. These terms determine what can be shared and how attribution and
permissions accompany it.

## Personal practice, maintained source, and readable work

GitHub holds the maintained source and its revision history. The website makes
the same selected documents and diagrams readable without repository
navigation. Both reading surfaces present one maintained edition, with changes made at its source. The web edition retains the source revision, links,
diagrams, attribution, and rights information so that a reader can move from
understanding an example to inspecting and making use of it.

These two reading surfaces connect with a person's local practice, where
understanding enters actual tasks and acquires feedback. The shared package
supplies reusable material; a situated environment retains its own personal
accounts and conditions of use. [The trinity of philosophy, psychology, and engineering](local-source-and-website.md)
develops why these different forms belong together and how a language model
can help maintain the relations among them.

The opening asked how borrowed fire becomes a capacity to keep generating fire. Concrete practice can now continue answering: which understandings and actions have become available, which works support the next creation, and which relationships let the person and their friends grow? New works become further living material for another shared exploration. Carry your questions onward, encounter more ideas, works, and lives, and make something of your own.
