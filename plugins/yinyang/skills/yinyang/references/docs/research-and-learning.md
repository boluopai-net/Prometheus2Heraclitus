# Research and Learning

Using models for research and learning can leave more attention for understanding: read widely to develop a sense of the whole, connections, and intuition, then practice, check reasoning, and evaluate at consequential points. Models can carry much retrieval, organization, derivation, and implementation, and prepare reusable training materials: broad coverage during preparation, followed by focused selection and added diagrams, examples, and supporting exercises during practice, so understanding can be checked and rebuilt when needed.

This page first develops this approach to learning and research, then introduces public resources and two teachers. Give resource links to a model and let it handle downloading, integration, and necessary configuration. The model also selects methods for your question. See [Using the Tools](https://boluopai.net/getting-started/en/) for the general tool and file arrangement.

## 1. Read widely and examine consequential points

### Learning has its own purposes; research also includes learning

Learning can serve curiosity, understanding the world, developing judgment, or enriching life, and has value in its own right. Research addresses an unresolved question through results that can be supported with reasons, tested, and revised. It requires learning existing knowledge and methods, and continues learning while forming questions, analyzing material, and revising conclusions. Research need not wait until everything has been learned.

The relationship is therefore asymmetric: learning need not serve research, while research includes learning. Developing research capability requires checking the reliability of a work and being able to revisit consequential knowledge. A model may complete an analysis, proof, or program, but the quality of the product and the person's understanding need separate judgments; one correct answer does not guarantee that understanding will not be forgotten.

### Broad reading and focused practice of consequential details

Broad reading opens a landscape of questions: its objects and relationships, how different disciplines describe them, where explanations support or conflict with one another, and where new questions might arise. Reading can combine diagrams, discussion, demonstrations, and lived experience without stopping to master every unfamiliar term. Models can recover definitions, unfold derivations, and compare sources as understanding moves between the whole and its parts.

Some details, however, support consequential judgments. Misunderstanding them can make otherwise fluent intuition fail at an important point. Knowing that a topic matters or that a model can check it may not develop the discrimination you want for yourself. For example, quantifier scope changes a proposition's meaning, the condition in a conditional probability cannot simply be reversed, and moving from correlation to a causal explanation requires further grounds. Such points may deserve personally comparing examples, carrying out necessary reasoning, repeatedly working exercises, and checking understanding after the situation changes.

The current question and its downstream dependencies determine these points, rather than a foundation checklist everyone must memorize. Work with a teacher to identify concepts whose misunderstanding would change the question, method, or conclusion; recurring confusions; and judgments you want to make before a model supplies its answer. Concentrate practice there, while models and the environment carry details that can be reliably retrieved or delegated. Foundational knowledge matters through what it supports, not simply because it comes early in a textbook.

### Learning, practice, testing, and evaluation do different work

| Activity | The question it serves here |
| --- | --- |
| Learning | How can understanding form, expand, and change, making new connections? |
| Practice | How can repeated attempts and feedback make a consequential judgment more reliable? |
| Testing | What concrete question, task, or experiment can provide evidence about capability or a result? |
| Evaluation | Does that evidence support the understanding or result needed for the current purpose, and what still needs improvement? |

Testing is one way to obtain evidence for evaluation; the two are not identical. Explanations in discussion, responses to counterexamples, actual works, and attempts to transfer understanding to a new situation can also supply evidence. A practice attempt can both develop understanding and reveal a problem. Results describe performance on that occasion, with the prompts and tools then available, helping decide what to reread, practice, or pursue next. They do not certify permanent mastery. Understanding can be forgotten or changed by a new question, so the materials need to remain reusable.

Evaluation during learning concentrates on necessary local understanding. For example, identify where two arguments differ, then change the example and judge whether that distinction still holds. After an error, return to the definition, premise, or inference, revise the understanding, and check it with a different question. When an important foundation remains unstable, several exercises or rounds of practice may be worthwhile rather than stopping after one explanation feels clear. The number of exercises serves the discovery and correction of problems. Existing materials can be revisited after forgetting or when the knowledge is needed again.

An examination covering all knowledge and demanding unaided recall of every detail is not the default aim here. Understanding the whole still matters: examine it through explanations connecting parts to the whole, comparisons of models, and use in new problems. Local checks serve that integrated understanding rather than dividing learning into disconnected question banks. When evaluating personal capability, identify what you did and what help the model supplied. A model answering for you establishes only that the collaboration produced an answer. Whether to temporarily remove prompts depends on whether independent judgment or supported capability is being examined.

### Have the model prepare reusable practice and assessment materials

Three kinds of technical study materials can work together: a **glossary** locates concepts and relationships, a **study-and-research guide** develops explanations and questions, and **training materials** provide written exercises, experiments or projects, with answers and grounds for evaluation. Testing and evaluation belong primarily in the third, with links back to the first two. Preparing these materials is a model contribution in its own right; the reader need not complete every exercise immediately, and the prepared scope need not equal the scope actually practiced.

**Preparation follows the overall knowledge structure.** Before the reader's level and particular needs are sufficiently known, have the model prepare relatively broad testing coverage across the textbook, course, or topic rather than retaining only a few presumed priorities. The model writes questions and prepares and checks answers. Each set identifies its concepts, definitions or original passages, and grounds for judgment. Answers develop necessary reasoning and common mistakes; open questions can include several reasoned responses and comparison criteria, while experimental tasks retain operating conditions, observations, and interpretation of results. This coverage leaves room for subsequent selection; it does not require every reader to complete every test.

**Practice reduces scope while adding support for understanding.** As the current question, existing understanding, and actual attempts become clearer, the model selects the few consequential parts from the prepared materials, leaving aside what is currently irrelevant, redundant, or sufficiently clear. It adds diagrams, concrete examples, counterexamples, and smaller supporting exercises for the remaining difficulties, unfolding the problem, changing conditions, and decisive relationships. Less testing coverage and more explanatory support can go together: fewer exercises, fitted more closely to the present need. For example, preparation might cover several kinds of questions across a chapter, while actual difficulty concerns a distinction in conditional probability. Concentrate on that point, adding a tree diagram, contrasting examples, and supporting questions rather than requiring completion of the whole chapter's exercises.

Present questions separately from solutions so the reader can attempt them before consulting hints, answers, and diagrams when useful. Keep the broad base materials and the selected, supplemented practice arrangement with the existing course or topic, linked to one another and to the glossary, guide, and sources. Reducing the current practice scope need not delete foundational questions that may be useful later. As needs change, select again, repeat a question, or have the model vary examples, parameters, and conditions for another check. When a question is ambiguous, an answer is wrong, or an explanation is insufficient, the model revises the relevant materials for subsequent use.

Repeatability means the questions, conditions, reference answers, and evaluation grounds can be recovered and checked again; it does not mean personal performance must remain identical. When comparing attempts is useful, retain the results with the prompts and tools used on each occasion. Returning after forgetting to review and retest is an ordinary use of these materials.

### Research evaluates its results and returns problems to learning

Models can carry substantial research detail, while their products require sufficient evaluation and repeated checking. Examine whether citations support claims, arguments rely on unstated premises, derivations and code realize the stated method, and data and results warrant the conclusion. Logical checks assess whether an inference follows; empirical claims must also face data, measurement, and counterexamples in the world. Different research questions require different checks, directed at what could actually change the conclusion.

Having a model check through another method, return to an original source, seek counterexamples, or reproduce a calculation supplies more comparable evidence than repeatedly asking the same model whether it is right. Models can prepare and perform these checks; researchers need to understand consequential assumptions, evidence, and limitations well enough to judge whether they answer the research question. Repeated polishing or multiple expressions of agreement do not automatically make a result reliable.

Learning and research thus return to one another. Evaluation during learning may reveal a critical concept that needs further explanation, practice, and checking. Research verification may uncover a flawed method or conclusion, prompting revision of the product and further learning. The former primarily examines consequential understanding; the latter primarily examines research results. Adjust depth to the conclusion's importance, the consequences of error, and existing evidence. Continue once the evidence sufficiently supports the current purpose, reopening the issue when new questions arise.

## 2. Prepare these two foundation libraries

### Scientific Agents: richer disciplinary references for models

[K-Dense Scientific Agents](https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/scientific-agents) collects expert-thinking material across disciplines and professions, including mathematics, physics, computing, statistics, and life sciences. It gives models more specific professional knowledge and ways of thinking to consult when understanding and explaining a question.

Ask a model to obtain the library and connect it to your research and teaching environment. When you ask about a concept, read an interdisciplinary paper, or discuss a research direction, the model and teacher can consult relevant experts' `AGENTS.md` files and other material. You need not read every profile or choose an expert role first.

### Scientific Agent Skills: practical research methods for models

[K-Dense Scientific Agent Skills](https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills) offers capabilities for literature retrieval, experimental design, statistics, machine learning, data processing, scientific figures, citation management, and related work. It can supply a broad foundation for research and learning across disciplines.

Give these two repositories to a model together and ask it to obtain their complete materials and connect the needed Skills. The first enriches disciplinary understanding; the second supplies concrete working methods. Let the model handle downloading, installation, and later use. Knowing these public sources exist is enough to begin; you need not learn every package name or operation first.

## 4. What Local Teacher and Web Teacher do

These are teaching and inquiry methods a model can use. They support continuing explanation, discussion, and material preparation around your questions rather than only producing a one-time article summary.

| Teacher | Useful ways to work with it |
| --- | --- |
| [Local Teacher](https://github.com/boluopai-net/Prometheus2Heraclitus/tree/main/skills/local-teacher) | Sustained study, textbook and paper reading, and concept review in local Codex; learning through local files, code, and experiments is also available. |
| [Web Teacher](https://github.com/boluopai-net/Prometheus2Heraclitus/tree/main/skills/web-teacher) | Discussing unsettled questions, comparing interpretations, exploring adjacent knowledge, and organizing course materials and continuity in Web conversations. |

The teachers share a substantive teaching method. These are practical emphases, not an exclusive local-practice versus Web-theory split. Begin through the entrance convenient for your current work.

**The teachers can use the AGENTS expert references and Skills methods in the repositories above to support both teaching and research.** A physics explanation can draw on disciplinary references, paper reading can use literature and statistical methods, and discussion of a research idea can call on modeling or writing capabilities. Ask the question directly and let the teacher select and read useful material; you need not separately direct expert and Skill selection.

When preparing the environment, give the model both teachers and the repositories above and ask it to establish actual reading and invocation access. You can then ask it to guide you through a paper, connect a concept to your earlier understanding, or discuss a research idea. Installation and connection details belong to the model; the reader needs to understand what these teachers and resources can offer.

For continuing study, teachers can also prepare glossaries, concept maps, study guides, and suitable exercises or experiments, revising them through discussion. Participate through reading, looking, questions, objections, and attempts. You can also directly request prepared materials without completing a lesson first.

A teacher can prepare broad testing and evaluation materials from the overall knowledge structure, including questions, answers, and explanations. During actual study, it narrows practice to consequential parts and adds diagrams, examples, and supporting exercises for difficulties, retaining the materials for review, repetition, and checks with changed examples. Direct explanation, practice, and evaluation can alternate with the current purpose; requesting an explanation or prepared material need not begin with passing a test.
