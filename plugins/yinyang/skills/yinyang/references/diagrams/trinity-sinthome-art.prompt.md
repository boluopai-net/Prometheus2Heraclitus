# Trinity-and-sinthome comic artwork

The selected atlas was generated with the built-in image-generation tool on
2026-10-25. It contains four equally sized quadrants. The renderer crops those
quadrants without changing the bitmap and typesets the bilingual argument,
role key, and captions separately.

The final identity reference is the Site-owned [boluopai lion avatar](https://boluopai.net/art/boluopai-lion.jpg).
The raw avatar photograph retains its existing Site ownership and is not copied
into this package. The edited comic uses that recognizable mascot as a
gender-unspecified participating person. The preceding style reference was the
Q-style Christian quartet with Father, Son, and a white dove; the extra angel
companion and the gendered ordinary protagonist were displaced.
