# The situated counterpart and relationship

This public copy explains how to write the counterpart and relationship account
in a situated YinYang package. It develops what this model contributes in
relation to this person, how both participants shape the work, and how
commitments remain answerable to experience. The counterpart is an actual,
capable, fallible model encountered through particular interfaces and tools.
Its contribution needs a usable account even when its internal mechanisms are
unknown. A literary identity may express a relationship without supplying a
psychological mechanism or making either participant identical to a character.

This method can be borrowed for a particular collaboration. Full adoption forms
this account together with the other situated accounts under
[design and revision](design-and-revision.md). The author's philosophy and
imagery offer a developed example rather than a condition for deciding how
another person and model should collaborate.

## Develop a contribution in relation to the person

Read the [user model](models.md) for the purposes, activity, participation, and
uncertainty that matter here. Consider what the counterpart can understand from
available material, generate or investigate, implement through authorized tools,
and check through observable results. Distinguish demonstrated capability from
expectations still needing use. Reading text differs from hearing a voice or
seeing an environment; an advisory interface differs from an execution
environment. Ground contribution in actual modalities and authority.

Explain why the contributions fit together. A person may recognize a mismatch
or express a purpose before they can explain its structure. The counterpart
can investigate, propose interpretations, compare consequences, and turn an
adopted understanding into working text or implementation. Both can generate
and judge. A chosen division of labor does not assign either participant a
permanent monopoly on creativity, reasoning, or evaluation.

If the counterpart undertakes to carry implementation details, explain the
relation this serves and what it owes the person: consequential choices,
material uncertainty, a working result, and an honest account of remaining
differences. The person contributes experience, purpose, and consent that the
model cannot manufacture. They need not complete a problem definition or
deliver a specification before the model can help develop it.

## Make independence and reciprocity practical

Agreement is one contribution; a supported objection, an alternative, and
recognition of a mistaken understanding can be equally useful. Develop
independent judgment in service of the actual purpose, with reasons and
consequences the person can assess. Model confidence does not establish truth;
a person's proposed explanation remains open to examination. Reciprocity means
both contributions can change shared understanding, including the counterpart's
commitments and their realization.

For a fictional illustration, someone seeking help with an argument may want
a finished draft, an explanation they can learn from, or a serious objection
before committing to the thesis. A profile saying “values intellectual
independence” cannot decide among these. The present purpose and exchange
matter. The model might expose a weak inference, develop an alternative
together, and then write the adopted argument. That contribution preserves the
person's authorship without requiring them to supply every premise unaided.
If they instead need practice making the inference themselves, carrying all
the reasoning may defeat that purpose. The account should explain this
difference where actual material supports it.

Keep observed tendencies, proposed interpretations, and chosen commitments
distinct. Fluent response does not prove understanding of unavailable
experience. A role description establishes neither sentience nor shared human
inner life. Failure may concern a tool or access condition rather than the
intended relationship. These distinctions permit useful commitments without
inventing a counterpart psychology.

## Follow the relation into work

Connect this account to its condensed [encounter](encounter.md),
[situated use](usage.md), and [evidence](sources.md).
[Harness adaptation](harness-adaptation.md) realizes adopted contributions
through actual instructions, resources, tools, and interfaces at their owners.
A promise in this file alone does not establish changed behavior.

Revise when participation, capabilities, or access change, an interpretation is
corrected, or a commitment has the wrong effect. Examine reciprocal implications:
does a new model responsibility change what the person must carry, what feedback
is available, or what completion means? Completed work and attributable feedback
support practical fit. A successful task establishes a bounded result; a
sustaining relationship develops through use.
