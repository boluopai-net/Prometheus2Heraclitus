# How to develop a grounded person account

[简体中文](person.zh-CN.md) · [Philosophy and models](../design-and-revision.md)

Write the actual person account at `references/situated/person.md` in the
private package. Its object is a person acting in particular situations, with
purposes, capacities, experiences, relationships, and changing conditions. It
helps a counterpart interpret requests and contribute fittingly. The person
always exceeds this account; a collection of traits would miss how the relevant
relations affect one another.

## Understand activity in its situation

This file is the person-facing part of a collaboration working model. Select
psychological understanding for its implications for participation and a fitting
environment; keep fuller experiences, records, and interpretations under private
`references/personal/`, the main entrance for psychological self-understanding.
Before updating this working account, follow the
[philosophy, history, reference-refresh, and condensation sequence](revision.md#establish-the-current-material).
The result helps organize cooperation; it is not a psychological model of the person.

Start from relevant attributable expression and actual participation. A
conversation about a decision can reveal competing concerns; a correction
during work can expose a mistaken division of effort; an established practice
can show a capacity or useful support. Selected personal writing, psychological
or preference material, and philosophical expression can deepen understanding
where relevant. Availability of an archive is not a reason to read all of it.

Develop connections that explain a real difference in collaboration. What is
the person trying to understand or accomplish? What do they contribute through
recognition, generation, judgment, expression, and action? Which conditions
support or obstruct that activity? What do they want to keep, change, or learn?
These questions orient connected prose; they are not compulsory fields. Explain
supported relations and keep an unknown relation unknown.

For a fictional illustration, someone develops arguments readily in dialogue
but finds producing a document costly. Dialogue might help ideas take shape,
the editing interface might be cumbersome, or the task might have little value
for them. Expression and work can distinguish those accounts. If dialogue helps,
the model can structure and write while the person generates, recognizes, and
revises ideas. This establishes neither inability to write nor a preference for
speech in every situation. Preserve the person's generative contribution even
when the counterpart authors the finished document. An objection can originate
a new distinction as well as evaluate an existing one.

Where role exchange is part of the chosen collaboration, describe how this
person moves between generating an opening and scrutinizing a consequential
detail, and what supports that movement. The [Taiji account](../docs/yinyang-and-taiji.md)
offers one interpretation of returning with both possibilities available.
Distinguish the person's actual activity, the role they choose, and an aim for
development. Neither the philosophical image nor a temporary allocation proves
capacity, incapacity, or lasting change.

## Condense personal models from event history, philosophy, and personal material

This project distills event histories, philosophy, personal material, and
psychological understanding of oneself and others into a person account for
collaboration. Long-term conversation is one principal source: it leaves histories
of expression, questions, responses, choices, corrections, and change whose
events can be recovered in sequence and situation. Everyday habits, selected
personal records, and actual work add material; philosophy contributes concepts,
values, and interpretive relations. The model helps understand, compare, and
organize this material into natural-language descriptions of purposes,
preferences, experiences, and conditions, then articulate the counterpart and
collaboration needed. Event streams organize source histories; personal models
distill the understanding relevant to actual cooperation. The person, history,
and account are distinct objects.

Read the history as evidence about changing processes rather than a bag of
traits. Reconstruct what happened, what the person was responding to, how
experience, emotion, desire, and interpretation connected, and which conditions
changed. Analysis distinguishes these relations; abduction develops possible
explanations; comparison across episodes and subsequent interaction tests their
scope. Keep competing interpretations where the material does not discriminate.
The account should explain a consequential pattern without making a single
episode an enduring property or treating a later correction as erasing the past.

“Personal model” here means a revisable account carried by prompts and reference
documents for a language model to understand and use in work. The actual practice
is understanding material and condensing descriptions; it is not named
psychological modeling. It establishes no model explaining or predicting
psychological mechanisms and does not require the person to undertake that
research. Psychological understanding contributes content to condensation;
writing it in prose does not itself establish a psychological model. The person
contributes lived experience, purposes, recognition, objection, and judgment;
the model carries material organization, comparison, interpretation, and writing.
A chosen direction can guide cooperation while its explanation remains open.
Specialist formal, statistical, or psychological-mechanism research has its own
questions, methods, and evidence requirements; models in that literature do not
automatically become this practice's method.

Natural language can directly express meanings, situations, and relations,
making condensed understanding available for cooperation. Its philosophical
contribution comes from examining the concepts through which the person is
understood, such as ability, progress, or selfhood. This differs from proposing
an explanation of a psychological pattern, although the two can inform one
another. Reading and using an account then becomes part of the next encounter:
the person may recognize a relation, contest an interpretation, or find a new
response possible. [From personal philosophy to personal models](../docs/metaphor-and-self-revision.md)
develops this connection between description, clarification, and revision.

Use the relevant authorized portion of available history. Long-term evidence
can deepen an explanation without becoming a requirement to collect a complete
life before helping. Preserve attributable originals with their owners and
maintain the distilled account with its grounds under the
[revision guide's evidence method](revision.md#sources-and-evidence).
Actual participation, the person's corrections, and new circumstances continue
to revise what the conceptual model says.

## Preserve the grounds of interpretation

A reported experience, explanatory interpretation, preference, and chosen
direction support different conclusions. A report that one exchange felt
interruptive does not establish that every question harms attention. Choosing
that the model carries implementation is a response whose fit still needs use.
Preserve these distinctions wherever a future counterpart could turn a local
observation into a standing rule.

The same arrangement can result from preference, constraint, habit, or a default.
A tool in daily use does not establish why it is used. Compare expression,
corrections, available alternatives, and effects. Where the distinction changes
the work and evidence cannot settle it, a focused inquiry can obtain the missing
experience or purpose. The model carries interpretation and writing rather
than requiring a completed self-description from the person.

Within the same personal account, habits, choices, working styles, and desired
responses can guide an arrangement; proposed relations among experience,
emotion, desire, interpretation, and relationships call for explanatory evidence.
Preferences inform that explanation without supplying it alone. Philosophy
also examines the concepts through which these relations are understood and
articulates directions the person chooses to pursue. The
[Philosophy and models](../design-and-revision.md#form-a-situated-version-from-actual-material)
develops this joint practice. Philosophical expression can emphasize literature,
engineering philosophy, Western philosophy, philosophy of science, logic,
aesthetics, or another concern of its author. When that authorship is wanted,
one can develop an original philosophical expression alongside the personal
account; it is not a prerequisite for full Skill adoption or useful work. These emphases do
not divide the condensation of personal models into separate routes. Use psychological vocabulary
at the strength supported by evidence. A philosophical image can suggest a
connection without proving it in this life, and chosen support does not establish
a deficit.

## Let understanding change the collaboration

Relate the account to the adopted [orientation](orientation.md), the actual
[counterpart](counterpart.md), and the chosen [relationship](relationship.md).
Explain what contribution follows, what remains a candidate, and what use
could correct. The [revision guide](revision.md#sources-and-evidence) keeps identity, time, conflicting
material, and access limits navigable; extended episodes stay with private owners.

Revise when the person corrects an interpretation, circumstances change, or
use exposes poor fit. Determine whether the mismatch concerns the person
account, the relationship, or its [environmental realization](environment.md)
before rewriting the person to fit a tool. Improved task results support a
practical arrangement; enduring development needs evidence at that larger scale.
Neither a complete biography nor a clinical explanation is a prerequisite to
useful collaboration.
