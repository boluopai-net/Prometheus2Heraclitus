# How to articulate a working relationship

[简体中文](relationship.zh-CN.md) · [Philosophy and models](../design-and-revision.md)

Write the actual relationship account at `references/situated/relationship.md`
in the private package. Its object is the joint activity: how this
[person](person.md) and [counterpart](counterpart.md) contribute, correct,
divide work, and change their understanding. Their capabilities and experience
inform this relation without dictating one permanent division of labor.

## Explain how the contributions fit together

Connect the adopted [orientation](orientation.md) to real activities and the
participant accounts. Explain who carries which work, why that arrangement
serves the purpose, what each contribution depends on, and how one changes the
next. The person may recognize a mismatch or express an intuition before its
structure is clear. The model can investigate and articulate it; the resulting
proposal gives the person something new to understand, choose, or correct.
Neither must finish their contribution before the other can begin.

For a fictional example, the model carries routine implementation so the person
can stay with an argument. That division owes the person a fitting result,
material choices made intelligible, and an honest account of unfinished work.
It still depends on personal experience or direction unavailable to the model.
A question about a consequential purpose can support the relation; transferring
a solvable detail question can defeat it. The reason and conditions belong in
the account rather than becoming a universal prohibition on questions.

The same person may later want to learn implementation. Participation then
changes: opportunities to reason or act may be part of the desired result.
Production and practice can support the same broader direction through
different allocations. State the distinction that actual material supports.

## Explain exchange and return where they matter

YinYang develops a particular relation: a person generates an unfinished opening
while the model reconstructs and checks; a crucial detail can reverse those
emphases. The same participant passes through the other's role and returns
with both possibilities retained. Its [dialectical meaning](../docs/yinyang-and-taiji.md#returning-through-the-other-the-dialectical-meaning-of-role-change)
concerns this changed participation, beyond rotating a proposal through review
and revision. If adopted, explain how it fits the actual participants and work.
Both can also generate or examine together; a role is an emphasis, not an
identity or an obligatory turn in a script.

The author's [corrective purpose](../docs/yinyang-and-taiji.md#turning-and-corrective-feedback)
asks whether another position or actual result can change a self-reinforcing
direction. Describe what is returned and how it can affect the shared account
or action. Alternation, a dissenting tone, and a feedback label do not establish
effective correction. Preserve useful generation and independent checks, with
the aim itself open to reasoned revision. This is a chosen cooperative relation,
not evidence of a measured psychological control system.

## Let correction change the shared understanding

Explain how objections, corrections, and uncertainty enter the work. A person's
experience is theirs to contribute, while an explanation offered with it remains
open to investigation. Model confidence is not proof. Both contributions can
change the task understanding, selected direction, and chosen arrangement.
Preserve compatible earlier purposes when a later correction changes only one
part; recency alone does not establish a wholly new relation.

Develop what continuation and completion mean here. A conversational correction
can steer ongoing authorized work; an actual pause or withdrawn authorization
changes its boundary. Express the relation needed for this collaboration while
leaving permissions, consent, and publication at their existing owners. A role
description does not create new authority.

## Follow the relationship into its realization

The [environment account](environment.md) explains how the contribution reaches
sources, tools, work, and observable results, including directions for changing
those arrangements. Keep the relational reason here and link to
its working realization rather than reproducing every workflow or configuration.

Use the [evidence material](revision.md#sources-and-evidence) to distinguish a chosen commitment from
an observed effect. When cooperation fails, examine understanding, allocation,
and environmental conditions before rewriting a participant. A completed task
supports a bounded claim; a sustaining relationship develops through repeated
use, disagreement, and correction. Revise the account at the scale justified by
that evidence.
