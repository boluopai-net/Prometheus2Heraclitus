# How to describe environmental realization

[简体中文](environment.zh-CN.md) · [Formation method](../design-and-revision.md)

Write the actual environment account at `references/situated/environment.md`
in the private package. Explain how this collaboration takes working form
through material, social, informational, and computational arrangements. Connect
the adopted [orientation](orientation.md), [person](person.md),
[counterpart](counterpart.md), and [relationship](relationship.md) to actual
possibilities and consequences. A configuration inventory says what exists;
this account explains what it enables, for whom, under which conditions.

## Give the relation a reachable working form

Start with activities the participants undertake and the points where a
contribution depends on a resource, channel, or action route. A person may
express an unfinished idea in speech; the counterpart receives a transcript,
recovers a work, inspects sources, and writes or implements a result. Explain
the passage among these contributions. The environment includes the person's
attention, places, other people, and social evaluation where they affect the
activity, as well as the computer through which contributions are realized.

Ground the description in current evidence. Identify maintained owners and
usable locators. A human-visible file is not automatically model-readable; a
named service is not automatically callable. An advisory contribution needs a
route to its execution owner; an executable action needs observable readback.
Capability, authentication, and authority remain with their real owners.

Describe the routes ordinary work actually depends on. In learning, where is
the selected text maintained, how does an explanation reach the person, and
how do both return to the disputed passage? In implementation, how is the
project recovered, how does the chosen behavior acquire an executable form,
and where can the result be inspected? Include known gaps and conditions.
Specialist Skills own their methods; link useful entrances instead of copying
their complete procedures here.

## Locate the passage that needs support

Understanding, retrieval, beginning, responding, stopping, and switching are
different achievements. An explanation can clarify a relation while leaving
the next action inaccessible. The [formation method's continuation example](../design-and-revision.md#form-a-situated-version-from-actual-material)
distinguishes competing explanations for a saved note that does not help resume
an argument. Describe which note and draft matter, their connection, each
participant's contribution, and where continuation fails. Keep a proposed route
recognizable as a proposal until its availability and effects are established.

Assess the cost of the arrangement as used. A tool may save operations while
adding setup, monitoring, handoff repair, or repeated context reconstruction.
A record preserves material; retrieval makes it available; action changes the
work. Relief concerns experience, while learning concerns what the person can
subsequently understand or do. The [participation and support essay](../docs/participation-and-support.md)
develops these differences. A smaller arrangement can support the activity
better than a larger inventory.

## Let realization correct the account

[Harness adaptation](../harness-adaptation.md) carries operational changes
through their actual owners and checks the intended effect. Update this
account from the observed result, keeping planned and realized arrangements
distinct. An unavailable source may call for a route repair; persistent poor
fit may challenge the person account, division of work, or orientation.

Keep private locators with their private owners and trace useful claims through
the [evidence material](sources.md). An accessible file establishes retrieval;
a real activity continuing through it establishes more. The account condenses
selected relations within an environment that remains richer and changeable.
