# How to revise

<a id="revision"></a>
<a id="revising-a-situated-yinyang-and-its-environment"></a>

[简体中文](revision.zh-CN.md) · [Philosophy and models](../design-and-revision.md) · [Environment](environment.md)

The author uses this approach to keep philosophy, personal understanding, and
actual work connected as they change. Revision can begin with a developed idea,
changed circumstances, an objection, or an arrangement that no longer fits.
Find what has changed, work through its consequences, and let the result correct
the understanding that guided the change.

Adapt the approach to your purpose, materials, and environment. You may be
writing a different philosophy, revising one personal interpretation, or changing
a tool. The connections below help identify what needs attention; their order
and extent follow the work. Updating the six personal working files uses the
material-refresh sequence below, with retrieval scoped to the affected question.
The method itself can also be revised when experience
shows a better way to proceed.

## Write your own actual revision method

Write the method fitted to your practice at `references/situated/revision.md`
in the private package. This guide explains how to write it; the author's
approach below offers adaptable material.

Explain what prompts revision, how relevant philosophy and personal material
are obtained, how local scope and connected changes are judged, and how
environmental changes reach a checked result. Name usable materials and routes,
and the judgments needing your participation. A new philosophy, no website,
or different working conditions can change the approach. Let the actual method
express these conditions instead of reproducing another author's environment.

## Scope and connected changes

Identify the result sought and the part that needs development. Distinguish
the **revision focus**, the **material used to judge it**, and **other parts
that need to change**. Reading philosophy to assess an interface does not
itself select the philosophy for rewriting.

| Current focus | Corresponding work |
| --- | --- |
| Refresh personal YinYang and its environment | Reconsider personal material in light of philosophy and current sources, assess the connected accounts, then inspect the relevant Skills and arrangements. |
| Revise a philosophical claim or personal interpretation | Develop the passage and follow changes in meaning into the accounts, methods, or representations that depend on it. |
| Repair a Skill, page, tool, or arrangement | Diagnose and fix that object. Return to a premise or personal interpretation if the result exposes a mistake there. |

Follow substantive changes through their actual dependencies. A wording or
presentation correction may finish at its source and reading surface; a changed
division of work may also require changing the instructions that implement it.
Preserve an explicit stopping boundary and explain any necessary consequence
that remains unfinished.

## Establish the current material

The private `references/personal/` material and the six files under
`references/situated/` serve different purposes. Personal material retains
psychologically relevant experiences, source records, and revisable interpretations.
It is the main entrance when the question concerns understanding one's own
psychology. The six files condense a **personal working model** of person–model
collaboration: enough direction, personal context, counterpart understanding,
relationship, realization, and revision method to make the environment fit the
actual person. They do not together constitute a psychological model of that person.

Whenever updating these six working files, refresh their grounds before
condensing them again:

1. **Read the person's philosophy.** Start with the adopted philosophical
   expression and relevant developed arguments. Use the current working files
   to locate what depends on them, without treating an old condensation as a
   substitute for that philosophy or its supporting material.
2. **Find relevant new references.** Check current conversation and available
   Computer History, dialogue histories, work/version history, or other source
   records for material bearing on the affected understanding. Follow useful
   summaries to originals when a consequential claim needs fuller context.
   Histories have different coverage; observing an action does not reveal its
   psychological cause. Use actual authorized sources and state material gaps.
3. **Refresh the personal references.** Add useful new evidence with its source
   and context, linking an existing original rather than copying it unnecessarily.
   Compare it and the philosophy with the maintained interpretations in
   `personal`; correct, qualify, reorganize, or retire inaccurate and outdated
   parts. Preserve attributable originals and meaningful uncertainty. A new
   reference is not merely another appended trait.
4. **Condense the refreshed material with the philosophy.** Revise the affected
   six-file content and check its connections with the other working files.
   Select what changes collaboration or environmental design, retaining a route
   back to the richer personal material. The account should explain what a
   counterpart needs to understand and do, not attempt a complete psychological
   portrait. Carry necessary changes into the environment and check their use.

This sequence is a dependency of working-model revision, not a demand to collect
new personal facts on every occasion or rewrite all six files each time. A
bounded correction may use the current exchange as its new source; inspect
relevant histories rather than surveying a whole life. If no new relevant
material is found, retain that result honestly. The person contributes experience
and judgments unavailable in the inspected sources, while the model carries
retrieval, reference maintenance, and condensation. Later feedback can return
the work to any earlier interpretation.

## Write for the actual use

State relevant objects, relations, evidence, conditions, responsibilities, and
consequences in clear language. Distinguish a reported experience, an
interpretation, a chosen commitment, and an operational requirement where they
would lead to different judgments or actions. Personal information supplies
contextual evidence; its presence in an account does not make it a command.

Use direct expression. A philosophical distinction or literary image can clarify
a relation; explain its meaning where that relation should guide work. Preserve
original expression as attributable material while making the maintained
interpretation and practical consequences explicit. Keep extended philosophical
arguments at their own source and link them where useful.

## Sources and evidence

Choose personal writing, conversation, memory, observed work, and philosophical
material because they bear on the question. Retain source identity, time,
relevant passages, and enough context to assess consequential claims. Where a
summary mediates access to an original, make that relation visible: its selection
and omissions can shape an interpretation.

Different materials support different claims. A person's report grounds what
they expressed or experienced in context; an explanation offered with it still
needs assessment. Observed work shows what happened under inspected conditions.
A chosen commitment states a direction; philosophy contributes concepts and
reasons. In a fictional reading example, reporting greater ease supports
experienced ease, and reaching the right passage supports access. Lasting
learning and the cause of improvement need further evidence.

Keep temporal scope and uncertainty intelligible. Later speech may correct an
interpretation, describe a new situation, or introduce unresolved conflict.
Explain important inferences: what supports them, why the connection matters,
and what limits their use. Several summaries of one source add no independent
support.

Keep extended episodes and unique originals with their existing owners; give
derived accounts usable locators and grounds. In the author's package, the
private source entrance is `references/personal/sources.md`; the
[package guide](../docs/skill-package.md) explains its placement. Preserve useful
originals while retiring redundant summaries. Access for revision does not
authorize unrelated personal collection or public disclosure of private
quotations, portraits, or unpublished work.

## Condensation and reopening

Condensation makes relevant understanding usable in the next collaboration.
Retain relations that change interpretation, response, or revision: what an
expression answers, its applicability conditions, who reported experience and
who proposed an explanation, and a material counterexample or alternative.
Write these as coherent prose with sources where needed, without requiring
the same fields for every item. Carry original words into a working account
when their wording bears meaning; complete originals remain with their source.

Develop accounts from reported experience, observable interaction, and actual
results while keeping model interpretations recognizable. Repeated citation of
a summary adds no independent evidence. Distinguish a narrated pattern, a
chosen collaborative direction, and a verified causal explanation. Do not invent
internal psychological states to complete a portrait or apply simulated-persona
state scores to actual people. These distinctions can coexist in one
natural-language model without splitting it into disconnected archives.

When new material arrives, determine whether it corrects a reading, reflects
changed conditions, or introduces an unresolved difference. Qualify, withdraw,
or reorganize the relevant account rather than only appending traits; retain
the original event and the time its interpretation formed. Check rewritten
understanding against currently relevant sources so repeated summarization does
not turn inference into fact. Recurring words can provide retrieval entrances;
frequency alone does not determine their relational meaning. Newer expression
does not override all older material merely by being later.

At useful maintenance points, examine whether the next counterpart can still
recover applicability, actual purpose, material open questions, and a path to
the grounds. Restore omissions that change those judgments; details irrelevant
to current use can remain at their source. A model response and its acceptance,
rejection, or modification may themselves become later material, but do not
automatically prove the model's prior interpretation. The argument is developed
in [Condensing revisable relations](../docs/metaphor-and-self-revision.md#condensing-revisable-relations).

## Let philosophy revise personal support material

Bring developed philosophy into contact with existing personal material.
Which interpretations does it clarify? Which distinctions were missing?
Which summaries now mislead? Rewrite derived material so experience,
interpretation, chosen direction, and uncertainty remain intelligible.
Philosophy helps formulate understanding; attributable expression and actual
use ground its personal application.

When a public argument is already maintained elsewhere, link it and retain
the private meaning and evidence that this account needs. This keeps the support
useful as the philosophy changes.

## Let philosophy and personal material revise working accounts

Read the philosophy alongside the personal references refreshed through the
[sequence above](#establish-the-current-material). The six files are working
models serving a fitting personal environment, with psychological understanding
among their grounds. In the author's
YinYang, orientation articulates adopted values and direction; person grounds
understanding of the actual person; counterpart relates available capacity to
wanted contribution; relationship explains joint activity; environment explains
its realization. The [writing guides](../design-and-revision.md#form-a-situated-version-from-actual-material)
develop these different responsibilities.

Preserve supported substance and rewrite the relations that changed. Distinguish
preference from explanatory hypothesis, wanted role from demonstrated capability,
and selected arrangement from observed effect. Read neighboring accounts together
so the revision remains coherent. Either participant can generate, object,
reason, and revise.

Reconsider the actual revision method too. If it repeatedly creates unnecessary work, obscures
a judgment, or fails to carry a change into use, revise the relevant guidance
and check whether the new approach helps. Another person's practice can call
for different objects, organization, or methods.

## Reread personal material and assess the working model

For understanding one's psychological experience, begin primarily with relevant
`personal` material and its sources: episodes, expressions, relationships,
reported experience, and tentative interpretations. The model helps locate
and organize these materials; the person can recognize fit or mismatch, offer
exceptions, or reconsider a reading. Their preservation supplies material for
understanding, not a guarantee that an interpretation is correct.

Read the six working files for a connected but different question: does this
understanding lead to a suitable counterpart, division of work, and personal
environment? Even `situated/person.md` selects what ordinary collaboration
needs. It does not replace the richer personal material. A useful workflow
change can establish better fit without validating a psychological explanation.

One path brings authored philosophy to the personal reference material and its
realization: how do these explanations understand self and others, what life
does the arrangement support, and which values and concepts need revision too?
Another begins with recurring experiences, comparing what happened, how the
person understood the other, what response they expected, and how their own
actions participated in the resulting relationship. Examine actual demands,
conditions, and dependencies alongside the proposed relational explanation.

Both paths retain revisable interpretations. Personal rereading is limited to
organizing experience, articulating questions, and increasing self-understanding.
Distinguish what the person said, what the model inferred, and what actually
changed in the environment. Explicit purposes and preferences can inform tool
and workflow changes. These records are not psychological measurements,
clinical conclusions, or grounds for self-directed intervention. For mental
health concerns, use them to communicate with qualified professionals rather
than undertake self-help psychological analysis, assessment, or treatment.
[Rereading one's patterns](../docs/embodied-experimental-philosophy.md#rereading-ones-patterns-philosophical-reflection-and-loosening-fantasy)
develops the philosophical resources; the
[use notice](https://github.com/boluopai-net/Prometheus2Heraclitus/blob/main/README.md#intended-use-and-mental-health)
states the evaluation limits. Document revisions and environmental changes
establish their respective results, not changes in psychological states or mechanisms.

## Let the whole YinYang revise Skills and the environment

Use the connected philosophy and personal understanding to assess the Skills
and arrangements relevant to the intended result. Do their purposes, instructions,
examples, resources, and actual behavior support the contribution wanted?
Include prompts, tools, and interfaces where their dependencies affect it.
Specialist Skills retain their own methods and task responsibilities.

Repair actual mismatches: contradictory instructions, inaccessible context,
an unsuitable interface, or machinery whose contribution has disappeared.
Remove displaced guidance and keep what still works. The
[environment writing guide](environment.md) develops realization;
use your environment's actual authoring, implementation, and publication routes.

## Make webpage revisions visible before publication

The author's practice includes a public website. When revising its diagrams,
the model shows the changed diagrams in the conversation. For a new or
substantially changed page, it shows a readable rendering of the actual
unpublished version and explains consequential changes before publishing.
A further material edit calls for an updated preview of that version.

This lets the person encounter the work while it can still change. Already
authorized publication continues, incorporating feedback when it arrives;
personal authentication, consent, and explicit stopping boundaries keep their
scope. A practice without a website can use the relevant equivalent: read a
draft, try an interface, or inspect a work before sharing it.

## Follow the result back into understanding

Compare a summary with its evidence, a generated copy with its source, and a
completion claim with the result reached through the actual entrypoint.
An unavailable account calls for delivery repair; an account faithfully followed
but producing an unsuitable contribution calls for examining its meaning or
the selected relationship.

Carry authorized changes through the affected sources and consumers, then read
back what is available to the intended reader or working model. Where copies
depend on an authored source, update them through their actual route. Keep
private material within its authorized audience.

Compare the intended result with the observable outcome and explain required
differences still open. Return to personal material, working accounts, or
philosophy when evidence challenges them. Useful output, collaborative fit,
and personal development have different grounds; let the claim match what
the revision and subsequent use actually establish.
