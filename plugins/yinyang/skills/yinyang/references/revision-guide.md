# Revision

<a id="revising-a-situated-yinyang-and-its-environment"></a>

[简体中文](revision-guide.zh-CN.md) · [Philosophy and models](design-and-revision.md) · [Environment writing guide](formation/environment.md)

This is the sixth working file: guidance for revision. Keep philosophy,
personal understanding, and actual work connected as they change.
The maintained set has six files: the five situated
accounts—orientation, person, counterpart, relationship, and environment—and
this revision guide. The accounts articulate the participants, direction, and
conditions; the guide explains their revision and is itself revised through
use. Maintain the six together according to these different duties. The public
guide contains reusable method. Actual accounts and supporting personal
material remain with their private owners.

A refresh can begin with developed philosophy, changed life circumstances,
an objection during work, or a result that fails to fit. Recover the intended
result and current evidence, then carry the affected connections through their
owners. When the request is a whole-environment refresh, use the three connected
passes below. A local repair can enter at the relevant point and follow the
dependencies it reveals. Neither case requires a new lifecycle, scheduler, or
complete rewrite whenever one sentence changes.

## Scope and connected changes

First identify the result being sought and the part that presently fails or
needs development. Distinguish that **revision focus** from the **material used
to judge it** and from **other parts that actually need to change**. Reading a
philosophy or personal account as evidence does not by itself select it for
rewriting. Work from the complete current six-file understanding already
available under the entrypoint's reading contract; the scope of an edit follows
the request and the dependencies found in the work.

| Current focus | Corresponding work |
| --- | --- |
| Refresh the whole personal YinYang and environment | Reconcile personal support with philosophy and current sources, assess all six files together, then inspect the requested Skills and environment. Change what no longer fits. |
| Revise a philosophical claim or personal interpretation | Develop that passage and check which accounts, methods, or representations actually depend on its meaning. Carry those changes together while retaining unaffected material. |
| Repair a particular Skill, page, tool, or arrangement | Use the relevant understanding to diagnose and fix that object. Return a mistaken premise or personal interpretation to its owner if the result exposes one. |

Follow a substantive changed meaning through its actual dependents. A wording,
link, or presentation correction can instead finish at its affected source and
reader. Neither a whole-environment refresh nor a local repair requires changes
everywhere: a change earns its scope through what the intended result needs.
Conversely, stopping at the first edited file is insufficient when a required
dependency still presents the old behavior. Retain any explicit user stopping
boundary and carry the compatible authorized work to its stated result.

## Establish the current material

Read the current philosophy and all six files, then select the personal support
needed for the question. Locate maintained originals and distinguish them from
summaries, generated copies, and publication snapshots. A source locator makes
material discoverable; reading its current content establishes what is actually
available. Keep consequential access gaps visible.

Current purposes, study or research directions, relationships, and working
conditions may have changed since a retained account was written. Compare
their relevant sources and actual exchange rather than preserving a summary
because it already exists or inventing an update because it seems plausible.
The model carries retrieval, comparison, and grounded writing; the person
contributes experience and consequential judgments unavailable in the material.
Reuse what they have already supplied.

## Write for the actual use

Write the five accounts under `references/situated/`, maintain the common
`references/revision-guide.md`, and keep derived support under `references/personal/`
as explicit model-facing context and instructions. State the relevant objects,
relations, evidence, conditions, responsibilities, and consequences in clear
natural language. Distinguish a reported experience, an interpretation, a chosen
commitment, and a requirement where they would lead to different judgments or
actions. Form means making these relationships usable for reasoning and work;
it requires neither a fixed template nor symbolic notation. Personal information
remains contextual evidence rather than becoming a command merely because a
model reads it.

Use direct expression first. Bring in a philosophical distinction or literary
image when it clarifies a consequential relation that the direct account does
not yet make intelligible. State its relevant meaning and scope; the counterpart
should not have to infer practical requirements from a character or metaphor.
Preserve original expression as attributed source material, while writing the
maintained interpretation and operational consequences explicitly. Keep developed
philosophical arguments at their reader-facing owners and refer to them as needed.

## Sources and evidence

Evidence grounds the six files; its private index is supporting material with
its own duty. Maintain that entrance at `references/personal/sources.md`,
locating the grounds of the accounts and relevant revision judgments with
enough explanation to know why to look. Public distribution supplies this
method, not a personal source index. Retrieve supporting evidence when the
question needs it. Keep useful topical material in the private tree or with
its existing owners.

Select attributable expression, personal writing, conversations, memory,
observed work, and philosophy because they bear on current understanding.
Retain source identity, time, the relevant passage or message, and enough
context to assess consequential claims. A trace records selected activity;
an interpretation explains its meaning. Where a maintained summary stands
between an original and an account, make that relation clear: its selection
and omissions can affect a disputed reading.

A person's report supports what they expressed or experienced in context,
without establishing every explanation offered with it. Observed work shows
what happened under inspected conditions while motive may remain unknown.
A chosen commitment states how work should proceed; behavior shows its
realization. Philosophy supplies concepts, reasons, possible interpretations,
and adopted direction without proving a personal mechanism. For a fictional
example, reporting that a reading arrangement feels easier supports experienced
ease; a record of reaching the passage supports access. Neither establishes
lasting learning or which interface change caused improvement. Later
participation on a new case can supply evidence at that larger scale. Several
accounts repeating one summary add no independent support.

Date changing conditions at the scale that matters: a tool available last
month, a temporary workload, and a continuing commitment have different scopes.
Later speech may correct an interpretation, describe a new situation, or add
unresolved conflict; recency alone does not decide which. Retain conflicting
evidence rather than smoothing it into a consistent portrait. Make consequential
inferences traceable in prose: what suggests the interpretation, why, and which
uncertainty limits its use. Reachability does not establish correspondence;
an inaccessible source leaves a coverage limit.

Keep extended episodes, precise excerpts, and unique originals at their actual
owners. Maintaining the accounts together does not require copying raw sources.
Derived private notes state source, scope, interpretation, and relevant
consequence under the [writing method](revision-guide.md#write-for-the-actual-use).
Their filenames follow actual material; no empty companion is needed for each
essay. Public philosophical references stay near their arguments. Preserve
useful originals and recoverability while retiring redundant active material.
Source maintenance authorizes neither unrelated biographical collection nor
publication of private paths, quotations, portraits, or unreleased works.
Return only authorized reusable insights to the public package.

## Let philosophy revise personal support material

First bring the developed philosophy into contact with the existing personal
material. Ask which interpretations it clarifies, which distinctions were
missing, and which summaries have become misleading. Rewrite the derived
accounts so relevant experience, interpretation, chosen direction, and
uncertainty remain intelligible. Philosophy can help formulate an account;
attributable expression and actual use ground its personal application.

Use the source distinctions above when grounds change, access fails, or a
correction changes interpretation. Retire stale summaries, duplicated
explanations, and public essays copied into private support when their current
common owner supplies them. Retain genuinely private meaning and useful
evidence rather than a second library of public theory.

## Let philosophy and personal material revise the six files

Read philosophy together with the refreshed personal evidence to revise the
five accounts. Read each affected account's complete
[formation guide](design-and-revision.md#form-a-situated-version-from-actual-material)
before writing. Orientation states adopted values and direction; person grounds
understanding of the actual person; counterpart relates available capacity to
wanted contribution; relationship explains joint activity; environment explains
how it becomes possible under actual conditions. These connected duties keep
the five from becoming repeated versions of one portrait.

Distill the relations needed for use, with enough reasons and evidence to make
them revisable. Distinguish a preference from an explanatory hypothesis, a
wanted role from demonstrated capability, and a chosen arrangement from its
observed effect. Both participants can generate, reason, object, and revise.
Changing a division of work does not establish incapacity; improving a text
does not establish personal development. Preserve supported substance, rewrite
the relation that changed, and read the accounts together for coherence. Leave
extended evidence at its supporting owner.

Then revise this sixth file where actual use has exposed a missing connection,
an obsolete direction, or a better way to carry revision through the environment.
Check that its method still serves the philosophy and grounded accounts, and
that those accounts can actually be maintained through it. A full refresh
therefore assesses all six files; each needs a change only where its own meaning
or contribution changes.

## Let the whole YinYang revise Skills and the environment

Use the whole YinYang, including the six maintained files and especially the
five grounded accounts, to assess all Skills
within the requested environment scope. Examine whether each relevant Skill's
purpose, instructions, examples, resources, and actual behavior support the
intended contribution. Specialist Skills retain their methods and authority;
the personal accounts inform their use without being copied into every package.
Assess the connected prompts, tools, and arrangements where their dependencies
affect the result.

Change actual mismatches: an obsolete division of responsibility, contradictory
instructions, inaccessible context, an unhelpful interface, or machinery that
no longer serves a current consumer. Remove displaced guidance instead of
adding another layer around it. An assessment can find that a Skill already
fits and needs no edit. Coverage of the requested scope matters more than
producing a cosmetic diff in every file. The [environment writing guide](formation/environment.md) develops realization;
use the environment's actual authoring,
implementation, and publication methods for realization.

## Make webpage revisions visible before publication

For webpage changes, show changed diagrams directly in the conversation. For
a new page or substantial page revision, show a readable rendered preview of
the actual unpublished version and explain its consequential changes before
publishing it. Identify which version the preview represents. If subsequent
edits materially change it, show the updated preview before publishing that
version. A source excerpt, proposed layout, or previously published page does
not establish what the current unpublished version looks like.

This is presentation sequencing, not an approval or waiting gate. Continue
already-authorized publication and verification without asking for permission
or waiting for a reply. Incorporate feedback if it arrives during the work.
Actual authorization, personal authentication, consent, and explicit stopping
boundaries keep their existing scope.

## Follow the result back into understanding

Check sources and results independently where correspondence matters: compare
a summary with its evidence, a generated projection with its owner, and a
completion claim with the result reached through the real entrypoint. Repeating
the same interpretation across several files supplies little new evidence.
An unavailable account calls for delivery repair; a faithfully followed account
that produces an unsuitable contribution calls for examining its meaning or
the chosen relationship.

Carry authorized changes through their actual source owners, affected consumers,
and publication routes. Regenerate dependent copies, retire displaced active
material, and read back the version available to the intended consumer. Keep
private records, quotations, portraits, and unpublished work out of public
projections. The [package guide](docs/skill-package.md) owns common/private
projection; this guide creates no additional synchronization mechanism.

Compare the intended result with the observable outcome and explain any required
difference still open. Let use return to personal material, any of the six files,
or philosophy when evidence challenges them. A completed work can finish while
the arrangement remains revisable; useful output, collaborative fit, and the
person's development retain their distinct evidence.
