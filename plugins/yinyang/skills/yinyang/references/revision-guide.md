# Revising a situated YinYang and its environment

[简体中文](revision-guide.zh-CN.md) · [Formation and revision](design-and-revision.md) · [Harness adaptation](harness-adaptation.md)

Use this guide to keep philosophy, personal understanding, and actual work
connected as they change. It is the sixth reference alongside the five situated
accounts: orientation, person, counterpart, relationship, and environment. It
explains how to revise their connected arrangement; it adds no sixth portrait.
The public guide contains reusable method. Actual accounts and supporting
personal material remain with their private owners.

A refresh can begin with developed philosophy, changed life circumstances,
an objection during work, or a result that fails to fit. Recover the intended
result and current evidence, then carry the affected connections through their
owners. When the request is a whole-environment refresh, use the three connected
passes below. A local repair can enter at the relevant point and follow the
dependencies it reveals. Neither case requires a new lifecycle, scheduler, or
complete rewrite whenever one sentence changes.

## Establish the current material

Read the current philosophy and the five accounts, then select the personal
support needed for the question. Locate maintained originals and distinguish
them from summaries, generated copies, and publication snapshots. A source
locator makes material discoverable; reading its current content establishes
what is actually available. Keep consequential access gaps visible.

Current purposes, study or research directions, relationships, and working
conditions may have changed since a retained account was written. Compare
their relevant sources and actual exchange rather than preserving a summary
because it already exists or inventing an update because it seems plausible.
The model carries retrieval, comparison, and grounded writing; the person
contributes experience and consequential judgments unavailable in the material.
Reuse what they have already supplied.

## Let philosophy revise personal support material

First bring the developed philosophy into contact with the existing personal
material. Ask which interpretations it clarifies, which distinctions were
missing, and which summaries have become misleading. Rewrite the derived
accounts so relevant experience, interpretation, chosen direction, and
uncertainty remain intelligible. Philosophy can help formulate an account;
attributable expression and actual use ground its personal application.

Preserve original evidence at its owner with its source and temporal context.
A later interpretation can change what earlier speech means in the maintained
account while its original wording remains recoverable. Retire stale summaries,
duplicated explanations, and public essays copied into private support when
their current common owner supplies them. Retain genuinely private meaning and
useful evidence rather than a second library of public theory. Select material
by its contribution to understanding, without collecting unrelated records.
The [private maintenance method](design-and-revision.md#maintain-the-private-material-through-use)
develops this work.

## Let philosophy and personal material revise the five accounts

Read philosophy together with the refreshed personal evidence to revise the
five accounts. Read each affected account's complete
[formation guide](design-and-revision.md#form-a-situated-version-from-actual-material)
before writing. Orientation states adopted values and direction; person grounds
understanding of the actual person; counterpart relates available capacity to
wanted contribution; relationship explains joint activity; environment explains
how it becomes possible under actual conditions. These connected duties keep
the five from becoming repeated versions of one portrait.

Distill the relations needed for use, with enough reasons and evidence to make
them revisable. Distinguish a preference from an explanatory hypothesis, a
wanted role from demonstrated capability, and a chosen arrangement from its
observed effect. Both participants can generate, reason, object, and revise.
Changing a division of work does not establish incapacity; improving a text
does not establish personal development. Preserve supported substance, rewrite
the relation that changed, and read the five together for coherence. Leave
extended evidence at its supporting owner.

## Let the whole YinYang revise Skills and the environment

Use the whole YinYang, especially the five accounts, to assess all Skills
within the requested environment scope. Examine whether each relevant Skill's
purpose, instructions, examples, resources, and actual behavior support the
intended contribution. Specialist Skills retain their methods and authority;
the personal accounts inform their use without being copied into every package.
Assess the connected prompts, tools, and arrangements where their dependencies
affect the result.

Change actual mismatches: an obsolete division of responsibility, contradictory
instructions, inaccessible context, an unhelpful interface, or machinery that
no longer serves a current consumer. Remove displaced guidance instead of
adding another layer around it. An assessment can find that a Skill already
fits and needs no edit. Coverage of the requested scope matters more than
producing a cosmetic diff in every file. [Harness adaptation](harness-adaptation.md)
owns the detailed reasoning; use the environment's actual authoring,
implementation, and publication methods for realization.

## Make webpage revisions visible before publication

For webpage changes, show changed diagrams directly in the conversation. For
a new page or substantial page revision, show a readable rendered preview of
the actual unpublished version and explain its consequential changes before
publishing it. Identify which version the preview represents. If subsequent
edits materially change it, show the updated preview before publishing that
version. A source excerpt, proposed layout, or previously published page does
not establish what the current unpublished version looks like.

This is presentation sequencing, not an approval or waiting gate. Continue
already-authorized publication and verification without asking for permission
or waiting for a reply. Incorporate feedback if it arrives during the work.
Actual authorization, personal authentication, consent, and explicit stopping
boundaries keep their existing scope.

## Follow the result back into understanding

Check sources and results independently where correspondence matters: compare
a summary with its evidence, a generated projection with its owner, and a
completion claim with the result reached through the real entrypoint. Repeating
the same interpretation across several files supplies little new evidence.
An unavailable account calls for delivery repair; a faithfully followed account
that produces an unsuitable contribution calls for examining its meaning or
the chosen relationship.

Carry authorized changes through their actual source owners, affected consumers,
and publication routes. Regenerate dependent copies, retire displaced active
material, and read back the version available to the intended consumer. Keep
private records, quotations, portraits, and unpublished work out of public
projections. The [package guide](docs/skill-package.md) owns common/private
projection; this guide creates no additional synchronization mechanism.

Compare the intended result with the observable outcome and explain any required
difference still open. Let use return to personal material, the five accounts,
or philosophy when evidence challenges them. A completed work can finish while
the arrangement remains revisable; useful output, collaborative fit, and the
person's development retain their distinct evidence.
