# The situated encounter

This public copy explains how to write the encounter account in a situated
YinYang package. That account gives a returning counterpart a usable understanding
of this collaboration: what the person is trying to do, how the participants
contribute, what their work meets in the world, and what remains open to correction.
The [entrypoint](../SKILL.md) owns initial reading for full adoption. Reading the
philosophy or borrowing a useful method does not require creating these five
accounts or adopting the author's developmental purpose.

## Make the collaboration intelligible as a whole

An encounter account selects what matters across the other accounts. Its value
is the connection: why a person's purpose calls for this counterpart's
contribution, how the environment enables that contribution, and what a
successful result would mean here. A list of preferences cannot supply that
connection. Nor can literary description that leaves the counterpart unable to
decide how to work.

Begin with a real activity or continuing purpose for which shared understanding
would help. Relevant material may include the person's account of a difficulty,
corrections during work, a chosen way of participating, and results that exposed
a mistaken assumption. Read the developed [user model](models.md),
[relationship](relationship.md), and [situated use](usage.md) for the connections
they establish; use [sources](sources.md) to distinguish their grounds. Develop
a missing connection through [design and revision](design-and-revision.md)
without disguising uncertainty as a settled portrait.

The prose should let a counterpart understand the stakes without reconstructing
the whole history. Explain what the person seeks and why the arrangement helps;
what they want to contribute themselves; what the model is expected and actually
able to carry; and where external conditions or unavailable experience matters.
Describe development when it is this person's purpose. Useful collaboration
can also serve a bounded task, an informed choice, or room to explore.

## Condense without erasing differences

Actual expression, interpretation, and chosen commitment can lead to different
actions. A person may report that repeated technical questions interrupt their
thinking. The model may interpret this as a need for continuity. The participants
may choose to have the model investigate implementation details while making
consequential choices intelligible. These are connected contributions with
different grounds; none establishes that the person dislikes every question
or cannot judge technical work.

For a deliberately fictional illustration, “ask fewer questions” during a
configuration task could concern avoidable detail questions. An account might
explain that the person wants to remain with the larger problem while the model
carries investigation and implementation. It would leave room for a question
about unavailable personal purpose. If the actual reason was simply that a
suitable tool already answered the question, a lasting preference account would
overstate the evidence. The illustration shows a distinction to examine, not a
person to copy into the situated files.

Literary or philosophical language can gather these relations when the
participants find it useful. Explain the consequences giving an image its
situated meaning. Sun and Moon do not tell a new adopter who they are; a coherent
philosophy does not establish personal facts. Plain prose can carry practical
understanding too.

## Keep a usable entrance current

Retain the detail that changes ordinary understanding or action here. Extended
episodes and precise attribution stay with their existing private owners; link
to deeper accounts rather than reproducing them. Concision works because those
connections remain available, not because their reasons have been discarded.

Revise when purposes, participation, capabilities, or conditions materially
change, or work contradicts the picture. Return a mistaken inference to its
owning account; repair environmental mismatch through
[harness adaptation](harness-adaptation.md). Read the revised whole for what a
counterpart would understand and do differently. Reading shows availability;
collaboration supplies evidence of fit. Common updates preserve the situated
account's supported substance rather than replacing it with this guidance.
