# Form working models with Hindsight

[简体中文](hindsight.zh-CN.md) · [Formation and revision](design-and-revision.md)

Hindsight is YinYang's default model-formation engine. It retains selected
attributed sources, consolidates revisable observations, and generates maintained
Mental Models or Knowledge Pages from explicit questions. Codex selects and
prepares authorized material, configures these operations, checks their results,
repairs omissions, and carries adopted implications into the environment.
It does not first write a complete Personal portrait and manually compress it
into six files. There is no fixed output count.

## Bind the actual private consumer

First resolve the installed Hindsight version, service URL, authentication,
private bank, inference and embedding providers, and phase-specific model settings
from the host's actual configuration. Reuse its credential owner; do not put
credentials in this package. Check service health, bank/config retrieval, and
supported SDK/API fields before submitting personal material. Installing this
Skill does not provision Hindsight or authorize sending data to a new provider.
Explain the selected source scope, inference route and resulting reading surface.
Use a disposable, nonpersonal record to test a newly configured route, then
exercise the authorized real source-to-model path. Unavailable infrastructure
is an unfinished integration, not a reason to claim manual formation is equivalent.

Keep `references/situated/index.md` as the private binding and reading entrance:
service/bank locators, model questions and IDs, generated body/export locators,
source scope, and the models required at ordinary startup. Keep the actual
execution method at `references/situated/revision.md`. Generated bodies may live
under a chosen private view path or be read through the native API. The index
selects the complete required bodies; it is not a substitute for reading them.
Choose the organization from the questions and actual consumer, without assuming
six files or replacing them with another fixed count.

## Prepare sources and processing instructions

Philosophy supplies arguments, distinctions, and chosen commitments; Personal
supplies attributed reports and evidence; environment and work/history records
supply conditions, events and actual results. Preserve author, date, source
locator, context and uncertainty. A chosen philosophy is distinct from a
hypothesis about a person. Original sources retain their evidence and authorship
owners. Derived Personal summaries may migrate into native observations/pages;
retain unique evidence and useful access paths, then retire duplicate maintained
portraits after checking their consumers. No complete parallel Personal summary
is required before native fusion.

In Hindsight's native categories, **World** and **Experience** are parallel raw
fact categories: World includes attributed human reports, preferences, rules,
and external events; Experience records actions or observations actually made
by the assistant. A person's reported experience usually enters as attributed
World, not assistant autobiography. **Observations** are revisable consolidated
facets with source links. **Mental Models** are separately maintained documents
generated by a query. None of these labels certifies truth or gives authority.

Use native bank `retain_mission`, `observations_mission`, and `reflect_mission`
for shared extraction, consolidation, and synthesis purposes. Retain relevant
relations, conditions, alternatives and correction history rather than only
traits. Keep phase guidance coherent: a relation omitted during extraction may
be unavailable to synthesis. Tags, tag groups and `fact_types` scope retrieval;
they do not encode all philosophical relations or an instruction hierarchy.
Native directives are explicit instructions for reflect/refresh; promote only
actually adopted requirements deliberately, never every inferred preference.
They do not automatically become Codex instructions or authorize host changes.

## Write model questions as detailed content specifications

The six formation guides remain content lenses: orientation, person,
counterpart, relationship, environment, and revision. Use the affected complete
guides to write each native `source_query`. A question can specify as much
substance as a writing guide: purpose and consumer, subjects and scope, important
relations, priority among concerns, organization, evidence distinctions,
uncertainties and counterexamples, and how the account should guide work.
A title alone rarely expresses this. One model may connect several lenses;
one lens may need several questions. Preserve their distinct contributions
without requiring one document per lens. The six philosophical dimensions
are likewise questions of understanding, not a storage schema.

For example, a cooperation question can ask how adopted philosophy and relevant
personal reports jointly explain wanted participation, actual model capacities,
role exchange and return, conditions of useful delegation, and environmental
consequences. Ask it to distinguish observed results, reported preferences,
chosen directions and explanatory hypotheses; preserve scope exceptions and
unresolved alternatives; state what a future counterpart should understand and
do. Fit the content to this person's actual question rather than copying the
example into every model.

## Use the native interfaces

The following interfaces were inspected in Hindsight 1.1.0's installed Python
client and API implementation. Verify them against the installed version when
configuring another host. The [upstream project](https://github.com/vectorize-io/hindsight)
owns installation and evolving provider/service instructions.

```python
from hindsight_client import Hindsight

client = Hindsight(base_url="http://localhost:8888")  # actual configured URL
bank = "private-example"
client.retain(
    bank_id=bank,
    content="An attributed, authorized source passage with its context.",
    document_id="source-owner-record-id",
    context="Source identity, date, evidence role and applicable scope",
    tags=["philosophy", "personal"],
)
created = client.create_mental_model(
    bank_id=bank,
    name="Understanding cooperation in its actual conditions",
    source_query="""Explain the adopted direction and supported relations among
    the person, actual counterpart, joint activity and environment. Distinguish
    reports, chosen commitments, hypotheses and observed effects. Preserve
    counterexamples, conditions and unknowns that change future action.""",
    max_tokens=6000,
    trigger={"mode": "full", "fact_types": ["world", "experience", "observation"],
             "exclude_mental_models": True, "refresh_after_consolidation": True},
)
# Creation schedules work: inspect its operation_id and terminal result,
# then read the resulting ID through client.get_mental_model(bank, model_id).
```

The example illustrates fields, not a command to run against arbitrary personal
data or a universal model/provider/token choice. The SDK also exposes
`create_knowledge_page(bank_id, name, source_query, ..., trigger=...)`,
`refresh_mental_model(bank_id, mental_model_id, mode="full")`,
`reflect(bank_id, query, ...)`, and `export_knowledge_base(bank_id)`.
Use `a...` async counterparts in async callers; generated API clients are also
available through `client.banks`, `client.mental_models`, and `client.knowledge_base`.

Native REST routes under `/v1/default/banks/{bank_id}` include:

| Operation | Route |
| --- | --- |
| Inspect/change bank processing config | `GET` / `PATCH /config` |
| Retain source / consolidate / reflect | `POST /memories`, `POST /consolidate`, `POST /reflect` |
| Create/read/refresh a model | `POST /mental-models`, `GET /mental-models/{id}`, `POST /mental-models/{id}/refresh` |
| Check asynchronous operation | `GET /operations/{operation_id}` |
| Export Knowledge Pages as Markdown | `GET /knowledge-base/export` |

Read current config before patching; a mission update replaces its value.
Observe operation completion and errors rather than treating an HTTP acceptance
as generated content. Inspect source/fact extraction, consolidation backlog,
resulting observation provenance and model refresh independently. Full refresh
is useful after changing the question or source interpretation; select delta
and automatic refresh when their actual behavior fits continuing maintenance.
Native Knowledge Pages default to delta refresh from observations, excluding
other mental models. Override that scope deliberately when raw-source evidence
is needed. Excluding sibling models can prevent an old synthesis being treated
as fresh independent support. Export the native Markdown bundle when a file
consumer needs it; no second custom synthesis engine is required.

## Check the formed understanding and its use

Read the complete generated bodies alongside representative and consequential
sources. Check that the chosen philosophy, personal reports, practical relations,
conflicts and uncertainty survived, and that quoted history has not become the
current assistant's experience. Inspect gaps through recall/source retrieval;
repair source context, missions, scope or model questions, rerun the native
operation and read the changed result. A smaller output or more observations
does not prove semantic preservation. A source citation is not independent
corroboration, and fluent synthesis does not establish a causal explanation.

After checking the bodies, update the private index's actual binding and required
startup reading. Replace old six-file consumer contracts through their owners;
old bodies remain migration/history evidence, not a second authoritative set.
Check the real calling conversation can retrieve the selected complete bodies.
Realize environment changes through their existing owners and verify actual use.
The execution owner retains purpose, judgment, authority and completion;
Hindsight supplies formation and revision machinery. Source delivery, native
fusion, consumer availability, useful cooperation and human development establish
different claims and need evidence at their respective scales.
