# How to organize sources and grounds

This public copy explains how to write the source account for a situated
YinYang package. Its purpose is to make the grounds of the other accounts
findable and assessable: what supports a claim, what it supports only partly,
what remains an interpretation, and where correction can begin. The situated
file is a navigable account of actual evidence, rather than this public
package's bibliography or a ledger of URLs.

Select sources because they bear on the present understanding. Attributable
user expression, personal writing, conversation, memory, observed work, and
authored philosophy can contribute differently. Use relevant authorized
material and retain it with its existing owner. Full adoption grounds all
five situated accounts in actual material; selective borrowing needs the
evidence appropriate to the selected use, without collecting a portrait.

## Explain what the evidence can establish

A source locator answers where to look. A useful source account also explains
why to look there and what limits the conclusion. Retain the author or speaker,
time, relevant passage or message identity, and coverage needed to recover the
claim in context. Where a maintained summary stands between an original and
the current account, make that relation intelligible. A summary can support
continuation while its omissions or interpretations still matter to a disputed
claim.

Different sources answer different questions. A person's report supports what
they expressed or experienced, subject to its context; it does not by itself
establish every external explanation offered with it. Observed work can show
what happened under inspected conditions; it may leave motive unknown. A chosen
commitment states how the collaboration should proceed; behavior is needed to
show its realization. Philosophy can supply concepts and reasons, suggest a
personal interpretation, or express an adopted orientation. It does not prove
that an individual has a particular psychological mechanism.

For a fictional example, a person says a new reading arrangement feels easier.
That report supports experienced ease in that encounter. A record showing that
the intended passage was reached supports access. Neither alone establishes
durable learning or identifies which interface change caused the improvement.
A later account of understanding a new case could add relevant evidence.
Preserve the scope of each contribution rather than using the successful
arrangement as proof of the whole personal model.

## Keep time, conflict, and inference available

Date changing conditions at the level that matters. A tool available last month,
a temporary workload, and a continuing commitment can have different temporal
scopes. A later statement may correct an earlier interpretation, describe a new
situation, or introduce an unresolved conflict. Its recency alone does not
decide which. Explain the relationship where it affects a current claim, and
retain conflicting evidence at its source instead of smoothing it into an
artificially consistent portrait.

Make a consequential inference traceable in ordinary prose. State which
expression or observation suggests it, why that connection is plausible, and
what uncertainty limits its use. Independent checking returns to the original
or another relevant observation; several accounts repeating one unsupported
summary add little evidence. An inaccessible source leaves a coverage limit.
A working link establishes reachability, not correspondence with the claim.

## Maintain an entrance to the actual owners

Keep enough orientation here for a counterpart to locate the support behind
the actual user model, relationship, encounter, and situated use. The guides to
[developing a user model](models.md), [describing the counterpart and relationship](relationship.md),
[writing an encounter account](encounter.md), and [describing situated use](usage.md)
explain the different accounts those sources support. Extended histories, precise excerpts, and private
detail remain with their existing owners or relevant supporting notes. The
[private reference map](personal/README.md) can locate those materials in a
situated package; do not populate it with an invented example person. Public
philosophical references remain near the arguments in their owning essays.

Revise this account when a claim changes its grounds, evidence becomes
unavailable, or a correction changes the interpretation. Return the changed
meaning to the affected account using [design and revision](design-and-revision.md).
Keep supported uncertainty rather than adding sources merely to make the
portrait appear complete. A useful source account makes disagreement
investigable while respecting actual access and publication boundaries.
