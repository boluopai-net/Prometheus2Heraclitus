# YinYang package maintenance

Use [MODEL.md](MODEL.md) for the package's source roles, interpretive boundaries,
and maintenance method. The [core views](references/docs/core-views.md) state the
numbered claims; essays develop their arguments. Read the affected source and
its dependencies rather than reproducing the full philosophy in every file.

The user-facing work should be readable both within each piece and across the
collection. Existing wording, section order, and examples are revisable.
Preserve the relations that carry the argument, useful source attribution, and
actual consumer contracts; remove repetition and obsolete scaffolding.

## Authored and situated sources

[SKILL.md](SKILL.md) owns invocation. [Design and revision](references/design-and-revision.md)
owns formation of the coupled accounts; [harness adaptation](references/harness-adaptation.md)
owns their realization in an actual environment. [The package guide](references/docs/skill-package.md)
owns common/situated structure and projection. Refer to these owners where a
document needs their method instead of restating it.

Distributed and situated editions share common content, including permanent
writing guides under `references/formation/`. A private situated package adds
the fixed five `references/situated/{orientation,person,counterpart,relationship,environment}.md`
accounts and its evidence entrance at `references/personal/sources.md`.
Public distribution contains neither private tree. `scripts/sync-common.py`
preserves both trees and refuses legacy layouts before writing. Do not retrieve
private portraits merely to maintain the public collection or invent people
to populate absent accounts. Other authors can change the structure and philosophy;
maintenance of this implementation preserves the five-account contract.

## Edition and publication coherence

Keep complete English and Chinese reader editions aligned; model-facing
maintenance prose is English. Preserve existing YY identifiers as references
to their claims. Repair links when changing titles, paths, or anchors.

Diagram candidate JSON owns the authored relationships. Regenerate HTML/SVG
and inspect the resulting relationships and readability when a diagram changes.
Website and plugin copies are projections; edit their actual source owner.
The native Site owns its independent essays and presentation.

Preserve licenses, third-party notices, and public/private boundaries under
[MODEL.md](MODEL.md) and [LICENSE](LICENSE). The philosophy is a usable example:
recipients may adopt, combine, challenge, or author a different work.
