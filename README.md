**Begin from this example, write your own philosophy, distill your private models,
and make the thought you choose to share into your own works and website.**
Philosophical writing helps explore an initially unclear self-understanding.
Clear, grounded relations are distilled into five core accounts for actual model
collaboration: **orientation, person, counterpart, relationship, and environment**.
They state adopted philosophy and direction, understand the participants and
their joint activity, and explain realization. Concrete identity, episodes, and original material remain with
private sources. Public work can express philosophy and selected understanding
while the person retains the complete personal model privately. The collection
offers this practice of creation and modeling, beyond replacing a ready-made configuration.

**This collection centers modeling, rather than providing a memory system.**
Its YinYang package begins with a particular person: philosophical, psychological,
and preference material grounds a dynamic user model. Its current
[psychological modeling from event history](skills/yinyang/references/formation/person.md#event-stream-psychological-modeling)
distills long-term conversation history and everyday life with philosophy into
a natural-language conceptual account, from which a corresponding
agent model and relationship are formed. Their work enters the world, and its
consequences revise understanding. Memory retains material and continuity;
modeling organizes meaning, relationships, and change so that understanding
participates in judgment and action.

Philosophy is a whole that can continue to be written. Literature, history,
aesthetics, computing, and engineering can all supply modeling material.
Different views can be retained, compared, or combined without first joining
one school. Accommodating conflict still distinguishes retained material,
currently adopted understanding, and its consequences. Specialist methods and
tools give the understanding usable forms; another person may develop an
entirely different philosophy and environment.

These are usable examples, including the Skills themselves. Take, combine, or
reauthor what helps you make your own work; a new philosophy may look quite
unlike YinYang. The shared ambition is creative capacity, not agreement with
one doctrine. [An example and a commons](skills/yinyang/references/docs/example-and-commons.md)
develops this invitation and distinguishes literary form from technical format.
The [Documents & diagrams](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/en/) page
presents the maintained documents and diagrams alongside their source links.

## How concepts and imagery are used

This project develops a personal philosophy and an engineering practice. It
draws on concepts and imagery from Buddhism, Christianity, and other traditions
to understand human experience, model collaboration, and creative activity.
These uses are selected, interpreted, and reorganized within this work, and
differ from their uses in religious studies, religious doctrine, and faith
practice. References to original texts identify their sources; contemporary
extensions rest on this work's own arguments and practice. The project develops
through open source materials, versioned revision, issue discussion, and
practical testing. Discussion of particular interpretations, arguments, and
effects in use is welcome.

## Philosophy, psychology, and engineering: develop the missing connection

**Philosophy articulates understanding and direction, psychology understands the
particular person, and engineering realizes the relationship in a usable
environment.** These contributions support one another. Enter through available
material and practice and develop the missing contribution; the person need
not first complete three academic disciplines independently.

**If a usable engineering environment is missing, develop it with the model.**
Files, retrieval, tools, execution, and verification make formation and continued
use possible; the person need not first master engineering. The maintained
[starting conditions](skills/yinyang/references/design-and-revision.md#start-from-available-material-and-practice)
distinguish actual philosophical writing, psychological or life material, and
working environments, singly and in pairs. They identify the next contribution
without making formation closed stages. Installed configurations can reflect
constraints, habits, or defaults, so tool inventory alone cannot establish the
person. Philosophy, grounded accounts, and environment continue to revise one
another. [YinYang's package guide](skills/yinyang/README.md#use-and-understand-yinyang)
explains its six working files and use: five grounded accounts and the revision guide.

YinYang supplies culture, thought, and reasoning forms; personal substance comes
from authorized actual material and the consuming model's collaboration with the
person. Its essays and diagrams belong inside
`skills/yinyang/references/`. They are accessories of that Skill. Other members
retain their own purposes and specialist methods; collection membership does
not make them YinYang references or impose its philosophy on their use.
In a combined practice, YinYang is an example of a user's philosophy, while
other Skills supplement it with concrete abilities. Readers can choose their
own combination and write a different philosophy around it.

The public packages preserve the shape and useful companion relationships of
the working local methods. Read each package’s dependency and configuration
guidance, download the companions needed for the selected task, and bind your
own tools and environment. A companion Skill supplies a method, not automatic
authentication, device access, or a running service. Private participant accounts,
credentials, personal records, and host-specific bindings are excluded.

## Use with Codex

Adopting the maintained YinYang includes developing one's own philosophical
expression and maintaining **six working files: five grounded accounts under
`references/situated/` plus `references/revision-guide.md`**. Their supporting
evidence belongs under `references/personal/` or its existing private owners.
Permanent public writing guides live separately in `references/formation/`;
distribution contains no private accounts. Installing does not
complete a situated version. The [getting-started guide](skills/yinyang/README.md#use-and-understand-yinyang)
states what to change, who carries the writing, and how to verify availability.

[Installation and updates](docs/codex.md)

All 22 Skills are individually installable from the **boluopai** plugin marketplace.
Give Codex the following commands to install YinYang without manual ZIP extraction:

```bash
codex plugin marketplace add boluopai-net/Prometheus2Heraclitus
codex plugin add yinyang@boluopai
```

Replace `yinyang` with the desired Skill name from the catalog. Add the market
once; subsequent plugins need only `codex plugin add <name>@boluopai`.
Start a new chat after installation.
Keep personalized YinYang material in a private source outside replaceable caches.
