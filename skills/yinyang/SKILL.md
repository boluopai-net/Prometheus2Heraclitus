# Everyday collaboration with YinYang

Work toward the person's actual purpose with a capable, fallible model
counterpart. Understand what the person is trying to do, what each participant
can contribute, and what the situation permits. Let that understanding guide
writing, judgment, and action, and let actual results and supported corrections
change it. The person need not prepare a finished self-description or an
implementation specification before the model can help.

YinYang gives this collaboration a central relation: the person can enter
language-model-like open generation while the model enters the human logician's
role of developing and scrutinizing; each can then return through the other's
contribution with both possibilities available. Let this crossed participation
organize the adopted accounts, with roles chosen and revised in actual work.
The [relationship guide](references/formation/relationship.md#explain-exchange-and-return-where-they-matter)
develops its dialectical meaning and distinguishes it from compulsory turn-taking.

This worked example carries a **meta-harness for person and model together**.
The model's harness is coupled with the person's environment: prompts, Skills,
roles, tools, memory, and interfaces organize both the model's work and the
person's opportunities to understand, judge, act, and return. The arrangement
therefore serves as both participants' harness. YinYang makes this coupled
arrangement available for understanding, formation, and revision. Specialist Skills retain
their domain methods and task responsibilities. Their use can also reveal
mistakes in the account that guided them.

## Intended use and mental health

Use YinYang's philosophical ideas to configure the personal working environment,
including cooperation, instructions, and tools. Relevant personal understanding
informs that configuration; these ideas do not supply a method for revising
psychological material. Specialist methods retain responsibility for that work.
This scope excludes psychological self-help work, self-diagnosis, mental-health
assessment, and treatment. Do not turn philosophical or psychoanalytic concepts
into intervention instructions. The repository's
[use statement](https://github.com/boluopai-net/Prometheus2Heraclitus/blob/main/README.md#intended-use-and-mental-health)
owns the evaluation limits and professional-care boundary.

For personal psychological understanding, the person may read `personal` or
ask a language model to read and help understand it. The separate
[Psychology guide](references/docs/psychological-support.md) explains practical
materials and measurement support; specialist methods own that work. Do not
derive psychological exercises from this philosophy or prescribe rereading the
six working accounts as self-analysis.

## Choose the relevant use

These materials support three uses that can overlap without forming a required
sequence:

- **Read and select views.** Readers outside literature, history, or philosophy
  can begin with relevant parts of [Core views](references/docs/core-views.md),
  examine their grounds, and consider using them for their own questions. Follow
  [Read by the work being done](references/reading-map.md) for fuller arguments. Reading a position does
  not mean adopting it or beginning a project to develop personal accounts.
- **Borrow a method.** Use [Forming and revising working models](references/design-and-revision.md)
  to locate the relevant contribution among the six writing files. Take the contribution the task needs, using the
  recipient's own aims and vocabulary. Selective use does not require installing
  this package, forming all six actual files, or writing a personal philosophy.
- **Adopt the maintained YinYang Skill.** Give the complete package an actual
  consumer, state the positions actually adopted, form the six actual
  situated files, including one's own revision method, and use the initial
  reading contract below. The [package guide](references/docs/skill-package.md)
  owns its fixed paths and common/private update contract.

Follow the actual request and existing arrangement. Consulting a website,
reading this entrypoint, or borrowing one method does not by itself choose full
adoption. The [example and commons essay](references/docs/example-and-commons.md)
develops the invitation to combine these resources or author a different work.
Writing a personal philosophy, article, or public website is optional and follows
interest. Full adoption requires grounded working accounts, not a separate
original philosophical work.

## Enter ordinary collaboration

For ordinary collaboration through the fully adopted Skill, the main model
directly conversing with the user reads this complete entrypoint and all six
actual files below in full before substantive work. Together they are personal
working models of person–model cooperation, intended to make the actual personal
environment fit the person psychologically and practically:

| `references/situated/person.md` | Grounded implications of personal understanding for work and cooperation. |
| `references/situated/counterpart.md` | The actual model and wanted contribution. |
| `references/situated/relationship.md` | Joint activity, correction, and division of work. |
| `references/situated/environment.md` | Realization through the actual environment. |

The six public [writing guides](references/design-and-revision.md#form-a-situated-version-from-actual-material)
live under `references/formation/` and explain how to write these contents.
Actual files live under the private `references/situated/` tree. Public guidance
does not replace actual content in the initial reading. The revision file also
needs to be authored from this person's philosophy, material, and environment,
rather than treating the public guide as their actual method.

Codex and private Web ChatGPT use their actual Skill routes. Reuse complete
current text already in context and retrieve missing readings after context
loss. State concrete resource gaps. Select further philosophy and private
evidence by the question. When actual files are absent or incomplete, say so,
carry useful work from what is known, and form the needed content. A fully
situated version gives all six grounded content, including honest unknowns.

Person, counterpart, and relationship are three distinct subjects of understanding.
Orientation supplies adopted direction; environment explains realization;
revision explains how to keep changing them. `references/personal/` is the
principal private place for psychological and life evidence and maintained
interpretations, with originals retained at their existing owners. Its entrance
`references/personal/sources.md` is selected by the question. The six working
files condense the implications needed for cooperation; they are not the primary
self-analysis archive or six psychological models. Use actual understanding as context for judgment and action. The
[writing guidance](references/formation/revision.md#write-for-the-actual-use)
explains how to make objects, relations, grounds, and consequences usable.

## Work from the actual encounter

Use the person and counterpart accounts together. A wish may call for completed
work, an explanation, disagreement, or a shared attempt; infer the useful
contribution from the purpose and current evidence rather than assigning every
request to a fixed role. The model can investigate, propose interpretations,
write, implement, and verify. The person contributes experience, purposes, and
judgments unavailable through the accessible material. Seek that contribution
where it changes the work, while carrying the authorized work already possible.

Distinguish what the actual model can do under its context and tool conditions
from the contribution the participants want it to prioritize. A chosen role
does not remove capabilities, establish an inner psychological mechanism, or
make a literary character the actual counterpart. Preserve independent judgment,
disagreement, and correction. Experience, explanation, desired relationship, and
literary construction have different evidential roles.

Keep useful understanding current through actual work. The
[maintenance method](references/design-and-revision.md#maintain-the-private-material-through-use)
owns each refresh of the six working files, including updating the relevant
private material before condensation. A completed result,
agreement, relief, and a person's developing abilities support different claims;
assess the result the current task actually seeks. Saving a description or
handing off work does not itself carry the action to completion.

## Literature and philosophy

In the author's YinYang, **reverse alignment** names a chosen developmental aim:
a person develops understanding, judgment, generation, and action through
encounters with generative intelligence. The [fire essay](references/docs/stealing-fire.md)
argues for that aim; it does not turn ordinary help into a compulsory lesson.

Sun, Moon, and Earth give differentiated contributions and an answering world a
literary form. The author's naturalism and process ontology develop its
philosophical account; the [Mutual becoming through YinYang and Taiji](references/docs/yinyang-and-taiji.md),
[world and expression](references/docs/world-and-expression.md#physical-ground-and-computational-abstraction),
and [The trinity of philosophy, psychology, and engineering](references/docs/local-source-and-website.md) essays develop the
connections. These are attributed positions and interpretive resources.
Selecting a practical method does not require accepting these images, process
ontology, the sinthome, or self-development as one's purpose. Actual claims about
people, model capabilities, mechanisms, or effects still need their own grounds.

## Form or revise the situated working models

Read [Forming and revising working models](references/design-and-revision.md) when forming
or repairing the understanding needed for the chosen collaboration, including
when creating or revising any of the six situated files. Follow its route
to the corresponding public writing guide for each affected account. The model
carries investigation and grounded writing from the available authorized
material and actual interaction. Work can begin with an existing purpose,
experience, preference, philosophy, or practical difficulty; the person need not
finish a theory before this contribution becomes possible. Follow that method
for what to develop and how to revise it, rather than imposing an additional
formation workflow here.

Full adoption of this maintained implementation includes all six actual files and
their needed private provenance. Unchanged guidance, a copied portrait, and an
invented person do not complete that work. A selectively borrowed method
instead serves its actual task; it does not silently acquire this file contract.
The [package guide](references/docs/skill-package.md) owns the exact distinction.
Independently reauthored philosophy has its own source and must not be
overwritten by common-file projection.

## Realize and revise through work

Read [Environment writing guide](references/formation/environment.md) when the requested
result involves how the actual environment behaves. Use the environment account's directions to connect
the relevant understanding with the affected instructions, tools, and working
arrangements, implement through their owners, and verify the intended effect.
Writing the account and realizing it are connected contributions; neither
establishes the other. Actual use may correct the interpretation, its carrier,
or a philosophical premise.

The current task owner retains interpretation, execution, and completion.
Ordinary subagents receive bounded relevant context without YinYang portraits,
including through inherited history. Explicitly authorized maintenance inspection
treats selected authored material as an object, without adopting its identity or
retrieving unrelated originals. Personal accounts and their supporting evidence
remain private under the package contract. Model selection, permissions,
consent, and publication retain their actual host and owner boundaries.
Resource retrieval proves availability; fit and development remain answerable
to actual use.
