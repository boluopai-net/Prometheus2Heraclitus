# Maintaining the YinYang formation Skill

**Prometheus2Heraclitus** is the project's theme. YinYang is its principal
Skill and primarily carries ontology and epistemology. Other Skills primarily
carry methodology; scripts, interfaces, and works realize it. Concrete methods
retain their specialist owners while YinYang connects them to the project's
purpose and understanding. The repository is `Prometheus2Heraclitus`; YinYang lives in `skills/yinyang/`. This file owns that member
and its supporting resources; collection maintenance stays with the root
`MODEL.md`. The collection and its member are distinct.

The common content in this directory supplies a reusable formation Skill for
a meta-harness, with views and philosophical metaphors for human–model
collaboration. The distributed edition contains formation guidance; the
situated edition adds its person's grounded accounts in the designated slots.
Neither replaces the actual participant's purposes and authorization boundaries.

This complete Skill, its philosophy, and its organization are themselves a
usable example. Recipients may adopt it, combine selected resources, or author
a work with different philosophy and form. The literary/philosophical format
offers a way to organize meaning; fixed paths are interfaces of the current
implementation. A work need not resemble YinYang to serve its person's purpose.
The [example and commons essay](references/docs/example-and-commons.md) owns
this framing. Keep maintenance parity within the maintained YinYang package;
do not impose it on independently authored works or use its synchronization
tool to overwrite them.

## Meaning and claim status

Attribute intellectual debts where they bear on the argument. When naming a
philosopher or borrowing a specific concept, provide the relevant original work
near that discussion, with chapter, section, fragment, or page when available.
An existing bibliography can serve through a working in-page citation link;
an author name or a distant link to another essay alone is a weak reading entrance.
Distinguish the source's claim from this collection's interpretation and further
application. Verify substantive correspondence instead of treating a working
URL as proof; citations do not confer reproduction rights. Chinese reader prose
uses 「…」 and nested 『…』 consistently for marked expressions and quotations,
while preserving source wording, code, and mathematics. Use plain link labels
without decorative title quotation marks. Align an article link with its
current title or a recognizable natural short form; a section link can name
the relevant relation. Update stale titles when the destination changes.

`references/docs/preference-harness.md` and its Chinese counterpart develop YinYang as a
preference harness by analogy. The same essay owns the repair consistency
arguments and the bounded comparison between query rewriting and Hegelian
sublation, alongside selection and inference. Public repair-based inconsistency-tolerant
querying supplies a concrete comparison: priority and repair strategy select local views,
while inference semantics determine answers. Rule-following and preference are
compatible dimensions; incomparability need not become an invented total ranking.
Preserve finer choices, including S/P/G/C repairs, AR/IAR/brave and parameterized
families, with their actual assumptions. Comparisons require their own
hypotheses and do not establish a universal total order for every combination.
The harness analogy explains the relationship of preference and reasoning;
ordinary model reasoning is informal natural language. Do not turn the analogy
into a requirement to construct preference relations, enumerate repairs, or
choose a calculus for collaboration. It supplies neither a formal implementation
nor a proof about human cognition.

**Material and intellectual work → available generative capacities → capacities cultivated through practice.** Materials, power, machines, accumulated knowledge, and ongoing iteration make capability available. Physical computation uses energy to form informational structures and outputs while dissipating heat; warranted knowledge concerns their meaning and evidence. The Sun's illuminating power images a capacity to bring forth and make visible. Cultivation develops the embodied person's participation in generation, with abilities internalized or reliably distributed across supports. Sun and Moon express YinYang's differentiated movements, while person and model remain actual participants. Earth is the answering material and shared living environment. AI use and successful outputs alone do not establish human growth.

self-understanding. This is not a clinical attribution or proof of knot repair.

The ontology models both the person and the model as interacting event streams. A stream is an organized continuation of occurrences and their relations, with differing timescales, retained context, and causal or interpretive dependencies; it is not merely a chronological transcript or a mandatory serial pipeline. Personal continuity and counterpart identity can be articulated through patterns and commitments within those streams. Describing, reading, and revising their models are themselves events that can affect later activity. This common ontology supports reciprocal modeling without asserting identical mechanisms or experiences.

The philosophical foundation develops Heraclitean fire, becoming, and opposition in a contemporary computational vocabulary. Preserve that foundational relationship rather than reducing Heraclitus to decorative imagery. Historical fire monism is one influential interpretation, not a settled attribution or the present physical-substance commitment. Paraconsistency supplies broad background for non-explosive reasoning, not an undifferentiated operating logic. The adopted analogy emphasizes a consistent working ontology and potentially inconsistent data. Favor coherence of the user's working concepts and relations for environmental stability, while retaining conflicting evidence and interpretations. A coherent revision can change that ontology; stability does not freeze it or establish that natural-language understanding meets a formal consistency test. Do not attribute a modern calculus to Heraclitus or claim that all paraconsistent systems require ontology-level inconsistency.

Do not rebuild the account around a physical inventory of human, model, world,
and artifact. Those terms matter through their roles in generation,
interpretation, action, and correction. Computational articulation does not
assert a literal digital algorithm for human activity, computational monism, or
an established reduction of experience. The framework adopts a common physical
basis: philosophy, psychological activity, and engineering occur within one
physical reality. Computational ontology abstracts its question-relevant
relations so finite inquiry can work. Particular mechanisms, bridge assumptions,
complete reduction, and feasible computation remain distinct questions. The
Trinity essay develops this unity of physical ground with differentiated
explanatory and practical levels; the layers diagram must show that basis.

Retaining incompatible candidates differs from jointly accepting their claims;
removing a candidate from a working view does not erase its source or prove it
false. An inference depends on its candidate and rules; evidence about actual
outcomes can constrain or defeat an interpretation. Consensus and internal
coherence cannot replace world-directed checks. The explicitly fictional
indicator example in YY-10 illustrates one local inquiry under a stated

Reverse alignment is the developmental purpose of the project, not a claim

Agreement with a model, successful task execution, and demonstrated human

The human-as-multimodal-generator account is a functional and developmental

The Sun/Moon relation is an expressive device for reciprocal generation,

## Revise through meaning and use

Preserve the reciprocal connection:

`expressive language ↔ understood relationship ↔ working realization ↔ evidence`

An image can sustain multiple interpretations. Develop the interpretation needed
for a specific use, make consequential assumptions inspectable, and let actual
results correct it. Do not flatten the account into generic productivity advice
or expand it into a total biography or theory of mind. A beautiful expression,
a clear instruction, and a successful practical result support different claims.

Separate a reported experience, an explanatory hypothesis, a chosen relation,
and a literary construction when the distinction changes a conclusion. Avoid
burdening every sentence with labels. Preserve uncertainty where it matters.

Use a currently strongest suitable available model for conceptual authorship,
following the user's explicit selection and actual host capabilities. Iterate
in short, reviewable changes rather than treating strength as a reason to add
ceremony. When adapting the ideas across multiple Skills, use parallel bounded
contributions for genuinely independent work, with the integrating owner
retaining conceptual coherence. Give contributors only the task-relevant public
concepts and requirements. A model name does not confer additional authority.

External repository comparison in this work concerns directory organization
only. Do not import another repository's philosophical content, authoring
method, or claims as part of that structural reference.

Keep public provenance public. User-provided examples explicitly intended for
this viewpoints collection can ground its development; do not publish private
quotations merely because they informed an abstraction. When a proposed
addition exceeds existing support, identify it as editorial synthesis or an
open proposal until further clarification warrants adoption.

## Publication boundary

Public updates contain general viewpoints, formation methods, and their
explanatory resources. Personal substance belongs to the consuming model's
situated version, not to prefilled demonstration Skills in this collection.
Unpublished manuscripts, reading editions, PDFs, figures, and source records are
excluded from public packages. Preserve general ideas through standalone exposition
in the existing essays, using independently public references where relevant.
Original or anonymous manuscript publication requires fresh explicit user
authorization. Exclude private source quotations, personal
biography, health or psychological records, relationship narratives, accounts,
private paths, conversation identifiers, and links exposing those materials.
Do not create a public source index that reconstructs the private portrait.

Review the whole public change, including assets, metadata, Git history being
published, and link destinations. Verify that explanations remain meaningful
without access to private sources. Public documentation should link to public
artifacts only; privacy is preserved by the content actually released, not by
an assertion that a repository is conceptual.

If a public wording becomes misleading, repair its existing owner and the
dependent diagrams or reader entrypoints. Do not create a second profile,
interpretation registry, automatic personal-data feed, or synchronization
service to maintain this small collection.

`references/sources.md` is the source-owner index; `references/personal/`
holds precise topical provenance and unreleased material. Maintain one authored
philosophy in the common essays, even when it is the person's own philosophy.
Keep actual environment use in `references/usage.md`, not in provenance notes.
