# Maintaining the YinYang formation Skill

YinYang is a philosophical and literary example for developing with generative
intelligence. It connects a person's understanding of themselves, a model
counterpart, and their relationship with the environments in which they act.
This file guides maintenance of that example; it is not another statement of
all its philosophical claims.

The [core views](references/docs/core-views.md) own the numbered propositions.
The essays develop their arguments, images, examples, and sources.
[SKILL.md](SKILL.md) owns ordinary invocation and routes to the formation and
adaptation methods. Use the [reading map](references/reading-map.md) to locate
the argument relevant to a change.

## Source and consumer roles

This package is one member of Prometheus2Heraclitus. Other Skills retain their
own purposes and methods. Within this example, YinYang primarily supplies
ontology, epistemology, and an articulated value orientation; specialist Skills
supply domain methods. These contributions overlap in actual work without
making YinYang the procedural owner of every task.

Keep the following uses of writing distinct:

- **Reader essays** develop a question into an argument. Each needs enough
  orientation to stand alone, while links lead to a genuinely different or
  deeper question rather than replacing a missing premise.
- **Formation and adaptation methods** explain how the understanding becomes
  a situated account and a working environment. They should use the philosophy
  without repeating its complete exposition.
- **Situated accounts and personal context** state the relevant understanding,
  evidence, conditions, and implications explicitly for model use. Follow the
  [writing method](references/design-and-revision.md#write-for-the-actual-use);
  maintain correspondence with human-readable philosophy without making its
  literary form the default for working instructions.
- **Maintenance instructions** locate sources, dependencies, publication, and
  acceptance. They preserve meaning across consumers without turning the
  essays into a second policy manual.

The [package guide](references/docs/skill-package.md) owns the shared/situated
structure and projection contract. The five files
`references/{encounter,models,relationship,usage,sources}.md` contain writing
guidance in distribution and grounded personal content in a situated package.
Supporting private material lives under `references/personal/`. Common updates
preserve those files. `scripts/sync-common.py` checks or applies that projection;
local and cloud owners install it and verify their actual consumers.

Authored public content lives here. `plugins/yinyang/`, website reading editions,
and local/cloud common files are generated or synchronized consumers, not
independent authoring sources. The native Site owns its five independent
philosophical articles and presentation code. Follow the actual source owner
when a change crosses these boundaries.

## Interpret the connected account

Reverse alignment is the developmental purpose: a person cultivates
understanding, judgment, generation, and action through encounters with
generative models. A completed output, an improved arrangement, and growth in
the person's available capacities support different claims. Development may
include abilities internalized by the person or reliably distributed across
supports; assess the relevant change through actual participation.

Modest naturalism names the framework's scope in one changing natural world;
its contemporary fire monism articulates that world's dynamic unity. This is
the physical ground of philosophy, psychology, and engineering. Engineering
ontology here is the computational articulation of a question-selected part
of that process: events, states, persisting objects, relations, constraints,
and transition rules that a computer can represent and use to simulate relevant
changes where needed. Person and language model form and revise the question,
model, realization, and interpretation of feedback together; their contributions
are distributed throughout the same chain. Usable representations connect
meanings to actual consumers and observations, including through clear
model-interpreted language where appropriate. Records and models do not exhaust
the streams being understood. [World, process, and expression](references/docs/world-and-expression.md#physical-ground-and-computational-abstraction)
owns the developed connection. Physical dependence, particular realization
mechanisms, full reduction, and feasible computation remain distinct questions.
The Trinity essay organizes the three physically realized contributions around
the concrete person and their unfolding life. Preserve the distinction between
that personal center and the common physical ground: the former gives this
practice its organizing concern, while the latter locates it in one world.
Its local environment, repository, and website are practical forms of the work.

The adopted process ontology develops Heraclitean fire, becoming, and opposition.
Its contemporary commitment and the disputed historical interpretation of
Heraclitus remain distinguishable. The preferred repair analogy uses
coherent working concepts while retaining conflicting sources and candidates.
Selection among views and inference from them have different responsibilities.
Keep the formal examples' actual assumptions and conclusions with their owning
arguments; ordinary collaboration does not require constructing a repair
calculus.

Sun and Moon express differentiated generation, reception, and shaping.
Person and model are the actual participants and exceed those roles; either
can reason, generate, object, or revise. Earth names the material and shared
environment where creations acquire independent consequences. Cultivation
concerns developing generative capacity, not becoming a celestial character.
The Taiji essay owns the image and its philosophical sources.

Individual-first gives the person's purposes, agency, and chosen development
priority in their own practice while preserving other participants' purposes.
Changing social arrangements, then software, before asking the person to adapt
is a design direction. Its value is judged in the actual situation. The chosen
daily rhythm is ordinarily synchronous with the model and asynchronous with
society, with temporary translation and alignment for social evaluation.
Local cultivation and restrained promotion serve concrete lives and relations;
they are not an empirical law of social acceleration.

Literary programming uses characters, images, and narrative to organize a
relationship that instructions and tools can realize. The sinthome essay
proposes a singular sustaining form for a person's philosophy and practice.
These are interpretive constructions with practical consequences. Their
coherence does not establish a psychological diagnosis, a hidden model
mechanism, or an empirical claim; such claims need their own evidence.

These distinctions preserve the argument's load-bearing relations. Their
developed explanations belong in the essays, not in repeated prefaces or
closing disclaimers throughout the package.

## Revise for standalone and collective coherence

Read a piece for its actual question, premises, movement, examples, and result.
Remove repetition that contributes no new relation. Expand where an unfamiliar
concept, inference, or change of scale would otherwise leave the reader behind.
A short piece can be complete; a difficult argument may need sustained detail.
Neither uniform length nor preservation of every existing paragraph is a goal.

Across pieces, assign the developed argument to its clearest owner. An umbrella
essay may give a short connective account; a method may state the principle it
needs; a core view may state the proposition. Their different roles justify
some recurrence, but each occurrence should do identifiable work. Use stable
terms where the relation stays the same, and explain a material change of sense.

Words, headings, examples, and argument structure can change. Stable YY IDs
remain locators for the same claims; when moving or renaming a section, repair
its incoming links or retain a useful anchor. Reconcile the corresponding
diagram, method, source summary, and reading route when its meaning changes.
A substantive revision can also make an old caveat, example, or auxiliary rule
unnecessary.

Keep English and Chinese editions semantically complete and aligned. English
is the primary model-facing edition; Chinese is a full reader edition. Chinese
prose uses 「…」 and nested 『…』 for marked expressions, preserving quotations,
code, mathematics, and exact source material. Use recognizable current article
titles in links. A bilingual website should lead each reader to the matching
language where available.

## Evidence, sources, and release

Attribute a borrowed philosophical concept near its use, with a useful source
locator. Distinguish what the source establishes from this collection's
interpretation or extension. Preserve the assumptions of formal claims and the
conditions of empirical comparisons. A working URL establishes access, not
substantive correspondence.

Personal claims must be grounded in attributable expression, relevant
experience, and actual use. Formation does not require a complete biography.
The [design method](references/design-and-revision.md) owns how to develop and
revise the situated account; [harness adaptation](references/harness-adaptation.md)
owns carrying it into an environment and learning from the result.
Use host policy for model selection, authority, and publication rather than
maintaining a second routing menu here.

The public package supplies reusable guidance and philosophy, never a copied
private portrait. Keep personal records, health and relationship details,
private paths, conversation identifiers, and unpublished manuscripts with their
private owners. General ideas can become standalone public exposition; original
private materials need their actual publication authorization.
