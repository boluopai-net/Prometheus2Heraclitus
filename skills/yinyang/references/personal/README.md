# Sources and private material

This directory retains useful private substance beyond the maintained public
philosophy. The distributed package contains this guide; the situated package
selects material from the actual person's environment and authorized sources.

Relevant material can include current work, study, research, and life purposes;
psychological or relational experience; and evidence of environmental use.
Keep a useful distillation with source, date, and consequential uncertainty,
or a direct route to the original when that suffices. Current public philosophy
and its owning source supply the conceptual account. Refresh private summaries
from it and retire duplicated theory, superseded interpretations, and material
that no longer helps. Public and private distinguish disclosure, not authorship.

[sources.md](../sources.md) locates source owners and collections; topic notes
here carry necessary private detail and its provenance. The actual
[encounter](../encounter.md), [user model](../models.md),
[counterpart and relationship](../relationship.md), and [use](../usage.md)
remain in their own files. Proactively maintain affected material during
relevant work under the [maintenance method](../design-and-revision.md#maintain-the-private-material-through-use).
Originals and extended history retain their existing source owners; they need
not remain duplicated in every active package.
The shared package must not reproduce a private portrait or source index.
