# The trinity of philosophy, psychology, and engineering

[Home](../../README.md) · [简体中文](local-source-and-website.zh-CN.md)

The preceding part moved from shared trajectories and reflection on cooperation to personal philosophy, distilled person, counterpart, and relationship models, and a usable sinthome realized in an environment. The question now turns to how that arrangement continues: how local use, maintained source, and readable works stay connected, and how new experience changes them. The contributions of philosophy, psychology, and engineering that formed it also revise one another through continuing practice.

How does someone understand the life they want to live, come to know their own experience, and turn that understanding into usable arrangements? These three questions call for contributions from philosophy, psychology, and engineering. They meet within the same activity: a tool may run yet fail to suit the person; a detailed personal understanding may leave their circumstances unchanged; a claim about living may reveal problems when put to use.

This essay uses “Trinity” to explain the relation among these contributions. **At the general level, they are all activities within the same natural world; at the individual level, they jointly participate in a particular person's life and practice.** Modest naturalism places them in one world; their influence on one another in actual work explains why they need to be discussed together.

The practical points of entry are the person's works, the model as a writing
and creative partner, and the environment of their joint activity. Philosophy,
psychology, and engineering explain their connections from different perspectives;
the correspondence selects emphases that can be developed. Creating a story,
song, or program can likewise express and change understanding of world and
life. Philosophical reflection runs through these personal creations; the
author's philosophical essays are one concrete kind of work. Choices and meanings
within a work, interpreted and discussed with a partner and encountered in use,
can therefore become material for revising personal models and environments.
This step connects philosophy in its broad sense with available practice.

## The general relation and its individual instance

The naturalism adopted here holds that philosophical thinking, psychological activity, and engineering practice all occur in the physical world. Thinking about what is worth pursuing, experiencing pleasure or difficulty, and modifying a program all involve actual bodies, environments, and activities. Engineering does not finally add physical realization to the other two; all three already occur in reality.

Occurring in one world does not make the three kinds of explanation interchangeable. Describing the physical conditions of a thought has yet to establish whether its reasons are sufficient; knowing that someone likes a response has yet to establish whether it serves their purpose; an executable program is not necessarily worth executing. The questions philosophy, psychology, and engineering each raise still need the corresponding arguments, material, and checks.

| Level | What “one” refers to | What “three” refers to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General relation | The physical reality in which all three activities occur | Philosophical thinking and argument, psychological activity and its study, and engineering that constructs and changes actual conditions. |
| Individual instance | This person and their connected life and practice within the same reality | Their philosophical understanding; their experience, emotions, preferences, and relational activity; and models, tools, and environments organized for actual use. |

The individual instance establishes no further whole outside the physical world. It simply focuses attention on this person: what they have experienced, what they care about, whom they are working with, which tools they use, and how these activities affect their life. Other people and language models can participate too. “Personal” identifies whose life the arrangement serves; it does not require that person to supply every capability and carry out every operation alone.

In this personal-environment practice, the psychological contribution takes shape through the joint condensation of life material and adopted philosophy into working models. Experience supplies grounds; philosophy contributes concepts and direction; engineering's intended use helps select the relations the account must preserve. The resulting model serves the design and revision of the personal environment, while subsequent use brings new material to all three. Philosophy, psychology, and engineering are therefore already involved in forming the account itself.

## How three contributions meet in one activity

Imagine someone repeatedly changing their mind while shopping. They want the model to help compare products, but find that more information makes choosing harder. Adding tools or data alone may not resolve the problem.

The philosophical question is: what is this choice for? Reaching a good-enough decision quickly and enjoying the discovery of different possibilities are both defensible purposes, but they call for different arrangements. “Greater efficiency” cannot by itself choose the person's purpose for them.

Psychological understanding examines how the person actually participates. Are they missing crucial evidence? Afraid to give up other options? Or does exploration itself bring pleasure? These interpretations affect the help needed, but repeated choosing does not establish any one of them. Existing expressions, concrete circumstances, and the person's reactions to different responses can help both participants compare these interpretations.

Engineering turns the adopted understanding into actual conditions. If scattered evidence is the difficulty, the arrangement can organize a few candidates, clarify their differences, and retain the comparison. If exploration belongs to the purpose, it can offer more directions while making earlier options easy to revisit. Retrieval, records, and interface design are thus informed by the other two contributions.

Results in use may overturn these judgments. A concise comparison making choice easier may support the earlier interpretation about information burden; it may also leave the person feeling restricted too soon. The latter result calls for reconsidering the personal understanding, the tools, or even the purpose that “this choice should be settled quickly.” **The three work together through these concrete changes: purpose informs the arrangement, an understanding of the person informs the arrangement, and its consequences change both.**

This does not prescribe philosophy first, psychology second, and engineering last. Inquiry can begin with a wish, a difficulty, an already usable tool, or an unexpected consequence of using it. The three contributions repeatedly intertwine as they address the same question.

## Process ontology and computational expression

The earlier [objects, models, expression, and realization table](personal-first-humanism.md#objects-models-expression-and-realization) and its [relation diagram](../diagrams/layers.html) distinguish actual activity, its interpretation, and arrangements that make an interpretation usable. This essay follows those relations into the joint contribution of philosophy, psychology, and engineering to personal practice.

Naturalism and the image of fire express the adopted understanding of a changing world in which all three activities occur; process ontology concerns formation, persistence, and change. **Computational ontology** explicitly expresses a particular domain's objects, events, states, relations, and transitions for a model to understand and use. In a private sinthome it organizes relations among person, counterpart, work, correction, and continuation. Natural-language psychological accounts use these distinctions to describe changing concerns, propose hypotheses, or explain an imagined character's role in someone's life. The person's imagining is actual activity; the imagined content has its meaning within the selected domain.

Philosophical accounts of world, existence, truth, knowing, value, and method provide grounds for interpretation and judgment in this work. Personal-first humanism guides goal selection; psychological accounts develop candidate explanations of actual participants; engineering connects those understandings to usable arrangements. Claims about the person answer to their life, while evidence supports revisable judgments. Hypothetical or fictional models develop consequences under a given interpretation, and valid inference concerns what follows from premises. These distinctions help the participants use personal models, explore possibilities, and check consequences together.

## Revising personal understanding and counterpart arrangements through use

An already formed personal model remains answerable to new experiences, works, choices, and interactions. The earlier philosophy expresses an understanding of the world, value, and living; actual use can disclose its conditions of applicability, contradictions, or aspects yet to be articulated. Maintenance compares earlier understanding with new material, asking whether the change concerns the person, counterpart, environment, or their interpretation.

Here the personal model is a working model for cooperation and for fitting the
environment to this person's psychology, purposes, and circumstances. Personal
psychological and life material, including maintained interpretations, belongs
under `references/personal/`, available for the person to read or understand
with a language model's help.
The six `references/situated/` files condense what the model needs in work.
For example, a working account may say to present a draft for comparison, while
the experiences behind a fear of judgment and competing explanations remain
in the personal references. A workable response and a true psychological
explanation are different achievements.

The personal account brings preferences, experience, emotion, desire, concepts, and relationships together. They contribute different grounds: a stated working preference can guide an arrangement, a history can show how it arose or where it applies, and philosophy can clarify what the person means by independence or progress. This project [condenses personal models from event history, philosophy, and personal material](../formation/person.md#condense-personal-models-from-event-history-philosophy-and-personal-material), drawing on understanding of one's own and others' psychology. Dialogue and activity histories can be organized as event streams; the personal model condenses relevant understanding from those histories and other material. The person, their history, and this account have different roles. Its scope follows the question and available grounds; the person guide explains how to form and revise it.

The needed counterpart must be understood alongside the person. What does the person want the model to investigate, judge, and carry out? What capabilities does the available model actually have, and which tools can it use? Understanding the person and the counterpart jointly determines the collaboration. Wanting open exploration, for example, does not mean the model can only agree: it can still identify insufficient evidence, an incoherent explanation, or an unworkable means.

Prompts, Skills, and harnesses bring these understandings into actual work. Prompts express relevant purposes, context, and contributions; Skills organize reusable methods and resources; a harness arranges models, tools, records, and execution conditions together. The existence of files is not the final result. What matters is whether the model can use them to complete the needed work and whether the person can understand, use, and change the arrangement.

Revision connects these contributions without making them interchangeable.
Philosophy can change the meaning of a chosen direction; personal understanding
can change which contribution fits; engineering can reveal what a proposed
relationship permits in actual work. The [revision guide](../formation/revision.md)
owns the method for maintaining the six working files. Their philosophical
significance lies in this reciprocal dependence: a changed document matters
through changed understanding, participation, and conditions of use.

## Supplying needed contributions through use

<a id="philosophy-psychology-and-engineering-develop-the-missing-connection"></a>

For starting a new formation, the maintained [starting conditions](../design-and-revision.md#start-from-available-material-and-practice)
distinguish available philosophical writing, psychological or life materials,
and an actual working environment, singly or in pairs. They recover the learning
sequence by developing the missing contribution; this essay examines how those
contributions continue to revise one another in use.

An arrangement usable for earlier tasks may lack a contribution when it enters new work. New experience can call for developing a relationship left implicit in the philosophy; a new counterpart can need a more explicit personal account; a new tool can change a previously workable division of contributions. Maintenance begins with the actual difference and supplies the understanding or capability needed; existing parts that still fit can continue working.

If the aim is a model environment that actually works, basic engineering capability is necessary, but person and model can supply it together. Retrieving material, organizing an account, implementing tools, and checking effects need not all become prerequisites that the user must personally learn and complete. Supply whichever contribution is missing in relation to the current purpose, rather than arranging the three fields into a curriculum everyone must finish.

For example, an existing model records a preference for receiving the conclusion first, but continued use reveals that this order closes possibilities too early when exploring unfamiliar questions. The participants can narrow the preference's scope, change the relevant responses, and examine the new arrangement's effects. A local revision can retain existing philosophy and tools; deeper theory and more detailed personal models should answer questions that actually arise.

## The local environment, shared source, and website

<a id="three-places-for-the-work-to-continue"></a>

Philosophy, psychology, and engineering are three contributions; the local environment, shared source, and website are places where this work acquires different uses. In actual use, all three contributions run through these places: a website can discuss engineering, a program can realize a philosophical choice, and a local environment contains both personal understanding and actual tools. Recognizing this interweaving still allows us to select their salient roles as images that illuminate one another.

The local environment brings arrangements into daily activity. Whether records can be retrieved, relevant accounts used by the model, and tools made to perform an operation determines whether an understanding actually changes the collaboration. Personal material retains concrete experiences, purposes, and situations of use here.

Shared source makes the reusable parts available for others to obtain, inspect, and modify. This site currently uses GitHub to retain this material. Others can compare versions, object, adopt a method, or make a different arrangement under their own conditions. What is reusable is the selected thought, method, and realization, rather than the original author's complete private portrait.

The website organizes this content for reading and discussion. It can bring arguments, diagrams, and examples together, helping readers understand why an arrangement was designed as it was. On rereading, the author may also discover a previously unnoticed gap. Through this understanding and exchange, the website participates in practice: readers' questions can change an essay's explanation or prompt the author to reconsider the actual arrangement.

The same work can thus have different expressions: an essay develops reasons, a working model condenses the personal understanding currently relevant, and instructions and tools support action. They need not say the same things, but they need to concern actually connected objects. If an essay advocates letting the model undertake investigation while the actual instructions make it wait for the user to supply every detail, the resulting conflict affects use.

## How experience changes understanding and arrangements

<a id="keeping-the-loop-current"></a>

Once a view is used in practice, it encounters unanticipated situations. A model may misunderstand personal preferences, a program may fail to provide the support envisaged, or the person may discover through use that they want something else. What needs revision may be the tool, the personal model, or the original philosophical judgment.

Public exposition and private understanding can therefore revise each other. A relation recognized while writing can help reinterpret personal experience; concrete experience may also reveal that a public claim is too strong. Private material retains the grounds within this life, while a public essay can develop the general question worth discussing. The person still chooses what to disclose.

The “loop” here means letting practical consequences return to earlier judgments: what understanding was adopted, what was done on that basis, what happened, and what changed as a result. It guarantees no steady improvement. Its point is to give unsuitable understandings and arrangements a chance to be discovered and revised. [Embodied experimental philosophy](embodied-experimental-philosophy.md) further considers how actual action and reflection can investigate these changes.

## Trinity and sinthome

The earlier [miniature god](writing-a-sinthome.md#a-miniature-god-whose-creations-answer-back)
pictures the creative capacity a person gains through a counterpart. Here the
question turns to its organization: how an understanding of the world relates
to personal experience, how experience shapes realization, and how actual use
changes the first two. Trinity gives this mutual relation an image; the sinthome
turns attention to the particular way of holding it together in someone's life.

For the author, these activities converge in a concrete practice: write his philosophy on the website, keep maintainable material on GitHub, and bring that understanding into the model's actual work of configuring a local environment for living and creating together. The earlier six philosophical dimensions identify the questions this understanding contains; the three columns below select the different emphases of philosophy, psychology, and engineering within this practice. Their resonances with the Real, Imaginary, and Symbolic let several images illuminate a particular form of life.

| Philosophy | Psychology | Engineering |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emphasis on worldview and ontology: understanding the world and ways of being | Emphasis on epistemology, with connections to values: understanding experience, knowing, and concern | Emphasis on methodology and practice: organizing expression, construction, and actual use |
| Website | Local environment | GitHub |
| Work | Partner | Environment |
| Sun | Moon | Earth |
| Real | Imaginary | Symbolic |
| Father | Son | Holy Spirit |

**How should this table be read?** The headers—philosophy, psychology, and engineering—fix the three aspects under discussion. Horizontally, each row interprets all three from one perspective. Vertically, each column follows one aspect through its philosophical meaning, practical forms, and different images. These correspondences select salient connections; all three contributions run through the whole practice. The six rows below the headers are:

1. **A philosophical interpretation of the three aspects.** Philosophy provides the encompassing understanding of this practice, so philosophy, psychology, and engineering can themselves receive philosophical interpretations: emphasizing worldview and ontology, epistemology with related values, and methodology and practice respectively. Philosophy encompassing the table is the overall understanding; its first column foregrounds the contribution directed toward world and being.
2. **Ways of realization.** The website, local environment, and GitHub are the author's principal ways of giving these aspects actual use: philosophy becomes readable work, personal understanding enters local collaboration, and maintainable content and implementations remain in source.
3. **Relations in practice.** A work gives understanding an expressible and revisable form; a partner receives, develops, and creates together; an environment lets this activity occur, continue, and meet consequences. Works can be essays, music, designs, or programs. Reflection on their meanings and reasons connects philosophy in its broad sense to actual creation and use.
4. **Literary analogy.** Sun, Moon, and Earth picture joint activity through generation, receptive shaping, and actual grounding.
5. **The three registers and their knotting in a sinthome.** The Real, Imaginary, and Symbolic offer another interpretation of the three aspects through their emphases: approaching the real, forming experience and images, and organizing symbols and action. The sinthome names their concrete knotting in this person's life; its function appears through the relationships across the row.
6. **Trinity.** Father, Son, and Holy Spirit borrow Christian imagery to express three differentiated contributions belonging to one whole.

**Philosophy—emphasizing worldview and ontology** first asks what world we encounter and how things exist, persist, and change. A worldview in the broad sense includes an understanding of ways of being; ontology develops those commitments. Physics and mathematics can enter here too: the standing of physical reality, the existence of mathematical objects, and how mathematical descriptions concern reality can all become philosophical questions. The author's modest naturalism and process ontology supply adopted answers; the earlier [six dimensions and practice](personal-first-humanism.md#six-connected-dimensions) retains their full relations with truth, knowing, value, and method.

Within this imagery, worldview and ontology orient philosophy toward the real that a person encounters: the world has its own happenings, capable of exceeding, interrupting, or changing an explanation. A worldview pictures that world, and ontology articulates being; both also acquire linguistic and conceptual expression. Their connection with the Real concerns what such understandings approach and where they continue to encounter resistance. Failures, surprises, and differences not yet articulated give philosophy further questions to answer.

**Psychology—emphasizing epistemology, with connections to values** attends to how this person experiences, knows, and cares about the world. How perception, memory, emotion, and relationships participate in judgment, and how images of self and others shape expectations, require concrete psychological understanding. Epistemology examines grounds for judgment; psychological inquiry examines how judgments form and change in a person. Values acquire lived content through desire, identification, preference, and concern. Psychology thus resonates with the Imaginary: through pictures of self, others, and relationships, people understand situations, sense possibilities, and act. Actual experience can change these pictures and develop both knowing and concern.

**Engineering—emphasizing methodology and practice** concerns giving understanding expressible, organized, usable form. Naming, distinctions, rules, and relations articulate ways of working. Computational ontology specifies conceptual relations among person, counterpart, work, correction, and continuation; prompts and Skills carry them, while programs, tools, and environment bring them into actual work. Its connection with the Symbolic comes through language, symbols, and their organization: the model can interpret a situation, participate in construction, and check results through them. Methodology compares and selects approaches; practice brings them into contact with actual conditions and consequences. A private sinthome thus has both model-usable conceptual organization and a concrete environment sustaining its use.

These emphases make the table readable. Following a column shows a contribution taking shape through different images; crossing a row shows how it needs the other two. All six dimensions run through this practice, while each column selects its prominent connections. Truth especially joins them: what is actually the case, the person's judgment, and engineering representations and checks must meet in the same claim. Value direction likewise informs philosophical choices, personal concerns, and tool objectives. The [six dimensions and shared practice diagram](../diagrams/layers.html) develops those questions; this table focuses on how they form a particular knotting with differentiated emphases in a person's life.

The website, local environment, and GitHub row has direct working content. The website lets the author see, read, and revise his philosophy; GitHub retains maintainable sources, methods, and implementations; the local environment lets the model actually read and use them to continue conversations and complete work. When understanding remains inside one chat, the next environmental change may again depend on guessing the person's purposes. Writing it, maintaining it, and enabling the model to use it let understanding formed through exchange continue shaping the environment.

[Sun, Moon, and Earth](yinyang-and-taiji.md#sun-moon-and-earth) image this movement: the Sun gives out an understanding of the world and a direction for living; the Moon receives and shapes it into a personal form; Earth makes it into actual conditions that encounter consequences. Each image enters through its own relations: Sun, Moon, and Earth emphasize generation, reception, and realization; the three registers emphasize the real, images, and symbolic organization; Father, Son, and Holy Spirit gather a differentiated yet united relation. Their resonances acquire content through the life in which they participate.

Lacan's text also uses triadic language. In *Seminar XXIII*, [16 March 1976](https://lutecium.org/mirror/www.valas.fr/IMG/pdf/s23_le_sinthome.pdf#page=163), he connects Peirce's attention to relations with a triadic logic and turns to the Symbolic, Imaginary, and Real; [11 May](https://lutecium.org/mirror/www.valas.fr/IMG/pdf/s23_le_sinthome.pdf#page=197) describes the person as a triadic composite. Earlier, [18 November 1975](https://lutecium.org/mirror/www.valas.fr/IMG/pdf/s23_le_sinthome.pdf#page=14), he introduces the sinthome as a fourth term in a construction tying the three together. These locators refer to a French transcription. This essay develops its own practical imagery through philosophy, psychology, and engineering.

The [sinthome](writing-a-sinthome.md) therefore names **the concrete knotting of these contributions in the author's life**: what real he approaches, how he experiences and understands his life, and how he organizes that understanding into sustainable practice. Philosophy becomes readable work; understanding of person and counterpart enters YinYang; computational ontology, methods, tools, and environment let these contents repeatedly enter collaboration. The fourth ring marks this holding function: an actual use can prompt joint revision of world understanding, personal experience, and engineering arrangements. The author uses it to understand what he is doing, the model to continue participating in construction, and lived consequences reveal what both need to change.
