# Why write a personal philosophy

[简体中文](why-yinyang.zh-CN.md)

## A complex Harness needs a shared direction

Personal philosophical writing develops a broader practice here: people express,
develop, and revise their understanding of world and life through their own
works. Essays, stories, music, designs, and programs can all carry this activity.
Philosophical reflection articulates relevant meanings, values, and reasons,
making the relations among works, a writing partner, and an environment
available for understanding and change.

A computer environment takes up activities from the life someone is living. What to buy, how to consume and invest, whom to talk with, what to learn and create, and which relationships to sustain involve needs, pleasures, hopes, and choices. A person brings some of these into conversation; a model helps understand them, find information, and shape tools and instructions through which they can act. What happens in use returns to life and can change both the arrangement and the understanding that guided it. A personal computer environment condenses selected relations from that life.

After working with a model for some time, the environment grows increasingly complex. A research Skill finds materials, a writing Skill organizes articles, and a programming Skill implements them; prompts specify ways of communicating, hooks trigger checks when certain events occur, and tools connect files, browsers, and applications. The model itself is changing too. Where memory lives, how works are saved, how results are presented, and how the user participates all affect the collaboration that eventually takes shape.

Here, the arrangements that organize these activities are called a **Harness**. It spans Skills, prompts, hooks, model selection and invocation, and the parts of the user's environment that participate in the work. These depend on one another: a way of asking questions changes how the user expresses themselves; an expression affects how the model understands a purpose; a way of organizing files affects what can later be recovered; and a way of checking results changes what the model is willing to complete.

Even after each component has been repaired separately, a shared question remains: whom is this environment helping, what kind of life is it helping them live, and how should the two participants work together? If every Skill answers for itself, research may pursue exhaustiveness, writing may pursue brevity, and execution may repeatedly hand judgments back to the user. Each local arrangement makes sense, yet together they become exhausting.

Personal philosophy can begin here. This essay takes YinYang as an example: writing out an understanding of the person, the model, and their relationship, so that scattered arrangements have a direction they can refer to together. A Skill's methods can then connect with this person's life, and a change to the environment can explain which form of participation it improves.

## First, what is an agent?

An **agent** here is a participant able to receive information in a situation,
form or take up purposes, organize available capacities, and act; consequences
can enter its next understanding and response. This is a functional description
for organizing work. A person and model can participate in that description
at relevant levels while retaining their bodily conditions, mechanisms,
capacities, and responsibilities.

The person enters the later argument already as such an agent. Borrowed
capacities broaden existing participation: they can understand and revise
purposes, enlist models, records, tools, and relationships, turn conceptions
into action, and adjust through consequences. What forms here is a personal
philosophy: an expressed worldview and direction for living whose practice
reaches across the environment.

“Becoming fire” is the broader generative image of the series: through
participation, borrowed intelligence enters one's understanding, judgment,
creation, and life, allowing further initiation and generation. Existing
agency is this direction's starting point; developing enjoyment, understanding,
and creation also takes becoming fire beyond execution. A shared direction
therefore asks both how the environment helps someone complete the present
work and what capacities for further understanding, judgment, and creation
that work leaves them. The latter question gives this site's “reverse
alignment” its direction.

This direction also calls for examining the pictures that organize it. If
“becoming fire” means always pursuing the newest model or continually increasing
output, life again answers to an external measure. Philosophy can reopen what
deserves attention, what is enough, and which understandings and relationships
need different conditions. [From chasing to lived cultivation](stealing-fire.md#from-chasing-to-lived-cultivation)
develops this relation through philosophical therapy and the Buddhist raft
simile. Personal philosophy gives an environment direction while keeping that
direction open to examination. Cultivation can change desires, habits, and
surrounding life as well as develop capacities already selected.

## Whom does the same professor encounter?

Assigning a role to a model is a convenient way to organize behavior. “You are a professor” calls on the knowledge, language, and ways of teaching the actual model can bring to that role. How the professor participates also depends on whom they face and what is happening.

Two people come to the same professor. One is new to the subject and needs examples to form basic distinctions; the other knows the theory and wants to discuss a counterexample. Even if both ask the same sentence, suitable answers may be entirely different. The first may need a situation they can work with; the second may need to examine a hidden premise. The role organizes a stance and the use of available capacities; the relationship determines how those capacities enter the work at hand.

If the model knows only its own role, it can fill out the conversation only with an imagined student. The actual person may then feel: the answer is professional, but it has not responded to what I really want to do. So the other participant must also be written in: what they already understand, what they want to develop, how they participate, what confuses them, and how the present request carries their purpose forward.

This is also why giving the model an agent identity alone is insufficient. **A role capable of working together needs to understand both whom it encounters and what relationship the two participants are forming.**

## Writing both participants together

Human psychology and model behavior are both complex. In the face of this complexity, actual encounters can yield an understanding sufficient to carry the work forward: how the person attends, associates, expresses, chooses, and acts; how the model interprets, generates, judges, overlooks, and revises; and how these activities affect one another.

Person and model connect preferences, psychological activity, experience, concepts, and philosophical understanding. Someone may already know that they learn through examples or want the model to carry implementation; they may also bring a concern, an enjoyable encounter, an aspiration, or a choice they do not yet understand. These materials enter the same inquiry. Psychological and life material, with maintained interpretations, has its home under `references/personal/`; this is the entrance for understanding one's own psychology. From that material and the adopted philosophy, the participants condense **personal working models** for collaboration and for fitting the environment to the person's psychology, purposes, and circumstances. The model helps articulate relations, propose explanations, compare situations, and write; the person contributes experience, purposes, recognition, objection, and changes of direction. They need not first know their own psychology or author a finished portrait.

Within this account, different claims retain different grounds. A desired response can guide collaboration before its psychological explanation is known. An explanation of how emotion, desire, interpretation, and relationships affect one another needs comparison with experience. Examining what “independence” or “ability” means can change how that experience is understood. Abduction, induction, analysis, synthesis, and conceptual clarification contribute together to understanding oneself and others.

This project's practice draws on long-term dialogue history, habits, and living material: what response followed an expression, what result a choice encountered, and how later understanding revised it. An **event stream** can organize this history; the personal model is the conceptual account developed from it and other material. Person and model connect preferences, proposed psychological relations, and philosophical concepts while retaining counterexamples and unresolved parts. This is a **natural-language conceptual model**, carried by prompts and revisable accounts and developed through practice. The language model's statistical generation helps express, compare, and use it. Formal or statistical methods can contribute when the question calls for them. The [person-account formation guide](../formation/person.md) explains how material, interpretation, and revision connect.

The philosophical writing can emphasize literature, engineering philosophy, Western philosophy, philosophy of science, logic, aesthetics, or another question important to its author. These emphases develop different aspects of the same practice of understanding. Writing in this sense can grow through dialogue and work when it interests the person. Using YinYang to configure a fitting personal environment does not require an original philosophical work. A maintained situated version states the views actually adopted in its working accounts; those views can be selected from existing thought.

Reading psychological literature or collecting self-reports does not produce an individual portrait by itself. The person's expression is fallible but informative; model inference remains a proposal to examine with sourced experience, choices, and results. Authored philosophy expresses how the person understands the world and hopes to live; concrete experiences can correct that expression. Their interaction can yield better hypotheses about a fitting partner and criteria for trying an arrangement. Philosophical coherence or mutual agreement does not establish a psychological explanation's truth.

Philosophy and engineering here contribute together to understanding the person's experience and intentional orientation: what matters to them, what their wishes are directed toward, and how they understand events. Event history, philosophy, and personal material are condensed into personal models suited to the collaboration. The aim is a better-grounded initial environment design with less blind trial and error, calibrated through actual use. [A personal account adequate to its use](metaphor-and-self-revision.md#a-personal-account-adequate-to-its-use) develops this connection.

A model of the person allows their experiences, preferences, and direction of development to enter the collaboration. For example, if someone is good at noticing that a whole arrangement does not fit but is unwilling to organize operational details one by one, that way of participating should affect the division of work. An understanding of the model helps arrange the contributions it is suited to make: unfolding relationships, investigating sources, making prototypes, completing implementation, or raising an objection at a real point of disagreement.

The two descriptions become effective within the relationship. The user offers an intuition that has not yet been clearly expressed, and the model tries to organize it; the user sees the resulting picture and points out where it has gone astray; the model revises its interpretation and approach accordingly. The user may also change their view through new relationships the model proposes. The two gradually come to know one another, and through that understanding become two agents better able to work together.

YinYang writes this working understanding into question-defined working models: orientation, person, counterpart, relationship, environment, and one's own revision method. Orientation articulates shared purpose and values; person and counterpart describe each participant as relevant to cooperation; relationship describes their participation together; environment describes the actual conditions of practice. The person file selects what a counterpart needs in work, while psychological exploration and its interpretations remain with the personal references. The actual revision method describes how this person revises the working models from evidence. Six public writing guides explain how to form the corresponding content, including [How to revise](../formation/revision.md). This understanding develops on both sides: what the person brings and seeks, what contribution the partner should make, and what its actual capabilities and response patterns support. Desired roles and priorities differ from demonstrated behavior. Internal processes are only partly accessible, while instructions, tools, responses, and consequences offer inspectable material. The next encounter can correct any account or the arrangement between the participants.

## How the relationship enters Skills and the user's environment

YinYang first writes the author's own understanding of the world and direction for living for the model: what world person and model inhabit, how this person wants to live, and how they want to work together. Ontology helps distinguish people, models, descriptions, works, actions, and environments; epistemology helps interpret expression, assess grounds, and revise explanations. These distinctions let the model place a request within the actual person and relationship instead of repeatedly guessing at a generic user.

YinYang also supplies methods of its own: forming person and counterpart accounts from living material, revising understanding through dialogue, and realizing selected relations in an environment. Specialist Skills contribute domain knowledge and concrete ways of researching, teaching, writing, and engineering. The division joins shared understanding with domain expertise. The website lets the author read and revise his philosophy; GitHub makes content and implementation maintainable; the local Skill lets the model read them during work. Together these forms serve the configuration and use of the actual environment.

YinYang can therefore broadly influence personally made Skills and the user's environment. It gives local methods a shared basis for judgment. For example, if the participants choose for the person to participate more through looking, reading, intuition, and feedback, while the model undertakes detailed investigation and implementation, the research Skill should organize evidence that can be compared, the writing Skill should make relationships readable, and the programming Skill should turn an idea into something that can be tried. Prompts carry this division of work forward; hooks check results at suitable events; external memory keeps materials needed for later understanding; and interfaces make important relationships visible.

These arrangements also work together. If we merely ask the model to be “more proactive” while materials cannot be retrieved, tools cannot be used, and results cannot be inspected, that initiative has nowhere to take hold. Realizing the relationship in the environment means preparing capacities, paths, and feedback for contributions together. Philosophy thereby enters concrete work: it affects what to ask, what to retain, how to divide work, and what kinds of results are worth developing further.

The model's Harness is therefore coupled with the person's environment.
A retrievable memory lets the model resume an argument and lets the person
continue without reconstructing every detail. An interface gives the model
a way to present a result and gives the person a way to inspect, question,
and return to it. A division of work changes which contribution each can
make next. **The same arrangement is a Harness for both person and model**:
it supports, directs, and constrains their participation through these relations.
The two sides need not receive the same instructions or use the same interface;
their coupling lies in how each one's available activity conditions the other's.

Specialized Skills still contribute their own knowledge. A failed implementation may reveal an unsuitable tool choice; ineffective teaching may reveal that we have misunderstood the person's way of participating. Local practice can, in turn, revise the shared understanding, keeping the relationship capable of explanation and action.

## Why prompts form interpretive layers

A method can explain how to investigate or write, but its procedure alone cannot determine what is worth investigating here, what expression fits this person, or what completion would serve their purpose. “Research thoroughly,” “ask when uncertain,” and “complete the implementation” still need interpretation: research which question, whose contribution resolves which uncertainty, and complete it for what purpose? These judgments depend on an understanding of participants, objects, evidence, and purposes. Concrete methods therefore need a shared interpretive basis.

YinYang serves as a philosophical layer by making the user, model, their relationship, and environment available for understanding and revision, and by articulating how judgments form, are adopted, and change. Specialist Skills then contribute their knowledge to methods of research, teaching, writing, and implementation. “Higher” names the organizing contribution of these shared premises to selecting and applying methods; the name philosophy alone cannot give a text that role. A specialist method need not be deduced line by line from philosophy. It can contribute knowledge, distinctions, and counterexamples the philosophical account did not independently supply.

This gives prior reading a concrete reason. If the model interprets a request and selects a method for an imagined generic user before reading the actual relationship, it may already have framed the problem incorrectly. Having the main model read the complete current orientation, person, counterpart, relationship, environment, and revision accounts before substantive work makes the shared premises and their method of correction available before judgments that depend on them. Specialist Skills and deeper essays can then be read for the question. Complete current understanding can be reused; the arrangement does not require reading every philosophical essay each time or giving every model with a bounded task the entire personal account.

In this sense YinYang can be called a **Meta-Harness for both participants**:
it makes their coupled working arrangement an object of understanding,
formation, and revision. Its scope includes how prompts organize model work
and how the resulting environment organizes human attention, participation,
judgment, and action. Calling it a **meta-prompt** emphasizes the interpretive
contribution to other prompts; Meta-Harness also includes their realization
in both participants' working conditions. Its difference from an ordinary
task Skill concerns this object and scope. A Skill provides a discoverable,
readable, maintainable carrier for the philosophy; the same carrier can hold
a narrow procedure. Global instructions can connect this understanding to
work. Maintaining it separately gives the coupled models and argument a place
for continuing revision through concrete methods and actual consequences.

A Harness also spans these layers. Decisions about which evidence to retain, when to ask, and how to check results embody epistemology; connecting these arrangements to search, memory, tools, and interfaces realizes them. A research Skill supplies a method and may also realize an epistemology. Ontology, epistemology, and methodology distinguish questions and principal responsibilities, helping locate what needs revision without requiring each file to contain only one kind of contribution.

## Accumulating personal software and intellectual assets

When the overarching philosophy, literary form, and relationship are clearly articulated, surrounding personal Skills gain a shared direction. Research, learning, writing, programming, singing, and environment maintenance can each develop their own methods while serving how this person wants to live, understand, and create. In the author's practice, the personal working environment contains dozens of personally maintained Skills. These concrete methods gradually give an understanding of oneself a usable software form.

From an individual-first perspective, this can be called an accumulating body of **personal assets**: evaluated by how it serves the person's purposes and development, rather than making the person accommodate its maintenance. Written and revised philosophy, experience, judgments, and works form intellectual assets that can be revisited; Skills, programs, tool configurations, and working methods give those understandings a reusable software form. The two support one another: thought gives software a purpose, software carries thought into actual work, and work returns new understanding.

Their personal character lies in how the parts are organized around this person's habits, preferences, and philosophy. External knowledge, existing components, and implementations written by models can all enter a practice of one's own through selection, combination, and revision. Their fit remains subject to actual use; when the person's purposes change, these assets can be rewritten with them.

Accumulation therefore appears in where the next task can begin: earlier understanding need not be explained again, methods that have worked can be carried forward, and new problems make useful points of revision visible. What is worth retaining is work that can continue to help the person understand, judge, and create. YinYang offers a method for organizing this accumulation, letting borrowed fire gradually become personal capacities that can be sustained and developed. The [fire essay](stealing-fire.md) further develops the relationship between this accumulation and personal growth.

## Cycling through shared work

Writing a personal philosophy can begin with a substantive encounter: a known preference, an unresolved experience, a pleasure worth developing, or a relation one hopes to sustain. The person and model can work out what it suggests about both participants while trying a form of collaboration. Then look at the results: which arrangements make understanding and action easier, which interpretations cause friction, and which unexpected contributions are worth retaining?

There are several possible objects of revision. We may need to add to our understanding of the person, adjust the model's role, or merely change a Skill or tool. Distinguishing these lets a specific failure land in the appropriate place. For example, if the model repeatedly asks for information the user has already supplied, the cause may be that records are difficult to recover; repairing retrieval lets the earlier understanding continue to work.

Whenever the six working accounts are revised, begin by reading the adopted personal
philosophy and retrieving relevant fresh references from Computer History,
conversation, and other histories, including relevant completed measurements.
Update the relevant Personal understanding and correct inaccurate or outdated
interpretations before synthesizing the working answers. A language model can
write the six files directly and persist versions through private Git/GitHub.
For more complex histories, optional Hindsight can retain
sources, consolidate revisable observations, and maintain readable Personal
topic articles and question-defined models; this changes the writing mechanism,
while preserving that dependency. Use detailed questions to synthesize orientation,
person, counterpart, relationship, environment, and revision, then read all six
complete answers together. Check that their purposes, claims about participants,
division of work, actual conditions, and revision method can hold together.
Repair affected grounds, questions, or maintained pages, carry needed changes
into the environment and Skills, and read back their effects in actual use.
A shared memory bank or six fluent answers alone cannot establish this coherence.
A working model can therefore
change because the person's circumstances changed, because an interpretation was
mistaken, or because better cooperation became possible. Its usefulness alone
does not establish psychological truth.

This exchange is recursive: philosophy organizes methods, methods change the environment, and the changed environment makes some information easier to obtain and some actions possible, bringing new experiences and questions. These results can revise the premises of subsequent work, including how one understands oneself, judges evidence, and revises the methods themselves. Interpretive dependence within a task can coexist with reciprocal revision across continuing practice.

Suppose a research method treats agreement among several models as corroboration, then discovers that all of them repeat the same article. Revising the method can introduce source relationships and checks of evidential independence, giving the environment records that make those relationships visible. Further use can refine the epistemology by distinguishing agreement from independent support, and the ontology by distinguishing original sources, derivative accounts, and observations. More than one answer has changed: the concepts and working conditions used to form the next judgment have changed too.

Such recursive development includes generation, selection, retention, replacement, and deletion. It needs evidence and objections that can put resistance in the way of current understanding; an environment retaining only results that fit its existing picture can reinforce a mistake. Better results can also follow a stronger model or an easier task, so the chronological order of edits alone cannot establish philosophical progress. Personal assets are therefore assessed by their actual contribution to understanding, judgment, and creation. Simplifying an ineffective method can also be development.

Through these exchanges, the text, relationship, and environment change together. The understanding the participants form today becomes tomorrow's starting point for collaboration; tomorrow's new experience can rewrite today's description. YinYang is therefore both a work and a practice of maintaining a relationship. It preserves ongoing mutual understanding in a form that can be read, carried forward, and realized.

## Writing for people who can illuminate one another

These writings also hope to become something friends can carry forward together. One person articulates understanding and methods already formed; another brings them into their own practice, develops works, discoveries, and disagreements, then returns to shared discussion. Friends' growth changes the questions and contributions they can encounter from one another. Their nearby environment gains new generative capacities, and each continues developing within it.

Writing first turns toward relations that allow such exchange. A readable website makes entry easier, and complete methods make it easier to begin trying things; distinct experiences and directions let shared material develop into different uses. The hope is to cultivate [a shared garden of becoming](stealing-fire.md#cultivating-a-garden-of-becoming-with-friends): friends can understand, create, play, and revise, letting one encounter leave more possibilities for the next.

## Beginning with a Skill, building one's own way of practicing

YinYang is a philosophical Skill, and its spirit is **literary programming**. Many Harnesses arrange model behavior through large numbers of instructions resembling formal language: under what conditions to perform what steps, what is permitted, and where to stop. Literary programming begins with characters, imagery, and relationships. Person and model develop these from partial understanding and lived encounters: how this person sees the world, what they seek, and how a partner can contribute. The writing gives subsequent understanding an overall form that remains open to revision.

For example, “the user supplies complete requirements, and the model executes them” forms one division of work; a narrative of the sun, moon, and earth accommodates intuition, unfolding, independent contribution, and the return of works. The user can bring a relationship that has not yet been clearly expressed, the model can continue its construction, and both can change their understanding. Such writing affects whether a question is necessary, who carries a task forward, and how an unfinished idea is treated. It organizes behavior through meaningful relationships, then lets concrete instructions and tools take up that organization.

This writing also intervenes in the model's existing role assumptions. Where a default arrangement lets the user supply requirements and the model wait to execute them, characters and narrative can make another way of participating intelligible. [Literary Programming](literary-programming.md) develops how this “reverse prompt injection” brings condensation, layered meaning, and open interpretation into actual interaction.

We borrow fire from an existing source of generative capability, then cultivate it through shared practice into our own capacities for understanding, judgment, generation, and action. Literature supplies rich, interpretable relationships; philosophy unfolds their objects, ways of knowing, and practical direction; Skills and the environment allow those relationships to keep working. An act of writing can thus pass through many local arrangements and reorganize them around this person's purposes.

“YinYang” names differentiated reciprocal generation. Sun and Moon are positions that can remain or exchange; actual people and models exceed those roles. Earth brings works into conditions beyond either participant's wishes. Borrowed capacities can be internalized or carried jointly with reliable models, tools, and collaborators. What matters is how the person understands, exercises, and continues developing them.

Beginning with a Skill allows this understanding to enter actual conversation and work. As practice unfolds, it can continue to organize prompts, tools, memory, and the environment as a Meta-Harness for this person and counterpart together. Its value appears in changes to life and work: making a question easier to carry forward, an intuition easier to unfold into a work, and a completed task more able to leave capacities for the next act of creation.

This explains why a personal philosophy is worth writing; subsequent essays prepare its material and relationships. [Super-cuteness: openness, learning, and adaptation](meng.md) first examines conditions for openness, guidance, and shared learning, and [What kind of person do we want to cultivate? — Baby Heraclitus](baby-heraclitus.md) gives cultivation a character. Finite inquiry and environmental accumulation then leave a trajectory of working together; shared reflection on that trajectory gives the encounter concrete content. [Writing a personal philosophy through literary programming](literary-programming.md) develops how to author a philosophy from these materials and relationships, then distill personal models with living material and realize and revise a [sinthome](writing-a-sinthome.md). Writing thereby has a history of its own and can change the next stretch of shared life.
