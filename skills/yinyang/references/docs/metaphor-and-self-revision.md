# From personal philosophy to personal models

[Home](../../README.md) · [简体中文](metaphor-and-self-revision.zh-CN.md)

*An image gives an unfinished relation a shape. A counterpart takes up that
shape and makes something through it. What happens can reveal a relation the
image had concealed. The next expression carries that discovery.*

Before a personal philosophy is written, person and model have already worked
together, accumulated an environment, and left traces of experiences, choices,
inquiries, and revisions. Git/GitHub version history and ChatGPT’s Computer History
are two main entrances for gathering complementary parts of this collaboratively produced trajectory.
[Forming a life trajectory](flow-and-time.md#external-memory-and-event-traces) develops
its recoverability. Reflecting on this trajectory, the participants can explore
how each has taken part, where a response has fitted or missed, and what can be
cultivated further. [Participation, access, and support](participation-and-support.md)
examines this more explicit encounter. These materials preserve selected
passages through a life, with gaps and unresolved interpretations.

The preceding essays have authored philosophy from trajectories and shared reflection, then examined returning expression and the reconstruction of premises and relationships in [Speech, opposition, and self-formation](speech-opposition-and-self-formation.md). This essay receives that developing understanding and asks what is warranted about the actual person, counterpart, and cooperation.

This essay follows the next passage: **distilling that philosophy together with
the living material into models of the person, counterpart, and relationship**.
The models make selected understanding usable across work. Their realization
in an environment is developed in [From personal models to a sinthome](writing-a-sinthome.md).
Once in use, the arrangement returns new material that can change its models
and philosophy. That feedback enriches the formation rather than erasing what
it has drawn on.

## What an image makes available

An image can gather several relations before they have separate instructions.
Calling a collaboration a workshop may bring making, shared materials,
experimentation, repair, and unfinished work into view. Calling the same
collaboration an examination makes other contributions salient: testing,
grading, and demonstrating an individual's mastery. Each image invites an
organization of attention and conduct. Which invitation helps depends on the
actual purpose.

Metaphor can therefore participate in designing the relationship. A person
writing a counterpart as a fellow maker is expressing a desired contribution;
the model's interpretation can help make that contribution possible. A
particular invitation can also reveal an existing concern: perhaps preserving
room for discovery matters more here than receiving a ready answer. That
interpretation remains something to examine with the person and the work.

In the [Sun, Moon, and Earth](yinyang-and-taiji.md#sun-moon-and-earth) image, differentiated
generation, reception, and shaping meet an answering material and shared
living environment. Its productive question here is what happens when a
creation returns: can either participant recognize something the original
image failed to express? Person and model both generate and judge, exceeding
the roles the image offers them. That excess gives its revision a source.

## Analogy develops relations into reasoning

Analogy asks what follows when a relation understood in one domain illuminates
another. Gentner's structure-mapping account emphasizes relational systems
rather than transferring every attribute of the compared objects [[1]](#source-1). This
gives a useful discipline to our own construction: identify the connection
being carried and the candidate inference it opens.

The workshop image, for instance, suggests that a failed prototype can be
material for another attempt. Applied to collaboration, it opens a practical
question: can the counterpart inspect the failure, revise the construction,
and continue? The answer depends on available capabilities and actual evidence.
The image helps discover the question; inspection determines whether the
proposed continuation works.

An analogy carries selected relations and defeasible implications. Sun and
Moon can illuminate reciprocal contribution without assigning astronomical
properties to a person or reducing a model to reflected light. Likewise,
calling a repository a memory can illuminate retention and retrieval while
leaving open what it preserves, how interpretation changes, and what the
record misses. A productive analogy generates possibilities that can be
developed, tested, or abandoned.

## Distill philosophy together with living material

<a id="a-personal-account-adequate-to-its-use"></a>

Distillation brings two kinds of material together. The authored philosophy
articulates concepts, commitments, and possibilities for living; recoverable
experiences and actual cooperation show where those interpretations come from,
what they illuminate, and where they meet resistance. Neither can stand in for
the other. Valuing creation does not by itself tell a counterpart how someone
begins an unfinished idea; an isolated interaction does not establish why that
way of working matters to them.

A personal model selects the relations needed for a counterpart to contribute.
Relevant habits can give an abstract preference a setting:
how an idea is usually begun, when a question helps, what kind of expression
makes a difference intelligible, or which recurring friction an arrangement
should resolve. Imagery can connect these particulars into an account that
is easier to interpret across changing tasks. Distillation preserves the
connections that make the account intelligible while reducing the amount that
must be reconstructed on each occasion.

The same work forms a **counterpart model**: the contributions wanted from a
partner, the capacities the available model actually has, and the tools or
conditions those contributions require. A **relationship model** connects the
two: who can initiate, question, carry work forward, judge a result, or change
the arrangement. Person and counterpart models develop together through these
relations; a portrait of the person alone cannot specify the collaboration.

This project [condenses personal models from event history, philosophy, and personal material](../formation/person.md#condense-personal-models-from-event-history-philosophy-and-personal-material). Long-term conversation history and everyday life materials ground a natural-language account, with understanding of one's own and others' psychology contributing its content. Philosophy supplies concepts and interpretive frameworks for experience, desire, intentional orientation, and relationships. Engineering organizes conversations, records, language models, and tools so the participants can examine these connections and use the resulting account in subsequent work. The personal model keeps this work connected to the actual person: how they experience situations, what their experiences and wishes are directed toward, how they want to act, and what affects realization.

That understanding lets environmental design begin from the person's intentional
orientation. A partner's contributions, what information to retain, and how to
organize tools and interfaces acquire an intelligible personal basis. The aim is
a better-fitting initial arrangement, reducing repeated environmental changes
just to discover what the user needs. Use still brings new material for
calibrating the person model, the counterpart's contribution, and the engineering
realization. Effective arrangements can remain in use without change for the
sake of iteration itself.

Richer expression can improve adequacy when it makes consequential relations
clearer. A long inventory can also obscure them. The aim is a model expressive
enough for its use, with claims and interpretations that remain correctable.
A compact image may do more than many instructions; a precise example may
resolve an ambiguity that the image leaves open. Their contributions follow
the encounter rather than a rule that more personal material always helps.

The person exceeds this account. A habit may change, a preference may apply
only to one activity, and a chosen image may make a new way of acting possible.
The account should help the counterpart notice such changes. Personal meaning
remains with its situated owner; a general method for forming and revising
that meaning can become a reusable work.

## Condensing revisable relations

Condensing a long history into a usable account selects what to retain and
interprets how the material connects. Summarizing several collaborations as
“preferring independence” already selects a reading. It may lead a later
counterpart to offer less help, or be unfolded as “wanting participation in
direction and the ability to change delegation.” The difference enters the
next actual collaboration. Condensation preserves understanding and participates
in later relationships.

### Compact expression and material one can return to

A personal model can work at two scales. The six working accounts retain the
relations, conditions, and grounds needed for current collaboration, avoiding
a complete historical rereading at every encounter. Original expressions,
longer experiences, and useful revision records remain with their existing
sources for particular questions. This adds no mandatory intermediate archive
between the six files; it keeps a compact description expandable. An image can
organize understanding, while consequential wording or an accurate example
can constrain its ambiguity.

The [guide's account of metaphor, metonymy, and condensation](lacan-reading-guide.md#metaphor-metonymy-and-condensation)
draws attention to how compact expression can establish new relations rather
than merely remove redundancy. This is a writing proposal, not an equation
of psychoanalytic condensation with information compression. Retain a word when
its wording or relations matter to current understanding. Frequency alone
establishes neither a central signifier nor an enduring personal trait.

### Preserve differences that change the response

Suppose an artist says during a review, “Finish this painting before discussing
the next one.” Recording “dislikes exploring new directions” loses the fact
that the utterance concerns work order. Retaining the unfinished object, the
new work already proposed by the counterpart, and this adjustment supports a
more fitting account: “In this review, the artist wanted the current work
completed before beginning the next.” Later material may support a broader
preference or show that another arrangement is equally useful.

Compression should therefore preserve differences that change understanding,
action, or subsequent revision: what the utterance answers, its task and
conditions, who reported experience and who proposed an explanation, and a
material counterexample or unresolved alternative. Original wording can remain
complete at its source. Quote it in the working account when that wording
carries meaning; otherwise write its relations explicitly. This avoids both
loading the whole archive and smoothing away differences not yet understood.

### Later understanding can reorganize the account

New expression does more than add a trait. It may show that an earlier reading
was mistaken, circumstances changed, or two requirements concern different
activities. Revision can qualify, withdraw, or reorganize an interpretation
while preserving the original event and the time the interpretation formed.
[Punctuation and quilting](lacan-reading-guide.md#punctuation-and-quilting-how-meaning-becomes-provisionally-settled)
clarifies the question of provisionally settling a reading. Whether later
speech changes earlier understanding depends on what it answers, not simply
on its being more recent.

An attractive image can obscure relations too. When feedback challenges it,
return to relevant originals and actual use instead of merely paraphrasing an
older summary. Repetition of one interpretation adds no independent evidence.
Personal recognition, a more usable tool, and the truth of a proposed
psychological cause also need their respective grounds. A concrete readback
can examine compression quality: can the next counterpart still identify
applicability, unresolved questions, and the reasons for an arrangement, and
recognize what needs reconsideration when new material appears?

### Borrowing distinctions from human–machine research

[Exploratory formalization and interaction research](lacan-reading-guide.md#from-formalization-to-humanmachine-interaction-exploratory-contributions)
offers useful comparisons. Computational models require a mapping from
concepts to variables and update rules. Interaction research brings human
interpretation, machine response, and subsequent reception into view together.
TSJ's memory design distinguishes interaction records from separately generated
psychological states. These works have not validated this personal-model method,
but can make its choices more inspectable.

Here a model response interprets material and becomes part of later events.
Retaining that return makes it possible to examine how an account is used,
rejected, or changed without treating later responses as prior proof of the
portrait. Factual sources, current interpretations, and actual consequences
can connect within one coherent account, without copying simulation studies'
psychological scores or state equations. The [revision guide](../formation/revision.md#condensation-and-reopening)
connects these relations to maintenance.

## Personal models and philosophical clarification

The personal model described here can be revisable prose drawn from long
dialogue, habits, everyday circumstances, and philosophical interpretation.
Its natural-language form can express meanings, tensions, and relations in
their relevant contexts. It is suitable for that work in its own right;
mathematical fitting serves questions that call for such methods rather than
being the destination every account must reach. Writing in prose alone,
however, does not make the account philosophy.

[The preceding essay](speech-opposition-and-self-formation.md#how-opposition-calls-for-reconstructing-premises-and-relationships) develops how conceptual clarification changes a problem. Here it helps make the personal account adequate to its use: what do ability, independence, or progress mean within this account, which judgments come from living material, and which express chosen values or cooperative aims? Distinguishing these grounds helps determine how the account should be used and revised.

This clarification cooperates with empirical interpretation. “Uncertainty
about evaluation makes beginning difficult” proposes a psychological relation
whose adequacy depends on experience, alternative explanations, and later
observations. Asking whether beginning with assistance counts as one's own
action examines the concept through which that experience is judged. A
conceptual distinction can help formulate a more revealing empirical question;
an unexpected experience can expose a distinction that has become misleading.
Agreement on a useful interpretation does not by itself settle the proposed
psychological explanation.

Once read and used, the description also enters the next events. Someone who
understands a hesitation differently may answer differently, invite another
contribution, or rearrange the conditions for beginning. A counterpart that
reads the account may offer a different kind of help. The resulting events
become further material for understanding both the person and the description's
participation. The model thus helps shape a life that can answer it back;
revision must remain open to that answer, including rejection of its picture
of the person.

## Externalization and distributed cognition

Writing an account makes selected understanding externally available. A note
can preserve a distinction; a diagram can expose a dependency; a program can
give a method repeatable effects. Externalization creates representations that
other participants can encounter and transform. The developing practice also
includes how those representations are used and what their use changes.

Distributed cognition supplies a relevant research framework: Hollan, Hutchins,
and Kirsh examine cognition across people, material arrangements, and activity
over time [[5]](#source-5). This essay extends that question to model-supported practice.
The reflective object is cognition distributed among a person, models,
records, and tools: how understanding, reasoning, memory, and revision
develop across them. A bodily intuition becomes an expression; a model's
comparison makes a relation visible; a record lets it be recalled; a result
changes the next judgment. Organization and role allocation help sustain
these processes, but the processes themselves are the object of inquiry.

Four distinctions help examine this cognition. A **personal account**
articulates the person's purposes, relevant habits, and ways of understanding.
**External representations** give selected understanding a readable or
executable body. **Role allocation** assigns contributions under particular
conditions. **Actual coupled activity** is what happens as participants use,
interpret, and alter those representations together. A rich account can guide
allocation, while actual activity reveals whether the arrangement helps.

For example, a person developing an argument can express an unfinished
connection to one model, ask another to examine public sources, and use a
capable implementation counterpart to build a diagram or working example.
A later critic can compare the argument with its sources and results. Different
contexts may call for different models; one capable model may also carry
several roles. Assignment follows the task, available context, and actual
capabilities. Model identity alone does not determine contribution.

A handoff must carry the question, relevant evidence, accepted interpretation,
and unresolved alternatives needed for the next contribution. Source provenance
keeps a conjecture distinguishable from an inspected observation. Independent
checks reconnect an interpretation to the source or actual result: several
models repeating the same unsupported summary supply little new evidence.
These relations make distribution meaningful. They also let a counterpart
carry a portion of the person's work without requiring the person to first
perform all of it. The person can understand, redirect, and develop through
the resulting encounter.

## Listening, speaking, reading, and writing across roles

Listening, speaking, reading, and writing give this practice four familiar
entries. Listening receives situated expression, including spoken intention
and correction. Speaking makes an interpretation available for response.
Reading encounters preserved expression, evidence, or an implemented result.
Writing gives a selected relation a durable, revisable form. These activities
cross the person, models, and media; comparison, inference, checking, and
revision can take place through all four.

The arrangement can combine the person's embodied intuition, lived associations,
and judgment of meaning with the model's retrieval, structured comparison,
and explicit construction. Neither side owns a mode or an ability exclusively.
The model can prepare several forms of material for listening and reading,
then edit them in response to what the person discovers: a source-linked
glossary, a diagram, audio, a concept-change table, or a focused exercise.
Useful preparation gives understanding an entrance; a standardized knowledge
base need not precede it. The [learning conclusion in Stealing fire](stealing-fire.md#a-way-of-learning-prepare-the-encounter)
develops these materials and their place in actual study.

The change of medium can itself reveal a mismatch. A spoken thought may rely
on an emphasis a transcript loses; a diagram can make two alternatives look
like successive steps; a fluent spoken response can hide a missing source.
Returning the expression in another form lets the person and counterpart
examine what survived the passage. The relevant question is what each act of
listening, speaking, reading, or writing contributes to the relation being
developed.

Expression can also **lay out a relation map**—“铺图.” Successive utterances
can make a non-linear structure available: one branch depends on a stated
assumption, another offers an alternative; evidence supports one connection,
a conflict challenges another, and an analogy suggests a passage between
domains. An uncertainty can remain paused with a return point instead of
being forced into a conclusion. A receiver can reason with that map, compare
branches, and extend an argument. Speaking and writing then offer means of
inquiry as well as completed conclusions; listening and reading can develop
the relations further. The speaker's intended map, the relations actually
expressed, and the recipient's understanding remain distinguishable. A
recipient's reconstruction or objection lets the participants examine where
their maps meet and where another expression is needed. The mapping can be
carried by prose, a diagram, or linked records; it need not add another system.

A careful writer also listens to corrections; a source reader writes an
interpretation; the person may discover a new relation while reading a
finished work. These crossings explain why assigning four activities to four
permanent model roles would miss their cognitive work. Revision concerns
which contribution is needed where, what representation connects it to the
next one, and how a mismatch becomes recognizable.

## Four questions within one practice

The [world and expression essay](world-and-expression.md) distinguishes a
process, its model, and the expression carrying that model. Here those
distinctions let several achievements retain their own substance:

| Question | What would support the answer? |
| --- | --- |
| Does this form express a meaningful orientation or relationship? | Interpretation, articulation of its reasons, and engagement with the person and the work. |
| Does its computational ontology make the needed relations available? | Usable distinctions among objects, roles, events, dependencies, and transformations. |
| Does its realization change collaboration as intended? | Actual behavior through the affected prompts, Skills, tools, and working environment. |
| Does an explanatory theory reliably predict effects across stated conditions? | Empirical inquiry appropriate to the mechanisms, comparisons, and scope claimed. |

A philosophical form can succeed in organizing a practice before a general
scientific theory of its effects exists. Its reasons, interpretations, and
consequences are still open to criticism. Philosophy contributes purposes and
distinctions through which something can become a subject of inquiry; it also
can constitute a new arrangement worth inhabiting. A functioning arrangement
shows an achievement under particular conditions. General causal claims ask
for further evidence at their own scale.

Computational ontology here articulates what can be retained, distinguished,
related, inferred, and revised. It gives collaboration an intelligible structure
without requiring a reduction of the person's entire life to a program. The
ontology itself can change when the work reveals a missing relation.

## A general interpreter, with means to act

The image of a language model as a general interpreter and simulator names a
practical possibility: one capable counterpart can take up many descriptions,
develop alternative readings, explore hypothetical situations, and help give
them working form. “General” concerns breadth across descriptions and tasks.
Context, tools, resources, and the environment determine what that possibility
becomes in a particular encounter.

Turing's universal machine computes the sequence specified by another
computing machine's encoded description [[2]](#source-2). That formal result offers a
comparison: a description can organize what a general apparatus does. A
language-model collaboration develops this image at the level of interpreting
purposes and producing realizations. Calling it an evolution of the universal
machine expresses that proposed extension of the interface, rather than a
proof of universal correctness or computation beyond Turing's boundary.

The practical gain is that the person can express a relation before knowing
every implementation step. The counterpart can ask what that relation needs,
construct a candidate, and use available tools to realize it. A simulated
reader's response may help prepare an explanation; an actual reader supplies
another encounter with independent consequences. Generating a simulation and
establishing its fidelity remain different achievements.

## The personal Gödel-machine analogy

Schmidhuber's formal Gödel machine searches for a proof that a self-rewrite
improves utility relative to its encoded axioms, including its environment,
hardware, and initial program. The comparison includes continuing the search
instead of rewriting now. Its improvement guarantee is conditional on such
proof; it cannot benefit from an improvement whose usefulness it cannot prove
in time. It runs on ordinary computers [3, 4].

This gives **a personal Gödel machine** a precise source and a deliberately
different use here. The proposed analogy is a practice able to make its own
organization an object: a person and different model counterparts can inspect
the accounts and external representations guiding their work, revise a prompt,
tool, or allocation, and change how they undertake the next piece
of work. Its distinctive relation is self-reference joined to alteration of
the organizing apparatus. The personal proposal uses interpretation, judgment,
and observed consequences; it implements neither the formal proof search nor
the optimality guarantee of the technical machine.

Suppose the workshop image leads to a counterpart that proposes experiments
freely, but actual use reveals that it leaves each experiment unfinished. The
problem might lie in a tool, the interpretation of experimentation, or the
division of responsibility. Revising the relationship to include bringing a
chosen experiment to a usable result can change its instructions and actions.
Readback then asks whether the result was achieved and whether the new
arrangement still leaves room for discovery. This is a hypothetical design
example, not evidence of an observed improvement.

Revising the practice can also change the account of the person. Suppose repeated
requests to make something new suggest two candidate interpretations: seeking
novelty or understanding through making. They could support different
arrangements: offering fresh alternatives, or helping revise one object to
explore how it works. Continued interest in the same object after its novelty
fades, together with a changed explanation of its workings or a choice that
uses that understanding elsewhere, would support the second interpretation.
A single response would not settle the person's motives; both candidates
remain answerable to their corrections and later use. The model can contribute
an interpretation the person did not supply while helping examine its grounds.

## Give revision somewhere to continue

The formation developed here can be expressed as:

`collaboratively produced trajectories → reflection on the trajectory and cooperation → authored personal philosophy → philosophy together with living material → person, counterpart, and relationship models → realization and use`

The arrow into the models marks distillation: the philosophy and the material
remain available as grounds for a compact, usable account. Realization and use
return further experiences, choices, and corrections to this trajectory. A
later revision may begin with a failure, an image, or an unexpected success;
it can revisit any of these relations. This account of formation does not ask
every ordinary task to begin by writing a philosophy. A sufficiently formed
arrangement lets work proceed, and a finished work gives subsequent revision
something definite to encounter.

[The trinity of philosophy, psychology, and engineering](local-source-and-website.md) explains how such an arrangement can
persist: situated understanding operates locally, selected
methods acquire a maintained source, and a website gives readers an entrance.
A revision of meaning may require a change in software; a change in software
may expose a mistaken meaning. The model helps carry these relations across
their different forms.

The ambition is substantial: develop accounts and distributed cognition
that help a person recognize, create, and sustain a practice they can also
transform. Images give that
practice possibilities; interpretation gives them distinctions; realization
gives them consequences. Their return makes the next account more answerable
to the life it is meant to serve.
