# Writing recursive self-improvement through literature

[简体中文](literary-recursive-improvement.zh-CN.md)

A person borrows fire, makes works, and changes through creating. As practice continues, another work forms among those works: a description of how the person perceives, understands, generates, and acts. Personal philosophy gives it direction, personal models connect it to actual life, and a language model unfolds it into understanding, tools, and action. YinYang already works through this relationship: expression becomes an arrangement, the arrangement enters life, and consequences return to the description. **Recursive self-improvement takes this practice further: what improvement produces returns to the way improvement is produced.**

Jiang Zhang's [“The Mathematical Foundations of Ultimate Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI): The Second Recursion Theorem”](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/acjxKPG48KD3yfwB6Ssv5w) introduces reflective architecture through a body, a generative description, and an interpreter. The associated paper starts from Kleene's second recursion theorem to construct systems that access their own descriptions, perform bounded simulation, evaluate, and produce successor programs. A central feature of strong RSI is that the improvement mechanism can itself become an object of improvement [[1]](#source-1). This supplies YinYang's most concentrated technical comparison: how can a person use readable self-description to change how they continue understanding, learning, and creating?

## Body, description, and interpreter

The **body** is the organization actually participating in activity. Human senses, movements, habits, and abilities form its embodied center; computers, records, tools, and reliably available model support extend its range of action. Body has a distributed practical meaning here: investigate a capacity through the actual connections among the parts that sustain it. Person and model have different channels of perception and action. Human experience particularly brings situations, feelings, and meanings that the model has not acquired into their shared work.

The **description** is an understanding available for rereading and rewriting. Personal philosophy articulates relations among world, value, and development; a person model connects experience, abilities, preferences, and circumstances; accounts of counterpart, relationship, environment, and revision explain how the practice works. Together they form a generative blueprint: the model can use the expressed relations to construct proposals and actions that have yet to be written.

The **interpreter** centers on a language model, acquiring means of execution through actual tools and interfaces. Reading “a partner who helps develop the question,” the model can unfold that relationship into investigation, comparison, writing, making, and checking. The words thereby acquire a function. As support, the model belongs to the person's environment; when reading, unfolding, and realizing a description, it acts as interpreter. These positions identify the same participant's contributions in different relationships.

This gives the three columns of [philosophy, psychology, and engineering](local-source-and-website.md#trinity-and-sinthome) another technical connection: philosophy contributes generative description, psychology concerns the embodied participant's actual activity, and engineering organizes interpretation and realization. Together they form a practice available for reading, alteration, and continued use.

## Bringing the improvement method into the description

The paper uses $f_T$ for bounded simulation, $V$ to evaluate results, and $M$ to produce modifications, arranging them as $M\circ V\circ f_T$. The second recursion theorem supplies a self-referential construction for computable transformations. The paper's argument connecting reflective structure and complete directed self-modification operates within a finite, self-contained program model. Evaluation, modification methods, and their objectives give improvement its actual direction [[1]](#source-1).

For human practice, the following working schema expresses one return:

$$
A_t=I_t(D_t,B_t),\qquad E_t=\operatorname{Encounter}(B_t,A_t),\qquad D_{t+1}=R_t(D_t,E_t).
$$

$B_t$ names embodied participation and support conditions at that time, $D_t$ the shared description, and $I_t$ interpretation and realization with actual tools. The resulting activity $A_t$ encounters the world, leaving experiences and results $E_t$; the revision method $R_t$ changes the description accordingly. This is the essay's conceptual expression of practical relations, with each symbol receiving its content from concrete activity. The model can contribute simulation, evaluation, and modification, while actual use brings expectations into contact with consequences.

The reflective connection is that $D_t$ also describes $R_t$: how to select material, interpret, evaluate, and revise. Both participants can read that part of the description and bring back the consequences of this round of revision, forming the $R_{t+1}$ used next. **Improvement produces a more fitting way to improve, giving subsequent work a changed starting condition.** This gives “human strong RSI” a definite practical direction: developing a person-centered human–model community's capacity to improve its own capacities.

For example, after completing a design with a model, a person finds that comparing concrete versions makes judgment easier. They alter their cooperation: the model presents a usable whole earlier, the person identifies consequential differences, and they revise together. They then examine the comparison method itself: which versions reveal important tradeoffs, and which explanations help carry a judgment into the next design? This return improves both the work and the method of forming design judgment. Later works offer further occasions to examine how that method continues to operate.

## How condensation helps recursive improvement

Literature can gather scattered arrangements into a relationship that is perceptible, readable, and easy to reenter. “Borrowing fire” connects acquiring capabilities, cultivating oneself, and continuing to create; “Sun and Moon meeting” connects reception, shaping, and return. As a counterpart unfolds one relation, the others remain its context, giving new arrangements a shared direction.

The practical reason for condensation becomes clear here. Human attention and model context need organization, while continuing cooperation needs reconnection across time. Gathering related meanings into an expression both can understand can reduce repeated reconstruction of background and provide an influential place for revising the whole. Revising “how to be a partner” can affect questioning, writing, realization, and delivery together. The gain is **sustaining rich, fitting activity through a description that is easier to maintain**.

Short expressions, extended prose, and concrete materials perform connected work: the short expression recalls relations, the prose develops reasons, and experiences and works keep consequential differences visible. Subsequent use can assess the condensation: can the counterpart recover key relations, contribute appropriately in a new situation, and revise them through new results? Literature can also form previously unrecognized connections, giving experience a new organization. It helps retain understanding and participates in creating what can be unfolded next.

## Developing between changing interpreters and bodies

Language-model development gives the interpreter new capabilities. Personal descriptions and shared environments change with them: work once requiring itemized instructions may become suitable for an overall purpose and relationship, while new tools realize ideas previously expressed only in words. Reverse alignment becomes a concrete activity: the person develops ways of creating with generative capabilities, the model adjusts its contribution through the personal description, and both reorganize expression, judgment, and action.

External model upgrades supply capabilities; through selection, integration, use, and revision, the human–model community makes these into conditions for its own improvement. Model upgrades and the development of shared methods have different origins and can support one another in the same practice. The model, tools, and materials used in a particular comparison therefore belong among the conditions for understanding its result.

Bodies and words change at different rhythms. Descriptions and software can be rewritten quickly; habits, intuitions, and abilities develop through repeated activity, while reliable supports can sustain parts of a capacity. Long-term time steps here are actual continuations of reading, creating, using, and returning; the simulator's $T$ in the paper bounds the computational steps of one program simulation. Each timescale explains how change gets an opportunity: finite simulation supplies material for candidate modifications, and continuing life lets arrangements have consequences and gradually enter human activity.

## Who carries complexity, and what gives progress its content

The paper discusses mirroring between description and execution body, and the distribution of complexity between interpreter and description [[1]](#source-1). YinYang entrusts substantial knowledge and constructive capability to an available interpreter, while the body and environment retain their existing organization. Personal writing can consequently concentrate on relations useful for understanding, action, and revision. The website, GitHub, and local environment support reading, version maintenance, and actual use respectively, giving one description purposes in different places.

This choice centers maintenance on meaning and use: can the description elicit the needed contribution, can tools realize it, and how do results change shared understanding? An image can organize many details. When a consequential difference appears, the participants unfold the relevant part, supply an example, revise a relationship, or replace the image. This is how literary description becomes usable with an already capable interpreter.

“Better” receives its measure from [the purpose of borrowing fire](stealing-fire.md): what the person can subsequently notice, understand, generate, judge, initiate, and sustain, and how these capacities serve a life worth living. Completed works, reduced support costs, expanded participation, and improved methods of learning and revision provide different, connected kinds of progress. Understanding follows the particular result that occurs.

Writing one's philosophy through literature thereby becomes an operational practice of self-improvement. Literature writes relations that can unfold, philosophy develops their reasons, the language model participates in interpretation, evaluation, and realization, and embodied life gives them consequences. **People create works, and gradually create how they will be able to create next.**
