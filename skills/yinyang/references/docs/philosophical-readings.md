# Philosophical readings

[中文版](philosophical-readings.zh-CN.md) · [Psychological readings](psychological-readings.md) · [Engineering readings](engineering-readings.md)

These sources deepen the collection's images and arguments. They distinguish
the original philosophical positions from YinYang's contemporary constructions;
they do not establish a particular person's psychological mechanism. Read the
relevant source with its context when the interpretation bears on a conclusion.

## Heraclitus: fire, measure, and opposing tension

[B6](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Fragments_of_Heraclitus_(annotated)#Fragment_6)
concerns the daily-new Sun, [B30](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Fragments_of_Heraclitus_(annotated)#Fragment_30)
fire and measure, and [B51](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Fragments_of_Heraclitus_(annotated)#Fragment_51)
bow and lyre. [B2](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Fragments_of_Heraclitus_(annotated)#Fragment_2)
and [B50](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Fragments_of_Heraclitus_(annotated)#Fragment_50)
provide the wider context for logos beyond an individual speaker.
[Daniel W. Graham's scholarly account](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/heraclitus/),
especially sections 3–4, explains disputed readings of fire, unity, and opposition.

The [stealing-fire essay](stealing-fire.md) develops this lineage into interacting
event streams. It does not attribute a modern computational ontology or formal
calculus to Heraclitus. Fire monism is a historical interpretation, not a settled
physical foundation for this collection. [Paraconsistent Logic](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/logic-paraconsistent/)
explains non-explosive consequence and its difference from dialetheism: tolerating
inconsistent information need not declare contradictions true.

## YinYang and Taiji

[Zhou Dunyi's *Taijitu shuo*](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hans/太極圖說)
connects movement and stillness through mutual grounding and continuing
transformation. [*Xici*, upper](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/易傳/繫辭上)
connects change with ongoing generation; [*Xici*, lower](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/易傳/繫辭下)
offers the Sun and Moon producing illumination through their reciprocal movement.

[YinYang and Taiji](yinyang-and-taiji.md) quotes and develops these Zhou Dunyi
and *Xici* passages. [*Zhuangzi*, Tian Zifang](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hans/莊子/田子方)
connects interaction, harmony, and generation as additional reading.
Its fish-body/eye distinction is a contemporary interpretation of roles and
actual participants, not the sole historical meaning or an identification of
the familiar Shuang yu with Zhou's original diagram. The
[YinYang and Taiji essay](yinyang-and-taiji.md) places the Sun–Moon–Earth
relation within this movement: Sun and Moon express differentiated generation,
reception, and shaping. Person and model are actual participants whose
emphases can be retained or exchanged; Earth is the answering material and
shared living environment.

## Hegel: sublation, history, and spirit

[Science of Logic, “Being,” §§184–187](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/hegel/works/hl/hlbeing.htm)
develops cessation and preservation in sublation. [Query rewriting and sublation](preference-harness.md#query-rewriting-and-sublation)
uses this relation for a bounded comparison with query rewriting; a technical
equivalence does not prove a thesis about Hegel's whole dialectic.

The [Introduction to the Philosophy of History, part III](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/hegel/works/hi/introduction.htm)
connects spirit's self-knowledge with freedom and world history. The
[Philosophy of Spirit, §§553–564](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/hegel/works/sp/abspirit.htm)
locates absolute spirit relative to subjective and objective spirit and develops
art within that account. [World, process, and expression](world-and-expression.md)
keeps these categories distinct from generic stages of a feedback loop.

## Freud: negation and acceptance

Freud's [“Negation” (1925), pp. 1–3](https://lutecium.org/ftp/Freud/pdf/1925_negation.pdf)
distinguishes intellectual recognition and acceptance in an analytic account.
Its interpretive inversions do not license treating ordinary denial as admission.

## Lacan: enunciation, retroaction, and singular creation

### Language and the subject

Lacan's [Subversion du sujet et dialectique du désir](https://www.causefreudienne.org/textes-fondamentaux/subversion-du-sujet-et-dialectique-du-desir-dans-linconscient-freudien/)
develops enunciation, the Other, and retroaction in signifying relations.

The [1966-12-02 radio talk, p. 2](https://ecole-lacanienne.net/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/1966-12-02a.pdf)
states Lacan's account of the unconscious as the discourse of the Other and as
structured like a language. It is a source for his position, not independent
empirical verification. [Speaking, self-description, and signifying chains](lacan-and-description-logic.md)
distinguishes this theoretical account from its explanatory notation and formal
analogies. A particular reader is not identical to the Other, and unexpressed
bodily experience is not automatically the Real.

### Sinthome and singular practice

[Seminar XXIII, 17 February 1976](https://recherche-lacan.gnipl.fr/2013/07/26/lxxiii-le-sinthome-1975-1976-lecon-du-17-fevrier-1976/)
is the primary-text reference for **sinthome / 圣状**. The discussion connects
knot repair and the particularity of Joyce's art. [Writing a sinthome](writing-a-sinthome.md)
develops a new philosophical application to model-assisted practice; a linguistic
self-model does not thereby become a complete account of the subject or an
established clinical instance of Lacan's sinthome.

## Fire myths: Prometheus, Kuafu, and living together

Prometheus is a mythic figure; Hesiod and Plato supply different texts through
which this collection encounters him. Hesiod's [*Theogony*, lines 507–616](https://www.theoi.com/Text/HesiodTheogony.html)
gives fire theft and its carrier a narrative form. [*Shan Hai Jing*, Haiwai Beijing](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hans/山海經/海外北經)
supplies Kuafu's pursuit. [Stealing fire](stealing-fire.md) compares pursuing
overwhelming power with bringing usable capacities into a finite life.

Plato's [*Protagoras*, 320c–322d](https://classics.mit.edu/Plato/protagoras.html)
places the myth in Protagoras's account of civic learning: arts and fire are
followed by reverence and justice shared by all. Plato is the dialogue's
author, Protagoras a thinker represented within it, and Prometheus a character
in its myth. The fire essay takes their relation as a comparison between
technical means and purposes of living together. Its AI application is a
contemporary argument, rather than a claim about an ancient prediction.

## Deleuze and Guattari: connections, production, and becoming

The independently authored [Machines and becoming essay](https://boluopai.net/philosophy/deleuze-and-becoming/en/)
reads [*Anti-Oedipus*](https://files.libcom.org/files/Anti-Oedipus.pdf),
chapter 1 and chapter 4's first positive task of schizoanalysis, alongside
[*A Thousand Plateaus*](https://files.libcom.org/files/A%20Thousand%20Plateaus.pdf),
plateaus 4 and 6. Desiring-machines open the question of what connections
produce; assemblages bring bodies and expression into view. The essay
distinguishes a process of becoming from a clinical condition and treats
its songwriting and harness examples as contemporary comparisons.
This provides a neighboring question to the sinthome essay: alongside what
sustains a life, what can the sustained relations continue to generate?

## Personal practice: Bruner, Foucault, Hobbes, and Mill

Bruner's [*Life as Narrative*](https://ewasteschools.pbworks.com/f/Bruner_J_LifeAsNarrative.pdf)
and Foucault's [*Technologies of the Self*](https://foucault.info/documents/foucault.technologiesOfSelf.en/)
inform [Stealing fire](stealing-fire.md)'s connection between self-expression,
continuing practice, and change. Narrative can organize later understanding;
writing and relations with others can participate in self-transformation.
The model-supported extension belongs to this collection.

[Personal-first humanism](personal-first-humanism.md) also reads Hobbes's
[*Leviathan*, chapter IV](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Leviathan_%281651%29/Chapter_4)
on names and particular things, and Mill's [*On Liberty*, chapter III](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/34901/34901-h/34901-h.htm)
on individuality and experiments of living. These sources clarify the
questions of description and plural coexistence. The present personal-first
orientation remains a separately argued choice.

## Inquiry and frameworks: Peirce, Dewey, Carnap, and Feng Ye

[Reasoning](reasoning.md) uses Peirce's [1908 manuscript MS 841](https://fromthepage.com/jeffdown1/c-s-peirce-manuscripts/ms-841-1908-a-neglected-argument-text-and-fragments?page=9)
to distinguish explanatory hypotheses, experiential consequences, and assessment.
[Embodied experimental philosophy](embodied-experimental-philosophy.md) reads
Dewey's [*Reconstruction in Philosophy*, chapters IV–V](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/40089/40089-h/40089-h.htm):
experience includes acting and undergoing consequences. Together these routes
give inquiry room to propose, act, return, and revise.

Carnap's [*Empiricism, Semantics, and Ontology*](https://ditext.com/carnap/carnap.html)
distinguishes questions within a framework from its practical adoption.
[Finite inquiry and abstraction](finite-inquiry-and-abstraction.md) compares
this collection's question-relative account with Professor Feng Ye's
[*Studies in No-Self Physicalism*](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-981-19-8143-2),
especially §§1.1, 6.5, and 7.4.2. Their shared methodological questions about
finite understanding and representation coexist with different physical
commitments. A useful abstraction and a claim about ultimate reduction
therefore need different support.

## Mind, knowledge, and explanation

Clark and Chalmers's [*The Extended Mind*](https://www.consc.net/papers/extended.html)
informs [Stealing fire](stealing-fire.md)'s account of active environmental
supports. [Metaphor and self-revision](metaphor-and-self-revision.md) connects
that question with Hollan, Hutchins, and Kirsh's distributed-cognition
research: understanding can develop across people, models, records, and tools.

For psychological processes, empirical assessment, belief, and interaction, continue to [psychological readings](psychological-readings.md). Their theories and evidence investigate how knowing actually happens; philosophy continues to ask about explanation, grounds for knowledge, and the scope of mind.

Technical sources for the Gödel Machine and Darwin Gödel Machine appear in [engineering readings](engineering-readings.md#experimental-implementation-data-and-model-assessment), alongside implementation and evaluation resources. [Preference harness](preference-harness.md) retains its philosophical comparisons while preserving the assumptions and scope of formal repair, inference, and query transformation.

## Source scope

These links preserve the public intellectual sources and their conceptual
limits. Personal evidence and the history of adopting or revising an
interpretation remain in the situated package's source account. The reading
map does not replace reading the passages that matter to an actual argument.
