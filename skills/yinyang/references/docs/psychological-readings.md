# Psychological readings

[简体中文](psychological-readings.zh-CN.md) · [Philosophical readings](philosophical-readings.md) · [Engineering readings](engineering-readings.md)

This reading list connects process modeling, psychological conflict and change, belief, and interaction. For experience and cases, enter the middle section on [psychological conflict, relationships, and change](#psychological-conflict-relationships-and-change) and its more accessible books. Modeling and research methods remain available for deeper inquiry. The sections connect by question rather than forming a compulsory course sequence.

Links lead to authors, publishers, and public originals, with general-reader, professional, and research purposes distinguished. Catalogs and samples are not free full texts; recency and reputation alone do not establish that an explanation fits the present question.

## Psychological process modeling and empirical assessment

Begin with Guest–Martin and Borsboom and colleagues on making theories explicit, then use the research-methods textbook and Wilson–Collins to examine relations between models and evidence. For extended computational examples, turn to Farrell–Lewandowsky.

| Book or paper | Where to begin and what it contributes |
| --- | --- |
| Guest and Martin (2021), [How Computational Modeling Can Force Theory Building in Psychological Science](https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691620970585) | Relations among theory, formal specification, and program implementation. Identify assumptions introduced during modeling and how they can develop the theory in return. |
| Borsboom et al. (2021), [Theory Construction Methodology](https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691620969647) | A proposal connecting phenomena, explanation, formal models, and assessment. Its distinctions can support work in which formalization and empirical inquiry move back and forth. |
| Jhangiani et al., [Research Methods in Psychology, 4th edition](https://kpu.pressbooks.pub/psychmethods4e/) | Open textbook. Start with measurement, reliability and validity, experimental and nonexperimental designs, and single-subject research; distinguish observed changes from explanations of them. |
| Wilson and Collins (2019), [Ten Simple Rules for the Computational Modeling of Behavioral Data](https://elifesciences.org/articles/49547) | Open paper and code. Simulation, parameter recovery, model recovery, and prediction checks help establish whether data can distinguish candidate models. Good fit alone does not establish a mechanism. |
| Farrell and Lewandowsky, [Computational Modeling of Cognition and Behavior](https://assets.cambridge.org/97811071/09995/excerpt/9781107109995_excerpt.pdf) | Publisher's first-chapter excerpt; [author code](https://github.com/psy-farrell/computational-modelling) connects computational models, estimation, and model comparison. Begin by implementing a small model. |

Read here about why experimental designs and measurements can answer a question. For programming, recording, and reproducing an experiment, continue to [engineering readings](engineering-readings.md#experimental-implementation-data-and-model-assessment). The same experiment needs both contributions; successful execution and a supported explanation are different outcomes.

For more technical accounts of cognition, consult Marr's [Vision, chapter 1](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262514620/vision/) and [Probabilistic Models of Cognition](https://github.com/probmods/chapters). The former distinguishes computational task, representation/algorithm, and physical implementation; the latter connects generative models, conditioning, and inference to programs.

## Psychological conflict, relationships, and change

As environments and relationships change, earlier wishes, commitments, and habits can pull against new circumstances. These readings address ambivalence about change, competing needs, self-criticism and protection, love and hostility toward the same person, and knowing something without being able to act on it. These are different problems, not one mechanism, and not all imply disorder.

### More accessible books

| Author and book | Reading entrance |
| --- | --- |
| Russ Harris, [The Happiness Trap, second edition (2022)](https://www.shambhala.com/the-happiness-trap-second-edition.html) | A general-reader entrance to acceptance and commitment therapy (ACT): entanglement with thoughts and feelings, values, and action. More approachable than beginning with its theoretical manual. |
| Richard C. Schwartz, [No Bad Parts (2021)](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9781683646686/nobadparts/) | A general-reader entrance to Internal Family Systems (IFS), using parts and their protective roles to understand self-criticism and internal opposition. Read it as a therapeutic model, not an independently established inventory of the mind. |
| Jeffrey E. Young and Janet S. Klosko, [Reinventing Your Life](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/327709/reinventing-your-life-by-jeffrey-e-young/9780452272040/) | An accessible schema-oriented account of recurring life patterns, needs, and coping, illustrated through cases. Pair with the professional schema-therapy text below. |
| Karen Horney, [Our Inner Conflicts](https://wwnorton.co.uk/books/9780393309409-our-inner-conflicts) | A classic entrance to the tensions among moving toward, against, and away from others. Read its diagnostic vocabulary in its historical context. |

### Contemporary psychotherapy: understanding conflict and change

These approaches developed over recent decades and are represented by maintained texts; contemporary does not mean every book is newly published. In professional books, concepts, cases, and dialogue can be read before technical detail. Their evidence bases differ; inclusion does not imply equal effectiveness.

| Approach and representative book | Question to bring to the reading |
| --- | --- |
| ACT: Hayes, Strosahl, and Wilson, [Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, second edition](https://www.guilford.com/books/Acceptance-and-Commitment-Therapy/Hayes-Strosahl-Wilson/9781462528943); Harris, [ACT Made Simple, second edition](https://www.newharbinger.com/9781684033010/act-made-simple/) | Professional theory and a more accessible practice introduction. How can action follow values without first eliminating inner experiences? Focus on psychological flexibility, defusion, acceptance, and committed action. |
| DBT: Marsha M. Linehan, [DBT Skills Training Manual, revised edition (2025)](https://www.guilford.com/books/DBT-Skills-Training-Manual/Marsha-Linehan/9781462556359) | A professional skills-teaching manual. How can acceptance and change coexist? Examine mindfulness, interpersonal effectiveness, emotion regulation, and distress tolerance. The companion handouts are a separate volume; a catalog link is not delivery of all materials. |
| Schema therapy: Young, Klosko, and Weishaar, [Schema Therapy: A Practitioner's Guide](https://www.guilford.com/books/Schema-Therapy/Young-Klosko-Weishaar/9781593853723) | A professional text on needs, early schemas, coping, and modes in recurring internal and relational conflicts. Pair with the accessible Reinventing Your Life. |
| IFS: Schwartz and Martha Sweezy, [Internal Family Systems Therapy, second edition (2019)](https://www.guilford.com/books/Internal-Family-Systems-Therapy/Schwartz-Sweezy/9781462541461) | A professional text on opposition, relationships, and change among protective parts; chapter 11 specifically addresses protector polarizations. |
| Emotion-focused therapy: Leslie S. Greenberg, [Emotion-Focused Therapy: Coaching Clients to Work Through Their Feelings, second edition](https://www.apa.org/pubs/books/emotion-focused-therapy-second-edition) | A professional text on emotional functions and expression, self-criticism, unfinished relational experience, and emotional transformation. This entry refers to Greenberg's emotion-focused approach. |
| Motivational interviewing: William R. Miller and Stephen Rollnick, [Motivational Interviewing, fourth edition (2023)](https://www.guilford.com/books/Motivational-Interviewing/Miller-Rollnick/9781462552795) | For helping professions and practitioners. Follow ambivalence about changing and reasons to retain the present arrangement through engaging, focusing, evoking, and planning, without choosing another person's purposes for them. |
| Coherence therapy and memory reconsolidation: Bruce Ecker, Robin Ticic, and Laurel Hulley, [Unlocking the Emotional Brain, second edition (2024)](https://www.routledge.com/Unlocking-the-Emotional-Brain-Memory-Reconsolidation-and-the-Psychotherapy-of-Transformational-Change/Ecker-Hulley-Ticic/p/book/9781032139128) | Advanced theory and cases concerning conflict between explicit wishes for change and implicit emotional learning. Distinguish reconsolidation research, the authors' clinical interpretation, and evidence for particular methods. |
| Mentalizing: Carla Sharp and Dickon Bevington, [Mentalizing in Psychotherapy (2022)](https://www.guilford.com/books/Mentalizing-in-Psychotherapy/Sharp-Bevington/9781462549962) | A professional introduction with dialogues and cases. How do people understand their own and others' mental states under relational strain while keeping interpretations revisable? Inferring another mind is not certain knowledge of it. |

### Classic conflict theories and psychoanalysis

| Author and original work | Reading purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Freud, [Introductory Lectures, Lecture XXIII](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/38219/pg38219-images.html); [The Ego and the Id](https://web.english.upenn.edu/~cavitch/pdf-library/Freud_SE_Ego_Id_complete.pdf) | Historical theoretical accounts of compromise between opposing tendencies and pressures among identifications, reality, and the ego ideal. |
| Freud, [Mourning and Melancholia](https://web.english.upenn.edu/~cavitch/pdf-library/Freud_MourningAndMelancholia.pdf) | Examine how love, hostility, identification, and self-reproach are related in the explanation; the historical account does not apply automatically to every experience of grief. |
| Melanie Klein, [Notes on Some Schizoid Mechanisms (1946)](https://melanie-klein-trust.org.uk/resources/reading-list-paranoid-schizoid-position/) | The trust's original-work reading entrance. Examine splitting, projection, and different aspects of the same object alongside the earlier depressive-position account; different authors' splitting concepts are not interchangeable. |
| D. W. Winnicott, [Playing and Reality](https://www.routledge.com/Playing-and-Reality/Winnicott/p/book/9780415345460); [Ego Distortion in Terms of True and False Self](https://academic.oup.com/book/12894/chapter/164044466) | Transitional space between creation and encounter, and the relationships among compliance, spontaneity, and true/false self. Preserving meaningful tension differs from accepting arbitrary contradictory assertions. |
| Nancy McWilliams, [Psychoanalytic Diagnosis, second edition](https://www.guilford.com/books/Psychoanalytic-Diagnosis/Nancy-McWilliams/9781462543694) | A professional conceptual and case reference comparing personality, defenses, and relational organization. Classification does not automatically judge an unfamiliar person. |

For Freud, Lacan, and philosophical discussions of will and self-deception, see [philosophical readings](philosophical-readings.md). These books help articulate experience and relationships; reading professional texts and practicing professional therapy are different activities.

## Belief, reasoning, and interaction research

This part connects how explanations should be updated with how participants actually change in interaction. Logic describes relations under rules; psychological research investigates actual processes. Each can inform the other without replacing its evidence.

| Paper or reference | Reading purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [Logic of Belief Revision](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/logic-belief-revision/) | Use AGM and related frameworks to distinguish expansion, contraction, and revision, while keeping formal rationality distinct from a person's actual changes. |
| [Dynamic Epistemic Logic](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/dynamic-epistemic/) | Compare information events, knowledge and belief states, and updates; public announcements do not represent every actual interaction. |
| De Jaegher and Di Paolo (2007), [Participatory Sense-Making](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11097-007-9076-9) | Include interaction, coordination, and sense-making in an explanation; ask what separate descriptions of participants' internal states might omit. |
| Fonagy and Allison (2014), [The Role of Mentalizing and Epistemic Trust in the Therapeutic Relationship](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1428567/) | Institutional manuscript entrance. Connect understanding mental states, epistemic trust, and social learning; this is a particular theoretical argument, not proof that every interaction is effective. |

### Research on conflict, fragmentation, and dynamic revision

| Paper | Further question |
| --- | --- |
| Elga and Rayo, [Fragmentation and Information Access](https://www.princeton.edu/~adame/papers/fragment/fragmentation-and-information-access-2019-10-29.pdf) | A 2019 author manuscript of the 2021 chapter. How does possessing information differ from accessing it now, and how are beliefs organized across contexts? |
| Grodniewicz (2024), [Belief Revision in Psychotherapy](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-024-04523-0) | A philosophical paper on why a new understanding in one setting may not generalize to another; not an efficacy trial. |
| Mansell and Huddy (2020), [Computational Models of Psychological Change and Recovery](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyt.2020.00624) | Theory and simulations: how can conflict and reorganization be represented when goals impose incompatible references on the same controlled variable? |
| Kronemyer and Bystritsky (2014), [A Non-Linear Dynamical Approach to Belief Revision in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy](https://doi.org/10.3389/fncom.2014.00055) | A theoretical proposal connecting dynamics with belief revision. Compare explanations of change without treating it as an established universal therapeutic mechanism. |

These resources make psychological modeling a learnable practice: propose explanations, specify relations, seek discriminating material, and revise through interaction and outcomes. They connect with philosophical understanding and engineering realization in [Trinity](local-source-and-website.md).
