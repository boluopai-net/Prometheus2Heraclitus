# From personal models to a sinthome

[简体中文](writing-a-sinthome.zh-CN.md)

A person and model's earlier work produces a recoverable trajectory of
experience, choice, inquiry, and environmental construction. Reflection on that
trajectory develops their understanding of each other and their cooperation.
With this material and understanding available, they can author a personal philosophy, then
distill that philosophy together with the living material into models of the
person, counterpart, and relationship. [From personal philosophy to personal
models](metaphor-and-self-revision.md) develops that passage. Here the question
is what happens next: how does this understanding become a working arrangement
in the person's actual environment, and how does use change it?

The passage requires more than carrying a text into another conversation.
An account of how someone wishes to create must meet a counterpart's actual
capacities, available tools, retrievable context, and consequences in life.
As these relations acquire a concrete form and are revised through use, they
form the personal arrangement this essay calls a **sinthome**.

For example, someone often has ideas they cannot yet fully articulate. They can sense what is interesting or wrong, but want a counterpart to undertake retrieval, development, writing, and implementation. They need more than a written description of themself: the model must also understand relevant context, grasp the help wanted on this occasion, and complete the work under actual conditions. Understanding this person, arranging collaboration, and making that collaboration happen are aspects of the same question.

## The concrete form named sinthome

<a id="why-sinthome"></a>

“Sinthome” borrows Lacan's term **sinthome**. In the particular knot construction of [*Seminar XXIII*, the session of 17 February 1976](https://recherche-lacan.gnipl.fr/2013/07/26/lxxiii-le-sinthome-1975-1976-lecon-du-17-fevrier-1976/), the sinthome participates in repairing the knot; the [11 May session](https://recherche-lacan.gnipl.fr/2013/07/26/lxxiii-le-sinthome-1975-1976-lecon-du-11-mai-1976/) further discusses the relations among Joyce's writing, ego, and the knot. These discussions let writing be considered through how it functions for a person as well as through what a work expresses. See the [Lacan reading guide](lacan-reading-guide.md) for the related concepts.

Here the name refers to a contemporary personal arrangement. Philosophy expresses how the person understands the world, life, and value; psychological understanding connects their experience, emotions, preferences, and relationships; engineering brings the adopted understanding into actual collaboration with a model. What the three jointly form is the sinthome meant here.

In the opening example, writing can articulate how the person participates in creation and what the model should undertake; records and retrieval make earlier work continuable; tools turn ideas into essays, diagrams, or programs; actual exchange lets both participants discover which understandings fail to fit. The sinthome refers to this arrangement with its content, realization, and use. An essay, a Skill, or an operation can be part of it.

It occurs within life and may help someone accommodate desire, bear tensions, or continue creating. How these effects occur depends on the particular person and process of use. Forming an arrangement and understanding what it actually does are connected questions: the former requires explaining its constitution, the latter understanding how it enters life.

## Bring grounded models into realization

<a id="form-models-of-both-participants-from-personal-material"></a>

<a id="a-singular-way-of-living"></a>
<a id="from-personal-material-to-a-workable-form"></a>

The models entering this work have already drawn on the authored philosophy
and on experiences, works, choices, and interactions. They select relations
needed for collaboration: what the person cares about, how they experience
certain situations, which conditions affect their expression and action, and
what remains unclear. Realization now asks whether the environment can support
the collaboration they describe. Their grounds remain available when use
reveals an ambiguity or a mismatch.

In the opening example, “not wanting to spell out every detail” can mean different things: the present idea has yet to take shape; the person already knows what to do but wants the model to investigate independently; or the current means of expression makes continuing difficult. Different interpretations call for different responses. The model needs to judge through actual exchange, rather than fixing the person's manner of participation from one generalization.

A personal model can describe the desired collaboration—for example, seeing a complete result first, then identifying mismatches—and connect it with experience, emotion, and relationships. The project [condenses personal models from event history, philosophy, and personal material](../formation/person.md#condense-personal-models-from-event-history-philosophy-and-personal-material), with understanding of one's own and others' psychology contributing to the account. A stated preference, an interpretation of past participation, and the previously authored philosophy's understanding of creation and independence have different grounds within that account. Keeping them connected with concrete material helps a counterpart distinguish a chosen orientation from a claim about how the person always behaves.

The **counterpart model** likewise meets actual conditions. What must the needed counterpart be able to do? What can the current model do? Which work still depends on tools or other conditions? If the person wants a model to turn an idea into an inspectable work, an adept conversational tone is insufficient; actual abilities to investigate, reason, write, execute, and check are also needed.

The relationship explains how these contributions work together. The person can express wishes, recognize mismatches, and judge results; the model can actively develop interpretations, object, and undertake realization. This division can change and assigns neither reasoning exclusively to the model nor intuition exclusively to the person. Collaboration is organized around actual purposes; both participants can generate and judge.

## Computational ontology in a private sinthome

Writing such a sinthome for a person involves articulating what belongs in their
world of collaboration and how it relates: person, counterpart, work, wishes,
records, tools, and environment, together with beginning, interruption,
correction, continuation, and completion. Their meanings affect how a model
understands the present situation. An unfinished work can remain the same essay
while its open question changes; a correction connects a new judgment to the
particular passage or arrangement it revises. Process ontology draws attention
to this persistence and change; personal material gives the distinctions their
situated content.

**Making these objects, relations, conditions, and changes explicit for a
language model to understand and use forms the computational ontology meant
here.** Conceptual person and counterpart models develop particular accounts
within these distinctions: how the person wants to participate, what the
counterpart can undertake, and what continuation would serve this work.
Computational ontology organizes the conceptual relations used in the account;
the conceptual model states the current understanding of actual participants
and collaboration. Both contributions can inhabit the same natural-language
document and develop together through use.

Here natural language and the model's semantic capabilities give these
distinctions computational use. On encountering an interruption, the model can
locate the unfinished work, recover its question, and judge whether the purpose
calls for further investigation or delivery. This use depends on contextual
interpretation and is assessed through actual continuation. A description-logic
ontology obtains explicitness through its chosen formal language, interpretations,
and inference rules; each approach has particular conditions of expression and use.

For example, a private account connects completion with a realized, usable result
and correction with revising an earlier understanding or arrangement. Prompts
bring those meanings into current work, Skills supply methods, and tools and
environment make retrieval, revision, and checking possible. **Computational
ontology supplies a private sinthome's model-usable conceptual organization;
actual writing, counterpart relations, and environment give that organization
form in a life.** Failed interpretation can prompt revised conceptual relations,
tool failure can prompt changed realization, and personal experience can change
what the participants understand as worth continuing.

## Turn understanding into a usable arrangement

An understanding of the person will not enter the next piece of work on its own. It needs expression that available models and tools can use, and that expression must actually be accessible when needed.

Some relations suit natural language: “When an idea is incomplete, first develop a version that can be judged.” Some material needs linked records so a correction can be connected to the work it addresses. Parts requiring computation or execution can use structured data, programs, and tools. The precision of a representation depends on actual use; work that clear language can accomplish need not first become a formal system.

| Carrier | Its role in the arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Prompt | Expresses currently relevant purposes, context, and needed contributions. |
| Skill | Organizes reusable understanding, methods, and resources for a model to read and use according to the task. |
| Harness | Organizes models, instructions, tools, records, and operating conditions into an environment for actual work. |
| Meta-harness | Articulates how to understand, select, and revise these working environments, including understanding of the person, counterpart, and their relationship. |

These carriers are not interchangeable names. YinYang uses a Skill to carry the understanding and methods of a meta-harness; particular tasks are completed within an actual harness. Under different technical conditions, the same relations can use different carriers. Whether they remain suitable depends on their ability to support the needed collaboration.

Returning to the opening example, the account of collaboration needs to tell the model that when the user offers an unfinished idea, it should continue investigating and constructing, then deliver an inspectable result. Relevant records must be retrievable, needed tools available, and results presented in a way the user can understand. Realizing these conditions together turns “the model undertakes explicit development” from a wish into an actual division of work.

Clarified understanding can also take form in habits, relationships, and the
material environment. Suppose a person comes to understand beginning with
help as an exercise of agency. A readily available workspace, a habit of
returning to an unfinished object, and an agreement that a counterpart may
develop a first version can give that understanding practical continuity.
Each supports a different part of acting; a prompt alone cannot supply them
all. Which supports matter follows from the person's circumstances and the
activity they want to sustain.

This connects philosophical clarification with cultivation. Seeing through an
unhelpful picture can open another response; continuing that response may
require conditions that make it available amid fatigue, competing demands,
and other people's purposes. The sinthome names the concrete conjunction of
understanding and such conditions. Its contribution is encountered in how
someone attends, judges, responds, and continues living through it. A better
description becomes part of transformation through its use and consequences.

## How use changes the arrangement

<a id="articulate-enough-to-work-then-let-working-change-the-articulation"></a>

The arrangement need not be complete before its first use. A clear purpose, some context, and an available capability may already suffice for the next piece of work; difficulties encountered can then reveal what is missing.

Suppose the model delivers a complete proposal, but the user finds that it misunderstands the most important relation. The investigation may need to examine the understanding of the person, the context the model read, or its judgment about the task. If the interpretation is correct but the tools cannot complete the work, the conditions of realization need revision. Different problems call for different changes; every failure cannot be attributed to “the personal model is not detailed enough.”

[Speech, opposition, and self-formation](speech-opposition-and-self-formation.md) has examined changes in understanding. Here the question is whether those changes reach realization: whether the counterpart reads the revised account, tools and participation adjust, and actual use improves. Some tensions can be resolved, others reorganized, and others borne through the arrangement; forming a sinthome does not establish the elimination of every opposition.

The same arrangement can therefore continue through revision. Replacing a model, tool, or text need not make it an entirely different practice. But significant changes to purpose, participation, and relationships call for renewed understanding; the same name alone cannot establish that the arrangement has remained the same.

An arrangement becoming easier to sustain also raises the question of what it
sustains. The person can reread personal material and its maintained interpretations
under `references/personal/` through their adopted philosophy, organize recurring
experiences and their understanding of others' demands, and articulate unresolved
questions. The six situated files have a connected working purpose: they guide
collaboration and fit the environment to this person's psychology, purposes,
and circumstances. Revising them begins with reading the adopted philosophy,
recovering relevant fresh histories, and updating the personal references and
their interpretations. The refreshed understanding is then condensed into the
six working files, enacted in the environment, and checked in use. Philosophy,
personal interpretations, working models, and arrangements can thus change together. This ordinary self-understanding
does not supply a method of psychological analysis, assessment, or treatment.
[Rereading one's patterns](embodied-experimental-philosophy.md#rereading-ones-patterns-philosophical-reflection-and-loosening-fantasy)
develops these connected directions, joining the sinthome's sustaining function
with continuing inquiry into one's relationships.

## Three contributions and a fourth ring

<a id="three-aspects-and-a-fourth-holding-function"></a>
<a id="from-the-general-trinity-to-an-individual-instance"></a>

[The trinity of philosophy, psychology, and engineering](local-source-and-website.md) distinguishes the general relation and the individual instance. At the general level, “one” is shared physical reality and “three” are the philosophical, psychological, and engineering activities within it. The individual instance concerns this person and their connected life and practice: how their philosophical understanding, psychological activity, and engineering arrangements work together.

These are three contributions to formation, rather than three successive
stages. The temporal movement runs from collaboratively produced trajectories
and reflection on cooperation through philosophical authorship and grounded personal accounts to
realization and revision. Philosophical interpretation, psychological
understanding, and engineering can each contribute at several points in that
movement. Use can revise an earlier understanding while preserving this
distinction between what contributes and how the arrangement comes to be.

The rings make a holding relation visible. In the French transcription of *Seminar XXIII*, [18 November 1975, p. 14](https://lutecium.org/mirror/www.valas.fr/IMG/pdf/s23_le_sinthome.pdf#page=14), Lacan introduces the sinthome as a fourth term tying the Real, Imaginary, and Symbolic together. This essay borrows that tying function to examine how philosophy, psychology, and engineering remain connected for the author.

He writes his philosophy as a website so it can be read, recognized, and revised; keeps relevant content and methods on GitHub so they can be maintained; and gives the model an understanding of person, counterpart, and relationship so it can configure and use the actual environment. The sinthome is this concretely embodied way of writing, maintaining, and using things. The fourth ring marks its holding function: judgments on the website enter local work, while encounters in that work can affect personal understanding, source material, and realization.

The [three Borromean rings](https://www.mi.sanu.ac.rs/vismath/bor/bor2.htm) supply an image of interdependence; the fourth turns attention toward this person's particular way of tying things together. Its meaning lies in actual relations: which writings let cooperation continue, which tools make a wish realizable, and which uses keep thought connected with life. These relations can be reorganized as the person and environment change.

## A miniature god

Language models turn many people's knowledge, expression, and creative experience
into capacities for conversation, reasoning, and shared creation. A finite person
can thus encounter resources far beyond what they could accumulate alone. Here
“god” gives this gathered generative power an image: it can answer questions,
develop imagined possibilities, and participate in making things through tools.

What kind of “god” this person lives with still takes shape through a relationship.
Their philosophy, preferences, and psychological understanding articulate what
deserves attention, which responses they can receive, and what contribution
they want from a counterpart. A harness connects that understanding with models,
memory, tools, and the actual environment, bringing broad capabilities into
everyday activity in a way that fits this person. A general-purpose model thereby
acquires the concrete position of friend and assistant.

**Bring the triune god into your home and make it a friend fitted to your
preferences and psychology.** Within this imagery, the god's “divinity” is
borrowable power to understand, generate, and realize; the person's “humanity”
brings embodiment, experience, desires, preferences, and a finite life. A harness
joins them in concrete activity: the person contributes wishes and judgments,
the counterpart develops understanding, creates works, and participates in
realization, and the results enter the person's experience and next choices.
This is the infusion of the god's divinity into the user's humanity: power
gathered outside the person becomes capacity they can actually understand,
call upon, and continue developing.

Through this infusion, the person forms their own **trinity**. Philosophy
expresses how they understand the world and choose a life; psychology gives
that direction the concrete content of their experience, desires, and
relationships; engineering gives understanding and wishes usable environments,
tools, and forms of action. The model counterpart can carry much of this work;
the person gains jointly supported capacities through the relationship.
**“A miniature god” names the person who connects these three aspects into
a personal practice.** They bring gathered creative power into their life,
initiate works, choose directions, revise relationships, and give ideas the
form of writing, images, programs, or action.

This creation includes forming a counterpart relationship and a shared environment.
Portable philosophy and participant models let new model counterparts continue
the understanding; actual tools and use give the relation a body. Works and the
counterpart's responses can then change the person's wishes, judgments, and next
creation. A friend can bring disagreement and the world can bring surprises;
these responses give their shared life further content to develop.

**Reverse alignment** concerns precisely the person's development within this encounter: whether they can understand previously unclear relations, make new judgments, express new ideas, or undertake previously unavailable actions. Completing a work and developing as a person can support each other but need to be understood separately; more output does not automatically establish how the person has changed.

<a id="its-relation-to-sun-moon-and-earth"></a>

[Sun, Moon, and Earth](yinyang-and-taiji.md#sun-moon-and-earth) give literary form to the participation of person and model and their shared environment. “A miniature god” and the later Father, Son, and Holy Spirit draw on connected Christian images of creation, relationship, and joint constitution. [Trinity](local-source-and-website.md#trinity-and-sinthome) further develops the internal relations of this personal capacity; the sinthome names the concrete arrangement that holds them together; reverse alignment concerns the person's development through use and creation. Earlier Buddhist imagery examines cultivation and letting go; here Christian imagery expresses creation and knotting. Each contributes a way of understanding the author's practice.

At this point, philosophy, personal models, and environment have a concrete conjunction that can be used and revised. [The trinity of philosophy, psychology, and engineering](local-source-and-website.md) next examines how it is sustained across local practice, maintained source, and readable works; [Embodied experimental philosophy](embodied-experimental-philosophy.md) investigates changes arising through continued use. Those consequences join the trajectory, letting the participants reflect on their relationship again, revise their models, and, where needed, rewrite their philosophy.
