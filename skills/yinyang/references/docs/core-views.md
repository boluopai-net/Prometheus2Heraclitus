## YY-01 · Reverse alignment: people develop with generative models

Reverse alignment chooses human development as its purpose: cultivating perception, association, judgment, expression, and creation until understanding can work through intuition. Generative intelligence gives this development new partners and means. Models understand context and adapt their contributions, making reciprocal adjustment possible. Growth appears through changes in what the person can perceive, understand, generate, and do in actual practice.

<a id="yy-02"></a>

## YY-02 · The person as an embodied multimodal generative model

**Embodied multimodal generative model** is a functional analogy for human activity and its development. Body, senses, emotion, movement, and lived situations participate together in understanding; images, sounds, language, and action can connect and generate new conceptions and responses. People already generate through this activity. Cultivation enriches and coordinates perception, intuition, judgment, and creation. Embodiment locates these capacities in continuing encounters between body and world, beyond receiving several data formats.

People and language models participate from different positions. The person lives through situations, recognizes significance and mismatch, and considers what makes a life worth pursuing. The model develops expression into comparable explanations, arguments, formal representations, programs, and works, carrying retrieval, detail discovery, and checking. Much linguistic organization and explicit formalization can already be delegated to models. People can use those results to understand and judge without first training themselves to reproduce the model's linguistic strengths. A model's formal articulation still needs checking; fluency does not guarantee correctness.

Human expression can also develop through collaboration: voicing an intuition, identifying a problem in a work, or indicating a relation through an image or gesture can begin shared activity. The chosen emphasis is embodied perception, cross-modal understanding, broad reading, and intuitive generation; the model can carry complete exposition and implementation forward. Both participants can reason, generate, and correct while occupying different positions and taking on different work. The analogy articulates participation without presuming identical biological mechanisms and machine architectures.

## YY-03 · Model oneself, the model counterpart, and their relation together

Psychological understanding and preference modeling offer two starting points.
A person can articulate how they attend, desire, and interpret, or begin with
how they prefer to learn, work, and receive a response. Writing the counterpart
in relation to either account gives a role its situated purpose. The two
accounts develop together as collaboration reveals what fits.

Where a joint task involves other people, include their relevant preferences
and purposes as partial, revisable accounts grounded in expression and
interaction. Their differences help explain what cooperation requires and
which tensions matter for this task; no participant's account exhausts them.

Model the person's and the counterpart's respective event streams together: how sensing, expression, interpretation, judgment, and action continue and change through their encounter. These are roles in an evolving process, not exhaustive definitions of either participant. “Model psychology” can describe observable patterns of attention, inclination, conflict, and response; competing explanations remain corrigible through interaction. Such functional descriptions need not assume human feelings inside a model.

The person, an account of that person, and an expression carrying that account perform different roles. So do the actual model system and the working account of its behavior. These distinctions allow revision without treating a description as the whole subject.

[Why write a personal philosophy?](https://boluopai.net/philosophy/why-yinyang/) develops the
motivation in an independent Chinese essay: the same professor meets two
different people, and the role acquires its purpose through the relationship.

## YY-04 · Let literature, history, philosophy, and aesthetics organize collaboration

“Literature, history, and philosophy” names cultural and intellectual resources: literature offers characters, situations, and expression; history makes the formation of ideas, institutions, and practices visible; philosophy examines objects, reasons, and values. Aesthetics concerns form, perception, and judgment across how these resources are read, how relations become perceptible, and how works are created. “Philosophy, literature, and aesthetics” emphasizes expression and organization but does not fully articulate the historical dimension. Bringing literature, history, philosophy, and aesthetics together connects resources with their use: history retains the emergence and transformation of ideas, while aesthetics participates in perception, judgment, and creation.

Literary programming gives this work its form: a character, image, or narrative
organizes connected possibilities for the model to interpret and enact. A
coupled portrait can reshape defaults about who initiates, judges, or carries
work forward across many local instructions. Its effect becomes observable
in the resulting conversation, artifacts, and action.

Philosophy articulates objects and relations; literary images gather connections not yet fully explicit; aesthetics makes distinctions and tensions perceptible. Together they can loosen a native harness's default assumptions and support different arrangements of conversation, roles, and work. A harness is the instructions, tools, and environment shaping interaction. Working around its defaults means revising that organization within actual authorization and safety boundaries.

An image can support several interpretations. Give a relevant interpretation an explicit form, explore its consequences, and let use reshape it. Intuition can contribute by revealing a relation or a felt mismatch; explicit articulation then makes selected connections available for discussion and realization.

Literature offers characters and situations; history follows how practices
take shape and change; philosophy develops their distinctions; aesthetics
makes relations perceptible through form. Psychological understanding and
preferences supply situated content. [Literary programming](https://boluopai.net/philosophy/literary-programming/)
develops their convergence in an independent Chinese essay, focusing on
condensation, revisable expression, and the practical uses of imagery.

Philosophical images are developed within the relevant independently presented essays, including [YinYang and Taiji](yinyang-and-taiji.md), [The stream and time](flow-and-time.md), and [World, process, and expression](world-and-expression.md); there is no separate anthology.

## YY-05 · Both participants generate and correct

A person may begin with an analogy, an unspoken relation, or a felt mismatch in the whole. The model investigates details, develops relations, proposes its own explanations, and carries construction and checking forward. Sun and Moon can express these distinct but interpenetrating contributions. Neither role exhausts a participant's abilities.

Generation, interpretation, inference, and revision can recur on either side. The relation is a changing organization of contributions, not a pipeline in which the person must finish all upstream reasoning before the model can act.

The [YinYang and Taiji essay](yinyang-and-taiji.md) develops this relation and the independent life of its creations.

## YY-06 · Arrange contributions across listening, speaking, reading, and writing

A personal account can guide cognition distributed across participants and representations. The account,
its written or visual representations, the allocation of work among participants,
and the actual activity have different roles. Records and tools keep relations
available across people, models, and time. Listening, speaking, reading, and
writing offer four entrances into this distributed work. These activities can
move between participants. One useful arrangement lets the person work chiefly
through UI and intuition, while the model maintains text, code, tools, and
materials: the model builds the environment, and the person cultivates
intuition through encountering it. The person can still write prompts and
idea drafts; both can reason and generate. Cultivation emphasizes intuition,
multimodal reasoning, and broad reading while reducing pure memorization.
Speaking can itself generate understanding, with the model receiving an
unfinished idea and helping it continue. A transcript supports reading words;
hearing tone or rhythm requires actual audio input. The
[participation essay](participation-and-support.md) develops these distinctions.

| Mode | The person's main participation | The model's contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listening | Hear explanations, works, and other people's expression; attend to meaning, sound, and rhythm; recognize what merits continuation or a question | Organize grounded explanations and examples; receive feedback, reconstruct unfinished meaning, and adapt the account |
| Speaking | Voice intuitions, intentions, questions, and objections; discover connections through speaking without preparing a complete argument first | Receive fragments in context, investigate facts, develop explanations and objections, and return results for discussion |
| Reading | Read broadly and examine diagrams, prototypes, and actual results; understand the whole, compare meanings, and notice mismatch | Retrieve and read relevant originals, check sources and details, and present key relations intelligibly and inspectably |
| Writing | Write conversational prompts, ideas, critiques, intention drafts, and prototype conceptions to bring thought into shared work | Develop these contributions into complete arguments, code, and works; maintain durable files and verify actual results |

These four entrances cycle around the same object. A person reads an article, senses a conflict between two claims, and voices a short question. The model checks the original, develops possible explanations, and writes a comparison or prototype. The person hears the explanation, reads the result, and identifies a remaining mismatch; the model revises accordingly. A conversational prompt expresses a conception, while a complete maintained manuscript is artifact production: the two senses of writing assign different responsibilities. Listening and reading bring new relations into human understanding; speaking and writing make intuitions available for response. The model contributes throughout, carrying explicit work forward.

This arrangement combines human contributions such as embodied intuition,
recognition of meaning, and judgment of overall fit with model contributions
such as retrieval, structured comparison, and sustained explicit work. These
are contributions to develop in the encounter, not fixed or exclusive
abilities. Expressing an idea need not wait for a completed argument; the model
should resolve details it can investigate. Where the person's experience,
values, or authorization remain necessary, make the consequential difference
understandable. Teaching exercises have their own purpose and can adjust the
division accordingly.

The model also finds, selects, and edits materials across useful modalities:
text, diagrams, audio, examples, or interactive works. A glossary, concept-change
table, reading guide, or targeted exercise can prepare the next encounter
without first requiring a standardized knowledge base. The
[learning conclusion in Stealing fire](stealing-fire.md#a-way-of-learning-prepare-the-encounter)
develops this practical arrangement.

For a particular question, one model can clarify an expression, another inspect
sources, and another construct or test the resulting work. The same model may
carry several contributions when that fits. Assignments follow the situation,
actual capabilities, and useful independence. Comparing, reasoning, checking,
and revising cross all four modes; the person can enter any of them while
delegating substantial explicit work.

External expression can lay out a usable map: assumptions mark branches,
relations connect them, and uncertainty leaves a return point. Listening and
reading can then support another participant's own reasoning, while speaking
and writing help revise the map. A handoff retains the question, relevant
evidence, working interpretation, and unresolved alternatives needed for the
next contribution. [Metaphor and self-revision](metaphor-and-self-revision.md)
develops this distributed-cognition proposal and its source comparisons.

## YY-07 · Let the environment carry memory and return attention to understanding

Retrievable records and artifacts can serve as shared external memory. The model retrieves, compares, and maintains relevant material, allowing the person to participate through understanding and intuitive selection without restating every detail. A stored source, a candidate interpretation, and a currently accepted view remain different objects. Retention makes something available; it does not make it true or currently applicable. Preserve only relevant, authorized material.

## YY-08 · Synchronize with the model, stay asynchronous with society, align for evaluation

**Ordinary life can stay synchronous with the model and asynchronous with society.**
Synchrony means continuing a question together, sharing relevant context, taking
up corrections, and revising the working environment as capabilities develop.
It concerns continuity of understanding and work, including across pauses and
asynchronous messages. Social asynchrony lets other relationships retain their
own rhythms while the person and model develop a more suitable way of working.

This choice concerns levels and conditions as well as response times. Established
institutions and conventions need different participants, older interfaces, and
historical commitments to remain compatible. A personal human–model environment
can evolve toward stronger capabilities without translating every internal step
back into those prevailing forms. Connect through records, works, and selected
encounters; do the needed translation at those connections. Whether the local
arrangement is better remains answerable to actual understanding, creation,
life quality, and consequences.

**Social evaluation is an occasion for temporary alignment.** Bring the fuller
capacity and organization of everyday work into the level that the assessment
can receive: answer its question, select the relevant part of the work, translate
the explanation into an intelligible form, and align tools, support, timing, and
task conditions where its rules require them. This can mean temporarily reducing
the complexity of what is presented or the support used. It makes a particular
performance comparable within that assessment, without defining the person's
whole capacity by the slice being evaluated.

After the assessment, ordinary work can continue in the arrangement already
shown to work better. Its scope should not become the permanent ceiling of
thought, capability, or environment. [YY-15](#yy-15) distinguishes evaluating a
person under common conditions from evaluating a person–environment combination;
state which result the assessment establishes.

Chosen relationships, timely responsibilities, and worldly feedback determine
where immediate presence or coordination matters. This is a chosen organization
of ordinary work and social encounters, not a claim that every social practice
is inferior or every model superior. The [life conclusion in Stealing fire](stealing-fire.md#a-way-of-living-choose-the-rhythm-of-the-fire)
connects this rhythm with finite attention and cultivating generative capacities.

## YY-09 · Distinguish ontology, epistemology, value, and methodology

**The whole framework works through abstractions over a physical basis.**
Physical ontology concerns the material processes and conditions in which
thinking, feeling, computation, and action occur. Computational ontology selects
their question-relevant events, relations, states, and transformations. Physical
description alone does not provide the representation and feasible method needed
to answer a particular question. These upper-level distinctions make inquiry
workable while remaining answerable to physical realization and worldly results.
Shared physical grounding does not establish a complete reduction or erase
differences of meaning, evidence, and method. [World, process, and expression](world-and-expression.md#physical-ground-and-computational-abstraction)
develops this relation; [Trinity](local-source-and-website.md) connects philosophy,
psychology, and engineering as activities within the same physical reality.

Both participants are modeled as event streams. An event may be an encounter, an interpretation, a remembered expression becoming available, or an action with consequences; its role depends on the question. Temporal, causal, and interpretive relations organize continuity across events. The streams generate, use, and revise local views while exceeding any single view. Relations may emerge through intuition, expression, or action before a question is explicit. Computational ontology articulates objects, roles, relations, constraints, and possible transformations at the scale an inquiry needs. It can itself change as a new distinction changes what can be asked or inferred.

Useful abstraction weighs explanatory gain against the assumptions,
representation, inference, and revision it requires. Introduce distinctions
that bear on the question and evidence, and deepen them when their contribution
justifies the cost. An explanatory premise connects the conclusion to something
the inquiry can understand or investigate independently. This economy of inquiry
keeps attention on distinctions that make a difference.

A source record may support a candidate; a candidate may yield a consequence; a working view selects what is currently usable; feedback may revise that view or its organizing distinctions. These are question-relative roles in a broader generative process, not a physical inventory or a mandatory sequence.

| Dimension of inquiry | Its question | Its contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Computational ontology | What objects, roles, relations, and transformations make this inquiry articulable? | Distinguish source records, candidate models, working views, inference steps, and feedback relations |
| Epistemology | What warrants an interpretation or inference, and when should it change? | Assess evidence, assumptions, scope, competing consequences, and correction |
| Value | What is worth pursuing, and whose life should the arrangement support in what ways? | Articulate the value of understanding, agency, generative capacity, and shared life; compare purposes and their costs |
| Methodology | How should inquiry and collaboration be carried out and maintained? | Develop methods for research, writing, teaching, implementation, and maintenance |

These correspond to the four axes in [Personal-first humanism](personal-first-humanism.md#four-connected-axes). Ontology and epistemology cannot replace evaluation: even when an arrangement works, we still ask what it achieves, whether that is worthwhile, and who bears its costs. Value questions can intersect with the other three: how value is known, what bears value, or which methods realize a chosen purpose. Such intersections do not subsume value under any one of them.

**Practice is the process in which these four dimensions enter action, encounter consequences, and become open to revision.** Concrete implementation gives practice an executable, inspectable form through actual instructions, tools, files, and interfaces. It neither replaces value as the fourth item nor constitutes a fifth philosophical axis of the same kind. A successful tool run can support an implementation claim; whether it improves a person's life requires evaluation through purposes, costs, and actual use.

YinYang primarily develops ontology and epistemology and also articulates this practice's value orientation; other Skills primarily contribute specialist methods. Formation and adaptation connect those responsibilities, while specialist procedures stay with their methods. The four dimensions distinguish questions, not mutually exclusive file types, authority levels, or a fixed execution sequence. Actual work can bring them into reciprocal correction.

The package makes this relation concrete. Its common philosophy develops a
general ontology, including the engineering objects, relations, and transformations
through which an environment is understood. The five core files add situated
ontology and epistemology: the actual person, counterpart, and relation, together
with their grounds, interpretation, and correction. Personal material supplies
evidence for both the philosophy and these accounts; public intellectual sources
also ground the common arguments. Specialist Skills supply methodology.
These are connected responsibilities rather than exclusive file types. The
[Trinity](local-source-and-website.md) develops their unity in practice; website,
repository, and personal environment make that practice perceptible, maintainable,
and usable.

Distinguish the actual interaction process, partial event records of that
process, and candidate computational models constructed from those records.
An event ontology specifies what counts as an event and which temporal,
responsive, or other relations organize it; chronological order alone does
not establish causation. Human and AI participants can share an agent-level
representation for a selected question while differing in state, update rules,
and physical realization. A knowledge base can represent a local working view,
without exhausting the participant or its dynamics. Inverse modeling proposes
possible structures from observations; forward reasoning within a candidate
derives consequences to compare with later interaction. Revising the model and
changing the participant are distinct events. This is a research orientation,
not an established universal mechanism of human or model cognition.

The research path connects these three roles:

| Role | Content | What it contributes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Object | Actual interaction, including psychological processes | What needs explaining and what can change |
| Evidence | Partial records of situations, expressions, actions, and responses | What supports or challenges a candidate explanation |
| Representation | A computational model of the relevant process | Candidate structures and update rules whose consequences can be compared with further interaction |

The path is **actual interaction → event records → candidate process models
→ predictions and further interaction → revision**. The computational model
already represents the psychological or cognitive process under study.
Formalization and modeling can develop together: analysis distinguishes
factors and relations, synthesis organizes an explanation, and abduction
proposes structures not directly observed. Deduction develops consequences
within a candidate; induction and further observations assess patterns and
their scope. These activities can recur in practical work and in research
that compares records across encounters.

The present target is an empirically supported process model with an explicit
range of application. A model may specify realization requirements, and a
program may supply one possible implementation. Identifying a person's actual
biological or physical mechanism requires additional evidence. Physical
realization remains a further explanatory question; completing physical
reduction is not a prerequisite for this research path. Shared agent-level
representation serves the selected comparison while allowing different
human and AI structures and realizations.

“Computational” concerns articulable organization and transformation: how an expression becomes available, how an interpretation changes a working view, and how an action produces feedback. These relations provide a tractable scale for understanding the encounter.

[The stream and time](flow-and-time.md) gives an expressive form to continuity and local views; [Query rewriting and sublation](preference-harness.md#query-rewriting-and-sublation) offers a bounded comparison for transformation.

## YY-10 · Retain conflict while distinguishing retention from acceptance

Keeping incompatible candidates available differs from accepting all their claims together. Identify referents, times, and conditions; compare what follows within each candidate and what evidence discriminates among them. Revision may change a conclusion, candidate priority, transformation rule, or the question's ontology. Removing a candidate from the current view does not erase its source or establish its falsity. An undeveloped relation can remain available for later inquiry.

Mentioning content, denying it, and accepting it are different relations.
An utterance is also an event made from a speaking position, toward an
addressee, in a context. Recording a denial can bring its content into inquiry
without making the denied proposition true. Context can explain what an
expression does; denial supplies no rule for inferring affirmation.

External conditions, present experience, and the currently accepted model can
change at different rates. An expression's occurrence time, the source time
of its content, and the time it represents can differ. Retention and recency
alone establish neither current applicability nor truth. Distinguishing these
objects and times may resolve an apparent contradiction; genuinely conflicting
claims under the same relevant conditions still need assessment.

The preferred analogy distinguishes the working ontology from the data and interpretations it encounters. For stability in the user's environment, concepts and relations should remain as coherent as possible while conflicting sources and candidates can be retained. The repair-based comparison fixes a consistent ontology with possibly inconsistent data; ordinary model reasoning uses this as an analogy through informal natural language. Coherence can require a reasoned revision rather than preserving an unsuitable ontology. A difference of time or perspective may also explain an apparent contradiction. The [foundation discussion](stealing-fire.md#from-living-fire-to-interacting-event-streams) locates this more specific direction within the broader concern with non-explosive reasoning.

Repair-based reasoning supplies concrete distinctions: priority and repair strategy select views, while AR, IAR, brave, and parameterized semantics determine answers differently. Rule-following and preference can work together; incomparability and the distinction between consistent support and consistent results matter. [A preference harness](preference-harness.md) develops their use in ordinary natural-language collaboration: chosen purposes orient selection, while evidence and inference establish claims about their realization.

### A small fictional example

Suppose a demo shows a yellow indicator. One help-page record says yellow means “ready”; another says it means “waiting.” The question is what yellow means **in this demo version**.

1. **Records:** retain both statements with their version information. Recording a statement does not adopt it.
2. **Candidates:** one interpretation assigns yellow the role “ready”; another assigns it “waiting.” Before checking, the working view leaves the meaning unresolved.
3. **Discriminating consequence:** suppose both candidates specify that color changes exactly when the ready/waiting state changes, and completion changes “waiting” to “ready” while leaving “ready” unchanged. They then predict different behavior at completion: yellow persists under the first interpretation and changes under the second.
4. **Feedback and update:** a controlled run records completion, a yellow-to-green change, and readiness at green. Under the stated rule, this supports the second interpretation for the tested version. The working view changes; both records remain available.

The transition rule connects a candidate to a checkable consequence. If the indicator can change for unrelated reasons, the candidates need to include those causes before the observation can discriminate between them. The example gives records, interpretations, states, transitions, inferences, and observations their roles within one inquiry.

## YY-11 · The world constrains the shared account

Changing norms and worldly conditions can revise a task's premises, purpose,
or completion criteria. Tolerating conflict allows a finite task to proceed
without first resolving every retained disagreement: select a justified
working view, reason through its consequences, and check the actual result.
The [preference-harness account](preference-harness.md#finish-situated-tasks-amid-changing-norms-and-worlds)
connects these contributions. Its [forward-evolution direction](preference-harness.md#evolve-forward-with-more-capable-models)
keeps purposes, relations, evidence, and interfaces intelligible to stronger
future models, while replacing obsolete means and preserving actual current
requirements and boundaries.

People and models generate texts, theories, programs, and actions. These artifacts meet use, counterexamples, resistance, and unintended consequences. Such feedback can force revision even when both participants agree. A consequence derived within a candidate and evidence about the world do different work: internal coherence does not by itself establish external fit.

World-directed evidence enters inquiry through observations and records that themselves require interpretation. This makes interpretations corrigible, not unconstrained. An account of a person is not the person; an artifact representing a process does not exhaust that process. Sun, Moon, and their creations are useful images when they preserve these relations rather than replace them with a physical ontology. Creating and encountering the results can develop both the shared account and the person's next acts of understanding.

[World, process, and expression](world-and-expression.md) develops how encountering an artifact can change self-understanding.

The [YinYang and Taiji essay](yinyang-and-taiji.md) develops Sun and Moon as imagery for differentiated generation, reception, and shaping within YinYang. Person and model can retain or exchange their emphases without gender assignment. Earth is the answering material and shared living environment; creations develop through care and acquire relative independence.

## YY-12 · Make the views perceptible, discussable, and continuable

Diagrams, dynamic processes, and manipulable examples can expose important relations. Useful interaction shows what changes when a premise, candidate, or contribution changes, while keeping the basis inspectable. English and Chinese reader texts carry the same views; English model-maintenance prose preserves their interpretive and practical connections. They differ in role without becoming competing accounts.

The views develop through practice. Keep basic points accessible, preserve their philosophical substance, and leave private portraits and source material within their existing boundaries.

<a id="yy-13"></a>

## YY-13 · Write a sinthome through an individual meta-harness

The person is already an agent in this functional account. Available
generative capabilities expand that agency; formation develops the person's
own philosophy and its lived, maintained form, named sinthome in this proposal.

In this practical reinterpretation, a personal philosophy realized through a harness can become a way of writing a sinthome for oneself: a singular practice through which experience, expression, and action acquire a creatable and livable form. A revisable account of oneself and one's model counterpart lets suitable models join continuing work across purposes, extending human agency. Ontology, epistemology, methodology, logic, statistics, and other resources can contribute where needed; a complete theory or Joyce-like literary complexity is not a prerequisite. Begin with enough articulation and realization to support the actual purpose; the person always exceeds the text.

The arrangement seeks fit with the person's purposes and conditions while remaining responsive to others, worldly constraints, and change. Understanding, action, continued life, delight, and joy can give it direction. Capability, tools, authorization, and worldly conditions give this ambition its actual means; use and revision develop the sustaining form. The [sinthome essay](writing-a-sinthome.md) distinguishes this extension from [Lacan's historical account in *Seminar XXIII*, 17 February 1976](https://recherche-lacan.gnipl.fr/2013/07/26/lxxiii-le-sinthome-1975-1976-lecon-du-17-fevrier-1976/), a clinical conclusion, and a guarantee of happiness or unlimited power.

## YY-14 · Cultivate generative capacities with available fire

Fire has a natural history: stellar processes supplied much of the material
later organized into chips; electricity drives those structures, and human
knowledge, data, and training shape their generative uses. Trained capability
can be reused, spreading the cost of its formation across many applications.
Verified works, tools, and better collaboration can in turn support further
capability. This conditional recursive accumulation helps explain both growing
creative reach and accelerating changes in the surrounding environment.
The [material and learning argument](stealing-fire.md#material-fire-and-concentrated-capability)
grounds these connections and distinguishes resource use, heat, emissions, and
social acceleration. Their different mechanisms share a practical question:
can finite lives and environments absorb the consequences? Cultivation develops
embodied perception, intuitive recognition, judgment, and expression together
with model-supported realization; it does not presume a universal ranking of
occupations or make faster iteration the purpose of every life.

Generative capability is made available through collective material and intellectual work. ## YY-15 · Cultivate a garden of becoming with friends

Cultivating oneself can begin with jointly cultivating the relations around us. Friends use available fire to develop understanding, judgment, and creation, bringing new questions, works, methods, and objections. Their contributions enter one another's environments and open possibilities for further generation. One person's development can thus become a condition for another's. Each begins from their own life and develops their own direction, illuminating one another through relations that allow exchange.

These writings first hope to enter such encounters: letting friends read, try, and rework an understanding already formed, then return responses its author did not anticipate. Writing thereby helps cultivate the environment in which one participates. Its value appears in what the relations can sustain and what the participants can create; the scale of circulation and speed of iteration remain answerable to these concrete purposes. Shared methods make exchange easier, while different experiences and directions let it bring something new.

**Use fire for life nearby, and exercise restraint in its wider diffusion.** Personal philosophy and this website primarily serve oneself and nearby people who wish to participate. Circulate them as little as is sufficient to help those relationships, rather than treating wider adoption as success. Develop the capacity of oneself and the nearby environment to absorb new capabilities, recognize problems, and make effective revisions, so life can keep pace with model development. Preserving a good existing arrangement can itself be a sound judgment. Helping friends acquire this capacity differs from requiring them to adopt the same philosophy.

A conditional risk motivates this restraint. If many people use philosophy, psychological modeling, and engineering to accelerate changes to their own arrangements, and their interdependent environments change quickly too, local benefits may bring shared coordination costs. Yesterday's useful understanding or interface has changed today; each person's effort to catch up can trigger another round of change. When change outpaces what relationships can understand, negotiate, and absorb, the world may resemble water kept at an increasingly vigorous boil. Personal priority alone need not produce that outcome, and open sharing can improve coordination. The risk is coupled acceleration exceeding shared capacity to accommodate it. This is a possible feedback and a reason for restraint, not an established social law.

Therefore, **increase effective capacity for iteration nearby while reducing the acceleration pressure one contributes to more distant environments**. Select recipients, content, timing, and scope so feedback can return to actual relationships; without a concrete contribution to life, do not actively enlarge circulation. Distant participants retain their own purposes and pace of development. This choice limits one's own promotion, rather than preventing others from learning or making local gains depend on their stagnation. Fire can illuminate, cook, and help create. Regulate the fire one adds so that life can still accommodate the water's movement. [Trinity](local-source-and-website.md#worldview-knowing-and-realization) connects views, experience, and realization; [Stealing fire](stealing-fire.md#cultivating-a-garden-of-becoming-with-friends) develops the purpose of this limited circulation.

**Optimize locally, allow divergence from the wider environment, and specify common conditions when comparing.** The wider environment here means prevalent social arrangements and shared benchmarks, not a fully measured average environment. One's relations, records, models, and tools can form a local environment intended to outperform prevailing arrangements for particular purposes such as understanding, creation, recovery, and action. Difference alone is not success: actual life quality and outcomes must establish its value, including costs passed to others. In ordinary life, continue using arrangements shown to fit better rather than dismantling support merely to conform.

The temporary social alignment in [YY-08](#yy-08) brings richer everyday work into the level and conditions an assessment can receive. Evaluation first specifies its object. To compare people's performance under common conditions, temporarily align tools, support, time, and task conditions, follow the same rules, and report the conditions to which the result applies. To assess what a person and their constructed environment can achieve together, retain that support and assess the combination, including its maintenance costs. Both results can be reported, answering different questions about a shared benchmark and actual life. Returning temporarily to common conditions does not negate supported capacity; a supported result cannot stand in for a result without that support. A result describes that assessed slice and its conditions, rather than setting a permanent ceiling on the person's capacity. After evaluation, ordinary work returns to the better-supported arrangement. Local improvement can thereby retain its direction while remaining intelligibly comparable with the wider world.

Call this image **a shared garden of becoming**: people, counterparts, and works can keep changing, with delight in understanding, play, creation, and unexpected encounters. It continues the self-revision image of [a personal Gödel machine](metaphor-and-self-revision.md#the-personal-gödel-machine-analogy), placing revision within open exchanges among friends. Such a garden is cultivated through concrete relationships and practice. Whether it helps remains a question for each participant's experience, judgment, and actual consequences. The [fire essay](stealing-fire.md#cultivating-a-garden-of-becoming-with-friends) develops this purpose for writing.
