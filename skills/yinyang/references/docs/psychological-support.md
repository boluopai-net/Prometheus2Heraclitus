# Psychology

Materials, understanding, and support can advance one another. A model can help find and retain materials, select questionnaires, check and interpret scores, find support, and understand statistics and change. Begin wherever the current need arises. Progress in one area can give the others new questions, evidence, or opportunities for action.

This page concerns evidence, measurement, and practical help. [Research and Learning](research-and-learning.md) offers resources for studying psychology; philosophical interpretation has its own place elsewhere in the collection.

Materials can inform questionnaire selection; scores and changes can reveal what still needs understanding or what support to seek. Actual support can generate new materials in turn. The sections below distinguish these contributions for use.

| Work the model can assist with | How it connects with the other contributions |
| --- | --- |
| Find and retain materials | Develop questions from experience and knowledge sources to inform measures and support; receive new material from counseling, psychotherapy, medical visits, and everyday attempts. |
| Find questionnaires | Select from the material and present question; new experience, earlier scores, or a support process can change what needs measuring. |
| Check and interpret scores | Produce inspectable results from actual answers and scoring rules; use results to identify information needs and inform statistics, discussion, or help-seeking. |
| Find support | Connect practical assistance, counseling, psychotherapy, or medical care; the process and subsequent experience can return to materials, measurement, and observation of change. |
| Understand statistics and change | Relate measurements, circumstances, and support to distinguish trends and unknowns; refine questions, source selection, or the next form of support. |

For example, counseling may bring new expression, knowledge, and questions for discussion. The model can help retain and understand these, then consider whether a questionnaire would help. Existing records may already support understanding a change or explaining a concern in the next consultation. Scoring, organizing materials, and finding support can proceed alongside one another. Choose measurement or direct support according to the current need.

## Find and preserve relevant materials

Start with the present concern and the help you want. The model can find original research, public guidance, and relevant records you authorize it to read, compare their claims, and preserve useful material with its source, date, and context. It should distinguish what was recorded or said from a later interpretation, keeping uncertainty and conflicting evidence visible.

**Materials include knowledge that makes help understandable.** Advice without an understanding of its purpose, grounds, and relation to the situation may be difficult to judge or use. The model can find reliable explanations for the current question, explain a measure's construct, a score, or differences between forms of support, and retain useful sources and explanations. Learning and receiving help can proceed together. [NICE's shared decision-making guidance](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng197/chapter/recommendations) provides a reference for understandable information and joint judgment in professional care.

An **Obsidian private vault**, or an existing comparable note environment, can retain this psychological material: readings, conceptual explanations, life records, and counseling, psychotherapy, or medical-visit material the person chooses to save. Link sources, circumstances, and subsequent changes. The model can help find, organize, and maintain the collection.

In YinYang, `personal` retains **dynamic, important material relevant to current understanding, together with its source entrances**. Examples include current concerns, questionnaire answers and results, significant changes, and an interpretation of one's own name and its personal meaning. Name interpretation is personal expression and interpretation. Full sources keep their existing place in the vault; `personal` can hold the records needed now or links to them. New experience and understanding can revise what belongs in this current selection.

Personal records stay with their private owner. In YinYang, use `personal` to read currently relevant material yourself or ask a language model to help read and understand it. The six working accounts select implications for cooperation.

## Choose and complete questionnaires

A questionnaire is useful when it measures something relevant to a concrete question. Ask the model to examine the intended construct, population, respondent, language, item content, and evidence for the proposed use. [HealthMeasures' selection guidance](https://healthmeasures.net/select-measures/how-to-select/) provides a useful reference for these choices.

The model should locate the actual instrument and instructions, verify the version and applicable access or use conditions, and explain its recall period and limits. For example, the [WHO-5](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/WHO-UCN-MSD-MHE-2024.01) asks five self-report questions about well-being during the previous two weeks and has an official Chinese edition.

You complete the items according to the instrument's instructions. The model can explain unfamiliar instructions while preserving their meaning. Scoring uses the answers you actually provide, retaining unclear or missing responses.

<a id="scoring"></a>

## Check and explain scores

The model can calculate and independently check scores using the exact version's rules. [HealthMeasures' scoring guidance](https://healthmeasures.net/implement/scoring/calculate-scores/) shows why the instrument and scoring method matter. Reversed items, missing answers, transformations, and separate domains must follow the relevant manual. Comparisons between versions require documented comparability or calibration.

Keep the version, language, completion date, recall period, original answers, missingness, calculation, and result together in the appropriate private record. In YinYang, retain currently important measurement records in `personal`, or link to the complete records in the vault, so subsequent understanding can use actual materials. You can ask for a short table that makes the calculation reproducible and explains the score's direction, reference population, and interpretive limits. Any cutoff or suggested follow-up must come from the instrument's relevant guidance. Diagnosis requires professional clinical assessment.

<a id="statistics"></a>

## Read patterns and changes

A few interpretable tables or trend charts can show what changed and what remains uncertain. Keep the context alongside the numbers: daily functioning, important events, changes in circumstances, and any relevant care. Choose a repeat interval suited to the instrument and the question.

An observed change can return work to another area: find knowledge explaining the measure, check scoring or comparability, develop a question for the next consultation, or reconsider what support fits. Without scores, the model can still organize dated events and experiences to clarify what is known and unknown. Such qualitative observation and questionnaire statistics retain their distinct grounds.

[Meaningful change](https://healthmeasures.net/interpret-scores/meaningful-change/) depends on context and on whether the question concerns an individual or a group. Repeated personal measurements need to account for dependence between that person's records. Time trends, related observations, and lingering effects can complicate comparisons, as the [AHRQ guide to N-of-1 trials](https://effectivehealthcare.ahrq.gov/products/n-1-trials/research-2014-1) discusses. Explaining causes requires additional study design and evidence. The model can use analysis suited to the available records to explain observations and their limits.

## Turn understanding into practical support

You can ask the model to help reduce a practical difficulty: make an activity easier to begin, adjust an overwhelming environment, find reliable general well-being information, or organize a manageable next step. What helps should be judged through your actual experience and revised if it adds burden.

Support also includes counseling, psychotherapy, and medical care. The model can explain their purposes, help locate qualified professionals suited to the present need, and organize questions and relevant material; the person and professional determine specific care together. When difficulties persist, worsen, or interfere with ordinary life, a concise timeline, effects on functioning, and records the person chooses to share can help communicate the concern. Clinical assessment considers duration, functioning, and context as well as symptoms; see [NICE's assessment guidance](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng222/chapter/recommendations#recognition-and-assessment).

**Support has a return path.** Counseling, treatment, medical visits, and everyday adjustments may leave new experiences, explanations, advice, and questions. Bring useful material back to the vault and `personal`, where the model can continue helping with understanding, research, suitable questionnaires, score checks, or observation of change. Progress in any contribution can change the others' work. Retain this return where it helps and adjust the burden of recording and measurement.

For immediate danger, serious injury, poisoning, or another urgent need, seek local emergency or urgent professional help promptly. A scale must not determine whether someone deserves care or predict suicide; [NICE's self-harm guidance](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng225/chapter/recommendations) explicitly rejects those uses of risk scales.

## Model assistance and professional support

The model carries much of the searching, reviewing, preservation, calculation, and preparation. Your answers and experience remain yours; you can correct an interpretation, decline a questionnaire, or ask for practical help without accepting a personal portrait.

The model assists with materials, score checking, and preparation. Appropriately qualified professionals provide clinical assessment and treatment, with the person and professional judging specific care together. The useful outcome here is a clearer question, trustworthy records, an understandable result where measurement helps, and a next step that fits the situation.
