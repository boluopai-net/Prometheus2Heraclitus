# Begin with this example, and make something of your own

[Home](../../README.md) · [Skill entrypoint](../../SKILL.md) · [简体中文](example-and-commons.zh-CN.md)

Everyone arrives with experiences, questions, loved works, and intellectual resources of their own. This collection can be one starting point for further exploration: read a concrete example, see how it gives understanding a form, and follow the resulting questions into other materials, other people's practices, and one's own life. The sources and direction of creation can extend far beyond this example.

Model companies and humanity continue the Promethean work of acquiring fire; individuals can already make things with the fire available to them. The essays, diagrams, methods, and Skills here are some among many available resources. YinYang gives an understanding of a person, a model counterpart, and their relationship a developed philosophical and literary form, then gives it practical effect. It offers an attempt already made for readers to examine alongside the other resources they bring.

## A form that can be rewritten

The invitation is to write your own philosophy and, through that writing,
abstract a usable personal model. An initially unclear understanding can find
form through a philosopher's concepts, literary images, argument, and dialogue.
When that expression becomes clearer than an existing personal account, use it
to revise the account. Private modeling can in turn expose questions for the
next essay. This is a continuing relation between forms, not a requirement to
finish one before beginning the other.

In this implementation, the five core files hold the distilled understanding
needed by a model counterpart: encounter, person model, counterpart and
relationship, situated use, and sources. Concrete identity details, episodes,
original expressions, and longer investigations stay in `personal/` or the
private originals it references. Distillation preserves the individual
differences that matter to collaboration, while keeping detailed evidence
reachable when needed. Someone adopting these five files must form their own
accounts rather than retain the author's personal substance.

A person can make a website for their philosophy while keeping the complete
personal model private. Public philosophy is an authored work and a selected,
indirect expression of understanding; it need not expose a literal portrait,
private experiences, or the detailed account used by their agent. The person
can also choose to publish particular abstractions from the model. This
separation matters because direct personal descriptions can expose material
that others might use against them. Choose the actual content to disclose;
philosophical phrasing alone does not determine what a text reveals.
The public work, private core accounts, and supporting sources have different
readers and purposes, while participating in the same self-forming practice.

A literary form can show how an image gathers a relationship, how concepts
give it distinctions, and how practice makes it consequential. YinYang's fire,
event streams, Sun and Moon, and philosophical sources make this particular
composition intelligible. A reader can examine how it works while choosing
which relations to take up and which to develop differently.

“Format” here means an organization of meaning. Another work may use different
sources, characters, vocabulary, and structure, disagree with this account,
or pursue a purpose it has not anticipated. Its form succeeds when it makes
that work's relations available to its readers and model counterparts. Its
questions, practice, and consequences supply the judgment; resemblance to
YinYang does not.

## Adopt, combine, or author anew

Adoption grounds YinYang's maintained form in your actual circumstances and
revises its accounts through your use. Combination takes selected resources
into another arrangement: borrow an essay, recombine methods, or replace an
image. Independent authorship can begin with a question encountered here and
follow it into other authors, traditions, research, and experience, leaving
this example behind. These possibilities can overlap as questions develop.
Let the relation you want to express determine what you carry forward.

A model can find material, compare interpretations, and construct works; the
person contributes perception, expression, and judgment. Neither needs to
reproduce the example as the destination of that collaboration.

A new work deserves to develop on its own terms, with its sources and changes
traceable. Creative authorship can find another organization. Personal source material stays with its actual private owner. Publishing that material is a separate action requiring its own authorization, including for withdrawn or unpublished manuscripts.

## A commons for further creation

Two contributions are especially welcome, with different purposes. For reusable
Skills and tools, improve an actual method or implementation, explain the
problem and result, and submit the change as a pull request; an Issue can first
describe a problem or proposal. A useful local improvement can benefit the
original author and other users when its reusable part is separated from
private context.

For philosophy, the primary invitation is to write your own work from your
questions, sources, and life. You can also propose corrections, objections,
clearer arguments, or improvements to this collection through Issues and pull
requests. These contributions can improve the existing work while independently
authored philosophies develop their own directions. Neither form of participation
requires publishing a private personal model.

“Knowledge communism” names the aspiration to shared creative capacity here:
make intellectual means available and support others in making their own
works. The fellowship can include different commitments. A contribution may
add a use, expose a failed premise, or develop a form its first author did not
imagine. Public discussion and attribution make these connections traceable.

This commons has concrete terms of reuse. The public GitHub repository can be read,
cloned, and downloaded without an application or approval, and supports forks,
Issues, and pull requests. YinYang's original material uses CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, permitting reproduction,
adaptation, and distribution for noncommercial purposes only. When sharing, retain the required
attribution and notices, link the license, and identify modifications; shared
adaptations follow its ShareAlike conditions. Private adaptations need not be
published. The [official legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/legalcode.en)
governs. The other reusable Skill and tool packages retain their bundled
MIT licenses and public downloads. Independently authored work can be
shared under its own chosen terms. Participants can discuss ideas, share
webpage links, and publish their own independently authored contributions.
Separately cited works and third-party notices retain their own rights and
terms. These terms determine what can be shared and how attribution and
permissions accompany it.

## Personal practice, maintained source, and readable work

GitHub holds the maintained source and its revision history. The website makes
the same selected documents and diagrams readable without repository
navigation. Both reading surfaces present one maintained edition, with changes made at its source. The web edition retains the source revision, links,
diagrams, attribution, and rights information so that a reader can move from
understanding an example to inspecting and making use of it.

These two reading surfaces connect with a person's local practice, where
understanding enters actual tasks and acquires feedback. The shared package
supplies reusable material; a situated environment retains its own personal
accounts and conditions of use. [Trinity](local-source-and-website.md)
develops why these different forms belong together and how a language model
can help maintain the relations among them.

Begin here if it helps, and carry your own questions onward. Encounter more ideas, works, and lives, and make something of your own.
