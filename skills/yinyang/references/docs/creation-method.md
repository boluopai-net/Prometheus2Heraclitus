Within **Prometheus2Heraclitus**, YinYang is the principal Skill for a
meta-harness, primarily carrying ontology and epistemology alongside
developed viewpoints and imagery within the relevant essays. Other Skills
primarily supply methodology; artifacts give the understanding and methods
working form. The formation guidance here connects these responsibilities
without making YinYang the owner of specialist procedures.
The meta-harness articulates the purpose and relations through which actual
working harnesses can be understood and revised. These include prompts,
Skills, hooks, roles, model selection, tools, and the user's working environment.
Skill authorship gives this understanding an accessible carrier; its effects
extend into how the whole arrangement supports human–model development.

The meta-harness has an interpretive role: methods for investigating, writing,
or implementing depend on an understanding of whose purpose they serve, which
objects matter, and what supports a judgment. YinYang articulates these shared
premises, while specialist methods contribute their own knowledge and can
correct them. Reading the encounter and complete coupled accounts before
substantive work makes that basis available when interpreting a request and
selecting methods. This reading dependency does not require the entire essay
collection for every task or the personal account for every bounded helper.
Current complete readings can be reused. A Skill is the carrier of the
philosophy; global instructions can also carry personal modeling or connect
this maintained account to the consumer. Interpretive scope, reading order,
and actual instruction authority are different relations.

The method gives practical form to the purpose developed in the [fire essay](stealing-fire.md): borrow available generative capacity from outside, then cultivate one's own understanding, judgment, and creative agency through practice.

## From an image to a working relation

YinYang is a philosophical Skill whose spirit is literary programming.
A person can begin with psychological understanding or with familiar preferences:
how they attend, desire, and interpret, or how they wish to learn, work, and be
answered. Write a counterpart in relation to that account. Images and narrative
then give the model a connected situation to interpret: who is here, what each
participant contributes, and what their work can become. This literary form
can reshape default role assumptions across many local instructions at once.
Its practical effect appears in changed interpretation, responses, and action.

This method is itself available for reinterpretation. The
[example and commons essay](example-and-commons.md) distinguishes adopting
YinYang's maintained implementation from composing a new work. Its particular
philosophy, images, and file roles need not survive that composition. Let the
person's developing understanding determine what to carry forward, with the
model contributing authorship rather than reproducing the example by default.

Begin with the purpose and the encounter a working harness should make possible.
Philosophy, literature, or an image can hold several connected possibilities
together before all of them are explicit. The [Sun, Moon, and Earth relation](yinyang-and-taiji.md)
image, for example, gathers generation, articulation, independent contribution,
and contact with a world that can answer back.

Develop the relations relevant to the actual work. Identify what each
participant contributes, which objects and distinctions matter, and how the
relationship can change. Both participants can generate and judge, even when
they choose different responsibilities. The image remains richer than the
particular interpretation used to change a working harness.

Give those relations a working realization in the affected environment. A prompt
can change how intentions are interpreted; a role can change who carries a
decision; a tool or memory interface can make a contribution possible. A Skill
can provide a discoverable entrance and route to deeper material. Instructions,
examples, and tools make selected relations effective. Use precise interfaces where
execution depends on them. Choose the depth and mechanics from the actual
consumer and task, rather than expanding every idea into another rule.

These connected levels can develop through repeated exchanges:

`expression ↔ relational understanding ↔ working realization ↔ evidence`

Metaphor can gather relationships before each has a separate formulation;
analogy develops selected connections into candidate implications. Their
external expression can provide branches, assumptions, and return points for
another participant's reasoning. Such representations can organize contributions
across people, models, and tools. [Metaphor and self-revision](metaphor-and-self-revision.md)
develops this account of distributed cognition and distinguishes a working
philosophical arrangement from formal or empirical guarantees.

The [preference harness](preference-harness.md) makes one such relation explicit.
Preferences can shape which possibilities receive attention and adoption while
rules govern the consequences drawn through them. The account separates repair
selection from inference semantics and preserves their finer alternatives.
In ordinary natural-language reasoning, the analogy helps distinguish choosing
a purpose, selecting a working interpretation, and drawing its consequences.

## Let the realization answer the account

A working harness's actual behavior can reveal a mistaken interpretation. Suppose an
image of complementary contributions becomes a rule that reserves all
generation to the person and all execution to the model. If useful model
judgment is suppressed, inspect the division itself. A better realization may
retain the chosen responsibilities while letting either participant propose
and correct the whole.

Likewise, protecting human attention can mean that the model carries
investigation and implementation details. A question becomes useful when the
person's experience or choice would materially change the next action. Recover
the relevant relationship and current purpose before revising instructions.

Inspect the whole affected passage and its practical consequences. A working
failure may require changing the carrier, an instruction, an interpretation,
or the underlying account. Keep the supported meaning with one maintained
owner and reconnect its dependent parts. The same method can revise itself
through these concrete failures and improvements.

This is recursive development when the results change the conditions of later
work: methods alter what the environment makes accessible or actionable; the
resulting experience can revise both those methods and the ontology or
epistemology that oriented them. A harness can embody epistemic commitments
through its evidence, memory, inquiry, and checking arrangements, so the levels
distinguish contributions rather than mutually exclusive file types. Retained
understanding and working means become personal assets for the next encounter.
The return must remain open to contrary evidence: a loop that selects only
confirming results can stabilize a mistake. Development can require replacement
or deletion, and its value needs actual consequences rather than a rising
count of revisions or artifacts.

## Give the account a readable body

The [YinYang and Taiji essay](yinyang-and-taiji.md) uses Sun, Moon, and Earth
for the user, model, and environment. The particular encounter must be
understood from actual material; the imagery does not provide its personal
content.

For direct adoption, the reusable Skill lives at [`skills/yinyang/`](../../SKILL.md)
within Prometheus2Heraclitus. It supplies culture, philosophical ideas, and
reasoning forms for developing a personal philosophy through this implementation. The consuming model reads the
authorized environment and sufficient psychological or preference material,
then writes actual grounded accounts in the same fixed personal slots. Psychological
and preference material are alternative starting points and can enrich one another
through use. The
common `SKILL.md` retains its purpose, method, and routes; read the condensed
[encounter](../encounter.md) with it every time.
[Design and revision](../design-and-revision.md)
explains the shared roles: condensed encounter, developed user and relationship
models, attributable sources, question-oriented reading, revision, and adaptation.
`references/{encounter,models,relationship,usage,sources}.md` carry formation
instructions in the distributed package and the situated accounts in private
use. Formalized user accounts remain personal content. Supporting private
materials live under `references/personal/`, guided by its
[README](../personal/README.md), with filenames appropriate to actual evidence.

The personal content belongs to the actual person and relationship. No copied
portrait or fictional mini substitutes for forming it. Fixed slots carry
formation instructions rather than pretending an empty file is an account. Keep
the situated content with its private owner and revise it through use. The format
and common content remain identical to the distributed package in this route.
An independently reauthored philosophy has its own form and maintenance;
its success is not measured by conformity to these slots.
[Harness adaptation](../harness-adaptation.md)
carries the resulting understanding into the actual working environment and
checks its effect. A written personal account alone does not complete that work.

The developed essays remain in `references/docs/`, and earlier diagrams in `references/diagrams/`.
These resources support the ideas; they are not additional Skills or a sequence
that every consuming model must finish before working. Together with method
references, the reading map, package administration, READMEs, and the OpenAI
YAML adapter, they remain byte-identical common material. The shared GitHub
package owns common edits; `scripts/sync-common.py` checks projection by default,
and `--apply` projects common source to a target while preserving all five slots
and all private supporting materials. Native owners realize local and private
Web use. This projects common content without exporting personal material or
introducing a service.

## Assess at the scale of the claim

Read back the Skill and the affected harness, follow the entrypoint to the
relevant resource, and inspect the behavior the realization is meant to change.
Check packaging through reachable resources, implementation through observable
results, and collaborative fit through actual work. Human development appears
over time in changes to understanding, generation, judgment, and action.

Keep the result open to correction without making every use a rewrite of the
entire account. [MODEL.md](../../MODEL.md) carries the YinYang package's maintenance
and publication boundaries.
