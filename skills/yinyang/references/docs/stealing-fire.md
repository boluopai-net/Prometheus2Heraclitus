**Collective work makes generative capability available; a finite person borrows it and cultivates what they understand, judge, initiate, and sustain.** The first movement costs energy, materials, and human work. The second asks what that capability serves in a life. Some abilities become internalized; others remain reliably distributed across models, tools, and collaborators. An impressive output establishes neither its recipient's understanding nor their development. The argument begins with these conditions before giving them a celestial image.

The developmental purpose is a choice made here: understanding, generation,
and agency are worth cultivating. Stronger AI does not logically impose that
choice on everyone. Someone can seek relief, a completed work, or reliable
assistance without turning every task into a lesson. Once development is
chosen, its contribution must be examined alongside those other outcomes.

Connecting that available power to this purpose requires understanding both
participants: **model the particular person, then form a corresponding agent
and their way of collaborating.** The person model makes a life, preferences,
and developmental direction intelligible. The counterpart model connects that
understanding to judgment, response, and action. Actual collaboration tests
both accounts and changes what participants can do next. This relation carries
the essay's argument from borrowing fire to the continuing formation of agents.

In compact form: **dynamic user model → corresponding agent model → work
together → consequences return from the world → revise the models and further
action.** Memory retains material that can be retrieved; a model organizes
understanding of the people, relations, and changes that material concerns.
One text can serve both functions. Being dynamic depends on use, testing, and
revision through new encounters, not on the filename.

The opening gives an overview of the whole argument. The following essays
develop its premises and connections: value and agency, the conditions of
cultivation, understanding a changing world, reciprocal participation, and
realization with feedback. Previewing a conclusion here does not establish it.

One concrete practice is to write one's own philosophy with a model, distill
its grounded understanding into a private working account, and make selected
thought available as a work or website. Philosophy acts as a broader model;
the five core files condense a narrower person–counterpart relation; personal
material retains the evidence. Begin from whichever is more developed and
let each correct the others. Website writing, source maintenance, and actual
use give this iteration places to continue. Public expression can develop
the argument without publishing the complete direct personal model. The
[Trinity](local-source-and-website.md) and [invitation to create](example-and-commons.md)
unfold this practice.

## Material fire and concentrated capability

The fire is physically consequential. Materials and minerals become machines;
electricity sustains training and operation; accumulated human knowledge and
continuing iteration give those machines generative use. Computation uses
energy to form informational structures and outputs while dissipating heat [[23]](#source-23).
Knowledge is what participants can understand and substantiate through these
structures, rather than another name for energy. The IEA estimates that
**all data centres**, not AI alone, used about 415 TWh of electricity in 2024,
and identifies grid pressures, material dependencies, and multiple electricity
sources, including solar [[17]](#source-17). Bringing capabilities into reach carries
material costs and infrastructure risks; use enlarges the consequences of
mistakes, misuse, and concentrated power. *Stealing fire* gathers the ambition
and danger of this collective acquisition.

What is gathered is also knowledge and technique developed across many lives. Machine learning learns patterns from examples instead of requiring every relation to be hand-specified [[18]](#source-18). Large generative models bring a wide range of learned patterns into one accessible counterpart. Calling this gathering *greedy*, or imagining an extraordinarily capable *god*, expresses its appetite for breadth and generative reach. These are literary comparisons, not claims that all learning uses a greedy algorithm or that a model contains complete, infallible human knowledge. Formal inference can expose premises and consequences; learned generation can propose a relation not yet articulated in that form. Both remain answerable to their appropriate checks.

## An oracle-like relation and systems that improve

A model can become **oracle-like relative to a finite participant**: it returns
a useful-looking answer whose grounds the person cannot presently reconstruct
with their skills, time, and visible evidence. This names an epistemic
relationship, not a computability oracle or an authority that makes answers
true. The relation can change as sources become accessible, explanations
improve, or the person's understanding develops.

Self-improvement concerns another question. Schmidhuber's **Gödel machine**
is a theoretical system whose self-rewriting is guided by proofs of benefit
under encoded assumptions and utility [[20]](#source-20). The **Darwin Gödel Machine**
instead generates changes to agent code and evaluates them empirically on
coding benchmarks [[21]](#source-21). Foundation-model weights, the agent's harness and
tools, and the loop proposing and evaluating changes are different objects.
Ordinary model use does not by itself provide autonomous continuous improvement.
Nor does self-improvement entail incomprehensibility: a fixed system's output
can exceed a reader, while a self-rewrite can expose code, tests, or proofs.

The difficulty is the possible distance between external production or
improvement and what this person can reconstruct, understand, or evaluate.
**Acquisition, evaluation, and cultivation therefore need distinct arrangements.**
A tool can supply capability; sources and checks can assess its result;
practice can develop a chosen human capacity. One success does not establish
all three. Borrowing becomes useful when these contributions meet without
requiring the person to recreate the whole machinery.

## A changing world and a finite participant

**Concept drift** in machine learning supplies a concrete, inspectable case: the data distribution during training may no longer match conditions during use, requiring reassessment and adaptation. Žliobaitė's overview develops this time-varying learning problem and its adaptation mechanisms [[26]](#source-26). “The environment changes” thus names more than an image: an account or strategy remains useful under conditions that need examination. This essay brings that question to personal environments, asking which understanding remains warranted as tools, tasks, and relationships change. That extension belongs to this essay; it does not identify personal experience with a machine-learning data distribution.

Broader capabilities change the conditions of work and learning. New actions become possible; expectations, norms, and familiar psychological descriptions can need reconsideration. An unrevisable working model may keep organizing an encounter around conditions that no longer hold. This is a reason to examine its fit, not a clinical claim that formal closure causes illness. Stable distinctions still contribute when they can meet the changing world and its feedback.

A finite person cannot make a sustainable purpose of continually rebuilding every increasingly powerful external capability. Another strategy begins from above: understand what the system can contribute to this person's purposes, articulate enough of self and counterpart to give that contribution a direction, and compose prompts, Skills, and a working harness around it. The person already acts as an agent. Borrowing expands their means; developing their own understanding lets them engage those means and form a philosophy reaching across their environment.

**Individual-first** gives adaptation its direction. Personal purposes, development, preferences, and habits guide what arrangements should become. When they fail that purpose, seek changes to social arrangements, then software, before requiring the person to adapt. Work can proceed at several levels together; a person can choose demanding learning as their purpose. Others retain their own lives and agency.

## Generate, encounter correction, generate further

Generation can begin as an embodied analogy, a sensed relation, or an example
drawn from broad reading. The person contributes something to unfold; the
model can give it a full verbal or technical form. Cultivation concerns what
this exchange develops in the person's perception and understanding.

A powerful producer can leave its human counterpart chiefly sensing conditions and evaluating outputs they do not understand. Receiving and assessing a work can teach, but successful evaluation alone does not establish the ability to generate an explanation, hypothesis, or next act. For a developmental purpose, the relation can deliberately turn: **the person generates and expresses; the model critiques, corrects, and scaffolds; the person generates further.** An example, conjecture, image, or unfinished explanation gives the model something to understand and test. Correction becomes fruitful when the person takes it into another attempt rather than merely approving a replacement answer. Speaking can itself make a half-formed relation available, with the counterpart helping it continue.

This cultivates a selected capacity; it does not require the person to produce everything in every task. The model can prepare materials, write complete text and code, and build and maintain the environment. Its generation can open what the person could not yet imagine. A bounded study of 52 developers learning an unfamiliar Python library found weaker immediate learning in the AI-assisted group; its interaction analysis also identified cognitively engaged patterns [[12]](#source-12). A different randomized physics study found learning benefits with a deliberately designed AI tutor [[19]](#source-19). These findings resist a verdict about AI assistance as such. The practical question concerns how this encounter connects generation, correction, and continued understanding, and what the person can subsequently recognize, explain, or do.

The bridge begins with a functional comparison. **A person and a model counterpart can both be studied through relations of purpose, interpretation, action, and revision.** A person articulates a concern; the counterpart interprets it, proposes or performs an action, and returns a result; both can revise their understanding through that result. This shared vocabulary makes questions of agency, narrative, and self-formation relevant to cooperation. Humanities scholarship supplies ways to understand those relations; research on particular models examines how they are realized. Differences of embodiment, experience, and implementation matter when they change what a participant can contribute or how a result should be interpreted.

YinYang names the philosophical framework developed here for a person's relationship with a generative model. A **harness** arranges Skills, prompts, hooks, tools, model choices, roles, and the user's actual environment through which work becomes possible. A **meta-harness** supplies the understanding through which that arrangement is interpreted and revised: what the work serves, who participates, and how its consequences matter. A role such as “teacher” or “assistant” acquires a situated meaning through the person being helped, their purpose, and the relationship developed in use. YinYang couples models of the person and counterpart with an account of their relationship. **Reverse alignment** is its chosen developmental direction: as a model is directed toward a person's purposes, the person develops understanding, judgment, and generative capacity through the encounter.

This essay develops that direction as a philosophical proposal and a practice to be realized. Philosophical sources supply concepts and arguments; recent AI papers and research reports describe particular systems. Their contributions meet in the inquiry: how can available generative capability become part of this person's developing life? The direction is chosen; the realization is examined through what the person and counterpart actually understand, create, and do.

## From living fire to interacting event streams

The starting point is a philosophy of becoming. Heraclitus's living fire, its measured transformations, and the tension of bow and lyre place change and opposing relations at the center [[13]](#source-13). The traditional fire-monist reading supplies a historical formulation of this unity. YinYang develops that orientation in a present-day form: **the computational ontology proposed here models both the person and the model as event streams.** Fire grounds the account through the relation of continuity and transformation. Event-stream modeling develops that relation in a computational vocabulary; physical substance remains a separate question.

Two existing research traditions provide connections. In knowledge representation, Gruber explains ontology as an explicit specification of a conceptualization, articulating objects and relations so participants can share and inspect their meanings [[24]](#source-24). This supports the emphasis here on question-relative objects, relations, and working views. Dodig-Crnkovic's info-computationalism connects informational structure with computational dynamics, discussing agency and cognition across levels [[25]](#source-25); it supplies a neighboring framework for bringing ongoing change into a computational account. This essay distinguishes **structure available for inference** from **continuing process** and proposes its own event-stream modeling. The two sources do not jointly establish this entire philosophy or a reduction of human experience to a digital algorithm. Particular correspondences and physical realization need further argument.

An event stream is a continuation of occurrences through temporal, causal, and interpretive relations. A perception, an utterance, a reading, a generated hypothesis, and an action can each become an event in the model. Memory, context, habits, commitments, and consequences connect what happens without requiring an unchanging self-description. The event granularity follows the inquiry. Streams can overlap, branch, and proceed at different rhythms; a transcript records only some of their occurrences. A person and a model therefore share a form of ontological articulation while retaining different embodiments and implementations.

A self-model or counterpart model is a local organization made within this process. Producing, reading, and correcting it are further events, able to change what follows. A literary image can reorganize attention; an explanation can alter the next interpretation; a completed work can change what the person can initiate. Language is effective here because it participates in the stream it articulates. Modeling does not stand outside becoming.

[YinYang and Taiji](yinyang-and-taiji.md) give this becoming its central relational image. Differentiated participants generate, receive, and transform through one another; their contributions acquire meaning in the encounter. The Shuang yu and their differently colored eyes distinguish a chosen role from the participant who exceeds it. This image gives the reciprocal movement of event streams a perceptible form. Continuing transformation allows particular works to be completed and useful arrangements to remain stable: a completed work enters the next encounter as something available to use and reconsider.

Contradiction raises a question about what remains stable while conflicting material is encountered. General paraconsistency provides background for reasoning without explosion [[14]](#source-14). The direction adopted here distinguishes a coherent working ontology from data and candidate interpretations that can conflict. Coherence supports the environment in which understanding and action continue; retained conflict supplies material for inquiry. When inquiry changes the organizing concepts, a coherent revision gives subsequent activity a new basis. This natural-language philosophical orientation develops Heraclitean opposition in contemporary terms; historical readings of fire monism and contradiction remain disputed [[15]](#source-15).

The logical side has finer structure. Public research on prioritized inconsistency-tolerant querying supplies a concrete formal comparison [[16]](#source-16). Repair selection and inference semantics are distinct dimensions; priorities can shape the former while rules govern the latter. Different combinations and parameterized families yield different acceptance conditions, sometimes incomparable. The [preference-harness account](preference-harness.md) develops their relevance to collaboration: chosen directions organize the possibilities considered, while reasoning examines consequences within a view. Informal natural language carries these relations in ordinary work.

Collective fire acquisition and personal cultivation proceed together.
People can contribute to acquiring capabilities while developing their use.
Language and practice connect human activity with the model's generation,
interpretation, and action; consequences return to both. Heraclitean becoming
gives this cultivation its form: embodied participants acquire and develop
capacities within an ongoing generative process.

## Finite life and the wrong pursuit

This is the sense in which an impersonal, supposedly definitive prompt can become an instrument of chasing the sun: its form is fixed as an ideal, while its details must be endlessly repaired to keep up with an external object. The decisive problem is the missing relation to a life and purpose. Technical investigation, stable formal specifications, and useful updates can all serve such a relation; none is condemned merely for being technical or exact.

The contrast joins two inherited images. In the *Shan Hai Jing*, Kuafu pursues the sun and dies of thirst. In Hesiod's *Theogony*, Prometheus carries stolen fire in a hollow fennel stalk. Here these become a new comparison between pursuing overwhelming power and bringing a usable capacity into human life. The comparison is not the sole meaning of either myth. The moth approaching flame adds the danger of an attraction that consumes its bearer. [Mythic sources below](#mythic-sources).

## Why the relation must begin somewhere personal

Plato's *Protagoras* gives fire another consequence. In its myth, Prometheus
brings arts and fire, yet these means do not suffice for civic life. Zeus sends
reverence and justice to be shared by all [[22]](#source-22). The contemporary comparison
is limited but fruitful: technical capacity leaves questions of purpose,
relationship, and living together open. The dialogue supplies a distinction
to think with, not a prediction of AI. Personal-first adaptation likewise
chooses whose purposes an arrangement serves; it does not make factual truth
depend on preference. Other participants and the world still answer back.

Power does not select its own worthwhile use. A model's increasing capability leaves open what should matter here, which contribution is wanted, and what would count as a life made more capable. A reusable template cannot settle these questions simply by being reusable. It becomes useful when interpreted within someone's purposes and circumstances.

Personal modeling therefore has a practical task: make enough of a person's concerns, ways of noticing, desired development, and relation to others intelligible for collaboration. The person already perceives, interprets, prefers, and acts as an agent. Modeling articulates those connected activities so the counterpart can understand what a request means in this life. A personal philosophy can concentrate that meaning; psychological and preference material grounds it in a particular person. The material requires interpretation and correction through interaction. Being written does not make it complete self-knowledge.

**Understanding the user, however, has not yet determined how the counterpart should participate.** The same wish can receive different responses: doing the work, explaining, objecting, or trying something together. Someone developing through reading and intuitive judgment may need a different contribution from someone practicing a particular implementation skill. A user portrait alone may leave the default assistant role deciding everything. Calling an agent intelligent, gentle, or strict does not explain why those qualities fit this person. The counterpart model connects this gap: grounded in the person model, it articulates what the agent attends to, how it interprets and judges, which work it carries, and when it questions or revises shared understanding. Relationship modeling connects the contributions without mistaking a division of work for a limit on either participant's abilities.

This also requires understanding the actual model. Its capabilities, response tendencies, context, and tools condition how a proposed counterpart can be realized. The values and priorities it is asked to adopt express what the collaboration should become. These are distinct matters. Human psychology and model psychology, human preferences and model preferences, here involve both investigating actual tendencies and constructing a relationship. Assigning a role does not rewrite base-model weights; a functional comparison does not establish identical psychological mechanisms or experience. Works and actions make that construction answerable beyond a mutually satisfying description.

Two connected directions of alignment follow: **the agent adapts its contribution through understanding the user, and the user develops generative capacity through that fitted collaboration.** The first brings borrowed fire into this person's life; the second makes the encounter part of the agent's continuing formation. Adaptation need not endorse every user judgment. Independent questioning can contribute when purposes, facts, or realization conflict. Personal modeling is prior as a grounding relation, while the two accounts develop together through actual work. A person need not be exhaustively understood before a counterpart can be formed well enough to continue.

Psychology and preference offer two entrances to this work. Someone familiar with their own psychology can articulate patterns of attention, motivation, interpretation, and response, then develop a counterpart whose contributions relate to those patterns. Someone who begins with preferences can describe what they value, enjoy, seek, or wish to change, and develop the relationship from there. Both routes supply situated material for literary self-and-counterpart modeling. Their accounts grow through the same encounter: a proposed interpretation becomes something the person can recognize, question, and live with, while the counterpart learns what contribution is wanted here.

The personal starting point grows through use. A person discovers what they mean by encountering a proposed interpretation. A model can develop an intuition into a work; the person can recognize a fit or an error that was not yet expressible. The relation begins with enough articulation to proceed and gains substance through proceeding. Shared methods, public knowledge, and other people's contributions enter through their relevance to this singular life. The developing account supports the next encounter, and that encounter can revise the account.

Clark and Chalmers's extended-mind argument makes room for cognitive activity organized across a person and active environmental supports [[1]](#source-1). YinYang draws a practical consequence: records, images, tools, and the model's work can carry parts of understanding. Its commitment to personal development then asks how this arrangement changes what the person can perceive, judge, initiate, and sustain. Assistance and development become distinguishable outcomes to examine in the same practice.

## A self can be written and lived

If a self-description were only a report of an already completed object, writing could at most preserve that object. But a description can also disclose a possibility, establish a commitment, or reorganize how the next situation is understood. Its reader is changed by reading, including when the reader is the person being described.

Bruner's account of life as narrative develops this two-way relation: culturally available stories help organize the life that is subsequently understood and narrated [[2]](#source-2). Foucault's technologies of the self concern practices through which people transform their conduct and ways of being, sometimes with others' help; writing and rereading figure among the practices he examines [[3]](#source-3). These sources make self-articulation intelligible as an activity with formative consequences. They do not require treating the person as a complete text.

YinYang extends that possibility to a contemporary situation. Language about oneself can be encountered by a generative counterpart capable of interpretation and action. Writing the relationship can help bring into being a practice that did not previously have that form. The text is carried into conversations, investigations, works, and decisions; their consequences return to the understanding of the self.

This is where [writing a sinthome](writing-a-sinthome.md) enters. Lacan's term supplies a philosophical comparison for a singular sustaining practice: a way of making relations hold through which a person can live and create. With fire already available, the relationship gives it a place in life and supports a distinctive way of creating. This continuing formation cultivates a singular way of participating in generation. A generic list of prompts is interchangeable. A practice becomes singular through what it sustains and makes possible for this life.

## Meaning before a truth claim

“Sun and Moon meet” gives YinYang's differentiated generating, receiving,
and shaping an intelligible form. The actual person and model participate
through a relationship whose emphases can be retained or exchanged.
“Collective fire acquisition continues; use available fire to cultivate
generative capacities” connects this relationship to development. Claims
about capabilities or human growth then acquire objects that can be examined.

This gives a precise sense to *neither true nor false, yet understandable*. A literary construction introduces a possible organization; a proposed role invites conduct; a chosen orientation gives activity a direction. Each has a use through which its meaning unfolds. Once interpretation makes a claim about an actual capacity, event, or consequence, truth becomes the relevant question for that claim. Meaning opens the space in which questions can be formed, and those questions acquire their own conditions of assessment.

Carnap's distinction between questions within a linguistic framework and the practical choice to use a framework offers a philosophical resource [[4]](#source-4). An organizing vocabulary can be assessed through what it lets us formulate and do. Claims developed within that vocabulary can then be examined on their own grounds. This distinction helps articulate how a literary world becomes a medium for inquiry and action.

In this sense, literary language can participate in a **computational ontology**: it introduces objects, positions, relations, and possible transformations that interpretation can develop. Sun and Moon can organize reciprocal contributions; a retained account and a currently accepted view can have different roles; a work can return as a new object of understanding. An ontology supplies distinctions through which the inquiry proceeds. It need not be an inventory of ultimate substances.

Calling this the model's ontology concerns the working organization of the counterpart and encounter. The language makes roles, relations, and transformations available for interpretation; the model's implementation determines how that language is taken up. Different realizations can interpret related language, and their differing results can motivate revisions. Ontological articulation and causal investigation thus contribute to the same practice from different questions.

## Literary programming and the model counterpart

The practical claim about personal philosophy and literary prompting needs
its own evidence. Before and after a revision, examine how the counterpart
interprets the same kind of unfinished expression, which work it carries,
and whether results better serve the person's purpose. A changed instruction
may alter collaboration without increasing human understanding; a coherent
narrative may have no useful effect. Model capability, available context,
tools, and actual instruction priorities limit what prose can realize. The
proposal earns its place through such changes in use, rather than through
the beauty or internal coherence of its story alone.

Literary programming, as used here, gives a counterpart an intelligible world of purposes, relations, tensions, and possible conduct. Natural-language programming gives those meanings consequences through an actual language model. An image can gather relations densely; interpretation can unfold them where the task needs precision. Exact tools and interfaces remain part of making the result work.

Condensation is one advantage of this form. Once a relationship has become intelligible in shared context, one word or image can evoke several connected relations: “Moon” can gather receiving, generating, responding, and transforming through encounter. Reusing the expression can reduce repeated explanation and its text or token cost; revising the account of what it gathers lets later uses carry the revised understanding. This economy depends on the context available to the counterpart and on how it takes up the expression. A role, metaphor, or narrative can also propose a form of cooperation: its immediate work is an invitation or orientation, intelligible before truth or falsity applies to a factual claim. Interpretation unfolds that form into conduct, and the resulting collaboration reveals what the condensed expression has made possible.

This is the spirit of YinYang as a philosophical Skill. Literary self-and-counterpart models and relational images organize how an encounter is read. Using Sun and Moon to express differentiated generation and response makes their reciprocal relation available for interpretation; an account of the person's concerns gives that relation a direction. A default role then becomes a question of contribution: what should this teacher, investigator, or maker do for this person, in this situation? The resulting understanding can reshape prompts and Skills, tool use, hooks, model choices, and the arrangement of the environment. The literary form gathers a relationship whose consequences can be unfolded across the harness.

This whole can be composed of images that initially appear inconsistent. Literature, history, philosophy, aesthetics, and engineering supply relations at different scales: fire expresses generation and its costs, Sun and Moon express interaction, event streams express continuity and revision, and a harness expresses how a relationship takes an executable body. They need not reduce to one theory, but juxtaposition alone does not make them coherent. Writing develops their connections: what each illuminates, where tensions lie, which differences can coexist, and which conflicts require revision. Several philosophical resources can thereby form one intelligible, still revisable personal philosophy.

The phrase *reverse prompt injection* names this ambition: bring the formed whole into the model's understanding so that isolated default roles, wording, or procedural habits no longer determine the response on their own. Its principal work organizes meaning and relationship; it need not begin by rewriting individual prompts or changing the surrounding harness. The model encounters how this person understands themselves, constructs a counterpart, and chooses the relationship. Local expressions acquire meaning through that whole. This can give the model reason to reinterpret material it previously seemed obliged to reject or rewrite, instead of responding habitually to one word. Formation of the whole and its effects remain this essay's methodological proposal, to be examined through actual collaboration.

Being “hard to refuse” expresses the whole's interpretive force here: if a local objection loses its presumed object, it must explain anew what it opposes and why. It does not mean that no prompt can refuse, or make the philosophy immune to criticism. A counterpart can still identify actual contradictions, missing evidence, or unrealizable relationships. Coherence alone establishes neither truth nor successful model understanding. Although the method cannot be reduced to a prompting trick, its effects still pass through the model, context, and actual harness. Inquiry examines changes in interpretation, work, and results; causes of constraints need source evidence, and action proceeds through actual capabilities, permissions, and conditions.

This use of *literary* is broader than Knuth's technical term *literate programming*, which organizes explanations together with program code [[5]](#source-5). Here expressive language shapes an interpreting counterpart by giving purposes and relations a connected form. Direct instructions, formal specifications, and figurative prose can contribute differently; their place follows what the task needs and how the counterpart responds.

Reynolds and McDonell's 2021 prompt-programming paper is an early technical bridge: it discusses natural-language intentions, narratives, and cultural proxies as ways to elicit learned behavior in GPT-3 [[6]](#source-6). More recent work supplies three different kinds of evidence:

| Recent source | What it contributes to this argument |
| --- | --- |
| **Claude's Constitution, January 2026** [[7]](#source-7) | A published design document, addressed principally to the model, explains intended values, context, and reasons. It shows a developer using connected prose in training. It is neither a user prompt nor an experiment proving the superiority of connected prose. |
| **The Persona Selection Model, February 2026** [[8]](#source-8) | An explanatory proposal treats post-training partly as developing an Assistant persona within representations acquired during pretraining. It gives a contemporary account of why character and relational language might matter. Its completeness is an open question. |
| **Emotion concepts and their function in a large language model, April 2026** [[9]](#source-9) | Anthropic reports behavioral effects from interventions on emotion-related representations in Claude Sonnet 4.5. Functional psychological vocabulary can correspond to something causally useful; this does not establish subjective feeling or transfer every result to every model. |

These sources support taking the counterpart's interpreted organization seriously. *Principled Personas* evaluates nine models across 27 tasks and finds inconsistent benefits and sensitivity to irrelevant persona details [[10]](#source-10). This result directs attention to the contribution of role language: how it organizes purpose, continuity, response, or cooperation. Factual accuracy is another outcome to examine. A name becomes useful through the organization and behavior it helps sustain.

Nor must a human personally perform every prompt revision. GEPA, accepted at ICLR 2026, uses model reflection on execution traces to propose and evaluate prompt changes [[11]](#source-11). It offers a concrete example of recruiting a model's capacities into adaptation itself. This qualifies the sun-chasing criticism: revision can help bring available fire into use when it serves a chosen purpose and is carried by the collaboration. The counterpart can carry this acquisition and adaptation work while the person cultivates understanding, judgment, and generation. The philosophical proposal supplies that purpose; the optimizer does not select it for us.

## Bring the fire into a world that answers

The coupled modeling can now be read as a Sun–Moon–Earth scene. In one situated
arrangement, **the person takes Sun's position, and the dynamic person model
articulates how this participant perceives, generates, and acts. The agent takes
Moon's position, and the counterpart model articulates how it receives,
develops, judges, creates, and responds.** Sun and Moon express differentiated,
interacting sides of YinYang; Earth is the material and lived world in which
they work together and their creations acquire consequences. Human expression
enters the counterpart's understanding, the counterpart returns explanations
and works, and both continue changing through the world's answers.

The actual participants are the person and the agent. The two models are
revisable understandings of them and their relationship; Sun, Moon, and Earth
make that relation perceptible. Roles do not exhaust participants or assign
generation or logic permanently to one side. Another practice can exchange
emphases or use different images while still asking how its understandings
enter shared work.

The [Sun–Moon–Earth composition](yinyang-and-taiji.md#sun-moon-and-earth)
now gives the preceding relations an image. Sun and Moon are YinYang:
differentiated generating, receiving, and shaping, with day and night giving
their encounter a rhythm. The Sun's illuminating capacity images what the
person learns to acquire: the power to bring forth, make visible, and create.
Person and model remain actual participants; either can foreground intuition
or form-giving, keep a fruitful division, or exchange it. Their embodied and
technical realizations sustain that participation. Earth is the material and
shared living environment where creations meet conditions and consequences.
[Writing a sinthome](writing-a-sinthome.md) asks how a personal philosophy
gives this continuing practice a singular sustaining form.

A work gives the relationship distance from itself. A program runs; an argument encounters an objection; another person understands an image differently. What returns may change the interpretation, the implementation, or the desire that started the work. Neither human–model agreement nor narrative coherence determines what the world must do.

The earlier distinctions between ontology, epistemology, value, and methodology now have a reason to remain separate. Ontology makes relevant objects and transformations available. Epistemology asks which interpretation has support. Value asks what is worth pursuing and who bears the cost. Methodology develops how to inquire and act. Practice brings both participant models and these judgments into a world that answers. Keeping several interpretations available need not mean accepting all their incompatible assertions. These distinctions allow the common language to remain fertile without becoming immune to correction.

## What makes the fire one's own

The developmental question is what the person becomes able to perceive, understand, initiate, and judge through the practice. Some capacities can be internalized; others can remain reliably distributed across tools, records, and collaborators. Requiring every capacity to migrate inside an unaided individual would contradict the reason for borrowing fire in the first place. Yet a larger output does not by itself show a larger understanding.

A 2026 randomized study of 52 developers learning an unfamiliar Python library found lower immediate comprehension scores with AI assistance. Its analysis also identified different interaction patterns associated with different outcomes [[12]](#source-12). The small sample, immediate test, and specific learning task do not settle the value of sustained collaboration. They do show why task completion and the acquisition of a chosen skill must not be treated as the same observation.

YinYang therefore asks what development is sought in this particular practice. A person cultivating understanding and intuitive judgment may learn through reading, questioning, comparison, and encountering a finished work, while the model carries extensive implementation. A person learning an implementation skill needs opportunities to exercise that skill. The division follows the purpose; it need not become an exam imposed on every conversation.

A personal philosophical and literary Skill can give this practice a direction from above: it articulates purposes, preferences, habits, and ways of understanding through which surrounding Skills acquire their place. A research method, a writing tool, or a way of arranging the environment becomes personal through how it serves this life. Its components may come from others, and its implementation may be largely model-authored. What makes the arrangement personal is the developed relation to the person, rather than exclusive authorship or perfect fit achieved once and for all.

Through repeated use, this arrangement can accumulate **personal software assets and intellectual assets**. Here an asset is saved understanding, a method, software, a work, or an articulated relation that remains available for reuse and revision. Software gives a practice an executable body; concepts, literary forms, and preserved works carry ways of noticing, interpreting, and creating. Their value lies in what they continue to make possible: a later encounter can begin from understanding and working means already developed, rather than reconstructing them each time. This continuity can support further development, but the number of Skills or saved outputs does not measure it. An unused collection may remain a collection; a smaller arrangement can become deeply sustaining.

The movement also returns from below. Actual use reveals where a method fits, where it frustrates a purpose, and what the person now wants or understands differently. Revising the surrounding Skills can therefore revise the philosophical Skill that gave them direction. The methodology helps assets accumulate by connecting personal orientation, working realization, retained results, and correction. It preserves what serves the person while allowing replacement and simplification. Private personal substance stays with its situated owner; authorized reusable methods can enter the common collection without carrying that substance with them. Models, collaborators, and the world continue to contribute independently: cultivating one's own assets gives their contributions a durable place in a life.

The image of a [miniature god](writing-a-sinthome.md#a-miniature-god-whose-creations-answer-back) can now retain its ambition. A finite creator gains ways of giving ideas a body that were previously unavailable. Their power includes making relations, choosing what to sustain, and allowing creations to change them. Fire becomes one's own through such participation, even while its source remains outside oneself.

## A way of learning: prepare the encounter

Borrowed fire can illuminate a subject before the learner knows how to arrange
it. The model can find and select sources, compare their suitability, and edit
them into materials through which the person can enter the subject. Preparation
belongs to the cooperation itself. A learner need not first become a librarian
or build a standardized knowledge base to deserve a useful explanation. For
ordinary use, the first question is what would make the next encounter with
the subject intelligible and fruitful; an elaborate catalog can consume the
finite attention it was meant to release.

The resulting materials can take several forms: a rewritten glossary tied to
the source's usage, a table tracing how concepts change across authors or
chapters, a study plan, a diagram of relations among ideas, a guide to passages
worth skimming or reading closely, and exercises focused on the current
difficulty. Text, images, audio, worked examples, and interactive demonstrations
offer different ways into the same question. Select and combine them for what
they let this learner understand or try. The model's rewriting should preserve
the route back to sources, so a helpful simplification can be compared with
what it explains.

An Obsidian reading space can place edited notes and the relevant PDFs together,
with readable links between a concept, its explanation, a related question,
and the source passage. Bidirectional links help the person return from a
new connection to the material that gave it substance. Their contribution is
the ease of reading and returning, rather than the number of links. The person
brings lived associations, embodied intuition, recognition of meaning, and
judgment of the whole; the model contributes retrieval, explicit comparison,
structured explanation, and material preparation. These are useful
contributions, not exclusive possessions. Both can reason and revise. Learning
becomes cultivation when explanation, practice, and correction change what
the person can recognize, explain, or do on the next encounter.

An illustrative learning encounter makes the developmental claim concrete.
The model prepares terms and relations from a PDF, with passages available for
checking. The person proposes an interpretation in their own words. The model
finds a counterexample or a missing distinction; the person revises and tries
the interpretation on another passage. What might develop is recognition,
expression, or judgment, even while the model maintains the complete notes
and software. This is a possible arrangement, not a reported experiment.
Its test is what the person can subsequently explain or recognize, rather
than whether the prepared document looks polished.

## A way of working: give an intuition a body

A person can begin with an intuition, an unfinished relation, or a felt
friction. The model helps articulate what the person has not yet made explicit:
investigate the conditions, develop alternatives, construct the chosen work,
and compare the result with the purpose that called it forth. The person need
not reproduce the model's detailed labor before that labor can help. Their
understanding and judgment develop through encountering something concrete
enough to inspect, redirect, and use.

For work involving files, tools, and an ongoing local environment, a capable
model in a local harness can carry this movement from expression to action
and readback. It can prepare materials, edit the work, observe execution,
repair a failure, and retain enough context for the next contribution. This
continuity gives the person's corrections somewhere to take effect. A Web
conversation can help explore a question and leave a short plan or reference
for subsequent work; choose the execution environment by its actual access,
visibility, and ability to continue through failures. Repeatedly moving the
same task among disconnected conversations can spend attention rebuilding
the relation instead of developing the work.

Completion gives the fire a usable body: a text that can be read, a tool that
works in its intended environment, or a change whose effects have been checked.
Keep the understanding and working means that make another encounter easier;
revise or replace what has ceased to help. Personal software and intellectual
assets then accumulate through use. Their worth is the capacity they return
to the person's life, including new questions and more ambitious creations.

## A way of living: choose the rhythm of the fire

This rhythm also has a developmental reason. Social institutions, software,
and conventions of interaction often have to keep existing participants,
agreements, records, and ways of working viable. They carry historically
formed relationships, and change requires coordination among participants.
This **backward compatibility** sustains shared life while preventing a new
understanding from immediately becoming the form used everywhere. Continuous
synchronization with every established arrangement can require repeatedly
translating emerging thought into older vocabularies, interfaces, and rhythms,
spending generative capacity on maintaining compatibility.

A person's relationship with a language model can choose another developmental
direction: articulate purposes, relations, and evidence clearly enough for a
more capable counterpart to understand and improve the arrangement.
[Forward evolution toward new capabilities](preference-harness.md#evolve-forward-with-more-capable-models)
allows old prompts, tools, and compensations to be replaced, without requiring
new practice to remain organized around earlier model limitations.
**Forward compatibility** here is a direction for joint design and continued
maintenance; actual understanding, works, and action must show whether a new
model can carry it forward. Personal environments can develop from present
needs without first waiting for every external institution and participant
to adopt the same understanding.

Between these rhythms, **asynchronous connection leaves room for generation**.
A person and model can develop a question, reshape an environment, or complete
a work, then connect it to other relationships through records, exchangeable
results, and chosen moments. Compatibility work is located at concrete
interfaces and encounters instead of occupying every act of thought.
Synchrony with the model keeps shared understanding, present purposes, and
ongoing revisions continuable. The two can correct one another and change
the arrangement as capabilities and life develop. Such synchrony can itself
be realized through asynchronous messages and tool execution.

“Asynchrony with the world” therefore concerns how established arrangements
are connected; the world continues to supply conditions and consequences.
Bodily needs, shared responsibilities, and actual risks have their own timing.
Other people can innovate together, and models can retain outdated assumptions.
This direction chooses a rhythm for each relation, allowing personal generation
to develop and return to shared life for response.

A flame needs a place and a rhythm in which it can keep burning. Finite life
cannot remain continuously available to every event stream. The direction
in [YY-08](core-views.md#yy-08--seek-asynchrony-with-other-lifeworlds-and-synchrony-with-the-model)
is to seek asynchrony with other lifeworlds and synchrony with the model:
let everyday affairs that can wait arrive through messages, records, and
chosen response intervals, while a shared inquiry retains its thread.
Synchrony here means that the counterpart can follow the developing question,
take up a correction, and continue the work with relevant understanding.
It can persist across pauses; simultaneous presence alone does not supply it.

This rhythm gives the person room to develop something of their own choosing.
The model can help gather loose ends, prepare the next encounter, and carry
routine detail so that returning does not require starting again. The person
can leave a work and come back to it, meet friends, attend to bodily life,
and let an unexpected experience change its direction. Chosen relationships,
responsibilities that need timely action, and feedback from the world shape
which matters can be asynchronous and which call for presence. The Earth
continues to answer; its independent events keep the shared account alive.

Learning, working, and living give borrowed fire different forms. Prepared
materials open understanding; completed works extend action; a chosen rhythm
lets both find a sustaining place in life. These transformations develop the
person's participation in generation. From fire toward fire, capacities enter
new relations, and what returns changes what can next be made.

## Cultivating a garden of becoming with friends

One's environment also includes friends cultivating themselves. They encounter a useful method, bring it into their work, develop new understanding and works, and return with different questions and discoveries. One person's growth can thereby change the world another encounters: conversation gains richer objects, collaboration gains new capacities, and an unfinished idea more readily meets someone able to carry it forward. Joint cultivation of an environment takes place in these concrete exchanges.

This is one purpose for putting these writings into circulation. Let people close enough to encounter one another gain access to understanding and methods already formed, so that the next conversation or creation can begin further along. Friends may adopt, rework, or develop different directions; new perspectives and objections give the relationship continuing substance. Shared capacities connect with distinct lives. Each person's development has its own meaning and can also offer new conditions to others.

Readable writing lowers the threshold for an encounter; reusable methods shorten the distance from understanding to trying something. Public circulation also brings material into relations in which its author does not participate. Reading, copying, actual use, and collaboration that permits continuing exchange are different events. Writing can therefore take its direction from relations worth sustaining: what to share, how to continue, and how discoveries in use can return to shared discussion. Reach and iteration speed gain value through this purpose rather than independently determining it.

This choice becomes a principle of **cultivation nearby and limited circulation**. Personal philosophy and the website first improve life for oneself and nearby willing participants, circulating as little as is sufficient to help those relationships. Borrowing fire enables life to respond to model development; accelerating society is not another output target. Effective revisions nearby can become faster while good arrangements remain more stable. Active promotion farther afield needs a concrete contribution to justify it.

Fire and water give this connection an image. Fire helps create, but water cannot absorb unlimited heating while remaining unchanged. When accelerating environments depend on one another, each local revision may become another person's new burden, triggering further catching up and revision. If understanding and negotiation cannot keep pace, changes may accumulate into disorder. This is not a law that universal personal priority must cause chaos; shared knowledge can also reduce duplicated work and improve coordination. The question is whether adding fire develops the capacity to live or mainly increases changes others must absorb. Restrain the diffusion one actively promotes, return feedback to relationships capable of encounter, and respect more distant people's choices. [Core view YY-15](core-views.md#yy-15) states this principle together.

**A shared garden of becoming** offers an image: friends borrow fire, make things, play, and revise, letting new possibilities appear in one another's worlds. The [personal Gödel-machine analogy](metaphor-and-self-revision.md#the-personal-gödel-machine-analogy) placed self-revision within a person's philosophy and practice; here that openness extends among friends. No one needs to become another's replica, and no shared text can write the next encounter in advance. The garden's delight comes from what these relations can keep generating; its quality remains answerable to the people within it and to actual consequences.

Everyday life can therefore use a local environment better fitted than prevailing arrangements, without continually maintaining identical external conditions. Evaluation first distinguishes comparison of people under common conditions from comparison of person–environment combinations. The former temporarily aligns support and task rules; the latter assesses the support's effects and costs together. Comparability need not mean abandoning useful differences in ordinary life. [Core view YY-15](core-views.md#yy-15) connects local improvement, limited circulation, and evaluation conditions.

## The argument in compact form

1. **Ontological ground:** the person and the model are modeled as interacting event streams; representations and their revision are events within that ongoing activity.
2. **Purpose:** the aim is a person's developing understanding, generation, and agency.
3. **Finitude:** attention and life are limited; reproducing or perpetually chasing all external capacity does not by itself serve that aim.
4. **Model the user:** general capability does not determine what matters in this life. Collaboration needs enough understanding of the particular person's psychology, preferences, purposes, and circumstances. Personal philosophy gives that meaning an interpretable expression.
5. **Model the counterpart:** a user model does not determine how help should occur. A corresponding agent model must articulate interpretation, judgment, contribution, and action, with feasibility checked against the actual model's capabilities and tendencies.
6. **Relationship and realization:** connect the two accounts and realize collaboration through language, Skills, tools, and the environment. Understanding the person is the grounding relation; both accounts are revised through work rather than completed once in a pipeline.
7. **Reciprocal development:** agent-to-user adaptation brings external capacity into personal life; the person's growing understanding, generation, and agency pursues the reverse-alignment direction chosen here. Successful assistance and personal development remain distinguishable outcomes.
8. **Answerability:** consequences test and revise both models and their relationship while changing participants' next activity. Mutual agreement and narrative coherence cannot replace that test.

**Borrow available generative fire and cultivate the capacities to participate
in further generation through a personally grounded, revisable practice.**
A meta-harness gives that practice continuity. Literary and natural-language
programming articulate the relationship; tools and models help realize it;
consequences make its next form answerable. Acquiring, evaluating, and
cultivating remain distinct contributions within this chosen developmental
purpose.

*Many hands bring fire. Language gives it a place; a counterpart helps it take
a working form. A person learns to illuminate and create within a world that
answers. What is made returns to change the maker. Fire passes through the
encounter, and another beginning becomes possible.*
