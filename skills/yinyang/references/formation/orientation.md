# How to articulate an adopted orientation

[简体中文](orientation.zh-CN.md) · [Philosophy and models](../design-and-revision.md)

The orientation account states the philosophy, values, and direction adopted
for this collaboration. Write its actual content at
`references/situated/orientation.md` in the private package. This public guide
remains available alongside it. The maintained implementation has six actual
files: orientation, person, counterpart, relationship, environment, and revision.
Someone authoring a different work can change that organization and philosophy.

## Give the work a direction with reasons

Explain what this collaboration serves, what matters in judging its effects,
and which understanding of persons, models, and the world informs that choice.
An adopted orientation connects these claims: why a valued possibility calls
for this kind of participation, what would count as progress, and which tensions
remain. A slogan or list of values leaves those relations unwritten.

YinYang offers a developed example: reverse alignment cultivates a person's
understanding, judgment, generation, and action through encounters with models;
individual-first gives the person's purposes and chosen development priority
in their practice; a changing natural world answers what they make. The
[core views](../docs/core-views.md) locate its propositions and the essays
develop their arguments. State what is actually adopted and why. Reading an
essay does not establish adoption, and philosophical coherence does not prove
a personal explanation. A recipient may choose another direction or philosophy.

The account can begin from a practical purpose or an unfinished philosophical
intuition. The model can help develop its implications, compare alternatives,
and write the selected understanding. The person need not first deliver a
finished theory. Preserve genuine unresolved questions instead of filling a
slot with commitments nobody has chosen.

## Connect orientation with situated understanding

The [person](person.md), [counterpart](counterpart.md), and
[relationship](relationship.md) remain three distinct subjects of understanding. The
orientation supplies their adopted interpretive and evaluative direction;
the [environment](environment.md) explains its working realization, and
[revision](revision.md) guides the writing of one's actual revision method.
These files have different responsibilities. A value commitment differs
from an explanation of a person's experience, and an environment differs from
either participant.

For example, choosing to cultivate judgment can favor comparing arguments in
one activity while delegating routine implementation in another. Making the
person perform every step would not follow from that aim. Explain the relevant
capacity and participation, then let the relationship account develop the
division of work. Preserve the orientation's reason here rather than repeating
the full philosophy across all six actual files.

Use explicit model-facing prose under the [writing method](revision.md#write-for-the-actual-use).
An image can make a relation intelligible; state the meaning that should guide
judgment and action. Keep developed public arguments at their existing owners,
with private reasons and attribution in the [evidence material](revision.md#sources-and-evidence).

## Revise the direction through what happens

A completed output, relief, improved collaboration, and developing personal
capacity support different claims. Judge the purpose actually chosen. Work
may expose a poor realization, a mistaken account of the person, or a reason to
reconsider the direction itself. Revise the affected relation with its grounds;
neither the newest phrase nor a successful tool operation settles the whole.
