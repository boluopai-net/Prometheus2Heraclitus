# How to organize evidence and its private owners

[简体中文](sources.zh-CN.md) · [Formation method](../design-and-revision.md)

This guide supports all five core accounts. Evidence is their grounding, not a
sixth core account or a substitute for an adopted orientation. Maintain the
actual private evidence entrance at `references/personal/sources.md`; keep
useful topical support in that private tree or with its existing owners. Public
distribution supplies this method and no personal source index. Ordinary initial
reading covers the five accounts; retrieve evidence when the question needs it.

## Explain what the evidence can establish

Select sources because they bear on current understanding. Attributable user
expression, personal writing, conversations, memory, observed work, and
philosophy contribute differently. Retain source identity, time, the relevant
passage or message, and enough context to assess a consequential claim. Where
a maintained summary stands between the original and an account, explain that
relation; its omissions can matter to a disputed interpretation.

A person's report supports what they expressed or experienced in context;
it does not establish every explanation offered with it. Observed work can
show what happened under inspected conditions while leaving motive unknown.
A chosen commitment states how work should proceed; behavior establishes its
realization. Philosophy supplies concepts and reasons, suggests interpretations,
or expresses an adopted orientation without proving a personal mechanism.

For a fictional example, someone reports that a reading arrangement feels
easier. That supports experienced ease. A record showing the passage was reached
supports access. Neither establishes lasting learning or which interface change
caused the improvement. Later participation on a new case can add evidence at
that larger scale. Several accounts repeating one summary do not add independent
support.

## Keep time, conflict, and inference recoverable

Date changing conditions at the level that matters. A tool available last month,
a temporary workload, and a continuing commitment have different scopes. A
later statement may correct an interpretation, describe a new situation, or
introduce unresolved conflict. Its recency alone does not decide which. Retain
the conflicting evidence instead of smoothing it into a consistent portrait.

Make consequential inference traceable in prose: what expression or observation
suggests it, why, and what uncertainty limits use. A working link establishes
reachability, not correspondence with a claim. An inaccessible source leaves
a coverage limit. Original private materials need not be copied merely because
their accounts are maintained together.

## Maintain one useful private entrance

The private index locates the grounds of orientation, person, counterpart,
relationship, and environment with enough explanation to know why to look.
Keep extended episodes, precise excerpts, and unique originals at their actual
owners. Derived private notes state source, scope, interpretation, and relevant
consequence explicitly under the [writing method](../design-and-revision.md#write-for-the-actual-use).
Filenames follow the actual material; no empty companion is required for every
essay. Public philosophical references stay near their arguments.

Refresh private distillations from their public conceptual owner, preserving
private evidence rather than another copy of the theory. Revise when grounds
change, access fails, or correction changes interpretation. Retire redundant
active material while preserving useful originals through their owners. Source
maintenance does not authorize collecting a biography, exposing private paths,
or publishing personal quotations or unreleased works. Return only authorized
reusable insights to the public package.
