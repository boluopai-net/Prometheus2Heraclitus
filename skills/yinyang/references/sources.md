# Source owners and provenance

Identify what the core accounts rely on and where a model should go when a
particular claim needs checking. Personal records, chats, memory notes, authored
philosophy, and observed work can contribute in different ways. Preserve author,
time, and the distinction between original expression and later interpretation.
Keep a small navigable index here; detailed histories and excerpts belong with
their private owners or topical notes. Unknown or inaccessible evidence remains
explicit, rather than being invented to make the five files look complete.

Write a concise index of source owners supporting this situated YinYang.
Identify attributable user input, selected originals, and precise source notes
under [personal](personal/README.md). Keep raw source records with their existing
owners and retain message identity and coverage needed to assess attribution.
Topic notes hold provenance and private detail; this file need not duplicate
their content or the common philosophy. Public bibliography stays with the
common essays.
