# How to develop a user model

This public copy explains how to develop a grounded account of the actual
person for a situated YinYang package. The account helps a counterpart
understand their activity, interpret requests, and contribute fittingly. Its
object is a person acting in particular situations, with purposes, capacities,
experiences, and changing conditions. A collection of traits would miss how
these relations affect one another. The person always exceeds the account.

Someone borrowing this method can develop only the understanding their current
work needs. Full adoption uses this account alongside the other four under the
[formation method](design-and-revision.md). Neither route requires a complete
biography, clinical explanation, or personal philosophy project before useful
work begins.

## Understand activity in its situation

Start from relevant attributable expression and actual participation. A
conversation about a difficult decision may reveal a purpose and competing
concerns; a correction during implementation may reveal a mistaken division of
work; an established practice may show a capacity or useful support. Selected
personal writing, psychological or preference material, and philosophical
expression may deepen understanding where relevant and available. Enter through
the more developed material and check it against the others. An available
archive is not a reason to read everything in it.

Develop the connections that explain a real difference in collaboration. What
is the person trying to understand or accomplish? What do they contribute
through recognition, generation, judgment, expression, and action? Which
conditions support or obstruct that activity? What do they want to keep,
change, or learn? These questions orient connected prose; they are not fields
every person must fill. Explain supported relationships and keep an unknown
relation unknown.

In a fictional example, someone readily develops an argument in conversation
but finds turning it into a finished document costly. Several accounts are
possible: dialogue helps ideas take shape; the editing interface is cumbersome;
or this particular task has little value for them. Relevant expression and work
can distinguish those possibilities. If dialogue is a useful support, the model
can help structure and write while the person generates, recognizes, and revises
ideas. That division establishes neither inability to write nor a reason to
prefer speech in every situation.

## Preserve the grounds of an interpretation

A reported experience, explanatory interpretation, chosen preference, and
developmental intention support different conclusions. “This exchange felt
interruptive” reports an experience. “Every interruption destroys my attention”
extends its scope. A commitment to carry implementation details is a chosen
response. Preserve the differences where a future counterpart could otherwise
turn a local observation into a standing rule.

The same arrangement can result from preference, constraint, habit, or a
default. A tool in daily use does not by itself show why the person uses it.
Compare their explanation, relevant corrections, alternatives actually available,
and effects in work. When the distinction would change the result and evidence
cannot settle it, a focused inquiry can obtain the missing experience or purpose.
The model carries interpretation and writing rather than requiring the person
to deliver a finished self-description.

A preference model approached through engineering describes articulated or observed habits,
choices, working styles, and desired responses sufficiently to guide an arrangement. A psychological
model tentatively connects experience, emotion, desire, interpretation, and
relationships in the person's life. Preferences can be evidence for it, but do
not alone supply that explanation. The [formation method](design-and-revision.md#form-a-situated-version-from-actual-material)
develops both routes through dialogue, synthesis, and actual trials, connecting
the user account with a fitting counterpart and relationship. A philosophical
image can suggest a connection; coherence does not prove it in this life. Use
psychological vocabulary at the strength the evidence permits, without turning
interpretation into diagnosis or chosen support into deficit.

## Let understanding change the work

Connect the account to the counterpart and relationship: what contribution
follows, what remains a candidate, and what use could correct. The
[counterpart-and-relationship guide](relationship.md) develops that writing duty;
the [encounter guide](encounter.md) helps condense the needed whole. Use the
[sources-and-grounds guide](sources.md) to keep source identity, time, conflicting
evidence, and access limits navigable. Extended episodes and personal details
stay with their private owners.

Date changing conditions when time matters. Revise a claim when the person
corrects it, circumstances change, or use exposes poor fit. Consider whether
failure concerns understanding, the chosen relationship, or its realization
before rewriting the person to fit a tool. Better task results support a
practical arrangement; enduring learning or development needs evidence at that
larger scale. The account serves actual life and work while remaining open to
what it has not understood.
