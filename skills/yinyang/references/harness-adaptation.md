# Realizing YinYang in a working harness

Use this reference when the intended result or an observed mismatch calls for
changing how the model works. A harness includes the instructions, methods,
tools, and environment shaping that work. YinYang's meta-harness supplies an
understanding of the participants, relationship, and developmental purpose;
adaptation gives selected relations a working form.

An entry can be as modest as “the notes are all there, but returning to the work
still takes too much effort.” The person need not first explain the mechanism,
select an instruction file, or supply a formed personal model. Recover the
intended activity and inspect what currently happens. Where relevant accounts
already exist, read their affected passages and the complete target instructions
alongside that behavior. Use [design and revision](design-and-revision.md) when
the understanding itself needs development. The task owner carries interpretation,
implementation, and completion through the target's existing owners. A request
to adapt an environment needs its working result, not only a portrait.
Reading YinYang alone does not authorize unrelated changes.

The reasoning below can also be borrowed for one repair without adopting this
philosophy or maintaining a situated Skill. Use the actual environment's
instructions, tools, and maintenance methods; an accessible conversation or
editable document may be enough. Full adoption retains the package's grounded
accounts and initial reading. Neither use requires the author's particular
computer, named tools, or personal arrangement.

## Relation to authoring and management methods

YinYang owns the situated understanding and purpose being expressed, including
its ontological and epistemic distinctions. Its formation and adaptation methods
connect that understanding with actual cooperation and environment design.
Specialist methods own how a capability is researched, taught, written,
implemented, or maintained. Where available, `prompt-and-skill-lifecycle`
supplies the connected method for authoring, reviewing, packaging, publishing,
and reading back prompts and Skills. Resolve it through the current host's
catalog; a named method may have no callable carrier on another host.

The lifecycle method connects purpose, relational form, and working realization.
It helps make the understanding usable in explicit instructions; its source and
consumer mechanics maintain that meaning in use. Reusable technique stays there. Understanding of this
person and relation stays here and in the situated accounts. Keep a short
relation and locator between owners instead of copying either's procedures or
portraits.

Actual consumer contracts determine operational roles. An advisory counterpart
can explore and suggest; the execution owner judges and realizes the result.
Shared philosophy does not erase that difference. The main task owner supplies
bounded relevant requirements to helpers under the entrypoint's personal-model
boundary. Host model selection, authority, and publication retain their scope.

## Choose the focus and trace both directions

Derive the repair focus from the user's purpose and actual problem. Distinguish
it from material used to judge it and targets whose necessary changes the
inquiry reveals. These are contextual roles: reading a page as evidence does
not select it for modification.

| Starting question | Focus and connected inquiry |
| --- | --- |
| Revise philosophy or situated understanding | Assess the affected claim or relation against experience and actual use; carry adopted meaning into dependent Skills, prompts, tools, or arrangements. |
| Repair an environment | Establish actual behavior at its owner; use relevant philosophy and accounts to articulate the intended contribution. Return a mistaken premise or interpretation to its owner too. |
| Repair a named local part | Serve the intended result through that part; widen mutable scope when an evidenced dependency makes another change necessary. |
| Borrow a method for an unformed difficulty | Develop the intended activity, competing explanations, and a useful comparison from available expression and actual work; change only the supported scope. |

Trace both directions:

`understood process and relation → computational articulation → contextual realization → observed result`

`observed result → examine correspondence, articulation, and realization → revised understanding`

A working failure can concern implementation, interpretation, or the relation
itself. A configuration can reflect a default or constraint, and success does
not establish the whole philosophy. Determine the affected level before writing.

Locate the failure by asking what each explanation would make different. If an
instruction says to recover the next action but the consumer never receives
that instruction, delivery is a plausible implementation failure. If the
consumer receives and follows it but produces a recap when a usable opening
was needed, the meaning of “recover” may be inadequate. If it provides an
opening and carries authorized work, yet this displaces a practice the person
wanted to learn, the chosen division of participation may be wrong. Similar
surface complaints can arise at these different levels; changing a prompt's
wording indiscriminately would leave their causes untouched.

For a semantic change—purpose, claim, role, judgment, or way of working—inspect
the affected connections, including a Skill's stated purpose and the rules
realizing it. Carry necessary source, website, personal-account, Skill, and
environment changes through the same bounded task. Publication can follow
dependencies; an early page release does not close remaining required work.
An equivalent label, quotation mark, or layout change usually needs its own
source and rendering checks. Follow a newly exposed semantic issue if one appears.

For example, allocating implementation judgment to the model may require
repairing a Skill that still sends those choices to the person. Actual use may
instead show that a particular judgment depends on personal experience
unavailable to the model. That result can refine both the allocation and the
Skill. Explain material scope changes through such dependencies.

## The preference-harness analogy

The [preference-harness essay](docs/preference-harness.md) develops the analogy
and its formal distinctions. Its practical relevance is that purposes and
preferences can guide a working view while evidence and inference still matter.
Keep coherent working concepts for stability, retaining conflicting evidence
and revising the concepts when warranted.

Ordinary reasoning remains informal natural language. The analogy requires
neither a formal preference model, repair enumeration, nor a chosen calculus
before action. A preference does not make a factual claim true, and coherence
is not a formal proof obligation for collaboration.

For example, one can prefer a counterpart that carries more of the work while
still discovering that it lacks a needed tool or relevant knowledge. The
preference helps select the contribution to pursue; it cannot establish that
the contribution is currently possible. A coherent working view lets inquiry
continue without pretending that the rejected possibilities have disappeared.
If the preferred arrangement repeatedly adds supervision or recovery work,
retain that evidence when reconsidering it. This is the practical force of
separating selection of a view from what follows under that view.

## Derive behavior in context

Recover the contribution the environment should make, the ongoing activity and
relation it should sustain, and what it actually does. This modeling belongs to
the cooperation between person and language model, starting with what problem
to pursue and continuing through inference, implementation, and feedback.
For the part to be represented or acted through a computer, jointly articulate a computational model at
the relevant boundary and scale: what occurs, which states and conditions
matter, what remains the same through change, and which relations and rules
govern possible transformations. A stable object or static view can capture
what the question needs to hold fixed within that activity.

Connect these meanings to observations and expected consequences. A state called
“complete,” for example, must correspond to the intended work having reached
its result under the relevant conditions; the presence of a message or label
alone cannot establish it. The distinction can be represented in natural
language that a model actually uses to interpret and continue the work. A
program or formal schema can make particular conditions precise where needed;
simulation additionally requires an account of the changes being simulated.
Choose that precision from the actual use rather than requiring a complete
simulation or formal portrait for ordinary collaboration. The person may
recognize an experience or mismatch that the model can unfold into states,
possible consequences, and checks. Either can generate, judge, and revise;
allocate contributions by their actual capabilities and available modalities,
letting the same cooperation carry the model into use and learn from its effects.

Realize the selected model through fitting instructions, tools, and interfaces,
with enough reason for the contribution to function independently. This is a
dependency of meaning and implementation, not a fixed order of drafting:
trying an interface can reveal a missing state or a mistaken process boundary.
A metaphor is not a lossless compiler to a universal prompt; downstream
contributors need the relevant model and fitting instructions rather than the
whole personal account or a repeated philosophical preface.

Write at the existing semantic owner: general interaction at its shared
instruction source, teaching at its teaching method, tool behavior at its tool
or workflow. Reconcile incompatible old and new requirements rather than adding
another layer. Different consumers need different articulation when their
contracts or observed behavior warrant it, not merely because their model names
differ. Metaphor does not change actual permissions, consent, or safety.

Consider the return-to-work difficulty above as a fictional diagnostic example.
A summary accurately preserves a question and previous discussion, yet the
person must repeatedly locate the current draft and reconstruct where to begin.
The record has preserved content; that observation alone does not show that
it supports resumption. Perhaps the summary is hard to find, perhaps it lacks
the relation between the open question and the draft, or perhaps every return
requires an unnecessary decision about how to proceed. Each possibility calls
for a different repair.

Inspect a return at the point where it stalls. If the record cannot be reached,
repairing its locator may suffice. If it is reached and understood but does not
make a next action available, a more detailed recap may simply add reading.
The model can instead connect the unresolved claim to the actual draft, prepare
an editable opening, or carry a bounded authorized revision. If the person
wants to practice constructing the argument, the useful contribution may be
an intelligible comparison of openings rather than a completed replacement.
The same wish to continue can need doing, explanation, disagreement, or a shared
attempt. Intended participation and actual capability determine which fits.

Suppose the record is already easy to find, the draft is accessible, and the
purpose is to continue producing the argument with model support. The selected
change is to make the existing continuation instruction connect the open
question to the draft and carry the next authorized piece of work. The model
can write that instruction and implement its dependencies from the available
evidence. A concise natural-language articulation might say: recover the open
question in the current draft, explain the unresolved difference, and make the
next authorized contribution usable there; ask for purpose that the model cannot
infer from the available evidence, while carrying the workflow reconstruction.
Its exact wording belongs to that environment and must be reconciled with its
existing instructions.
This is enough meaning to try the change without inventing a universal state
schema or a new record store. The [participation essay](docs/participation-and-support.md#continuation-records-and-relief)
develops why a record, a carried action, relief, and development remain distinct.

## Attention, participation, and inquiry

Determine which contributions the person wants to make and what the model can
carry. Both can generate and judge; a chosen division of work does not establish
incapacity. Inspect actual listening, speaking, reading, and writing channels
where participation depends on them. Existing memory and visual capabilities
can support the encounter without creating another portrait or storage system.

Attention has costs. A small implementation question can require reconstructing
a local problem and then recovering the broader thread. Carry that work when
evidence and model judgment can settle it. A recognition of mismatch is useful
input from which the model can develop an explanation and result; the person
need not supply a specification.

Make consequential tradeoffs visible. Investigate uncertainty and perform
authorized work rather than silently guessing or stopping early. A genuinely
unavailable fact or user-owned purpose, preference, or consent stays with its
actual owner; applicable inquiry and interaction methods obtain it. This
reference adds no approval gate or question quota.

Teaching questions can develop understanding; research can retain competing
interpretations while an execution choice is already possible. Those methods
own their judgments. Assess whether avoidable detail questions decrease,
important choices remain intelligible, repeated user repair decreases, and the
work reaches its purpose. Counting questions alone would reward concealed error.

In the [attention example](design-and-revision.md#move-between-expression-relation-and-realization),
the chosen relation would change a source that still delegates routine layout
choices to the person. Read that complete source so an older “ask about every
choice” rule does not survive beside the new allocation. At the consumer, a
relevant task should show the model resolving the routine choice from evidence,
making its important consequences intelligible, and carrying the authorized
work; a question about unavailable purpose should still be possible. If the
model asks no questions but chooses the wrong goal, the result challenges the
repair rather than confirming attention protection. This is a real-behavior
criterion to use when such a task occurs, not a claim that this fictional case
has been tested.

## Choose a fitting realization

Choose carriers that preserve the relevant states, relations, and changes.
Use natural language for relations and flexible judgment; use deterministic
carriers for concrete mechanics where their actual boundary justifies them.
Either can become rigid or unnecessarily elaborate. Repair an existing conflict
or remove machinery where possible before introducing another layer. An edited
instruction or Skill can be the complete implementation; this method implies
no optimizer, background rewrite service, registry, or new state protocol.

## Carry the change to evidence and continuity

Keep the interpretation and expected effect reviewable in the task. Implement
through the actual source and publication route, then read back the consumer
and check the observable consequence against the modeled meaning and purpose.
Where it differs, examine the process selected, the representational correspondence,
and the implementation rather than assuming one of them is settled. Retrieval establishes availability.
A short real-case comparison can substantiate a bounded behavior; lasting
collaboration and human development need actual use. Neither requires a new
permanent evaluation platform.

For the continuation example, read the revised source through the same entry
by which the working model receives it, then inspect an actual return to the
draft within the authorized task. Does the model connect the open claim to the
right passage and produce a usable next contribution? Does the person still
have to recover the same missing context or repair the direction? A correct
instruction file with unchanged consumer behavior suggests a delivery,
precedence, or capability issue to investigate. A faithfully followed instruction
that adds reading without enabling continuation challenges the interpretation
of support. A finished passage that removes wanted practice challenges the
chosen relation. Those outcomes identify different next changes.

Judge the whole arrangement's costs. An extra summary, handoff, or status check
may save retrieval while adding supervision; a seemingly helpful suggestion
may leave the actual action to be organized again. Compare what became possible
with what the participants had to do to obtain it. A usable continuation in one
task establishes a bounded contribution. Relief requires the person's experience;
reliable collaboration requires later use and correction. Development further
concerns what the person can understand, generate, judge, or do, including
through dependable supports. These are related observations, not interchangeable
success measures. If the arrangement consistently optimizes output while
frustrating the development it was meant to serve, return to that orientation
as well as the instructions that express it.

Compare the intended result with the observable outcome. Continue at the
implementation, interpretation, or conceptual owner when a required difference
remains. Retain useful rationale and evidence at existing owners; one task
result does not establish a permanent personal trait or require rewriting the
whole model. The person contributes recognition and direction while the model
carries the explicit work of maintaining this reciprocal connection.
