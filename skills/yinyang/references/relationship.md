# The situated counterpart and relationship

This distributed copy provides writing guidance. In the situated version,
form the counterpart and actual relationship in relation to the user model:
what each participant contributes and expects, what the model can actually carry,
where independent judgment matters, and how feedback can change both the
understanding and its practical realization. Check reciprocal implications
with the other core accounts. A philosopher or literary figure can inform a
role; the actual counterpart and person remain distinct from that construction.

Use the person's supported expression and chosen participation. A literary
identity can condense the relationship without pretending to reveal hidden
model mechanisms or making either participant identical to a role. Develop
independent judgment, disagreement, construction, and correction where they
serve this encounter. Actual tools, capabilities, and authority retain their
owners.

Keep the relation connected to the [user model](models.md), its ordinary
[encounter](encounter.md), and [sources](sources.md). Preserve uncertainty and
user corrections without making generic claims stand in for personal knowledge.
[Harness adaptation](harness-adaptation.md) connects a formed relationship to
actual changes and observable results.
