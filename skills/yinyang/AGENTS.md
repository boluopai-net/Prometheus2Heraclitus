# YinYang package maintenance

YinYang is a working philosophical and literary example, including the Skill
itself. Preserve its developed account while allowing recipients to combine
resources or author a different philosophy and organization. The parity and
fixed-slot rules below maintain this implementation; they do not constrain
independent works. See `references/docs/example-and-commons.md` for this scope.

Read `MODEL.md` before maintaining this package. It owns the model-facing
interpretation, reader/maintenance division, source limits, and publication
boundary. English is the first reading language for English-capable readers and
models. `references/docs/core-views.md` owns the English viewpoints;
`references/docs/core-views.zh-CN.md` is their complete Chinese counterpart. Keep both
README editions, viewpoint editions, and corresponding diagrams semantically
aligned. Model-facing maintenance prose remains English.

`SKILL.md` is the reusable formation entrypoint within the
Prometheus2Heraclitus collection. Its `references/` own design and revision,
adaptation, and reading by purpose. The main conversational model reads the
entrypoint and complete `references/{encounter,models,relationship,usage,sources}.md` before
substantive work, reusing complete current text already in context.
Distributed and situated packages share the same
structure and byte-identical common content; only personal content differs.
The five fixed slots `references/{encounter,models,relationship,usage,sources}.md`
contain formation instructions in the distributed package and grounded accounts
in the situated package. Formalized user accounts remain personal. Provenance and unreleased
private materials live under `references/personal/`, whose filenames follow
actual evidence rather than a universal private file list.

Keep `SKILL.md`, documents, diagrams, method references, reading map, package
administration, READMEs, and the OpenAI YAML adapter common. The shared GitHub
package is their canonical owner. `scripts/sync-common.py` checks by default;
`--apply` projects common source into a target, preserving all five situated
slots and all of `references/personal/`. Native owners realize local and private
Web consumers. Do not export personal material, create a personal-upload service,
or fork the common schema. Formation relates the chosen purpose, existing
philosophy, authorized actual environment, work, and relevant personal material.
Ground personal claims in attributable evidence, drawing on psychological or
preference material where needed. Preserve the formation method's conditional
practical routes without turning them into a universal sequence or requiring
a complete portrait before creation and implementation. A copied portrait or
fictional demonstration cannot supply personal substance.

`references/docs/creation-method.md` owns the creation method. Philosophical
images are developed within the relevant essays and presented independently;
there is no separate anthology. The essays have different roles; the homepage
explains their relations without numbering them as a compulsory sequence.
External repositories inform directory structure only, not the substance of
the philosophical account.

Sun and Moon express YinYang through differentiated generating, receiving,
shaping, and returning. They are relational imagery, not identities attained
by person or model. Roles and emphases can be retained or exchanged without
gender assignment. Cultivation develops generative capacities, pictured as
acquiring the Sun's illuminating power within an ongoing movement from fire
toward fire. Earth is the material and shared living environment that answers
work; creations grow through care and acquire relative independence there.
Keep dependent passages aligned with `references/docs/yinyang-and-taiji.md`
and its complete Chinese counterpart. Preserve ceaseless transformation,
mutual grounding, the role/participant distinction, and the wider world's
independent conditions.

`references/docs/writing-a-sinthome.md` owns the philosophical application of Lacan's
sinthome to individual meta-harness writing and human agency. Keep the complete
Chinese counterpart and YY-13 aligned. Preserve its source attribution,
singular ongoing practice, and actual conditions of agency; do not turn it into
a diagnosis, total self-knowledge requirement, or omnipotence claim.

The ontology is computational: question-relative objects, roles, relations,
transformations, inference, and feedback within a generative process exceeding
any single view. Physical realization and reducibility are separate open
questions. Preserve the distinction between retained sources, competing
candidates, and accepted working views. Diagrams explain these same claims;
they do not substitute a physical scene or mandatory pipeline for the ontology.

Do not retrieve private personal materials merely to work on this package.

`references/sources.md` is the source-owner index; `references/personal/`
holds precise topical provenance and unreleased material. Maintain one authored
philosophy in the common essays, even when it is the person's own philosophy.
Keep actual environment use in `references/usage.md`, not in provenance notes.
