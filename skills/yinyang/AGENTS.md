# YinYang package maintenance

Use [MODEL.md](MODEL.md) for the package's source roles, interpretive boundaries,
and maintenance method. The [core views](references/docs/core-views.md) state the
numbered claims; essays develop their arguments. Read the affected source and
its dependencies rather than reproducing the full philosophy in every file.

The user-facing work should be readable both within each piece and across the
collection. Existing wording, section order, and examples are revisable.
Preserve the relations that carry the argument, useful source attribution, and
actual consumer contracts; remove repetition and obsolete scaffolding.

## Authored and situated sources

[SKILL.md](SKILL.md) owns invocation. [Forming and revising working models](references/design-and-revision.md)
connects philosophical expression and models. The six writing guides
under `references/formation/`, including [how to revise](references/formation/revision.md),
own their actual directions. Environmental realization belongs to the environment
guide; revision guidance belongs to the revision guide. [The package guide](references/docs/skill-package.md)
owns common/situated structure and projection. Refer to these owners where a
document needs their method instead of restating it.

Shared editions retain six public formation guides as content lenses. Hindsight is
now the default formation engine: selected Philosophy, Personal, environment and
history sources become native facts, revisable observations and question-defined
Mental Models/Knowledge Pages. [Native formation](references/hindsight.md) owns
configuration and exact operations. There is no fixed output count or mandatory
manual Personal condensation stage.

Private `references/situated/index.md` binds the service/bank, model questions,
IDs and generated bodies and selects complete startup reading;
`references/situated/revision.md` states the actual execution method. Source
evidence retains its owners. Personal keeps readable topic articles in its four
subject groups; native pages may maintain them, with observations backstage.
Direct editing and organization remain available. Migrate affected articles as
revision needs, preserving their reading paths. Old six-file bodies are migration/history, not a second
maintained authoritative set. Public packages contain no private portraits.
Common projection protects both private trees and never changes bank state.
Preserve detailed philosophical content and distinct formation contributions
while changing their realization; six philosophical dimensions remain valid.

## Edition and publication coherence

Keep complete English and Chinese reader editions aligned; model-facing
maintenance prose is English. Preserve existing YY identifiers as references
to their claims. Repair links when changing titles, paths, or anchors.

Diagram candidate JSON owns the authored relationships. Regenerate HTML/SVG
and inspect the resulting relationships and readability when a diagram changes.
Website and plugin copies are projections; edit their actual source owner.
The public package owns the philosophical essays and practical guide; the
native Site owns their presentation.

Preserve licenses, third-party notices, and public/private boundaries under
[MODEL.md](MODEL.md) and [LICENSE](LICENSE). The philosophy is a usable example:
recipients may adopt, combine, challenge, or author a different work.
