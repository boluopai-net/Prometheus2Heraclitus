# Work-plan reference binding

Resolve `work_plan` through [recipient configuration](configuration.md) only when
the learning decision depends on the recipient's actual work priorities or
constraints. The recipient's existing work-plan owner retains its authoritative
body and updates. This reference contains no personal work plan and must not
become a competing copy or execution log.

Read the relevant bounded portion and distinguish attributable priorities,
observed project constraints and teacher proposals. A career or project choice
is not settled by a course mapping or a hypothetical employment-market claim.
Teaching, research context, local attention and actual execution retain their
own owners; a work-plan link does not grant implementation or publication
authority. Missing access limits that comparison, not ordinary explanation.

If an authorized change belongs to the work plan, apply it at its actual owner
with read-before-write, preservation of unrelated content and readback. Otherwise
retain a clearly unsaved proposal and the evidence needed for the owner to
consider it. Neither Teacher creates a cross-conversation messaging protocol.
