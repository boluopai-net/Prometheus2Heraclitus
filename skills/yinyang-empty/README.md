# yinyang-empty

**Keep reverse alignment, YinYang dialectics, and the formation methods; develop your own philosophy and personal version.**

[简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) · [Daily collaboration](SKILL.md) · [Full edition](https://github.com/boluopai-net/Prometheus2Heraclitus/tree/main/skills/yinyang)

Empty includes the six formation guides, Core views as attributed suggestions,
the practice overview, formation and revision, daily collaboration, and guidance
for organizing Personal. It does not contain `docs/` or `diagrams/`. The author's
developed essays and diagrams remain in Full and on the website for optional reading.

Reverse alignment and crossed participation with dialectical return are shared
core commitments of these editions. Other suggestions do not become your adopted
philosophy merely because you installed the package. Your actual philosophy,
personal material, and question-defined working models are formed with you, from your
purposes, experience, sources, and environment. The model can carry the writing
and implementation; you need not do it unaided.

The `references/formation/` directory contains six guides, each in English and
Chinese: orientation, person, counterpart, relationship, environment, and revision.
[Hindsight](references/hindsight.md) forms question-defined native models. The private `references/situated/index.md` binds their actual service, bank and bodies; `revision.md` states execution. Output count follows the questions. Evidence and
maintained personal understanding belong in private `references/personal/`;
your philosophical writing can stay at its existing owner or under private
`references/philosophy/`. Empty includes four unfilled Personal areas and six
Situated starter files marked `status: unfilled`; their existence does not mean
the actual accounts have been formed. The Personal areas are preferences and
selection, psychological experience and assessment, personal conditions and
everyday life, and self-expression and works. Fill them from actual material
with the model; no personal facts or portraits are supplied.

The [Codex installation guide](https://github.com/boluopai-net/Prometheus2Heraclitus/blob/main/docs/codex.md)
offers `yinyang-empty@boluopai`. Choose Full or Empty for the same personal version,
and keep personal additions outside replaceable plugin caches. Public method
updates retain your own content; they do not install Full's philosophical corpus.
