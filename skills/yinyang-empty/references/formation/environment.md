# How to describe and realize the environment

<a id="how-to-describe-environmental-realization"></a>

[简体中文](environment.zh-CN.md) · [Forming and revising working models](../design-and-revision.md) · [Revision](revision.md)

Write the actual environment account at `references/situated/environment.md`
in the private package. Explain how this collaboration takes working form
through material, social, informational, and computational arrangements. Connect
the adopted [orientation](orientation.md), [person](person.md),
[counterpart](counterpart.md), and [relationship](relationship.md) to actual
possibilities and consequences. In this YinYang, the central relation to realize
is the participants' passage between open generation and consequential scrutiny,
with each able to return through the other's contribution.
A configuration inventory says what exists;
this account explains what it enables, for whom, under which conditions.

Include the directions needed to use, maintain, and change this environment.
A **Harness** is its working arrangement of prompts, Skills, tools, interfaces,
and operating conditions. Here the model's Harness and the person's environment
are coupled, so the arrangement serves both participants. Explain what a change
makes available to each: retrieval affects model continuation and the person's
need to reconstruct context; presentation affects model delivery and human
understanding and judgment. Realization belongs in this account: how understood
relations become behavior, how to change that behavior, and how observed effects
return to understanding. People and models can develop and use this guidance
together; neither needs a finished theory before useful construction begins.

## Give the relation a reachable working form

Start with activities the participants undertake and the points where a
contribution depends on a resource, channel, or action route. A person may
express an unfinished idea in speech; the counterpart receives a transcript,
recovers a work, inspects sources, and writes or implements a result. Explain
the passage among these contributions. The environment includes the person's
attention, places, other people, and social evaluation where they affect the
activity, as well as the computer through which contributions are realized.

Ground the account in current evidence. Identify maintained owners and usable
locators. A human-visible file is not automatically model-readable; a named
service is not automatically callable. An advisory contribution needs a route
to its execution owner; an executable action needs observable readback.
Capability, authentication, and authority remain with their actual owners.

Describe ordinary routes: where a selected text lives, how an explanation
reaches the person, how both return to a disputed passage, how a project is
recovered, and where a result can be inspected. State present gaps and conditions.
The account should let its reader find and use the arrangement, without copying
a complete configuration inventory.

Give this relation three connected supports: writing that preserves an
understanding for reconsideration; interaction through which a contribution
can reach and change the other participant's work; and resources through which
the work becomes usable and meets independent consequences. Explain what each
support enables in the actual setting.

Make the passage between whole and detail usable. A claim, its source or counterexample, and the
changed result should be reachable without reconstructing the entire history.
The model can carry the source checking while exposing grounds the person can
question. A preview or editable draft can let the person's next contribution
change the work; it need not become a compulsory approval step. Describe the
actual channel and remaining cost, rather than adding machinery merely to
represent the philosophical image.

## Choose the focus and trace both directions

For local repair and whole-environment refresh, follow the revision guide's
[scope and connected-change guidance](revision.md#scope-and-connected-changes).
Reading an account as evidence does not select it for rewriting. A substantive
dependency can nevertheless require changing another account, Skill, or tool.

## Derive behavior in context

Connect intended activity to an observable contribution:

`understood process and relation → computational articulation → working realization → observed effects`

`observed effects → examine meaning and realization → revised understanding`

These dependencies do not prescribe a fixed order. Trying an interface can
reveal a missing distinction; observing a result can change the chosen division
of participation or the purpose itself. Either participant can propose,
reason, object, and revise throughout this work.

For the part a computer will represent or act on, articulate relevant objects,
events, states, conditions, and changes. What persists through revision? What
changes when an operation succeeds? A “complete” state must correspond to the
intended result under relevant conditions, rather than merely to a closing
message. The account selects relations within activity; it does not exhaust
the person or process represented. [World, process, and expression](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/world-and-expression.html#physical-ground-and-computational-abstraction)
develops these distinctions.

Clear natural language can give them usable computational form when a model
actually interprets and acts on it. Typed data, equations, programs, or schemas
help where use needs more precise conditions. Simulation additionally specifies
how represented situations change under assumptions. Choose precision by the
question and actual consumer; ordinary support need not simulate a whole person
or activity. A literary image can disclose a relation without compiling losslessly
into a universal prompt.

<a id="the-preference-harness-analogy"></a>

Purposes and preferences take working form through the model, tools, interfaces,
and everyday conditions. The environment can participate in the person's extended
selection capacity, including patterns made visible only through later comparison.
Explain what operates, why it is understood that way, and how the person can
reshape it. A chosen direction does not by itself establish tool availability
or the effectiveness of a particular realization.
Keep working concepts coherent enough to guide action while retaining conflicting
evidence and unresolved alternatives. The [Preference Harness essay](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/preference-harness.html)
develops this distinction between selecting a view and judging what follows from
it. Ordinary cooperation needs no formal preference model or repair calculus.

## Locate the passage that needs support

Understanding, retrieval, beginning, responding, stopping, and switching are
different achievements. An explanation can clarify a relation while leaving
the next action inaccessible. Describe the actual passage that needs support,
the contributions involved, and where it fails. Keep a proposed route recognizable
as a proposal until its availability and effects are established.

Consider a fictional return to an unfinished argument. The saved note accurately
recalls the discussion, yet leaves the person to find the draft and reconstruct
where to begin. The missing relation joins the open question, its passage in
the current draft, and an available next contribution. More recap may preserve
content without supplying that relation.

A fitting direction could ask the model to recover the question in the draft,
explain the unresolved difference, and prepare or carry the next authorized
contribution there. A direct passage link or editable opening can make it usable.
If the purpose is to practice constructing the argument, comparing possible
openings may fit better than a completed replacement. The instruction, interface,
and allocation express the same relation; actual use tests whether they do so.

## Attention, participation, and inquiry

Allocate contributions by actual capability, available channels, and wanted
participation. A person may recognize a difficulty through use while the model
reconstructs its dependencies; either can offer a better explanation. A chosen
division does not establish either participant's incapacity.

Carry implementation choices that available evidence and model judgment can
settle, make consequential differences intelligible, and obtain purpose or
experience that remains genuinely unavailable. Reconcile complete instruction
sources so incompatible old and new allocations do not survive together.
Specialist inquiry and teaching methods judge when a question develops
understanding or obtains a needed judgment; no question quota follows.

Check actual listening, speaking, reading, and writing channels where participation
depends on them. A transcript and access to sound support different contributions.
Assess the whole cost: a tool may save operations while adding setup, monitoring,
supervision, handoff repair, or repeated context reconstruction.

## Choose a fitting realization

Use natural language for relations and flexible judgment, and deterministic
mechanisms for concrete operations where the boundary warrants them. An edited
instruction or Skill may be the complete implementation. An inaccessible draft
may need a retrieval path; a difficult comparison may need an interface keeping
alternatives visible. Repair conflicts and remove displaced machinery rather
than surrounding it with another layer.

Give each consumer enough context and reason to perform its contribution
independently. Different contracts or observed behavior can justify different
instructions; model names alone do not. The environment account records which
routes fit here, without introducing a registry or background rewrite service.
Existing permissions, consent, safety, and publication authority retain their scope.

## Relation to authoring and management methods

YinYang connects purpose and situated understanding with the contribution an
environment should make. Specialist Skills keep their domain methods and task
responsibilities. General interaction belongs at its shared instruction source,
teaching at its teaching method, and tool behavior at its tool or workflow.
Link these owners rather than copying their procedures or personal portraits.

For prompts and Skills, use the actual environment's authoring method; where
available, `prompt-and-skill-lifecycle` supplies review, packaging, publication,
and readback. An advisory counterpart and an execution owner have different
operational responsibilities. Helpers receive bounded relevant context under
the entrypoint's personal-model boundary. Philosophy creates no extra authority.

## Let realization correct the account

Implement through the actual source and publication route, then read back what
the intended consumer receives. Check behavior through the real public entrypoint,
or a faithful isolated substitute when appropriate. A changed source establishes
an edit; retrieval establishes availability. Acceptance also needs the relevant
observable consequence.

For the return-to-writing example, inspect an actual return in an authorized
task. Does the open claim lead to the correct passage and a usable contribution?
If the instruction never reaches the model, investigate delivery or precedence.
If it is followed but adds only recap, revise the interpretation of support.
If a finished passage displaces wanted practice, reconsider the division of
participation. These outcomes call for different repairs.

Keep planned and realized arrangements distinct. An unavailable route may need
repair; persistent poor fit may challenge the person account, relationship,
or orientation. A record preserves material, action changes work, relief concerns
experience, and development concerns what the person can understand or do,
including through dependable supports. The [participation essay](https://boluopai.net/repositories/prometheus/source/skills/yinyang/references/docs/participation-and-support.html#continuation-records-and-relief)
develops their different grounds. One successful task cannot establish them all.

Use the revision guide's [result and revision guidance](revision.md#follow-the-result-back-into-understanding)
to carry necessary changes through their owners and compare the intended result
with what is available. Keep private locators and extended evidence with their
private owners. This account condenses a richer, changing environment; a work
can finish while the arrangement remains revisable.
